Hip synovitis (inflamed joint lining)

Swelling of the thin lining inside the hip joint, causing hip or groin pain

ConditionBones, joints & spinePelvisBones & muscles

What it is

The thin lining inside the hip joint becomes inflamed and swollen. In children it is often short-lived, and it can make them limp.

  • Hip, groin or thigh
  • Often young children
  • Hip pain, limp
Which doctor
Family doctor, paediatrician for children, bone doctor
Common in
Young children, often after a cold or virus; also adults
Contagious
No, but a recent virus may have started it
Course
Often settles in days to a couple of weeks, but needs a doctor's check

Common signs

More signs

  • The hip is stiff and hard to move, especially when turning outwards
  • A child may keep the leg bent and turned out to feel more comfortable

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Often follows a cold, sore throat or other virus
  • In adults, arthritis or overuse of the joint

See a doctor first if

  • A child with hip pain and fever, or a child who looks very unwell: go to hospital today
  • A hot, red, swollen or very painful hip, because it may be an infection
  • A child who will not put any weight on the leg: see a doctor promptly
  • Hip pain after a fall or injury, or a leg that looks bent or out of shape
  • A limp that lasts more than a few days or keeps coming back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Let the hip rest and avoid running or jumping until a doctor says it is safe
  • Offer plenty of fluids and quiet play while it settles
  • Do not give any medicine to a child without asking a doctor or pharmacist

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy
