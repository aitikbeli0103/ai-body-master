Broken heel bone

A break in the heel bone at the back of the foot

ConditionAnkle & footBones & muscles

What it is

The heel bone takes the weight of the body when we stand and walk. A hard landing from a height can crack or crush it, causing strong pain and swelling in the heel.

  • Heel
  • After a fall from a height
  • Heel pain, cannot stand
Which doctor
Emergency department; orthopaedic doctor
Common in
People after falls from a height or road accidents; also workers on ladders
Contagious
No
Course
Often slow; it can take months to walk comfortably again

Common signs

More signs

  • Strong pain in the heel right after a fall
  • The heel may look wider or out of shape

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Landing hard on the feet after a fall from a height
  • Weak bones can break with a smaller force
  • Sometimes a tiny crack from repeated heavy stress

See a doctor first if

  • Heel pain after a fall from a height: do not walk on it, go to hospital
  • Back pain with the heel pain after a fall: keep still and call 999
  • A foot that is cold, pale, blue or numb: go to hospital now
  • Bone showing through the skin or a deep wound
  • Heavy swelling or blisters on the heel, or a calf that is swollen and painful

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not put weight on the foot until a doctor says you can
  • Raise the foot on pillows above the level of the heart to ease swelling
  • Use crutches or a walking frame the way you were shown

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Weak bones
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Recent back injury or surgery

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
