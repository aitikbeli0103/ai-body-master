Reactive arthritis

Joint pain and swelling that starts after an infection elsewhere in the body

ConditionAutoimmune & rheumaticBones & muscles

What it is

Some weeks after an infection in the gut or the urinary and genital area, a joint can become painful and swollen, often a knee or ankle. The joint itself is not infected.

  • Knees, ankles, feet
  • After an infection
  • Painful, swollen joint
Which doctor
Joint doctor (rheumatologist), family doctor
Common in
Young and middle-aged adults after an infection
Contagious
The arthritis is not, but the first infection may be
Course
Often settles over weeks to months, but can come back

Common signs

Why it happens

  • An earlier infection of the gut, such as from bad food
  • An earlier infection of the urinary or genital area
  • The body's defence system reacts too strongly after the infection
  • Some people are more likely to react because of their genes

See a doctor first if

  • A hot, red, very painful joint with fever: see a doctor today
  • Red, painful eye or blurry vision: see a doctor promptly
  • Cannot put weight on the leg
  • Burning when passing urine or a discharge
  • Bloody or long-lasting diarrhoea

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
