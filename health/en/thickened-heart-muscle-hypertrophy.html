Thickened heart muscle (hypertrophy)

The wall of the heart muscle has become thicker than normal

SymptomHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

The heart muscle grows thicker, often because the heart has been working harder than usual. Some people feel nothing; it is found by a heart check.

  • The heart
  • More common with age
  • Tires, short of breath
Which doctor
Heart doctor (cardiologist)
Common in
Adults with long-standing high blood pressure; some people inherit it
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long term; a heart doctor can explain checks and care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A narrowed heart valve or other heart disease
  • It can run in families
  • Very heavy training in athletes can thicken it a little

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, or trouble breathing at rest: call 999
  • Fainting, especially during or after effort: call 999
  • A racing or very uneven heartbeat that does not settle
  • Swollen legs or waking up short of breath at night
  • A close relative who died suddenly from a heart problem

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Thickened heart muscle (hypertrophy)?

The wall of the heart muscle has become thicker than normal. The heart muscle grows thicker, often because the heart has been working harder than usual. Some people feel nothing; it is found by a heart check.

What are the common signs of Thickened heart muscle (hypertrophy)?

Short of breath on stairs · Tight, heavy chest · Racing, pounding heart · Feeling faint · Swollen legs and ankles

Why does Thickened heart muscle (hypertrophy) happen?

High blood sugar or blood pressure · Extra body weight

How can I look after myself with Thickened heart muscle (hypertrophy)?

Take medicine as your doctor says · Check your blood pressure at home · Keep a healthy weight · Stay active every day · Stop smoking
