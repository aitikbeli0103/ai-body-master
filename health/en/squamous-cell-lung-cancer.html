Squamous cell lung cancer

A type of lung cancer that starts in the flat cells lining the airways

ConditionCancer & tumoursChest

What it is

This is one type of lung cancer. It usually begins in the larger airways near the middle of the lung and is strongly linked to smoking.

  • Lungs and airways
  • Linked to smoking
  • Cough that does not go away
Which doctor
Family doctor first; chest doctor (respiratory), cancer doctor (oncologist)
Common in
Older adults; current or past smokers
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on how early it is found; your care team can explain

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Air pollution and a family history of lung cancer

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden chest pain or severe trouble breathing: call 999
  • Coughing up blood
  • A cough that lasts for weeks, or a cough that has changed
  • Chest pain, a hoarse voice or wheezing that does not go away
  • Weight loss without trying, or feeling very tired
  • Fever with a cough that will not clear

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop or change treatment on your own
  • If you smoke, ask for help to stop, and stay away from smoke
  • Ask your team about breathing, pain, food and emotional support

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • A heart condition
  • High blood pressure
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Fever or an active infection

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Squamous cell lung cancer?

A type of lung cancer that starts in the flat cells lining the airways. This is one type of lung cancer. It usually begins in the larger airways near the middle of the lung and is strongly linked to smoking.

What are the common signs of Squamous cell lung cancer?

Cough with phlegm · Short of breath on stairs · Sharp pain when breathing in · Losing weight without trying

Why does Squamous cell lung cancer happen?

Smoking · Smoke, haze and strong smells · Dust or chemicals at work

How can I look after myself with Squamous cell lung cancer?

See your doctor for check-ups · Rest well

Can an AI Body Master session help with Squamous cell lung cancer?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Squamous cell lung cancer and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
