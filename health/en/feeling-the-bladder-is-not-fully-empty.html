Feeling the bladder is not fully empty

Feeling that urine is left behind after you finish passing it

SymptomKidneys & urinaryAbdomen

What it is

After going to the toilet, the bladder still feels full, so you want to go again soon. Sometimes urine really is left behind in the bladder.

  • Bladder, lower belly
  • Older men, also women
  • Still full after going
Which doctor
Family doctor; a urine doctor (urologist) if it continues
Common in
Older men with a large prostate; women after childbirth or menopause
Contagious
No, though an infection behind it may be
Course
Depends on the cause; a simple check can show if urine is left behind

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Bladder infection or irritation of the bladder
  • Nerve problems, for example from diabetes

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine at all, with a painful full lower belly: go to hospital now
  • Fever, shaking chills, or pain in the back or side
  • Blood in the urine
  • Weak legs, numbness in the groin, or loss of control of bowel or bladder: go to hospital now
  • Leaking urine, or getting steadily worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

More ways to look after yourself
