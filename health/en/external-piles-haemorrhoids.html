External piles (haemorrhoids)

Swollen veins under the skin around the anus that can itch, hurt or bleed

ConditionStomach & intestinesPelvis

What it is

The small blood vessels under the skin around the anus swell up, often from straining, which can cause itching, soreness, a lump and sometimes bright red blood after passing stools.

  • Around the anus
  • Common in adults and pregnancy
  • Itch, soreness, a lump
Which doctor
Family doctor; a bowel specialist (colorectal surgeon)
Common in
Adults; more often with constipation, pregnancy or long sitting
Contagious
No
Course
Mild piles often ease in days to weeks, but they can come back

Common signs

More signs

  • A soft, tender lump or lumps around the anus
  • Discomfort when sitting, or after a bowel movement
  • Sudden strong pain with a hard lump, if a clot forms inside one

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-lasting diarrhoea
  • Sitting a long time, heavy lifting, or being overweight

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy bleeding that does not stop, or bleeding with feeling faint or dizzy: go to hospital now
  • Sudden severe pain with a hard, bluish lump: see a doctor today
  • A hot, red, swollen area with fever, or a lump that oozes pus
  • Blood mixed in the stools, black stools, or bleeding that keeps happening: it is not always piles
  • A change in bowel habit that lasts for weeks, or losing weight without trying

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Eat more fibre (vegetables, fruit, whole grains) and drink enough water
  • Do not strain or sit for long on the toilet; do not ignore the urge to go
  • Sitting in a shallow tub of warm water for a few minutes can ease soreness

Tell your therapist

  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pregnancy
  • Broken, red or sore skin

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
