Herpes simplex encephalitis

A serious infection of the brain caused by the herpes simplex virus

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

The herpes simplex virus can reach the brain and make it swollen and inflamed. It needs urgent care in hospital.

  • Brain
  • Caused by a virus
  • Fever, headache, confusion
Which doctor
Go to hospital: brain and nerve doctor (neurologist)
Common in
Can happen at any age, in people who carry the herpes virus
Contagious
The virus spreads by close contact; the brain infection itself does not spread easily
Course
Needs hospital care; recovery varies and can take weeks to months

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The virus that stays quiet in the body can wake up and reach the brain

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with a bad headache and confusion: go to hospital now or call 999
  • A fit (seizure) for the first time: call 999
  • Very sleepy, hard to wake, or passing out: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • A baby or child with fever who is very unwell: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not wait at home if you suspect it: go to hospital
  • Follow the hospital team and finish the treatment they give
  • Ask your doctor about help with memory, speech and walking

Questions and answers

What is Herpes simplex encephalitis?

A serious infection of the brain caused by the herpes simplex virus. The herpes simplex virus can reach the brain and make it swollen and inflamed. It needs urgent care in hospital.

What are the common signs of Herpes simplex encephalitis?

Fever, feeling hot · Confused or muddled · Fits (seizures) · Sleepy in the daytime · Weak arm

Why does Herpes simplex encephalitis happen?

Germs (infection) · The cause is not fully known · A weak immune system

How can I look after myself with Herpes simplex encephalitis?

See your doctor for check-ups
