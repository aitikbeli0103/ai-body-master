Low blood sugar

Sugar level in the blood that falls too low for the body

ConditionBlood sugar, fats, uric acid & thyroidBlood

What it is

When blood sugar drops too low, the brain and body run short of fuel, so you may feel shaky, sweaty, hungry or confused.

  • In the blood
  • Often in people with diabetes
  • Shaky, sweaty, faint
Which doctor
Family doctor, hormone doctor (endocrinologist)
Common in
People with diabetes; also after long gaps between meals
Contagious
No
Course
Often eases quickly once the body gets sugar, but it can come back

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Diabetes medicines or insulin, especially with a missed or late meal
  • Long gaps between meals, or a lot of exercise without eating

See a doctor first if

  • Passing out, a fit, or cannot be woken: call 999. Do not put food or drink in the mouth
  • Confusion, slurred speech or strange behaviour that does not clear quickly
  • Low sugar episodes that keep coming back
  • Low sugar episodes in the night, or with no warning signs
  • Symptoms that do not ease after eating something sweet

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • If you are awake and can swallow, a sweet drink or sweets may help; then eat a proper snack
  • Eat regular meals and do not skip them
  • If you have diabetes, follow your doctor's plan and carry something sweet
  • Tell your doctor about every episode; do not change your medicine yourself

Tell your therapist

  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A heart condition
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Low blood sugar?

Sugar level in the blood that falls too low for the body. When blood sugar drops too low, the brain and body run short of fuel, so you may feel shaky, sweaty, hungry or confused.

What are the common signs of Low blood sugar?

Sweating a lot · Shaking hands · Racing, pounding heart · Worried and on edge · Feeling faint

Why does Low blood sugar happen?

Drinking alcohol · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Low blood sugar?

If you are awake and can swallow, a sweet drink or sweets may help; then eat a proper snack · Eat regular meals and do not skip them · If you have diabetes, follow your doctor's plan and carry something sweet · Tell your doctor about every episode; do not change your medicine yourself
