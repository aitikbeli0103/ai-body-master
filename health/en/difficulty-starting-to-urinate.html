Difficulty starting to urinate

Waiting a while before the urine starts to flow

SymptomKidneys & urinaryAbdomen

What it is

You stand at the toilet and feel the urge, but it takes time before urine starts. It is common in older men, and it can have several causes.

  • Bladder and urine outlet
  • More common in older men
  • Slow to start passing urine
Which doctor
Family doctor; a urine doctor (urologist) if it continues
Common in
Older men; also after some surgery or with some medicines
Contagious
No
Course
Often slowly gets worse if the cause stays; a doctor can look for it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A weak bladder muscle, or nerve problems such as from diabetes
  • Feeling nervous or tense, especially in a public toilet

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine at all, with a painful full lower belly: go to hospital now
  • Fever or shaking chills with trouble passing urine
  • Blood in the urine
  • Weak legs, numbness in the groin, or loss of control of bowel or bladder: go to hospital now
  • Getting steadily worse over weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Take your time and relax your shoulders and belly when you pass urine
  • Sit down to pass urine if standing is hard

Tell your therapist

  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Recent back injury or surgery

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Difficulty starting to urinate?

Waiting a while before the urine starts to flow. You stand at the toilet and feel the urge, but it takes time before urine starts. It is common in older men, and it can have several causes.

What are the common signs of Difficulty starting to urinate?

Needing the toilet often · Waking at night to pass urine · Leaking when coughing · Burning when passing urine

Why does Difficulty starting to urinate happen?

An enlarged prostate · An injury or fall · A side effect of some medicines

How can I look after myself with Difficulty starting to urinate?

Cut down on alcohol · Ask your pharmacist
