Vasculitis

Inflammation of the blood vessels

ConditionAutoimmune & rheumaticBlood

What it is

In vasculitis the body's defence system causes inflammation in blood vessels. The vessels can narrow or weaken, so some parts of the body get less blood.

  • Blood vessels anywhere
  • Many types, any age
  • Rash, pain, tiredness
Which doctor
Rheumatologist (immune doctor), family doctor first
Common in
Any age; the type varies with age
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the type; some are short, some need long-term care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body's defence system attacks the vessel walls
  • Some infections or medicines may set it off in some people
  • Other immune illnesses can be linked to it

See a doctor first if

  • Coughing blood or trouble breathing: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Face drooping, arm weakness, speech trouble or sudden vision loss: call 999
  • Severe belly pain or blood in the stools
  • Dark or bloody urine, or swelling of the face and legs
  • Purple spots on the skin, numbness or a long fever

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Vasculitis?

Inflammation of the blood vessels. In vasculitis the body's defence system causes inflammation in blood vessels. The vessels can narrow or weaken, so some parts of the body get less blood.

What are the common signs of Vasculitis?

Red rash · Tired all the time · Fever, feeling hot · Losing weight without trying · Aching all over

Why does Vasculitis happen?

The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Vasculitis?

Keep your regular tests · Ask your pharmacist · Rest well · Wash your hands often
