Thoracic spinal stenosis

Narrowing of the space for the spinal cord in the middle of the back

ConditionBones, joints & spineBack

What it is

The channel in the mid-back spine that holds the spinal cord becomes narrow and can press on it, which may cause back pain, and weakness or numbness in the legs.

  • Middle of the back
  • More common with age
  • Stiff back, heavy weak legs
Which doctor
Family doctor, then spine or nerve specialist
Common in
Older adults; people with spine wear, past back injury or bone disease
Contagious
No
Course
Usually slow and long-term; a doctor can check how much the nerves are affected

Common signs

More signs

  • Numbness or tingling in the legs, or a band-like feeling around the trunk

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Thickened ligaments or bony growths in the spine
  • Some bone diseases; often the exact cause is not known

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden leg weakness, or you cannot stand or walk: go to hospital now
  • New trouble passing urine or stools, or leaking: go to hospital now
  • Numbness that spreads up the body or around the trunk
  • Chest pain or trouble breathing with back pain: call 999
  • Back pain after a fall or with fever, or weight loss without trying

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep moving with gentle walking in short stretches, with rests
  • Use a firm chair and change position often

Tell your therapist

  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Questions and answers

What is Thoracic spinal stenosis?

Narrowing of the space for the spinal cord in the middle of the back. The channel in the mid-back spine that holds the spinal cord becomes narrow and can press on it, which may cause back pain, and weakness or numbness in the legs.

What are the common signs of Thoracic spinal stenosis?

Ache between the shoulder blades · Heavy, tired legs · Unsteady when walking

Why does Thoracic spinal stenosis happen?

Getting older · An injury or fall

How can I look after myself with Thoracic spinal stenosis?

Avoid heavy lifting · Make the home safe from falls · Ask your pharmacist
