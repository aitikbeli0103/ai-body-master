Duodenal bulbitis

Swelling and redness (inflammation) of the lining of the first stretch of small bowel after the stomach

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

The lining of the duodenal bulb, right after the stomach, becomes red, swollen and irritated. Some people feel an upset or burning upper belly, and some feel nothing.

  • Upper belly
  • Can come and go
  • Upset, burning upper belly
Which doctor
Family doctor, stomach and bowel doctor (gastroenterologist)
Common in
Adults; people with H. pylori, smokers, heavy drinkers
Contagious
No, but the H. pylori germ linked to it can spread between people
Course
Often eases with the right care but can come back; see a doctor if it lingers

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Extra stomach acid washing into the duodenum
  • Alcohol, smoking and very spicy or oily food can irritate the lining

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood or passing black or bloody stools: go to hospital now
  • Sudden, very severe belly pain or a hard belly: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Feeling faint, very weak or pale: go to hospital now
  • Repeated vomiting, or you cannot keep food or drink down
  • Pain that keeps coming back, wakes you at night or lasts for weeks
  • Weight loss you cannot explain, or losing your appetite for weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See your doctor; ask about a test for H. pylori if the pain keeps coming back
  • Avoid alcohol, smoking and food that you notice upsets your stomach
  • Rest well and find ways to relax, as stress can make the discomfort feel worse

Tell your therapist

  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Questions and answers

What is Duodenal bulbitis?

Swelling and redness (inflammation) of the lining of the first stretch of small bowel after the stomach. The lining of the duodenal bulb, right after the stomach, becomes red, swollen and irritated. Some people feel an upset or burning upper belly, and some feel nothing.

What are the common signs of Duodenal bulbitis?

Pain in the upper belly · Bloated, gassy belly · Feeling sick, nausea · No appetite

Why does Duodenal bulbitis happen?

Germs (infection) · A side effect of some medicines · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Duodenal bulbitis?

Eat small, regular meals · Ask your pharmacist
