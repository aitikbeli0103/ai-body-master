Small bumps on the arms

Small raised spots on the skin of the arms, often rough like goose bumps

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Many small bumps appear on the arms, often the upper arms. They may feel rough or itchy and are often harmless, but the cause can differ.

  • Arms, often upper arms
  • Can happen to anyone
  • Tiny, rough, sometimes itchy
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor (dermatologist) if it is itchy or spreading
Common in
Anyone; often teens, and people with dry skin or eczema
Contagious
Some types can be, such as scabies; most are not
Course
Harmless types often come and go; others depend on the cause

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Keratosis pilaris: dead skin plugs the hair openings, very common and harmless
  • Heat and sweat, which can cause heat rash (tiny itchy bumps)
  • Irritated hair openings (folliculitis) from shaving, tight clothes or germs
  • Allergy, eczema, insect bites or itchy mites (scabies)

See a doctor first if

  • Swollen face, lips or tongue, or trouble breathing with a rash: call 999
  • Bumps with fever, pain, pus, or redness that spreads
  • Very itchy bumps, worse at night, especially if others at home are itchy too
  • Bumps that appeared after starting a new medicine: ask your doctor, and do not stop it on your own
  • A bump that bleeds, grows, changes colour or does not go away

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Bathe in lukewarm, not hot, water for a short time and use mild soap
  • Ask a doctor or pharmacist before using any cream or medicine on the bumps

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • A severe allergy
  • Fever or an active infection
