Inverted nipple

A nipple that points inward instead of sticking out; it is often there from birth

ConditionWomen's healthChest

What it is

The nipple is pulled in or lies flat; this is usually a harmless trait from birth, but a nipple that newly turns inward needs a doctor's check.

  • The nipple
  • Often there from birth
  • Pulled in, usually painless
Which doctor
Family doctor or breast clinic; a lactation consultant can help with feeding
Common in
Women and men; most often there since birth
Contagious
No
Course
A nipple that is inward from birth often stays the same; a new change needs a check

Common signs

  • The nipple points inward or lies flat instead of sticking out
  • It may come out a little with touch or cold, or it may stay pulled in
  • Often on both sides, and often there since birth
  • The skin in the fold can get moist, sore or smelly
  • Breastfeeding can be harder, but many mothers still manage with help

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Less often, a growth in the breast pulls the nipple in, so a new change must be checked

See a doctor first if

  • A nipple that has newly turned inward or changed shape: see a doctor soon
  • A new breast lump, or skin dimpling, thickening or an orange-peel look
  • Blood-stained discharge, or a rash or sore on the nipple that does not get better
  • A red, hot, painful breast with fever or chills: see a doctor today
  • Pus or foul-smelling fluid coming from the nipple
  • A baby who is not feeding well or not gaining weight: take the baby to a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wash the nipple and the fold gently with mild soap and water, and dry well
  • Know how your nipples normally look, so you can notice a change early
  • If you want to breastfeed, ask a midwife, nurse or lactation consultant for help early

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Broken, red or sore skin

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
