Excessive sleepiness (hypersomnia)

Feeling very sleepy in the day, or needing far more sleep than usual, even after a full night's rest

ConditionSleepWhole body

What it is

Heavy sleepiness through the day, long naps or sleeping far longer than usual, even after a full night's sleep. A doctor can look for the reason.

  • Whole body: low energy
  • Can affect any age
  • Very sleepy, even after rest
Which doctor
Family doctor; a sleep or lung specialist if needed
Common in
Anyone; more often with too little sleep, snoring or some medicines
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; a doctor can look for the reason and explain what may help

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Breathing pauses during sleep (sleep apnoea), often with loud snoring
  • Some medicines, alcohol, or long-lasting low mood
  • Health problems such as an underactive thyroid or diabetes
  • Sometimes the cause is not known, or it is a sleep condition such as narcolepsy

See a doctor first if

  • Suddenly very drowsy, hard to wake or confused, with a bad headache, weakness or trouble speaking: call 999
  • Sleepiness after a head injury: go to hospital now
  • Falling asleep while driving or at work, or nearly having an accident: do not drive, see a doctor soon
  • Loud snoring with pauses in breathing, choking at night or morning headaches: see a doctor soon
  • Sudden muscle weakness when laughing or upset, or seeing things as you fall asleep: see a doctor
  • Sleepiness that lasts for weeks, or gets in the way of work, school or daily life: see a doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep a regular bedtime and wake-up time, even on days off
  • Do not drive or use machines when sleepy; pull over safely and rest
  • Get daylight and a short walk in the morning; keep your room dark and quiet at night
  • Write down your sleep times for a few weeks, and ask your pharmacist if any medicine can cause sleepiness

Tell your therapist

  • High blood pressure
  • A heart condition
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
