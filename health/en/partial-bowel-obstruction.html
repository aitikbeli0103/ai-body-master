Partial bowel obstruction

The bowel is partly blocked, so food and wind pass only with difficulty

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

Something partly blocks the bowel, so some food and wind can still pass. It can cause cramping belly pain, bloating and sickness, and needs a doctor's check.

  • The bowel
  • More often in older adults
  • Cramping pain, bloating
Which doctor
Family doctor, and a surgeon or hospital doctor
Common in
People who have had belly surgery, and older adults
Contagious
No
Course
Can settle with hospital care, but it can get worse, so it needs close watching

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Scar bands in the belly after an operation
  • A hernia that traps part of the bowel
  • Growths or swelling inside or pressing on the bowel
  • Long-term bowel inflammation, which can narrow the bowel

See a doctor first if

  • Repeated vomiting, a swollen belly, and not passing wind or stools: go to hospital now
  • Severe belly pain that does not ease or keeps getting worse
  • Fever, a fast heartbeat, or feeling faint
  • A painful lump in the groin or belly that cannot be pushed back
  • Blood in the stools or vomiting blood: call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not ignore the signs; get checked early, because it can turn serious
  • Follow your doctor's advice about what to eat and drink
  • Ask your doctor before adding more fibre or taking laxatives

Tell your therapist

  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Pregnancy
  • Fever or an active infection
