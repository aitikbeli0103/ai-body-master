Endocarditis (inflamed heart lining)

Infection and inflammation of the inner lining of the heart and its valves

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

Germs in the blood can settle on the inner lining of the heart or on a heart valve and cause infection. It needs a doctor's care, often in hospital.

  • Inside the heart
  • Higher risk with valve problems
  • Fever that keeps coming back
Which doctor
Heart doctor (cardiologist), infection doctor
Common in
People with heart valve problems or an artificial valve
Contagious
No
Course
Needs prompt medical care; a doctor will explain the treatment plan

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Germs from the mouth, skin or another infection enter the blood and reach the heart
  • A damaged or artificial heart valve, or a heart defect from birth
  • Poor dental care and gum disease
  • Using injected drugs with unclean needles

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, severe trouble breathing, or fainting: call 999
  • Weakness on one side, face drooping or speech trouble: call 999
  • Fever that lasts more than a few days, especially if you have a heart valve problem
  • New swelling of the legs, or getting more breathless
  • Small red or purple spots on the skin, fingers or nails

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
