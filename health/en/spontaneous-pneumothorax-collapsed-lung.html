Spontaneous pneumothorax (collapsed lung)

A collapsed lung caused by air leaking into the chest without an injury

ConditionLungs & breathingChest

What it is

Air leaks from the lung into the space around it and presses the lung smaller. It can start suddenly and needs a doctor's check straight away.

  • One side of the chest
  • Tall, thin young men; smokers
  • Sudden chest pain
Which doctor
Emergency doctor first; lung doctor or chest surgeon
Common in
Tall, thin young men; smokers; people with lung disease
Contagious
No
Course
Needs a doctor's check; it can come back, so follow-up matters

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A small blister on the lung surface that bursts
  • Long-term lung disease such as COPD

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden chest pain with trouble breathing: go to hospital now
  • Blue lips, fainting or a very fast heartbeat: call 999
  • Breathing getting worse over minutes or hours
  • Chest pain after a fall or injury to the chest
  • Pain that returns on the same side after it settled

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

Questions and answers

What is Spontaneous pneumothorax (collapsed lung)?

A collapsed lung caused by air leaking into the chest without an injury. Air leaks from the lung into the space around it and presses the lung smaller. It can start suddenly and needs a doctor's check straight away.

What are the common signs of Spontaneous pneumothorax (collapsed lung)?

Sharp pain when breathing in · Short of breath on stairs · Racing, pounding heart · Dry, tickly cough

Why does Spontaneous pneumothorax (collapsed lung) happen?

Smoking · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Spontaneous pneumothorax (collapsed lung)?

Stop smoking · Avoid heavy lifting · See your doctor for check-ups
