Tear sac infection (dacryocystitis)

Infection of the small tear sac beside the nose

ConditionEyesHead

What it is

The tear sac, at the inner corner of the eye next to the nose, becomes blocked and infected, causing pain, swelling and watery eyes.

  • Inner corner of the eye
  • Babies and adults
  • Red, sore, swollen corner
Which doctor
Eye doctor (ophthalmologist), family doctor
Common in
Babies with a blocked tear duct; adults, more often women over middle age
Contagious
No
Course
Needs a doctor's care; it may come back if the blockage stays

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain, redness and swelling at the inner corner of the eye

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A blocked tear duct, so tears cannot drain
  • In adults, an old nose or eye injury, sinus problems or ageing

See a doctor first if

  • Red, hot swelling spreading to the cheek or eyelids, with fever: see a doctor today
  • Pain when moving the eye, double vision or loss of vision: go to hospital now
  • A baby with a red, swollen eye corner or fever: see a doctor promptly
  • Pus that keeps coming back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Germs (infection) · Present from birth
