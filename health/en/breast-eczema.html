Breast eczema

Itchy, red, dry skin on or around the breast or nipple

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Eczema on the breast is a skin condition that makes the skin red, itchy, dry or flaky, often under the breast or around the nipple.

  • Breast and nipple skin
  • Can affect anyone with eczema
  • Itchy, red, flaky skin
Which doctor
Family doctor, skin doctor (dermatologist); breast clinic if needed
Common in
People with eczema, allergies or very dry skin; some breastfeeding mothers
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes; gentle skin care can help, but it may come back

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Skin that is naturally dry or prone to eczema, sometimes with allergies or asthma
  • Rubbing and sweat under the breast, especially in hot, humid weather
  • An allergy to something touching the skin, such as metal (nickel) in bra hooks or fabric dyes
  • For some mothers, a wet or irritated nipple while breastfeeding

See a doctor first if

  • Swelling of the lips or face, or trouble breathing after using a new product: call 999
  • A hot, red, very painful breast with fever or feeling very unwell: see a doctor today or go to hospital
  • A rash or scaly, crusty skin on just one nipple or the dark skin around it: have a doctor check it soon
  • A new breast or armpit lump, a nipple pulling inwards, dimpled skin, or bloody nipple fluid: see a doctor soon
  • Weeping, yellow crusts or pus, or sudden painful blisters, with fever: see a doctor today
  • Itching so bad you cannot sleep, or no better after about two weeks of gentle care: see a doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
