Vulval atrophy in older women

Thinning and drying of the skin around the vagina after menopause, caused by lower oestrogen

ConditionWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

After menopause, lower oestrogen can make the skin around the vagina thinner, drier and less stretchy, which may cause dryness, itching, soreness or pain during sex.

  • Skin around the vagina
  • After menopause
  • Dryness, itching, soreness
Which doctor
Family doctor, gynaecologist (doctor for women's problems)
Common in
Women after menopause; also after removal of the ovaries or some cancer medicines
Contagious
No
Course
Often continues or slowly worsens without care; a doctor can explain safe options

Common signs

More signs

  • Thin, pale or fragile skin that cracks or tears easily
  • The vaginal opening may feel tighter or smaller

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Scented soaps and vaginal washes can make the dryness and soreness worse

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy vaginal bleeding, or feeling faint: go to hospital now
  • Any bleeding from the vagina after menopause, even a little
  • A lump, sore, or white, red or dark patch that does not go away or keeps growing
  • Fever, a bad-smelling discharge, or pain in the lower belly
  • Burning when passing urine, needing the toilet often, or blood in the urine
  • Pain so bad that sex, walking or sitting is hard

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Vulval atrophy in older women?

Thinning and drying of the skin around the vagina after menopause, caused by lower oestrogen. After menopause, lower oestrogen can make the skin around the vagina thinner, drier and less stretchy, which may cause dryness, itching, soreness or pain during sex.

What are the common signs of Vulval atrophy in older women?

Itchy skin · Pain during or after sex · Burning when passing urine

Why does Vulval atrophy in older women happen?

Hormone changes · A side effect of some medicines · Getting older · Smoking

How can I look after myself with Vulval atrophy in older women?

Wash gently and pat dry · Wear loose cotton clothes · Keep the skin moisturised
