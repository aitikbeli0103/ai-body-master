Anaerobic bacterial infection

An infection by bacteria that grow where there is little oxygen, such as deep wounds or abscesses

ConditionInfectionsWhole body

What it is

These bacteria live harmlessly in the mouth and gut, but can cause infection in deep wounds, abscesses or tissue with poor blood supply.

  • Wounds, mouth, gut or belly
  • After surgery or injury
  • Fever, pain, bad smell
Which doctor
Family doctor, infection doctor or surgeon
Common in
People after surgery or deep injury; people with diabetes or weak defences
Contagious
Usually not passed between people
Course
Needs a doctor; often medicine and sometimes draining the pus

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain, swelling and warmth in or around a wound
  • Pus or discharge with a bad smell
  • An abscess, which is a painful lump full of pus

Why it happens

  • Bacteria already in the mouth or gut entering a deep wound or surgery site
  • Dead tissue or poor blood supply in an area
  • Diabetes or a weak body defence
  • Dental infections, bites, or bowel and belly problems

See a doctor first if

  • High fever with fast heartbeat, confusion or feeling very weak: call 999
  • Skin that turns dark, blistered or crackles when pressed: go to hospital now
  • Redness or pain that spreads quickly
  • Severe belly pain, or swelling in the face or neck with trouble swallowing
  • A wound that smells bad or leaks pus

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Take medicine your doctor gives exactly as told; ask your doctor or pharmacist if unsure
  • Keep blood sugar well controlled if you have diabetes

Questions and answers

What is Anaerobic bacterial infection?

An infection by bacteria that grow where there is little oxygen, such as deep wounds or abscesses. These bacteria live harmlessly in the mouth and gut, but can cause infection in deep wounds, abscesses or tissue with poor blood supply.

What are the common signs of Anaerobic bacterial infection?

Fever, feeling hot

Why does Anaerobic bacterial infection happen?

Bacteria already in the mouth or gut entering a deep wound or surgery site · Dead tissue or poor blood supply in an area · Diabetes or a weak body defence · Dental infections, bites, or bowel and belly problems

How can I look after myself with Anaerobic bacterial infection?

See your doctor for check-ups · Keep wounds clean and covered
