Disc extrusion (ruptured disc)

A disc between the spine bones tears and its soft centre pushes out, which can press on nearby nerves

ConditionBones, joints & spineBones & muscles

What it is

The soft centre of a spinal disc pushes out through a tear in its tougher outer layer. It can cause back or neck pain, and pain, tingling or weakness down an arm or leg.

  • Disc in the spine
  • Adults, often 30 to 50
  • Back pain, pain down a limb
Which doctor
Family doctor, then an orthopaedic or spine doctor
Common in
Adults, especially with heavy lifting, long sitting or past back strain
Contagious
No
Course
Many people slowly feel better over weeks to months; some need further care from a doctor

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Wear of the discs with age makes them weaker
  • A sudden strain, fall or injury
  • Long sitting, being overweight or smoking
  • Sometimes there is no clear cause

See a doctor first if

  • Loss of control of urine or stools, or numbness around the groin or buttocks: go to hospital now
  • Weakness in a leg, foot or arm that is getting worse
  • Pain after a fall or accident: go to hospital
  • Severe pain that does not ease with rest
  • Back pain with fever or unexplained weight loss

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep moving gently; long bed rest is usually not helpful
  • Bend your knees, not your back, when picking things up
  • Change position often and do not sit for hours

Tell your therapist

  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

What is Disc extrusion (ruptured disc)?

A disc between the spine bones tears and its soft centre pushes out, which can press on nearby nerves. The soft centre of a spinal disc pushes out through a tear in its tougher outer layer. It can cause back or neck pain, and pain, tingling or weakness down an arm or leg.

What are the common signs of Disc extrusion (ruptured disc)?

Lower back pain · Pain shooting down the leg · Numb, tingling feet · Legs feel weak · Hard to bend or lift

Why does Disc extrusion (ruptured disc) happen?
