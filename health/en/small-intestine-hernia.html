Small intestine hernia

A part of the small intestine pushes through a weak spot in the belly wall

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

A loop of the small intestine bulges through a weak spot in the muscle wall of the belly, often seen as a soft lump that may come and go.

  • Belly or groin area
  • Can affect men and women
  • A lump that may ache
Which doctor
Family doctor, general surgeon
Common in
Adults who strain or lift a lot; older adults; after belly surgery
Contagious
No
Course
Usually does not go away by itself; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

More signs

  • A dull ache or pulling feeling near the lump
  • The lump may go flat when lying down
  • Discomfort when lifting, bending or straining

Why it happens

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden strong pain at the lump with vomiting: go to hospital now
  • A lump that is hard, cannot be pushed back, or turns red or purple: go to hospital now
  • Not able to pass wind or stools, or a swollen hard belly
  • Fever together with belly pain
  • A new lump in the belly or groin that is growing or painful

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See a doctor to confirm what the lump is
  • Eat fibre and drink water so you do not strain on the toilet

Tell your therapist

  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A heart condition
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
