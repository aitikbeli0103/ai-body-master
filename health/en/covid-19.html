COVID-19

An infection from the coronavirus SARS-CoV-2 that mostly affects the nose, throat and lungs

ConditionInfectionsChest

What it is

COVID-19 spreads from person to person; many people have a mild illness like a cold or flu, but some become very sick, especially older people and those with other health problems.

  • Nose, throat and lungs
  • Spreads from person to person
  • Fever, cough, sore throat
Which doctor
Family doctor or clinic; hospital if very unwell
Common in
Anyone; higher risk of serious illness in older adults and people with other illnesses
Contagious
Yes, spreads through the air from coughs, sneezes, talking and close contact
Course
Many people feel better in about one to two weeks; tiredness or a cough can last longer

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • It spreads more easily in crowded, closed or poorly ventilated places
  • A person can spread it before they feel ill, or even without any signs
  • Touching something with the virus on it and then your eyes, nose or mouth can spread it, but this is less common

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, chest pain that does not go away, or blue or grey lips or face: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • New confusion, being very hard to wake, fainting or a fit: call 999
  • A baby or child who breathes fast, is very sleepy, or will not feed or drink: go to hospital now
  • Signs of dehydration: you cannot keep fluids down, pass very little urine, or feel dizzy when you stand
  • A very high fever that will not come down, or any fever for more than three days (it could be another illness, such as dengue)
  • Feeling better and then suddenly getting worse again, with a new fever, cough or trouble breathing
  • If you are older, pregnant, or have a long-term illness or weak immunity: see a doctor early, even if the signs are mild

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Stay home and keep away from others until you feel better and have no fever; follow the latest advice from the Ministry of Health
  • Eat light, simple food and have warm drinks if your throat is sore

Questions and answers

What is COVID-19?

An infection from the coronavirus SARS-CoV-2 that mostly affects the nose, throat and lungs. COVID-19 spreads from person to person; many people have a mild illness like a cold or flu, but some become very sick, especially older people and those with other health problems.

What are the common signs of COVID-19?

Fever, feeling hot · Dry, tickly cough · Sore, scratchy throat · Blocked nose · Aching all over · Headache across the forehead

Why does COVID-19 happen?

Germs (infection) · Crowded places and droplets · Getting older

How can I look after myself with COVID-19?

Rest well · Wash your hands often · Ask your pharmacist · Get the recommended vaccines

Can an AI Body Master session help with COVID-19?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure COVID-19 and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
