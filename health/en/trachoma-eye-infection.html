Trachoma (eye infection)

A long-lasting eye infection that can scar the eyelid and eye

ConditionEyesHead

What it is

Trachoma is an eye infection caused by bacteria. Repeated infection can scar the inside of the eyelid and, over time, harm the clear front of the eye.

  • Eyes and inner eyelids
  • Linked to poor hygiene
  • Itchy, red, watery eyes
Which doctor
Eye doctor (ophthalmologist), family doctor
Common in
Children and families with crowded living and poor clean water
Contagious
Yes, through eye and nose fluids, hands, cloths and flies
Course
Needs a doctor; untreated repeated infection can scar the eye

Common signs

More signs

  • Later, eyelashes turning inward and scratching the eye

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Spreading by hands, towels, cloths or flies
  • Children carry the germ most often and pass it on to others

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden loss of vision or a very painful red eye: go to hospital now
  • Eyelashes turning inward and rubbing the eye
  • A cloudy or white spot on the eye, or vision getting worse
  • Red, sticky eyes in a baby or several people at home: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
