Hormonal imbalance (endocrine disorder)

Hormone levels in the body that are too high, too low or out of rhythm

ConditionBlood sugar, fats, uric acid & thyroidWhole body

What it is

Hormones are chemical messengers that help control growth, energy, sleep, periods and mood. "Hormonal imbalance" is a general term, and a doctor needs tests to find out which hormone is involved.

  • Whole body
  • Any age, often women
  • Tired, mood or period changes
Which doctor
Family doctor; hormone doctor (endocrinologist)
Common in
Women at puberty, pregnancy or menopause; any adult
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; some changes settle, others need long-term care

Common signs

More signs

  • Weight gain or loss that is hard to explain

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Natural life stages: puberty, pregnancy, breastfeeding and menopause
  • Thyroid problems or diabetes
  • Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, a very fast heartbeat or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Sudden severe headache or sudden loss of vision: go to hospital now
  • Very thirsty, vomiting and unusually sleepy or confused
  • Heavy bleeding, or bleeding or severe pain while pregnant
  • A lump or swelling in the neck, or fast weight change
  • Feeling hopeless or thinking of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Hormonal imbalance (endocrine disorder)?

Hormone levels in the body that are too high, too low or out of rhythm. Hormones are chemical messengers that help control growth, energy, sleep, periods and mood. "Hormonal imbalance" is a general term, and a doctor needs tests to find out which hormone is involved.

What are the common signs of Hormonal imbalance (endocrine disorder)?

Tired all the time · Low mood, no interest · Period comes late or early · Pimples and breakouts

Why does Hormonal imbalance (endocrine disorder) happen?

Stress · A side effect of some medicines

How can I look after myself with Hormonal imbalance (endocrine disorder)?

Rest well · Eat healthy, balanced meals · Find ways to relax
