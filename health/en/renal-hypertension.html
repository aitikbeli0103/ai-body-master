Renal hypertension

High blood pressure caused by a problem in the kidneys or their blood vessels

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsLower back

What it is

The kidneys help control blood pressure. When they are damaged or do not get enough blood, blood pressure can go up, and high pressure can harm the kidneys further.

  • Kidneys, either side of the back
  • Adults, sometimes young people
  • Often no signs, high pressure
Which doctor
Kidney doctor (nephrologist), family doctor
Common in
People with kidney disease, diabetes or long-term high blood pressure
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long-term and needs regular checks; a doctor guides the care

Common signs

More signs

  • Headache, especially in the morning, or feeling dizzy

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-term kidney disease or kidney damage
  • Narrowing of the artery that brings blood to a kidney
  • Kidney inflammation, cysts, or a kidney problem from birth

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, trouble breathing or fainting: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or trouble speaking: call 999
  • Very high blood pressure with bad headache, blurred vision or confusion: go to hospital now
  • Passing much less urine, blood in the urine, or swelling that is getting worse
  • Blood pressure that stays high even though you take your medicine

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Renal hypertension?

High blood pressure caused by a problem in the kidneys or their blood vessels. The kidneys help control blood pressure. When they are damaged or do not get enough blood, blood pressure can go up, and high pressure can harm the kidneys further.

What are the common signs of Renal hypertension?

Often no signs at first · Swollen legs and ankles · Waking at night to pass urine · No appetite

Why does Renal hypertension happen?

High blood sugar or blood pressure

How can I look after myself with Renal hypertension?

See your doctor for check-ups · Check your blood pressure at home · Eat less salt · Ask your pharmacist · Stop smoking
