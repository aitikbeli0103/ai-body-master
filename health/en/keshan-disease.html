Keshan disease

A rare heart muscle disease linked to a lack of selenium in the diet

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

A rare disease of the heart muscle that has been seen in some areas where the soil and food are very low in selenium. The heart can become weak and enlarged.

  • The heart muscle
  • Rare; certain regions
  • Tired, short of breath
Which doctor
Heart doctor (cardiologist), family doctor
Common in
Rare; seen mainly in some low-selenium regions
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; a doctor can explain what to expect and what care is needed

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Living a long time on food grown in soil very low in selenium
  • A poor, limited diet over a long time
  • It is rare and seldom seen in Malaysia

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, fainting or sudden trouble breathing: call 999
  • Breathlessness that is getting worse or wakes you at night
  • Swollen legs or quick weight gain
  • A fast or very uneven heartbeat that does not settle
  • Feeling very weak or dizzy

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
