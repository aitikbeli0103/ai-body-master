Womb-lining (endometrial) cancer

Cancer that starts in the inner lining of the womb (uterus)

ConditionCancer & tumoursReproductive

What it is

Womb-lining (endometrial) cancer is when cells in the lining of the womb grow out of control, and the most common first sign is unusual bleeding, especially after menopause.

  • Lining of the womb
  • Mostly after menopause
  • Unusual vaginal bleeding
Which doctor
Family doctor first, then a women's doctor (gynaecologist) and a cancer doctor
Common in
Mostly women after menopause; overweight and diabetes raise the risk
Contagious
No
Course
Often noticed early because of bleeding; the cancer team explains the plan

Common signs

More signs

  • Bleeding from the vagina after menopause

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Never having had children, a very early first period or a late menopause

See a doctor first if

  • Very heavy bleeding that soaks through pads quickly, or bleeding with faintness: go to hospital now
  • Any bleeding from the vagina after menopause, even if light: see a doctor soon
  • Bleeding between periods, or periods much heavier than usual
  • Watery, blood-stained or bad-smelling discharge that does not clear up
  • Pain in the lower belly that keeps getting worse, or weight loss you cannot explain

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Go to your appointments and follow your cancer care team's plan
  • Never stop or change treatment or medicine on your own; ask your team first
  • Ask your team about pain, food, intimacy and emotional support
  • Talk with family, friends or a support group; you do not have to cope alone

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

What are the common signs of Womb-lining (endometrial) cancer?

Heavy or long periods · Unusual discharge or itching down below · Dragging ache in the lower belly · Pain during or after sex

Why does Womb-lining (endometrial) cancer happen?

The cause is not fully known · Hormone changes · Extra body weight · Runs in the family
