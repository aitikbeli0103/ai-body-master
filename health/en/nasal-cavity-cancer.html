Nasal cavity cancer

Cancer that starts in the space inside the nose

ConditionCancer & tumoursHead

What it is

Nasal cavity cancer starts inside the nose. Early on it can look like a long-lasting cold or blocked nose, so one-sided problems that do not clear need a doctor's check.

  • Inside the nose
  • Rare; mostly older adults
  • Blocked nose on one side
Which doctor
Ear, nose and throat doctor (ENT), cancer doctor
Common in
Rare; mostly adults, more often men and smokers
Contagious
No

Depends on how early it is found; your team can explain

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Some viruses, such as HPV, may play a part

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy nosebleed that will not stop after 15 minutes of pinching: go to hospital now
  • Trouble breathing or swallowing: go to hospital now
  • Sudden vision change, double vision or a swollen eye
  • A blocked nose or nosebleeds on one side lasting more than a few weeks
  • A new lump in the neck or face, or numbness of the face

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Nasal cavity cancer?

Cancer that starts in the space inside the nose. Nasal cavity cancer starts inside the nose. Early on it can look like a long-lasting cold or blocked nose, so one-sided problems that do not clear need a doctor's check.

What are the common signs of Nasal cavity cancer?

Blocked nose · Nosebleeds · Loss of smell · Numb or weak face · Watery eye

Why does Nasal cavity cancer happen?

The cause is not fully known · Smoking · Dust or chemicals at work

How can I look after myself with Nasal cavity cancer?

See your doctor for check-ups · Stop smoking · Do not squeeze or pick at it
