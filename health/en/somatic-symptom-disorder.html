Somatic symptom disorder

Real body symptoms such as pain or tiredness, with strong worry about them

ConditionTold by a doctor: anxiety, fears & stressWhole body

What it is

The pain, tiredness or other symptoms are real and are not made up. The worry about them takes up a lot of time and energy. A doctor can help with both the body and the worry.

  • Can be felt anywhere in the body
  • Adults; can start at any age
  • Pain or tiredness plus worry
Which doctor
Family doctor first; psychiatrist or psychologist if needed
Common in
Adults; often with long-lasting stress or other illness
Contagious
No
Course
Symptoms can last a long time and change; steady care with one doctor often helps

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Another illness that makes you worry a lot about your health
  • A hard time in the past, or family members who were often ill

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting or weakness on one side: call 999
  • Thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999
  • A new symptom, weight loss, fever or bleeding: see a doctor to check it
  • Symptoms that stop you working, sleeping or enjoying life
  • Feeling very low, hopeless or very anxious

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.
