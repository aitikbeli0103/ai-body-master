Mental illness

A health problem that affects thinking, mood or behaviour

ConditionTold by a doctor: otherWhole body

What it is

Mental illness is a health problem, not a weakness. It can change how a person thinks, feels or acts, and it can be looked after with the right help.

  • Mind and mood
  • Can affect any age
  • Changes in mood or thinking
Which doctor
Family doctor, psychiatrist, psychologist
Common in
Anyone; many people are affected at some point in life
Contagious
No
Course
Differs from person to person; many people do better with support and care over time

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Often a mix of body, family history and life events
  • Other illnesses, long-term pain, or poor sleep
  • Alcohol or drug use

See a doctor first if

  • Thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life: see a doctor now or call 999
  • Hearing or seeing things others do not, or strong fears that people want to harm you
  • Thoughts of harming someone else
  • Not eating, not sleeping, or not able to look after yourself for days
  • Low mood or worry that lasts for weeks and affects work, study or family life

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Talk to someone you trust, and do not keep it all inside
  • Keep taking your medicine and attending your appointments; ask your doctor before any change
  • Avoid alcohol and drugs
  • Be kind to yourself; asking for help is a strong step

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Epilepsy or fits
  • A heart condition

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Mental illness?

A health problem that affects thinking, mood or behaviour. Mental illness is a health problem, not a weakness. It can change how a person thinks, feels or acts, and it can be looked after with the right help.

What are the common signs of Mental illness?

Low mood, no interest · Worried and on edge · Cannot fall asleep · Short-tempered, on edge · Foggy, cannot focus · Tired all the time

Why does Mental illness happen?

Stress · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Mental illness?

Get good sleep

Can an AI Body Master session help with Mental illness?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Mental illness and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
