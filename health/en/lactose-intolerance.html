Lactose intolerance

The body cannot digest the sugar in milk well, causing belly upset

ConditionEating & drinkingAbdomen

What it is

Some people make too little of the substance that breaks down milk sugar (lactose). Milk and dairy foods can then cause gas, bloating and loose stools.

  • Belly and gut
  • Common in adults
  • After milk or dairy
Which doctor
Family doctor; a dietitian can help
Common in
Many adults, especially in Asia
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long-lasting, but easy to manage by changing what you eat

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body makes less lactase, the helper that digests milk sugar
  • This often happens naturally as people grow older
  • A stomach or gut infection or gut illness can bring it on for a while

See a doctor first if

  • Blood in the stool or black stools
  • Severe or constant belly pain, or a swollen hard belly
  • Losing weight without trying, or fever with the belly upset
  • A baby or child with watery stools, vomiting or signs of dehydration: see a doctor promptly
  • Symptoms that last for weeks even after you cut down dairy

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Try smaller amounts of milk, or milk with meals
  • Try lactose-free milk or other milk drinks, and read the labels
  • Get calcium from other foods, such as green vegetables and tofu

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
