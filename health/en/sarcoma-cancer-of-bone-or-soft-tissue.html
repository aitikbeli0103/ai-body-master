Sarcoma (cancer of bone or soft tissue)

Cancer that starts in bone or in soft tissue such as muscle, fat or nerve

ConditionCancer & tumoursWhole body

What it is

Sarcomas are cancers of the body's support tissues. They are uncommon and can appear almost anywhere, often as a lump or bone pain.

  • Bone or soft tissue anywhere
  • Any age; not common
  • A lump or bone pain
Which doctor
Cancer doctor (oncologist), bone or general surgeon
Common in
Any age, including children and young adults; not common
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the type and stage; the cancer care team can explain what to expect

Common signs

More signs

  • A lump that grows, often not painful at first
  • Swelling near a bone or joint
  • A bone that breaks after a small knock

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Past radiation to the body may raise the risk
  • It is not caused by a knock or a bump

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, chest pain or coughing up blood: call 999
  • A bone that breaks, or you cannot put weight on a limb: go to hospital
  • A lump that is growing, is bigger than a golf ball, or is deep and hard
  • Bone pain that does not go away or wakes you at night
  • Weight loss without trying, or a limb that goes numb or weak

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop or change treatment on your own
  • Ask the team about pain, food and emotional support

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Weak bones
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
