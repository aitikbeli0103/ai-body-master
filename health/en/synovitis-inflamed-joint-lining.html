Synovitis (inflamed joint lining)

Swelling and irritation of the thin lining inside a joint

ConditionBones, joints & spineBones & musclesWhole body

What it is

The soft lining inside a joint becomes irritated and swollen, so the joint can feel puffy, warm, stiff and sore.

  • In a joint, often the knee
  • After strain, injury or illness
  • Swollen, stiff, aching joint
Which doctor
Family doctor, bone doctor (orthopaedic) or joint doctor (rheumatologist)
Common in
Adults; people with joint overuse, past injury or joint disease
Contagious
No
Course
Often settles in days to weeks, but it can come back; a doctor can look for the cause

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Overuse or a strain of the joint
  • Joint wear with age, or joint diseases such as arthritis or gout
  • Sometimes an infection in the joint, or the cause is not known

See a doctor first if

  • A hot, red, very painful joint with fever or chills: go to hospital now
  • Swelling after a fall or hit, or a joint you cannot move or put weight on
  • Swelling that lasts more than a few days or keeps coming back
  • Several joints swollen together, or a joint that locks or gives way

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Raise the joint when you sit or lie down
  • A cool cloth for short periods may ease swelling; keep a cloth between ice and skin

Tell your therapist

  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Weak bones
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
