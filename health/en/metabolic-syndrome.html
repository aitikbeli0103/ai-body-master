Metabolic syndrome

A group of linked problems: belly fat, blood pressure, blood sugar and blood fats

ConditionBlood sugar, fats, uric acid & thyroidWhole body

What it is

Several health problems occur together, such as extra belly fat, higher blood pressure, higher blood sugar and unhealthy blood fats, which can raise the risk of heart disease and diabetes.

  • Whole body
  • More common in midlife and later
  • Often no clear symptoms
Which doctor
Family doctor, hormone doctor (endocrinologist)
Common in
Adults with a large waist, little exercise or family history
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long-term; healthy habits and check-ups can help keep it in check

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain or pressure, or sudden breathlessness: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or trouble speaking: call 999
  • A very bad headache with high blood pressure readings
  • Very thirsty, passing lots of urine and losing weight without trying
  • Swollen legs or a numb, painful foot that does not heal

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Metabolic syndrome?

A group of linked problems: belly fat, blood pressure, blood sugar and blood fats. Several health problems occur together, such as extra belly fat, higher blood pressure, higher blood sugar and unhealthy blood fats, which can raise the risk of heart disease and diabetes.

What are the common signs of Metabolic syndrome?

Often no signs at first · Gaining weight easily · Tired all the time · Very thirsty, drinking a lot · Dark, velvety skin in folds

Why does Metabolic syndrome happen?

Extra body weight · Unhealthy food · Runs in the family

How can I look after myself with Metabolic syndrome?

Stop smoking · Check your blood pressure at home

Can an AI Body Master session help with Metabolic syndrome?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Metabolic syndrome and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
