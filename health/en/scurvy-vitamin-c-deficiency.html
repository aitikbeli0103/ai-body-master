Scurvy (vitamin C deficiency)

A lack of vitamin C over a long time, causing bleeding gums and bruising

ConditionEating & drinkingBlood

What it is

Vitamin C helps the body keep skin, gums and blood vessels strong. With very little of it for weeks to months, a person can bleed and bruise easily and feel tired.

  • Gums, skin, blood vessels
  • Poor diets, older adults
  • Bleeding gums, aching joints
Which doctor
Family doctor; a dentist for the gums
Common in
People who eat almost no fresh fruit or vegetables
Contagious
No
Course
Usually improves once the diet is corrected under a doctor's advice

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Eating almost no fresh fruit or vegetables for a long time
  • Very limited diets, or older people living alone
  • Illnesses that make it hard to eat or absorb food

See a doctor first if

  • Bleeding that will not stop: go to hospital now
  • Vomiting blood, or black or bloody stools: go to hospital now
  • Shortness of breath, chest pain or fainting
  • Joints that are swollen and very painful, or a child who cannot move a limb
  • Gums that bleed often, or loose teeth

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
