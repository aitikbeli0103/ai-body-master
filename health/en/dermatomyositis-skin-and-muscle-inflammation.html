Dermatomyositis (skin and muscle inflammation)

A rare immune condition that inflames the skin and muscles, causing a rash and weakness

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Dermatomyositis is a rare condition in which the body's defence system attacks its own skin and muscles. It can cause a purple-red rash and weak muscles.

  • Skin and muscles
  • More often in women
  • Purple-red rash, weak muscles
Which doctor
Rheumatologist and skin doctor (dermatologist), often working together
Common in
Adults and children; more often women
Contagious
No
Course
Long-lasting; it can flare and settle, so regular doctor follow-up matters

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body's defence system (immune system) attacks its own skin and muscles
  • The exact reason is often not known
  • In some adults it is linked with an underlying cancer, so doctors may do extra checks

See a doctor first if

  • Severe trouble breathing or blue lips: call 999
  • Chest pain, fainting, or a racing or uneven heartbeat: call 999
  • Weakness getting worse fast, such as not being able to stand up or hold your head up: go to hospital now
  • Trouble swallowing, or choking or coughing when you eat or drink: see a doctor today
  • A purple-red rash with weak muscles, a new cough, or weight loss without trying: see a doctor soon
  • A child with a rash and weak muscles: see a doctor promptly

What are the common signs of Dermatomyositis (skin and muscle inflammation)?

Why does Dermatomyositis (skin and muscle inflammation) happen?

Germs (infection) · Too much sun
