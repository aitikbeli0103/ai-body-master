Sweating a lot

Sweating much more than the body needs to cool down

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Sweating far more than usual, even without heat or activity, which may affect the hands, feet, underarms or face, or the whole body.

  • Hands, feet, underarms, face
  • Can start at any age
  • Wet skin, drips of sweat
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor (dermatologist) if it is troublesome
Common in
Teens and adults; may run in families; sometimes linked to other medical problems
Contagious
No
Course
Can last for years and may change over time; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Hot weather, stress, worry or strong emotions
  • Menopause, an overactive thyroid or diabetes
  • Fever, infections or other long-term illness

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy sweating with chest pain or pressure, pain in the arm or jaw, or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Heavy sweating with fainting, confusion or great weakness: go to hospital now
  • Soaking night sweats, fever, or losing weight without trying
  • Sweating with a racing heart, shaking hands, or feeling hot all the time
  • Heavy sweating that starts suddenly in an adult, or after starting a new medicine
  • Sweating so much that it affects your sleep, work or daily life

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Use an antiperspirant on clean, dry skin; a pharmacist can suggest a suitable one
  • Shower daily, dry well, and keep a small towel and a spare shirt handy
  • Tell your doctor if it bothers you; there are ways to help, so you need not put up with it

Tell your therapist

  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A heart condition
  • Pregnancy
  • Broken, red or sore skin

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
