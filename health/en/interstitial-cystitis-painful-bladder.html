Interstitial cystitis (painful bladder)

A long-lasting condition with bladder pain and pressure and a need to pass urine often

ConditionKidneys & urinaryPelvis

What it is

The bladder feels painful, full or under pressure for weeks or months, even though a urine test shows no infection. The cause is often not known.

  • Lower belly and bladder
  • More often in women
  • Pain, pressure, urgency
Which doctor
Urinary specialist (urologist), family doctor
Common in
Adults, more often women
Contagious
No
Course
Long-lasting; signs often come and go in flare-ups

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The lining of the bladder may be irritated or weaker than usual
  • It may go along with other long-term pain conditions

See a doctor first if

  • Fever, shaking chills or pain in the back or side: see a doctor today
  • Blood in the urine
  • Cannot pass urine at all: go to hospital now
  • Pain that is getting worse, or is stopping you from sleeping or working
  • Feeling very low or hopeless because of the pain: talk to a doctor; if you think of harming yourself, call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep a simple diary of foods, drinks and stress that make it worse
  • Try gentle walking, a warm bath or a warm pack on the lower belly

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Broken, red or sore skin

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
