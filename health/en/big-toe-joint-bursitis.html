Big toe joint bursitis

A painful bump at the base of the big toe from an inflamed joint pad

ConditionBones, joints & spineArms & legsBones & muscles

What it is

A small fluid sac that cushions the big toe joint can become swollen and sore. The area may be red, tender and rub against shoes.

  • Base of the big toe
  • Often with tight shoes
  • Sore, swollen toe joint
Which doctor
Family doctor; foot doctor (podiatrist), bone doctor
Common in
Adults who wear tight or pointed shoes, or have bunions
Contagious
No
Course
Often settles over days to weeks when pressure is reduced

Common signs

More signs

  • A sore, swollen bump at the side of the big toe joint
  • Pain when wearing shoes or when walking
  • Stiffness or an aching toe

Why it happens

  • Tight, narrow or high-heeled shoes that press on the toe
  • A bunion or a change in the shape of the foot
  • Repeated rubbing or pressure on the joint
  • Arthritis or gout in the toe joint

See a doctor first if

  • A hot, very red, swollen toe with fever: go to hospital promptly, it may be infected
  • Red streaks spreading from the toe or pus coming out
  • A toe that turns pale, blue or cold
  • Sudden severe pain in the joint, as in gout
  • Foot problems if you have diabetes: see a doctor early

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Avoid high heels and pointed shoes for a while
  • Pad the bump so shoes do not rub it
