Laryngeal papilloma (voice box)

Small wart-like growths on the voice box that can change the voice

ConditionEars, nose & throatNeck

What it is

Small, non-cancerous wart-like growths form on the voice box. They are linked to a common virus and can make the voice hoarse or breathing noisy.

  • Voice box in the throat
  • Children and adults
  • Hoarse, weak voice
Which doctor
Ear-nose-throat doctor (ENT); family doctor first
Common in
Young children and adults; it is not very common
Contagious
The virus behind it can spread, but most people do not get this
Course
Can stay or come back, so regular check-ups with the ENT doctor matter

Common signs

More signs

  • Noisy breathing, especially when breathing in
  • A weak cry in a baby or young child
  • A long-lasting cough, or feeling short of breath

Why it happens

Other causes

  • In children, the virus may pass from mother to baby at birth
  • In adults, it is often not clear why it appears

See a doctor first if

  • Struggling to breathe, blue lips, or noisy breathing at rest: call 999 now
  • A baby or child with a weak cry and trouble breathing: go to hospital now
  • Hoarse voice lasting more than a few weeks
  • Trouble swallowing, coughing up blood, or weight loss without trying

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself
