Joint infection (septic arthritis)

A germ infection inside a joint that needs urgent medical care

ConditionBones, joints & spineBones & muscles

What it is

Germs get into a joint, usually through the blood or a wound. The joint becomes hot, swollen and very painful. See a doctor at once.

  • Usually one joint, often knee
  • Urgent: needs a doctor
  • Hot, swollen joint with fever
Which doctor
Go to the hospital emergency department; bone doctor (orthopaedic)
Common in
Babies, older adults, people with weak defences or a joint already damaged
Contagious
No, it does not pass from person to person
Course
Needs quick medical care; waiting can harm the joint

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A cut, bite, injection or surgery near or in the joint
  • A joint that is already damaged or has an artificial part

See a doctor first if

  • A hot, red, swollen, very painful joint, especially with fever or chills: go to hospital now
  • You cannot move the joint or put any weight on it
  • A baby or child who is very unwell, will not move a limb or cries when it is touched: go now
  • Confusion, fast heartbeat, fast breathing or feeling faint with the fever: call 999
  • A red streak, pus or an open wound near the joint

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
