TB pleurisy (tuberculous pleurisy)

TB infection of the lining around the lungs, causing chest pain and fluid

ConditionInfectionsChest

What it is

The TB germ infects the thin lining around the lungs. Fluid can collect there, which may cause sharp chest pain and shortness of breath.

  • Lining around the lungs
  • Can affect any adult
  • Chest pain, dry cough
Which doctor
Family doctor, then lung or TB specialist
Common in
Adults and young people, more so with weak immunity
Contagious
Some types can be; TB in the lungs spreads by coughing
Course
Needs a long course of medicine from a doctor; follow-up is important

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The TB germ reaches the lung lining from the lungs or the blood
  • Weak immunity, for example HIV or diabetes

See a doctor first if

  • Severe trouble breathing, blue lips or chest pain that is crushing: call 999
  • Coughing up blood
  • Chest pain with breathing that keeps getting worse
  • Cough or fever lasting more than two weeks
  • Weight loss or night sweats with a cough

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

What are the common signs of TB pleurisy (tuberculous pleurisy)?

Sharp pain when breathing in · Dry, tickly cough · Fever, feeling hot · Short of breath on stairs · Soaking sweats at night · Losing weight without trying

Why does TB pleurisy (tuberculous pleurisy) happen?

Close contact or sharing items · Crowded places and droplets

How can I look after myself with TB pleurisy (tuberculous pleurisy)?

Take medicine as your doctor says · Stop smoking
