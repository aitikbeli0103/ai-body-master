Zinc deficiency

Too little zinc, a mineral the body needs for growth, healing and defence

ConditionEating & drinkingWhole body

What it is

Zinc is a mineral the body needs every day. When there is too little, growth, skin, taste and the body's defence may be affected.

  • Whole body
  • Children, pregnant, older adults
  • Slow healing, poor taste
Which doctor
Family doctor, dietitian
Common in
Children, pregnant or breastfeeding women, older adults, people with gut problems
Contagious
No
Course
Often improves when the cause is found and food is balanced; a doctor decides

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Not enough zinc-rich food in the daily diet
  • Gut problems that stop the body absorbing zinc well
  • Long diarrhoea, or drinking a lot of alcohol
  • Pregnancy, breastfeeding and growing children need more zinc

See a doctor first if

  • A baby or child who is very unwell, not feeding or not growing: see a doctor promptly
  • Diarrhoea that goes on for many days, or signs of dehydration
  • Wounds or sores that do not heal, or skin infections that keep coming back
  • Fast weight loss or losing your appetite for a long time
  • Pregnant and not eating well: see your doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Eat a varied diet with meat, fish, eggs, beans, nuts and whole grains
  • Get a check-up if you have long-lasting gut problems

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin

Questions and answers

What is Zinc deficiency?

Too little zinc, a mineral the body needs for growth, healing and defence. Zinc is a mineral the body needs every day. When there is too little, growth, skin, taste and the body's defence may be affected.

What are the common signs of Zinc deficiency?

Cuts heal slowly · Hair coming out · No appetite · Loose, frequent stools · Brittle, ridged nails · Tired all the time

Why does Zinc deficiency happen?

Not enough zinc-rich food in the daily diet · Gut problems that stop the body absorbing zinc well · Long diarrhoea, or drinking a lot of alcohol · Pregnancy, breastfeeding and growing children need more zinc

How can I look after myself with Zinc deficiency?

Ask your pharmacist · Cut down on alcohol

Can an AI Body Master session help with Zinc deficiency?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Zinc deficiency and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
