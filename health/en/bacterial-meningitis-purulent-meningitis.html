Bacterial meningitis (purulent meningitis)

A pus-forming germ infection of the thin layers around the brain

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

Bacteria cause the lining around the brain to swell and fill with pus. It is an emergency that can become serious within hours, so go to hospital at once.

  • Brain and spinal cord lining
  • An emergency
  • Fever, stiff neck, headache
Which doctor
Go to hospital emergency; then a specialist team
Common in
Babies, young children, older people, weak immunity
Contagious
Some germs spread by coughs, sneezes or close contact
Course
Can worsen within hours; quick hospital care matters

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A head injury, ear infection or brain surgery

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with stiff neck and bad headache: go to hospital now or call 999
  • A fit (seizure), confusion, or being very hard to wake: call 999
  • A baby or child who is very unwell, sleepy, will not feed or has a bulging soft spot: see a doctor now
  • A rash that does not fade when pressed
  • Ear pain or runny ear with fever and a very bad headache

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Bacterial meningitis (purulent meningitis)?

A pus-forming germ infection of the thin layers around the brain. Bacteria cause the lining around the brain to swell and fill with pus. It is an emergency that can become serious within hours, so go to hospital at once.

What are the common signs of Bacterial meningitis (purulent meningitis)?

Fever, feeling hot · Stiff, aching neck · Vomiting · Confused or muddled · A baby who feeds poorly

Why does Bacterial meningitis (purulent meningitis) happen?

Germs (infection) · Close contact or sharing items · A weak immune system

How can I look after myself with Bacterial meningitis (purulent meningitis)?

See your doctor for check-ups · Wash your hands often · Get the recommended vaccines
