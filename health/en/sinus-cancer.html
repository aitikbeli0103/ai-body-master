Sinus cancer

A cancer that starts in the nose or the air spaces around it (the sinuses)

ConditionCancer & tumoursHead

What it is

This is a cancer that starts in the lining of the nose or the sinuses. It is not common, and early signs can look like a long cold or sinus trouble.

  • Nose and sinuses
  • Adults, mostly older
  • Blocked nose on one side
Which doctor
Ear, nose and throat doctor, cancer doctor (oncologist)
Common in
Adults, more often older people
Contagious
No
Course
Differs from person to person; your cancer team can explain it

Common signs

More signs

  • Watery or bulging eye, or loose teeth

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A long-lasting infection of the nose or sinuses may play a part

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden loss of vision or double vision: go to hospital now
  • Heavy nosebleed that does not stop: go to hospital now
  • A blocked nose or bloody mucus on one side lasting weeks
  • A new lump in the face, neck or roof of the mouth
  • Numbness in the face or loose teeth

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop treatment on your own
  • Ask your team about pain, eating and emotional support

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy
  • Fever or an active infection

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Sinus cancer?

A cancer that starts in the nose or the air spaces around it (the sinuses). This is a cancer that starts in the lining of the nose or the sinuses. It is not common, and early signs can look like a long cold or sinus trouble.

What are the common signs of Sinus cancer?

Blocked nose · Nosebleeds · Numb or weak face · Loss of smell

Why does Sinus cancer happen?

The cause is not fully known · Smoking · Dust or chemicals at work

How can I look after myself with Sinus cancer?

See your doctor for check-ups · Stop smoking

Can an AI Body Master session help with Sinus cancer?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Sinus cancer and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
