Inflamed tooth nerve (pulpitis)

Inflammation of the soft inner part of a tooth, where its nerve is

ConditionMouth & teethHead

What it is

The soft centre of a tooth (the pulp), which holds its nerve and blood vessels, becomes inflamed, most often from deep decay or a crack, and it can cause toothache that needs a dentist.

  • Inside a tooth
  • Any age, even children
  • Toothache, hot or cold pain
Which doctor
Dentist; a tooth-nerve specialist (endodontist) if needed
Common in
Anyone with deep decay, a cracked tooth or a deep filling
Contagious
No
Course
Does not usually clear up by itself; a dentist needs to check the tooth

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain from sweet food, or when biting down
  • A visible hole, crack or dark spot on the tooth

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Deep tooth decay (cavities) is the most common cause
  • A deep filling, or repeated dental work on the same tooth
  • Teeth worn down by grinding, or gum disease that reaches the root
  • Sugary food and drink, and brushing poorly, make decay more likely

See a doctor first if

  • Swollen face or neck with trouble breathing or swallowing: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Fever, a swollen cheek or face, or pus and a bad taste in the mouth: see a dentist or doctor today
  • Severe or throbbing toothache that keeps you awake: see a dentist as soon as you can
  • Toothache that suddenly stops, but the tooth is still dark, loose or tender: still see a dentist
  • Tooth pain in a child, or in a person with diabetes or a weak immune system: see a dentist promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Avoid very hot, very cold and very sweet food and drink on that side
  • Chew on the other side, and brush gently around the sore tooth
  • Ask your doctor, dentist or pharmacist before taking any medicine for the pain
  • Cut down on sugary snacks and drinks, and brush twice a day
