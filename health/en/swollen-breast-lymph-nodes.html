Swollen breast lymph nodes

Small bean-shaped glands near the breast or armpit that become bigger

SymptomWomen's healthChest

What it is

Lymph nodes are small glands that help fight germs. Those near the breast or armpit can swell, often because of an infection, but a doctor should check a lump.

  • Breast and armpit
  • Can happen to anyone
  • A small lump or tenderness
Which doctor
Family doctor, breast clinic or breast surgeon
Common in
Anyone; often after a cold, skin infection or small injury
Contagious
No, but an infection behind it may spread
Course
Often goes down in a few weeks if caused by an infection; a lump that stays needs a check

Common signs

More signs

  • The lump may be tender or sore to touch
  • Redness or warmth of the breast skin if there is an infection

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Infections such as a cold, a skin or breast infection
  • A small cut or injury on the arm, hand or chest
  • Less often, long-term illness or cancer, which is why a check is needed

See a doctor first if

  • A hard lump that does not move and does not hurt
  • A lump that stays or grows for more than a few weeks
  • A new breast lump, nipple discharge, skin dimpling or a change in breast shape
  • Weight loss, night sweats or long-lasting fever without a reason
  • Fever with a red, hot, painful breast

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Rest and drink enough fluids if you feel unwell
  • See a doctor if it has not gone down in a few weeks
  • Go for breast screening as your doctor advises

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Pregnancy
