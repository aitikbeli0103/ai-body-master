Symptomatic epilepsy syndrome

Repeated fits (seizures) that are caused by a known problem in the brain

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

Brain cells sometimes send out sudden bursts of electrical activity, causing fits. Here a known problem in the brain, such as an old injury, is behind them.

  • The brain
  • Any age; needs a doctor's care
  • Sudden fits or blank spells
Which doctor
Neurologist (brain doctor), family doctor first
Common in
People with a past stroke, head injury, infection or brain growth
Contagious
No
Course
Often long term; with a doctor's plan many people manage well

Common signs

More signs

  • Staring blankly and not responding for a short time
  • Strange smells, tastes or feelings just before a fit

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A past stroke or bleeding in the brain
  • A head injury or brain surgery

See a doctor first if

  • A fit that lasts more than five minutes: call 999
  • One fit after another without waking up in between: call 999
  • Trouble breathing, or an injury during a fit: go to hospital now
  • A first fit, or fits that are getting more frequent
  • Fits during pregnancy

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Sleep regularly and avoid long nights
  • During a fit, clear the space and turn the person on their side; do not put anything in the mouth
  • Take care near water, heights and when driving; ask your doctor

Tell your therapist

  • Epilepsy or fits
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

What are the common signs of Symptomatic epilepsy syndrome?

Fits (seizures) · Confused or muddled

Why does Symptomatic epilepsy syndrome happen?

Germs (infection) · Present from birth

How can I look after myself with Symptomatic epilepsy syndrome?

Take medicine as your doctor says · Cut down on alcohol
