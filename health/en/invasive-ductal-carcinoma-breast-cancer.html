Invasive ductal carcinoma (breast cancer)

The most common type of breast cancer; it starts in the milk ducts and grows into nearby breast tissue

ConditionCancer & tumoursWhole body

What it is

Invasive ductal carcinoma is breast cancer that begins in the milk ducts and has spread into the breast tissue around them. It can be found early by checks.

  • Breast
  • Mostly women, rarely men
  • A new breast lump
Which doctor
Breast doctor or surgeon, cancer doctor (oncologist)
Common in
Women, more often after middle age; men can get it too
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on how early it is found; your care team can explain the options

Common signs

More signs

  • Dimpled, red or scaly skin on the breast
  • The nipple turns inward, or fluid or blood comes from it

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Getting older, and a family history of breast or ovarian cancer
  • Inherited gene changes in some families
  • Being overweight after menopause, heavy drinking and little exercise
  • Some women's hormone factors, such as starting periods very early

See a doctor first if

  • A new lump in the breast or armpit
  • Skin changes such as dimpling, redness or an orange-peel look
  • Nipple turning inward, or blood or fluid from the nipple
  • Bone pain, a cough that does not go away, or losing weight without trying
  • Trouble breathing or severe new pain: go to hospital now

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop treatment on your own; ask your team about pain, food and tiredness
  • Know how your breasts normally look and feel, and report any change
  • Ask your doctor when to go for breast screening

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Weak bones

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
