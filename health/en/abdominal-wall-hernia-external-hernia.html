Abdominal wall hernia (external hernia)

A soft bulge where part of the gut or fat pushes through a weak spot in the belly wall

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

Part of the gut or some fat pushes through a weak spot in the muscle wall of the belly or groin, making a soft bulge that shows more when you stand, cough or strain.

  • Belly wall or groin
  • Any age, more in men
  • A bulge that may ache
Which doctor
Family doctor first; a surgeon can explain the options
Common in
Any age; more in men, older adults, and after pregnancy or surgery
Contagious
No
Course
Usually does not go away by itself and may slowly grow

Common signs

More signs

  • The bulge shows more when you stand, cough or strain, and may flatten when you lie down
  • A dull ache, pulling or heavy feeling at the spot
  • More discomfort with lifting or long standing
  • Sometimes no pain at all, only a lump

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A weak spot in the belly wall muscles, from birth or from ageing

See a doctor first if

  • A hard, painful bulge that will not go back in, with vomiting or a swollen belly: go to hospital now
  • The skin over the bulge turns red, purple or dark, or there is a fever: go to hospital now
  • You cannot pass wind or stools and the belly swells up: go to hospital now
  • A new bulge in the belly or groin, even if it does not hurt: see a doctor
  • A bulge that keeps getting bigger or more painful: see a doctor soon

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Eat more fibre and drink enough water so you do not strain on the toilet
  • When you lift, bend your knees and keep the load close to your body
  • Ask your doctor before using a belt or support over the bulge

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Broken, red or sore skin

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
