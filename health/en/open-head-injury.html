Open head injury

A head injury where the skull is broken or open, so the brain may be exposed

ConditionHead & neckHead

What it is

The skin and skull are broken open by an injury, so the inside of the head may be exposed. This is an emergency and needs hospital care straight away.

  • Skull and brain
  • After a serious injury
  • Wound, bleeding, pain
Which doctor
Emergency doctor; brain surgeon (neurosurgeon)
Common in
People in serious accidents, falls from height or violent attacks
Contagious
No
Course
Needs urgent hospital care; recovery depends on how severe it is

Common signs

More signs

  • A deep wound on the head, often bleeding
  • A visible dent or broken piece of skull

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Falls from a height
  • Violent attacks or being hit by a sharp or heavy object
  • Work or industrial accidents

See a doctor first if

  • Any open wound on the head with a dent or exposed tissue: call 999
  • Heavy bleeding that does not slow with firm pressure: call 999
  • Fainting, a fit, or cannot be woken: call 999
  • Clear fluid or blood from the nose or ear
  • Fever, a stiff neck or a wound that turns red, swollen or smelly later on

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Call 999 straight away
  • Press gently around the wound with a clean cloth; do not press on a dent or push anything back in
  • Keep the person still and do not move the neck
  • Do not give food or drink; do not remove objects stuck in the wound

Tell your therapist

  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Epilepsy or fits
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Fever or an active infection

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Open head injury?

A head injury where the skull is broken or open, so the brain may be exposed. The skin and skull are broken open by an injury, so the inside of the head may be exposed. This is an emergency and needs hospital care straight away.

What are the common signs of Open head injury?

Confused or muddled · Vomiting · Weakness on one side of the body

Why does Open head injury happen?

An injury or fall

How can I look after myself with Open head injury?

Call 999 straight away · Press gently around the wound with a clean cloth; do not press on a dent or push anything back in · Keep the person still and do not move the neck · Do not give food or drink; do not remove objects stuck in the wound
