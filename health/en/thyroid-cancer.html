Thyroid cancer

Cancer that starts in the thyroid, the small butterfly-shaped gland at the front of the neck

ConditionCancer & tumoursNeck

What it is

Thyroid cancer is when abnormal cells grow out of control in the thyroid gland in the neck, often first showing as a lump in the neck.

  • Front of the neck
  • More often in women
  • A lump in the neck
Which doctor
Family doctor, hormone (endocrine) doctor, ENT or neck surgeon, cancer doctor
Common in
Adults of many ages; more often in women; past radiation to the neck raises the risk
Contagious
No
Course
Many types grow slowly, but it varies; the cancer team explains the plan

Common signs

More signs

  • A cough or breathing trouble that is not from a cold

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Past radiation to the head or neck, especially in childhood
  • Being a woman; it is found more often in women than in men

See a doctor first if

  • Severe trouble breathing, noisy breathing or choking: call 999
  • A new lump in the neck: see a doctor soon (most thyroid lumps are not cancer, but it needs checking)
  • A hoarse voice that lasts for weeks
  • Trouble swallowing, or a cough that does not go away and is not from a cold
  • Swollen glands in the neck that do not go down, or weight loss you cannot explain

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop or change treatment or medicine on your own; ask your team first
  • Ask your team about voice, swallowing, pain, food and emotional support

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • A heart condition
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Thyroid cancer?

Cancer that starts in the thyroid, the small butterfly-shaped gland at the front of the neck. Thyroid cancer is when abnormal cells grow out of control in the thyroid gland in the neck, often first showing as a lump in the neck.

What are the common signs of Thyroid cancer?

A lump under the skin · Hoarse, weak voice · Hard or painful to swallow · Swollen glands in the neck

Why does Thyroid cancer happen?

The cause is not fully known · Runs in the family

How can I look after myself with Thyroid cancer?

Follow your care team's plan · Take medicine as your doctor says · Talk to a counsellor
