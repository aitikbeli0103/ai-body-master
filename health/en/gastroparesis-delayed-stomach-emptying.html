Gastroparesis (delayed stomach emptying)

The stomach muscles work slowly, so food leaves the stomach late

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

The stomach muscles do not squeeze food along as they should, even though nothing is blocking it, so you may feel full and sick.

  • In the stomach
  • Often with diabetes
  • Full early, sick, bloated
Which doctor
Stomach doctor (gastroenterologist), family doctor
Common in
People with diabetes; also after stomach surgery or a virus
Contagious
No
Course
Often long-lasting but can be managed with a doctor's help

Common signs

Why it happens

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood or black stools: go to hospital now
  • Severe belly pain or a hard, swollen belly
  • Vomiting again and again, or cannot keep drinks down
  • Dehydration: very dry mouth, dizzy, very little urine
  • Losing weight, or blood sugar that is hard to control

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Choose soft, low-fat foods and chew well
  • Keep blood sugar steady with your doctor's advice

Tell your therapist

  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A kidney or liver condition
