Spinal cord compression

Pressure on the spinal cord, the bundle of nerves in the back bone, which can harm nerve signals

ConditionBrain & nervesBones & muscles

What it is

Something presses on the spinal cord and can cause back or neck pain, weakness, numbness or trouble with the bladder and bowel. It can be serious.

  • Spine and spinal cord
  • More often in older adults
  • Back or neck pain, weakness
Which doctor
Spine or nerve surgeon, brain and nerve doctor; hospital if sudden
Common in
Older adults; people with spine wear, a past injury, or cancer
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; quick care gives the best chance to protect the nerves

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A spine injury or broken bone
  • A slipped disc
  • A growth or cancer that has spread to the spine

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden weakness or numbness in the legs or arms: go to hospital now
  • Trouble passing urine or stools, or leaking: go to hospital now
  • Numbness around the groin or buttocks
  • Back pain after a fall or accident
  • Back pain with fever, weight loss, or a history of cancer
  • Pain at night that does not ease with rest

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Spinal cord compression?

Pressure on the spinal cord, the bundle of nerves in the back bone, which can harm nerve signals. Something presses on the spinal cord and can cause back or neck pain, weakness, numbness or trouble with the bladder and bowel. It can be serious.

What are the common signs of Spinal cord compression?

Lower back pain · Stiff, aching neck · Tingling down the arm · Legs feel weak · Unsteady when walking

Why does Spinal cord compression happen?

Wear and tear with age · Germs (infection)

How can I look after myself with Spinal cord compression?

Avoid heavy lifting · Make the home safe from falls · Ask your pharmacist
