Cracked tooth (hairline crack)

A very fine crack in a tooth that can cause pain when biting

ConditionMouth & teethHead

What it is

A hairline crack can form in a tooth and be hard to see. It may cause sharp pain when you bite down or when the tooth meets hot or cold.

  • In one tooth
  • More often in adults
  • Sharp pain when biting
Which doctor
Dentist
Common in
Adults; people who grind teeth or chew hard food
Contagious
No
Course
Does not go away by itself; a dentist can advise what to do

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain that comes and goes, hard to pinpoint

Why it happens

  • Biting on hard food, ice or other hard things
  • Grinding or clenching teeth
  • Large fillings that weaken the tooth
  • Sudden hot then cold drinks
  • A past knock to the face or getting older

See a doctor first if

  • Swelling of the face or jaw with trouble breathing or swallowing: call 999
  • Facial swelling or a swollen gum with fever: see a doctor or dentist now
  • Severe toothache that does not settle
  • A small pimple or pus on the gum
  • A piece of tooth breaks off, or you can see the crack grow

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • See a dentist soon so the tooth can be checked
  • Avoid chewing hard food on that side
  • Do not bite ice, bones or hard sweets
  • Avoid very hot and very cold drinks
  • Keep brushing gently and keep your mouth clean

Tell your therapist

  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
