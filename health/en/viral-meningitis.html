Viral meningitis

Swelling of the covering around the brain and spinal cord, caused by a virus

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

A virus irritates the thin layers that cover the brain and spinal cord. It is usually milder than the bacterial type, but it still needs a doctor to check it.

  • Head and neck
  • Children and young adults
  • Bad headache, fever
Which doctor
Family doctor first; hospital if unwell
Common in
Children and young adults, more in warm months
Contagious
The virus behind it can spread, usually by droplets or touch
Course
Many people get better in one to two weeks; needs a doctor's check

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Mosquito-borne viruses in some cases

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with a stiff neck and a very bad headache: go to hospital now
  • Confusion, very sleepy, hard to wake, or a fit: call 999
  • A purple or red rash that does not fade when pressed: go to hospital now
  • A baby that is very sleepy, feeds badly or cries in a high pitch: see a doctor at once
  • Weakness of the face or limbs, or trouble speaking: call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Viral meningitis?

Swelling of the covering around the brain and spinal cord, caused by a virus. A virus irritates the thin layers that cover the brain and spinal cord. It is usually milder than the bacterial type, but it still needs a doctor to check it.

What are the common signs of Viral meningitis?

Fever, feeling hot · Stiff, aching neck · Bright light hurts the eyes · Feeling sick, nausea

Why does Viral meningitis happen?

Germs (infection) · Close contact or sharing items · A weak immune system

How can I look after myself with Viral meningitis?

Ask your pharmacist · Wash your hands often
