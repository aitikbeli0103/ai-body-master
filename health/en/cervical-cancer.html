Cervical cancer

Cancer of the cervix, the narrow neck of the womb that opens into the vagina

ConditionCancer & tumoursReproductive

What it is

Cervical cancer starts in the cells of the cervix. It is usually linked to long-lasting infection with a virus called HPV. Regular screening can find early changes.

  • Neck of the womb (cervix)
  • Women; mostly linked to HPV
  • Often no signs early on
Which doctor
Women's doctor (gynaecologist); family doctor for screening
Common in
Adult women, most often found in middle age
Contagious
No, but the HPV virus behind it can spread through sexual contact
Course
Often grows slowly over years; your doctor can explain what to expect

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-lasting infection with a high-risk type of HPV (many HPV infections clear by themselves)
  • Never or rarely having smear tests, so early changes are missed
  • Often several factors act together, and sometimes no clear reason is found

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy vaginal bleeding, or bleeding with dizziness or fainting: go to hospital now
  • Any bleeding after menopause, after sex, or between periods: see a doctor soon
  • Bloody or bad-smelling discharge that does not go away
  • Pain in the pelvis, back or legs, or swelling of one or both legs
  • Blood in the urine or stool, trouble passing urine, or weight loss with no reason
  • An abnormal smear test result that has not been followed up yet

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Go for regular smear tests or HPV tests, as your doctor advises
  • If cancer is found, follow your cancer team's plan, keep all appointments and never stop treatment on your own
  • Ask your team about pain, food and emotional support; you do not have to cope alone

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Cervical cancer?

Cancer of the cervix, the narrow neck of the womb that opens into the vagina. Cervical cancer starts in the cells of the cervix. It is usually linked to long-lasting infection with a virus called HPV. Regular screening can find early changes.

What are the common signs of Cervical cancer?

Often no signs at first · Bleeding between periods · Unusual discharge or itching down below · Dragging ache in the lower belly · Losing weight without trying

Why does Cervical cancer happen?

Smoking · A weak immune system

How can I look after myself with Cervical cancer?

Get the recommended vaccines · Stop smoking
