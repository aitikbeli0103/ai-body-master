Liver fibrosis (scarring of the liver)

Slow build-up of scar tissue in the liver

ConditionLiver, gallbladder & pancreasAbdomen

What it is

Liver fibrosis means scar tissue slowly builds up in the liver after long-term injury. Early on there are often no signs, and the cause can be looked after.

  • Inside the liver
  • Adults over many years
  • Often no feeling early
Which doctor
Family doctor; liver specialist (hepatologist)
Common in
People with fatty liver, hepatitis or long alcohol use
Contagious
No, but hepatitis viruses behind it can spread
Course
Usually slow over years; it may not get worse if the cause is looked after

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Fatty liver, linked to weight, diabetes or high blood fats
  • Hepatitis B or C infection
  • Bile tube problems or some medicines

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood or black stools: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Confusion or very sleepy
  • Yellow skin or eyes
  • A swollen belly or swollen legs
  • Weight loss you cannot explain, or fever with belly pain

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
