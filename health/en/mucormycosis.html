Mucormycosis

A rare but serious fungus infection, often of the nose, face and eyes

ConditionInfectionsWhole body

What it is

Mucor moulds from soil and rotting plants can grow in people whose defences are weak. It can spread fast and needs a doctor urgently.

  • Nose, face, eyes, lungs
  • People with weak defences
  • Needs urgent care
Which doctor
Hospital emergency; infection and ENT or eye doctors
Common in
People with poorly controlled diabetes or weak defences
Contagious
No, it does not spread from person to person
Course
Can worsen quickly; early care by a hospital team matters

Common signs

More signs

  • Black or dead-looking skin on the nose or inside the mouth

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Breathing in mould spores from soil, dust or rotting plants
  • Weak body defences, cancer treatment or an organ transplant

See a doctor first if

  • Black or dead-looking skin on the nose, face or mouth: go to hospital now
  • Sudden vision change, a swollen or bulging eye, or double vision
  • Facial pain or swelling on one side, with fever or bloody nose discharge
  • Confusion, fits, coughing blood or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Very high blood sugar readings with these signs

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Go to hospital quickly; do not wait or treat it at home
  • If you have diabetes, keep your blood sugar controlled with your doctor
  • Wear a mask in dusty places and protect skin wounds when gardening

Tell your therapist

  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Fever or an active infection

Questions and answers

What is Mucormycosis?

A rare but serious fungus infection, often of the nose, face and eyes. Mucor moulds from soil and rotting plants can grow in people whose defences are weak. It can spread fast and needs a doctor urgently.

What are the common signs of Mucormycosis?

Blocked nose · Numb or weak face · Swollen eyelid · Fever, feeling hot

Why does Mucormycosis happen?

High blood sugar or blood pressure · Long-term use of some medicines

How can I look after myself with Mucormycosis?

Take medicine as your doctor says
