Ulcerative proctitis

Inflammation and small sores in the lining of the rectum

ConditionStomach & intestinesPelvis

What it is

The rectum is the last part of the bowel before the anus. Its lining becomes inflamed, so you may feel an urgent need to go and see a little blood or mucus.

  • Last part of the bowel
  • Adults of any age
  • Urgency, blood or mucus
Which doctor
Gastroenterologist (bowel specialist), family doctor
Common in
Adults; it is a form of long-lasting bowel inflammation
Contagious
No
Course
Long-term and may come and go; a doctor can guide you

Common signs

More signs

  • Feeling an urgent need to pass stools
  • A little blood or mucus with the stool
  • Feeling you have not emptied the bowel

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body's defence system may act against the bowel lining

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy bleeding from the bottom, or fainting: go to hospital now
  • Severe belly pain with a swollen belly, vomiting and fever: go to hospital now
  • Blood in the stool that keeps happening
  • Weight loss, tiredness or a change in bowel habit lasting weeks
  • Signs of dehydration, such as very dry mouth and little urine

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep seeing your doctor and keep taking your medicine as told
  • Drink enough fluids and eat regular meals
  • Keep the area clean and gently dry
  • Do not stop any medicine on your own
