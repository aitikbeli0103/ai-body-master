Mycoplasma pneumonia

A lung infection caused by a small germ called Mycoplasma, often milder than other pneumonia

ConditionLungs & breathingChest

What it is

This is a lung infection that often starts like a cold, with a dry cough and tiredness that can last for weeks. It is sometimes called walking pneumonia.

  • Lungs and airways
  • Children and young adults
  • Cough, fever, tired
Which doctor
Family doctor; lung doctor if it is severe
Common in
Children and young adults; crowded places such as schools
Contagious
Yes, through droplets when an infected person coughs or sneezes
Course
Often mild, but the cough can linger for weeks

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A germ called Mycoplasma pneumoniae
  • A weaker body defence can make it more serious

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, fast breathing or blue lips: call 999
  • A baby or young child that is very unwell, not feeding or very sleepy: see a doctor promptly
  • Confusion, strong chest pain, or coughing up blood
  • High fever that lasts for days, or signs of dehydration
  • Older people, pregnant women and those with lung or heart disease: see a doctor early

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Mycoplasma pneumonia?

A lung infection caused by a small germ called Mycoplasma, often milder than other pneumonia. This is a lung infection that often starts like a cold, with a dry cough and tiredness that can last for weeks. It is sometimes called walking pneumonia.

What are the common signs of Mycoplasma pneumonia?

Dry, tickly cough · Fever, feeling hot · Sore, scratchy throat · Tired all the time · Shivering with chills

Why does Mycoplasma pneumonia happen?

Crowded places and droplets · Close contact or sharing items

How can I look after myself with Mycoplasma pneumonia?

Wash your hands often · Take medicine as your doctor says

Can an AI Body Master session help with Mycoplasma pneumonia?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Mycoplasma pneumonia and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
