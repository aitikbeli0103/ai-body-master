Myelitis (spinal cord inflammation)

Swelling and inflammation of the spinal cord that can affect movement and feeling

ConditionBrain & nervesBack

What it is

The spinal cord is inflamed, so messages between the brain and body are disturbed. This can cause weakness, numbness or bladder problems.

  • Spinal cord
  • Any age
  • Numb or weak legs
Which doctor
Brain and nerve doctor (neurologist); needs prompt care
Common in
Any age; sometimes after an infection or with an immune condition
Contagious
No
Course
Varies a lot; some people recover well and some have lasting problems

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body's defence system attacking the spinal cord
  • Related to other immune conditions such as multiple sclerosis

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden leg weakness or not being able to walk: go to hospital now
  • Not being able to pass urine, or losing control of urine or stools: go to hospital now
  • Numbness that spreads upwards quickly
  • Trouble breathing: call 999
  • Fever with severe back pain

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Get seen by a doctor quickly; do not wait to see if it passes
  • Move carefully at home and use support when walking
  • Check your skin often if you cannot feel it well

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Pregnancy
