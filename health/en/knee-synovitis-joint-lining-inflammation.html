Knee synovitis (joint lining inflammation)

Swelling and soreness of the thin lining inside the knee joint

ConditionBones, joints & spineArms & legsBones & muscles

What it is

The soft lining inside the knee becomes irritated and makes extra fluid, so the knee feels swollen, tight and sore.

  • Inside the knee joint
  • Any adult, often after strain
  • Swollen, tight, sore knee
Which doctor
Family doctor, bone doctor (orthopaedic) or joint doctor (rheumatologist)
Common in
Adults after knee injury or strain; people with arthritis
Contagious
No
Course
Often settles over days to weeks with rest, but can return if the cause stays

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A knee injury, strain or too much activity
  • Other kinds of arthritis, such as gout
  • Sometimes an infection, which needs a doctor quickly

See a doctor first if

  • A knee that is hot, red, very swollen and painful, especially with fever: go to hospital now
  • You cannot move the knee or put weight on it after a fall
  • A knee that is bent out of shape
  • One calf that is suddenly swollen, tight, red or painful
  • Swelling that keeps coming back or does not go down after a few days

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Raise the leg when sitting or lying down
  • Gentle movement as the pain eases helps stop the knee going stiff

Tell your therapist

  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Weak bones
