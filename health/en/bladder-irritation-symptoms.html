Bladder irritation symptoms

A group of bladder signs: urgency, frequency and burning when passing urine

ConditionKidneys & urinaryReproductive

What it is

This is not one illness but a set of signs: needing to pass urine suddenly, often, or with burning. A doctor can look for the cause.

  • Bladder and urine tube
  • Men and women
  • Urgent, frequent, burning
Which doctor
Family doctor; urologist or gynaecologist if needed
Common in
Women and men of any age, more with infection or an older prostate
Contagious
Some causes can be, such as certain infections
Course
Depends on the cause; many improve once it is found and managed

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Infections of the genital area that spread between partners
  • Irritation from some soaps, drinks or medicines

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with shivering, or pain in the side or back: see a doctor today
  • Blood in the urine
  • Cannot pass urine at all, or a very painful full bladder: go to hospital now
  • Unusual discharge, sores or pain in the genital area
  • Pregnant, or the signs last more than a few days

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See a doctor so the cause can be found
  • Go when you need to; do not hold urine for long
  • Keep the area clean and avoid strongly scented soaps

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Fever or an active infection

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

What are the common signs of Bladder irritation symptoms?

Burning when passing urine · Needing the toilet often · Waking at night to pass urine · Dragging ache in the lower belly · Leaking when coughing

Why does Bladder irritation symptoms happen?

Germs (infection) · An enlarged prostate

How can I look after myself with Bladder irritation symptoms?
