Facial muscle spasm (hemifacial spasm)

Twitching or tightening of the muscles on one side of the face

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

The muscles on one side of the face twitch or tighten without control, often starting around the eye. It is usually not painful but can be tiring and embarrassing.

  • One side of the face
  • Often in middle age or later
  • Twitching face
Which doctor
Brain and nerve doctor (neurologist), family doctor
Common in
Adults in middle age or later; a little more in women
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long-lasting and may slowly get stronger; a doctor can suggest ways to manage it

Common signs

  • Twitching around one eye that slowly spreads down the cheek and mouth
  • The eye may squeeze shut for a moment
  • Twitching worse with tiredness or stress
  • It can continue during sleep in some people

Why it happens

  • Often a blood vessel pressing on the facial nerve near the brain
  • Sometimes after facial palsy or a facial nerve injury
  • Rarely a growth or other problem near the nerve
  • Often the exact cause is not found

See a doctor first if

  • Face drooping with arm weakness or speech trouble coming on suddenly: call 999
  • Facial weakness or drooping that is new
  • Twitching on both sides of the face
  • Twitching with hearing loss, ringing in the ear, or bad headache
  • Twitching that makes it hard to see or drive

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
