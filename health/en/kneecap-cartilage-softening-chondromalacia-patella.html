Kneecap cartilage softening (chondromalacia patella)

Roughening and softening of the cartilage under the kneecap, causing knee pain

ConditionBones, joints & spineArms & legsBones & muscles

What it is

The smooth cushion under the kneecap becomes soft and rough. The front of the knee then aches, especially on stairs or when squatting.

  • Front of the knee
  • Often young, active people
  • Aching knee on stairs
Which doctor
Family doctor, sports doctor or bone doctor (orthopaedic)
Common in
Young active people, runners, and women; also after kneecap injury
Contagious
No
Course
Often eases over weeks to months with the right exercise and less strain

Common signs

More signs

  • More pain on stairs, squatting, kneeling or after sitting a long time
  • A grinding or cracking feel when the knee bends

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Too much running, jumping or stair climbing
  • The kneecap not sliding smoothly in its groove
  • Weak or tight thigh muscles, or flat feet

See a doctor first if

  • A knee that is hot, red and very swollen, especially with fever: go to hospital now
  • After a fall or knock, a bent knee or you cannot put weight on it
  • A knee that locks, gives way or the kneecap slips out of place
  • One calf that is suddenly swollen, tight, red or painful
  • Pain at night or at rest, or pain that does not ease after weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ease off running, jumping and deep squats while it is sore
  • Choose gentle options such as swimming or easy cycling
  • Strengthen the thigh muscles slowly; a physiotherapist can show you how

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
