Overly large breasts (macromastia)

Breasts that are so large they cause aching, skin soreness or trouble with daily life

ConditionWomen's healthChest

What it is

The weight of very large breasts can pull on the neck, shoulders and back and irritate the skin underneath. It is a physical problem, not just about looks.

  • Breasts, neck and back
  • Women, often from puberty
  • Heavy feeling, aching back
Which doctor
Family doctor; a breast or plastic surgeon to talk about options
Common in
Women, often starting in puberty or after weight gain or pregnancy
Contagious
No
Course
Size may change with weight, pregnancy and age; the doctor can explain the options

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Breast tissue that reacts strongly to normal hormones, often from puberty
  • Weight gain, because breasts hold fat
  • Rarely, some medicines or a hormone problem; ask your doctor if you are worried

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, trouble breathing, or pain spreading to the arm or jaw: call 999
  • A new lump, dimpled or thickened skin, or a nipple that turns inward
  • Fluid or blood coming from the nipple without squeezing
  • One breast growing much faster than the other, or very fast growth
  • A red, hot, painful breast with fever: see a doctor today
  • Numb or tingling hands, or skin under the breasts that is raw, cracked or smelly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wear a well-fitted, supportive bra with wide straps and a firm band; a trained bra fitter can help
  • Keep the skin under the breasts clean and dry, and wear soft, breathable cotton
  • Do gentle neck and shoulder stretches and sit and stand tall; stop if they hurt more
  • If size affects your health or confidence, talk to your doctor about the options

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pregnancy
