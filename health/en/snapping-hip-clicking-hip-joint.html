Snapping hip (clicking hip joint)

A clicking, snapping or popping feeling at the hip when you move

ConditionBones, joints & spineArms & legs

What it is

A tendon or muscle slides over a bone at the hip, or the joint itself clicks, when you walk, get up or swing the leg. It is often painless, but can sometimes be sore.

  • Side or front of the hip
  • Active people, dancers
  • Click or snap when moving
Which doctor
Family doctor, orthopaedic doctor or physiotherapist
Common in
Young active adults, runners, dancers and cyclists
Contagious
No
Course
Often harmless and can come and go; see a doctor if it becomes painful

Common signs

  • A click, snap or pop at the hip when walking, standing up or swinging the leg
  • Often no pain at all
  • Sometimes a mild ache or a feeling of catching at the side of the hip
  • Soreness after a lot of exercise

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A tight tendon or muscle sliding over the bony part of the hip
  • Tight or weak hip and thigh muscles
  • Sometimes a problem inside the joint, such as a tear in the cartilage

See a doctor first if

  • Hip pain after a fall or accident, or you cannot put weight on the leg: go to hospital
  • A hip that locks, gives way or feels very unstable
  • Clicking with a lot of pain, swelling or a limp
  • Hip pain with fever, or a hot, red hip
  • Pain at night or at rest that keeps getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • If it does not hurt, it usually does not need any special care
  • Warm up before exercise and stretch the hip and thigh muscles gently
  • Ease off the activity that brings on the pain for a while
  • Build up exercise slowly rather than suddenly

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
