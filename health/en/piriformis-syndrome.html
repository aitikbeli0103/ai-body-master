Piriformis syndrome

Tightness of a small buttock muscle that can irritate the nerve and cause buttock and leg pain

ConditionBones, joints & spinePelvisBones & muscles

What it is

The piriformis is a small muscle deep in the buttock. When it is tight or irritated, it can press on the nearby sciatic nerve and cause pain in the buttock that may run down the leg.

  • Buttock and hip
  • After long sitting or activity
  • Deep ache, may reach the leg
Which doctor
Family doctor, physiotherapist, orthopaedic doctor
Common in
Adults who sit a lot, runners and people who cycle
Contagious
No
Course
Often eases over weeks with rest and gentle care; it can come back

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Sitting for a long time, especially on a hard seat or a thick wallet
  • Overusing the hip muscles in running, cycling or climbing
  • Weak or tight hip and back muscles

See a doctor first if

  • Loss of control of the bladder or bowels, or numbness around the groin or inner thighs: go to hospital now
  • Sudden weakness in a leg or foot
  • Pain after a serious fall or accident
  • Fever, or a hot, red, swollen buttock
  • Pain that is getting worse, is very bad at night, or does not ease after some weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Get up and move about often if you sit for long periods
  • Avoid sitting on a thick wallet or on very hard seats
  • Rest from the activity that brings on the pain, then return slowly
  • Do gentle stretches only as far as is comfortable
  • Ask your doctor or physiotherapist for safe exercises

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
