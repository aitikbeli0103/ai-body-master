Demyelinating disease

Damage to the protective coating around nerves, which slows messages in the body

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

Nerves are wrapped in a coating called myelin. When it is damaged, messages travel slowly or get lost, which can cause numbness, weakness, vision or balance problems.

  • Brain, eyes, spinal cord
  • Often starts in adults
  • Numb, weak, unsteady
Which doctor
Neurologist
Common in
Mostly young and middle-aged adults; more often women
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes over time; your neurologist can explain what to expect

Common signs

More signs

  • Trouble passing urine or with bowel habits

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body's own defence system mistakenly attacks the nerve coating
  • Some types follow an infection or a vaccine, but this is rare

See a doctor first if

  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble that is sudden: call 999 now
  • Trouble breathing or swallowing: call 999 now
  • Sudden loss of vision in one eye: go to hospital now
  • Weak legs with loss of control of urine or stools: go to hospital now
  • New numbness or weakness that lasts more than a day

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop or change medicine on your own; ask your doctor or pharmacist
  • Pace your day and rest before you get too tired
  • Avoid getting too hot, as heat can make signs worse

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Demyelinating disease?

Damage to the protective coating around nerves, which slows messages in the body. Nerves are wrapped in a coating called myelin. When it is damaged, messages travel slowly or get lost, which can cause numbness, weakness, vision or balance problems.

What are the common signs of Demyelinating disease?

Tingling in the hand · Legs feel weak · Blurry vision · Tired all the time

Why does Demyelinating disease happen?

The cause is not fully known · Germs (infection)

How can I look after myself with Demyelinating disease?

See your doctor for check-ups · Make the home safe from falls
