Tear gland inflammation (dacryoadenitis)

Swelling and soreness of the gland that makes tears

ConditionEyesHead

What it is

The tear gland, found above the outer corner of the eye, becomes inflamed. The outer part of the upper eyelid can swell and feel sore.

  • Upper outer eyelid
  • Less common; any age
  • Swollen, tender eyelid
Which doctor
Eye doctor (ophthalmologist)
Common in
Children and young adults after a viral infection; also older adults
Contagious
Some causes can be, such as viral infections
Course
Depends on the cause; a doctor must find it

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain or tenderness around the eye
  • A drooping, S-shaped upper eyelid

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Bacterial infection
  • Inflammatory conditions of the body
  • Often the cause is not found at first

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with a very swollen, red eye area: see a doctor today
  • Pain when moving the eye, double vision or loss of vision: go to hospital now
  • The eye seems pushed forward
  • A child with a swollen eye and fever: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
