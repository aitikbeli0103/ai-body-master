Pilonidal sinus

A small pit or tunnel in the skin at the top of the buttock crease that can collect hair and get infected

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsPelvis

What it is

A small opening in the skin near the tailbone can trap loose hairs and debris; it may cause no trouble, or it can become a painful, swollen lump with pus.

  • Near the tailbone
  • Young adults, more often men
  • Pain when sitting
Which doctor
Family doctor first; a surgeon for drainage or an operation
Common in
Teens and young adults, more often men; people who sit a lot or have thick body hair
Contagious
No
Course
Can flare up and come back; a doctor can explain how to lower the chance

Common signs

More signs

  • A small dimple or hole in the skin at the top of the buttock crease
  • Pus or blood coming out, sometimes with a bad smell
  • Pain when sitting, bending or walking

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Loose hairs can dig into the skin in the buttock crease, and the body reacts as it would to a splinter
  • A deep crease between the buttocks, or thick, coarse body hair
  • Extra body weight and tight clothes can add to the rubbing

See a doctor first if

  • Fever, chills or feeling very unwell with a painful swelling: see a doctor today
  • Redness or swelling that is spreading, or red streaks on the skin
  • Diabetes or a weak immune system with a red, sore or swollen area: see a doctor promptly
  • Severe pain that makes it hard to sit, walk or sleep
  • Pus or blood from the opening, or a bad smell
  • It keeps coming back, or a wound near the tailbone does not close up after an operation

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wear loose, breathable clothes to cut down rubbing and sweat
  • Ask your doctor how to keep hair in the area short, and how to lower the chance of it coming back

Questions and answers

What is Pilonidal sinus?

A small pit or tunnel in the skin at the top of the buttock crease that can collect hair and get infected. A small opening in the skin near the tailbone can trap loose hairs and debris; it may cause no trouble, or it can become a painful, swollen lump with pus.

What are the common signs of Pilonidal sinus?

A lump under the skin · Fever, feeling hot

Why does Pilonidal sinus happen?

Standing or sitting still for long

How can I look after myself with Pilonidal sinus?

Wash gently and pat dry · Do not squeeze or pick at it · Take breaks from sitting
