Urinary fistula

An abnormal channel that lets urine leak out through the wrong opening

ConditionKidneys & urinaryReproductive

What it is

An unwanted connection forms between the urinary tract and another part, such as the vagina or bowel, so urine leaks without control.

  • Lower belly and private area
  • Seen mostly in women
  • Constant leaking of urine
Which doctor
Urologist or gynaecologist
Common in
Women after childbirth, surgery or pelvic radiation
Contagious
No
Course
Usually needs a doctor's care; many people can be helped

Common signs

  • Urine leaking all the time, day and night
  • Urine coming out of the vagina or the bowel opening
  • Skin around the private area that is sore or red
  • Repeated urine infections or a bad smell

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Difficult or long childbirth
  • Radiation to the pelvis, or some cancers
  • Infection or long-term inflammation in the area

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with chills or pain in the side: go to hospital now
  • Urine leaking after an operation or childbirth: see a doctor promptly
  • Blood in the urine
  • Stools or gas leaking from the wrong place
  • Sore, broken skin that is not healing

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep the skin clean and dry, and change pads often
  • Use a gentle skin barrier cream if your doctor agrees
  • Talk to someone you trust; this is a medical problem, not your fault

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Pregnancy
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Broken, red or sore skin

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
