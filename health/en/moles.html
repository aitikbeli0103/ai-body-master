Moles

Small brown or dark spots on the skin that are harmless in most people

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Moles are small spots where pigment cells in the skin group together. Most stay the same and cause no trouble, but a mole that changes should be looked at by a doctor.

  • Can be anywhere on the skin
  • Seen at every age
  • Small brown or dark spots
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor (dermatologist) for any change
Common in
People of all ages; fair-skinned people and those with much sun often have more
Contagious
No
Course
Most stay the same; some fade or become raised as you get older

Common signs

  • A small brown, black, tan or pink spot on the skin
  • Can be flat or raised, smooth or slightly rough
  • Most are small, round or oval, with an even colour
  • Can appear alone or in groups on any part of the body
  • Does not usually hurt, itch or bleed

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Pigment cells grow together in one spot; the exact reason is often not known

See a doctor first if

  • A mole that grows, changes colour or shape, or looks different from the others: see a doctor soon
  • A mole that bleeds, oozes, or forms a crust or a sore that stays open
  • A mole that becomes itchy, tender or painful for no clear reason
  • A new dark mole in an adult that keeps changing
  • A mole with several colours, a blurry border or two uneven halves
  • A large mole present since birth: ask a doctor to look at it

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Check your skin every month with a mirror, including the back, scalp and feet
  • Take a photo of any mole you worry about and compare it a few weeks later
  • Never cut, burn or scrape a mole, or put acid or strong home remedies on it
  • Want a mole removed? Ask a doctor first, not a beauty shop or home method

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Pregnancy
