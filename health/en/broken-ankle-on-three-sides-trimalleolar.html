Broken ankle on three sides (trimalleolar)

A break involving three parts of the ankle joint

ConditionAnkle & footArms & legsBones & muscles

What it is

The ankle is formed by the shin bones and the foot bone. In this injury the outer bone, the inner bone and the back edge of the shin bone are all broken, so the ankle becomes unstable.

  • Ankle joint
  • After a twist, fall or accident
  • Pain, swelling, cannot stand
Which doctor
Emergency department; orthopaedic doctor
Common in
Adults after a bad fall, sports injury or road accident
Contagious
No
Course
Often needs a cast or an operation; recovery usually takes months

Common signs

More signs

  • The ankle may look bent or out of shape

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A hard twist of the ankle while the foot is planted
  • Weak bones make a break more likely

See a doctor first if

  • The ankle or foot looks bent or out of place: go to hospital now
  • Cannot put weight on the foot or move the ankle: go to hospital
  • A foot that is cold, pale, blue or numb: go to hospital now
  • Bone showing through the skin or a deep cut: call 999
  • After treatment: a cast that is too tight, a swollen painful calf, or fever

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep weight off the foot and get to a doctor quickly
  • Keep the cast dry and clean, and use crutches the way you were shown
  • Follow your doctor's plan for walking and exercise; ask about pain relief

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Weak bones
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
