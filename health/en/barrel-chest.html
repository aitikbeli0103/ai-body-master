Barrel chest

A chest that looks rounded and deep from front to back, often linked to long-term lung disease

ConditionLungs & breathingChest

What it is

A barrel chest is a shape of the chest, not a sickness by itself. It can be a sign that the lungs hold too much air, so it is worth asking a doctor why.

  • Rib cage
  • Often older adults
  • Round chest, short breath
Which doctor
Family doctor; chest or lung specialist
Common in
Older adults, smokers, people with long-term lung disease
Contagious
No
Course
The shape usually stays; the cause behind it is what a doctor looks at

Common signs

More signs

  • A chest that looks deep and rounded from front to back

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-term lung disease such as emphysema or chronic bronchitis
  • Asthma that has been present for a long time

See a doctor first if

  • Severe trouble breathing, blue lips or cannot speak in full sentences: call 999
  • Chest pain, or coughing up blood: go to hospital now
  • Breathing that is getting worse over days, or new fever with more phlegm
  • Swollen legs, or losing weight without trying
  • A cough that has lasted more than three weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Barrel chest?

A chest that looks rounded and deep from front to back, often linked to long-term lung disease. A barrel chest is a shape of the chest, not a sickness by itself. It can be a sign that the lungs hold too much air, so it is worth asking a doctor why.

What are the common signs of Barrel chest?

Short of breath on stairs · Cough with phlegm · Wheezing breath

Why does Barrel chest happen?

Smoking · Getting older

How can I look after myself with Barrel chest?

See your doctor for check-ups · Stay active every day · Get the recommended vaccines
