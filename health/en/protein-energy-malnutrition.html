Protein-energy malnutrition

Severe lack of protein and energy from food, seen mostly in young children

ConditionEating & drinkingHeadNeckChestAbdomenPelvisArms & legsSkinBlood

What it is

The body does not get enough protein and energy from food. The person becomes very thin and weak, and some have swelling. It needs a doctor's care.

  • Whole body
  • Young children, frail elders
  • Very thin and weak
Which doctor
Doctor at once; a paediatrician for a child
Common in
Young children and very frail or ill older adults
Contagious
No
Course
Needs a doctor's care; many people improve with proper feeding

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Too little food, or food with too little protein and energy
  • Repeated infections or diarrhoea that drain the body
  • Long illness, gut problems or cancer
  • Older people who cannot chew, swallow or shop for food
  • Poverty or neglect

See a doctor first if

  • Very sleepy, hard to wake, fits or fainting: go to hospital now
  • Swollen feet, legs, hands or face
  • Fast breathing, cold hands and feet or fever
  • A child who refuses food or drink, or keeps vomiting or has diarrhoea: see a doctor promptly
  • Very thin, listless or not growing

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • See a doctor early; feeding should be restarted with medical guidance
  • Offer small, frequent, soft meals with eggs, fish, beans or tofu
  • Keep breastfeeding a baby unless the doctor says otherwise
  • Keep hands, bottles and food clean to avoid stomach infections

Tell your therapist

  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • A heart condition
  • Cancer or cancer treatment

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Protein-energy malnutrition?

Severe lack of protein and energy from food, seen mostly in young children. The body does not get enough protein and energy from food. The person becomes very thin and weak, and some have swelling. It needs a doctor's care.

What are the common signs of Protein-energy malnutrition?

Losing weight without trying · Tired all the time · Legs feel weak · Swollen legs and ankles · Hair coming out

Why does Protein-energy malnutrition happen?

Too little food, or food with too little protein and energy · Repeated infections or diarrhoea that drain the body · Long illness, gut problems or cancer · Older people who cannot chew, swallow or shop for food · Poverty or neglect

How can I look after myself with Protein-energy malnutrition?

See a doctor early; feeding should be restarted with medical guidance · Offer small, frequent, soft meals with eggs, fish, beans or tofu · Keep breastfeeding a baby unless the doctor says otherwise · Keep hands, bottles and food clean to avoid stomach infections
