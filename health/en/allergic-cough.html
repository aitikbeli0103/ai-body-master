Allergic cough

A dry, tickly cough set off by an allergy to things like dust, pollen or pet fur

ConditionAllergiesHead

What it is

The throat and airways react to something the body is allergic to, so the cough comes again and again, often with an itchy throat but little phlegm.

  • Throat and airways
  • Children and adults
  • Dry, tickly cough
Which doctor
Family doctor; allergy or lung (respiratory) doctor if it lasts
Common in
People with hay fever, asthma or eczema; children and adults
Contagious
No
Course
Often returns whenever the trigger is around, and may last for weeks

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Mucus dripping down the back of the throat from an allergic nose can set off coughing
  • It can go together with asthma or hay fever, and tends to run in families with allergies

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, wheezing, or blue or grey lips: call 999 now
  • Swelling of the lips, tongue, face or throat after something you ate, touched or breathed in: call 999
  • A baby or child who is breathing fast, struggling to breathe or very unwell: go to hospital now
  • Chest pain with the cough, or coughing up blood: go to hospital now
  • Fever, or thick yellow or green phlegm: it may be an infection
  • A cough that lasts more than two weeks, or comes with weight loss, night sweats or tiredness

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Notice when the cough starts and what is around you, to help find the trigger
  • Keep away from known triggers; wash bedding often and keep the home clean and dry
  • Stay away from smoke, strong smells and dusty places; wear a mask when the haze is bad
  • Sip water often to keep the throat moist

Tell your therapist

  • A severe allergy
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Fever or an active infection

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Allergic cough?

A dry, tickly cough set off by an allergy to things like dust, pollen or pet fur. The throat and airways react to something the body is allergic to, so the cough comes again and again, often with an itchy throat but little phlegm.

What are the common signs of Allergic cough?

Dry, tickly cough · Sore, scratchy throat · Sneezing, runny nose · Blocked nose

Why does Allergic cough happen?

Dust, pollen or pets · Smoke, haze and strong smells · Acid coming up from the stomach
