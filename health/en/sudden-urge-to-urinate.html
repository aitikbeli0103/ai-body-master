Sudden urge to urinate

A strong, sudden need to pass urine that is hard to put off

SymptomKidneys & urinaryAbdomen

What it is

You feel a sudden need to pass urine right away. It is a sign that the bladder is irritated or overactive, and it has several possible causes.

  • Bladder
  • More common with age
  • Sudden, strong urge
Which doctor
Family doctor; urologist or gynaecologist
Common in
Adults of any age, more often older adults
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; many people do well once it is found

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Urine infection
  • Stones, or sometimes a nerve problem

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine, or a swollen painful lower belly: go to hospital now
  • Fever with chills or pain in the side or back
  • Blood in the urine
  • Very thirsty, passing a lot of urine and losing weight
  • Urgency that lasts for days or disturbs your sleep

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Try to wait a little each time and breathe slowly
  • Keep a simple diary of drinks and toilet visits for your doctor

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin

Questions and answers

What is Sudden urge to urinate?

A strong, sudden need to pass urine that is hard to put off. You feel a sudden need to pass urine right away. It is a sign that the bladder is irritated or overactive, and it has several possible causes.

What are the common signs of Sudden urge to urinate?

Needing the toilet often · Waking at night to pass urine · Leaking when coughing · Burning when passing urine

Why does Sudden urge to urinate happen?

Getting older · Drinking alcohol · An enlarged prostate

How can I look after myself with Sudden urge to urinate?

Drink enough water · Cut down on alcohol

Can an AI Body Master session help with Sudden urge to urinate?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Sudden urge to urinate and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
