Laryngeal cancer (voice box cancer)

Cancer of the voice box (larynx), which can change the voice and sometimes makes swallowing or breathing harder

ConditionCancer & tumoursHeadNeck

What it is

The voice box (larynx) sits in the neck at the top of the windpipe and holds the vocal cords, and a common first sign of cancer there is a hoarse voice that does not get better.

  • The voice box, in the neck
  • Smokers; mostly older adults
  • Hoarse voice, sore throat
Which doctor
Family doctor first; then an ENT (ear, nose, throat) or cancer specialist
Common in
Mostly older adults; people who smoke or drink a lot of alcohol
Contagious
No
Course
Differs from person to person; your care team explains the plan and what to expect

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Sometimes the cause is not known

See a doctor first if

  • Noisy or struggling breathing, or choking when you swallow: call 999
  • Coughing up blood: go to hospital now
  • A hoarse voice that lasts more than three weeks, especially if you smoke: see a doctor promptly
  • A lump in the neck, or a sore throat or ear pain on one side that does not go away
  • Pain when swallowing, or food that gets stuck
  • Weight loss without trying, or a cough that will not go away

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask your team about help with speaking, swallowing, food and pain
  • Never stop treatment on your own; talk to your team first

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A heart condition
