Menopause

The natural time in life when periods stop and hormone levels change

ConditionWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

Menopause is when the ovaries slow down and periods stop for good, usually in the late forties or fifties. It is a normal stage, though some women have uncomfortable changes.

Questions and answers

What is Menopause?

The natural time in life when periods stop and hormone levels change. Menopause is when the ovaries slow down and periods stop for good, usually in the late forties or fifties. It is a normal stage, though some women have uncomfortable changes.

What are the common signs of Menopause?

Period comes late or early · Sudden hot flushes · Soaking sweats at night · Cannot fall asleep · Low mood, no interest

Why does Menopause happen?

Hormone changes · Getting older · Smoking

How can I look after myself with Menopause?

Stay active every day · Eat healthy, balanced meals · Cut down on coffee and tea

Can an AI Body Master session help with Menopause?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Menopause and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
