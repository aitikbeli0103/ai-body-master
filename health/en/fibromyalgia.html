Fibromyalgia

A long-term condition with pain in muscles and soft tissues all over the body

ConditionAutoimmune & rheumaticBones & muscles

What it is

Fibromyalgia causes aching all over the body, with tiredness and poor sleep. It is real, though scans and blood tests usually look normal.

  • Muscles all over the body
  • Adults, more often women
  • Deep ache and tiredness
Which doctor
Family doctor; rheumatologist; pain doctor
Common in
Adults, more often women, often in middle age
Contagious
No
Course
Long-term; symptoms often come and go, and many people learn to manage them

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body may feel pain more strongly than usual
  • It can start after an illness, an injury or a very stressful time
  • It sometimes runs in families, and poor sleep can make it worse

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999
  • A hot, red, swollen joint, or fever with the pain
  • Weak muscles, numbness, or losing weight without trying
  • Pain that is new, sudden or getting worse quickly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Do gentle activity such as walking, slowly building up
  • Keep a regular bedtime and a quiet, dark bedroom
  • Warm baths or a warm pack may ease aches
  • Pace yourself: rest between tasks and avoid overdoing good days
  • Talk to family or friends; ask your doctor before any medicine

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • A heart condition
  • High blood pressure

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Fibromyalgia?

A long-term condition with pain in muscles and soft tissues all over the body. Fibromyalgia causes aching all over the body, with tiredness and poor sleep. It is real, though scans and blood tests usually look normal.

What are the common signs of Fibromyalgia?

Aching all over · Tired all the time · Cannot fall asleep · Foggy, cannot focus · Low mood, no interest

Why does Fibromyalgia happen?

The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Fibromyalgia?

Do gentle activity such as walking, slowly building up · Keep a regular bedtime and a quiet, dark bedroom · Warm baths or a warm pack may ease aches · Pace yourself: rest between tasks and avoid overdoing good days · Talk to family or friends; ask your doctor before any medicine
