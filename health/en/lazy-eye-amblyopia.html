Lazy eye (amblyopia)

Poor sight in one or both eyes in a child, because the brain does not use that eye well

ConditionEyesHead

What it is

The eye looks normal but sight is weaker, because the eye and brain did not learn to work well together while growing up. Finding it early in a child gives the best chance to help.

  • Eye and brain link
  • Starts in early childhood
  • One eye sees less well
Which doctor
Eye doctor (ophthalmologist); for a child, a children's eye doctor
Common in
Young children; sometimes found only when older
Contagious
No
Course
Best helped when found early in childhood; an eye doctor plans the follow-up

Common signs

More signs

  • Squinting or closing one eye
  • Tilting the head, or bumping into things on one side
  • An eye that turns in or out, or poor depth sense

Why it happens

Other causes

  • An eye that turns in or out (a squint)
  • A big difference in glasses strength between the two eyes
  • Something blocking the clear view, such as a cloudy lens or a droopy eyelid

See a doctor first if

  • A child who seems not to see well or does not follow objects: see a doctor promptly
  • A white or grey look in the middle of the eye, seen in photos or in person
  • A new squint, or an eye that droops
  • A child who is very unwell with eye changes: go to hospital now

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Have your child's eyes checked early, even without complaints
  • Use glasses or other help exactly as the eye doctor says
  • Be patient and encouraging, as it can take time

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

What is Lazy eye (amblyopia)?

Poor sight in one or both eyes in a child, because the brain does not use that eye well. The eye looks normal but sight is weaker, because the eye and brain did not learn to work well together while growing up. Finding it early in a child gives the best chance to help.

Why does Lazy eye (amblyopia) happen?
