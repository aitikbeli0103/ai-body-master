Small cell bladder cancer

A rare, fast-growing cancer that starts in the lining of the bladder

ConditionCancer & tumoursPelvis

What it is

This is a rare type of bladder cancer made of small round cells. It can grow quickly, so signs such as blood in the urine should be checked early.

  • The bladder
  • Mostly older adults
  • Blood in the urine
Which doctor
Urinary specialist (urologist), cancer doctor (oncologist)
Common in
Mostly older adults; smokers are at higher risk
Contagious
No
Course
It can move quickly; your cancer team can explain it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Past cancer treatment in the pelvis
  • Long-lasting bladder irritation or infection

See a doctor first if

  • Unable to pass urine at all, or heavy bleeding with clots: go to hospital now
  • Any blood in the urine, even once
  • Fever with shivering and burning urine
  • Severe pain in the side or lower belly
  • Losing weight without trying, or bone pain

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Small cell bladder cancer?

A rare, fast-growing cancer that starts in the lining of the bladder. This is a rare type of bladder cancer made of small round cells. It can grow quickly, so signs such as blood in the urine should be checked early.

What are the common signs of Small cell bladder cancer?

Pink or red urine · Needing the toilet often · Burning when passing urine · Lower back pain · Losing weight without trying

Why does Small cell bladder cancer happen?

Smoking · Dust or chemicals at work

How can I look after myself with Small cell bladder cancer?

See your doctor for check-ups · Stop smoking

Can an AI Body Master session help with Small cell bladder cancer?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Small cell bladder cancer and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
