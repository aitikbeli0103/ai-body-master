Chest tightness

A feeling of pressure, squeezing or not being able to breathe deeply

SymptomHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

The chest feels tight, heavy or squeezed, and a full breath feels hard. Causes range from stress to heart or lung problems, so it is worth taking seriously.

  • Chest area
  • Any age
  • Tight, squeezed, heavy
Which doctor
Family doctor; heart or lung doctor if needed
Common in
Anyone; often with stress, asthma, colds or heart problems
Contagious
No, but a chest infection that causes it can spread
Course
Depends on the cause; if new or it keeps coming back, see a doctor

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Asthma, a cold, or a chest infection
  • Heart problems, such as a narrowed heart artery
  • Stomach acid, or tired chest muscles from coughing

See a doctor first if

  • Tightness with chest pain, sweating or pain spreading to the arm or jaw: call 999
  • Severe trouble breathing, blue lips, or being unable to speak in full sentences: call 999
  • Fainting or feeling about to faint
  • Tightness that comes on when you walk or climb and eases with rest
  • Fever with a cough, or coughing blood
  • Swollen legs together with breathlessness

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Chest tightness?

A feeling of pressure, squeezing or not being able to breathe deeply. The chest feels tight, heavy or squeezed, and a full breath feels hard. Causes range from stress to heart or lung problems, so it is worth taking seriously.

What are the common signs of Chest tightness?

Tight, heavy chest · Short of breath on stairs · Wheezing breath · Racing, pounding heart · Dry, tickly cough

Why does Chest tightness happen?

Stress · Smoking

How can I look after myself with Chest tightness?

Find ways to relax · Stop smoking

Can an AI Body Master session help with Chest tightness?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Chest tightness and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
