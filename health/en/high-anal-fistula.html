High anal fistula

A small tunnel between the inside of the bottom and the skin nearby, deeper in the muscle

ConditionStomach & intestinesPelvis

What it is

A tunnel can form from inside the back passage to the skin near it, running higher through the muscle. It often follows an abscess and may leak pus or fluid.

  • Around the back passage
  • Adults, often after an abscess
  • Pain, swelling, leaking
Which doctor
Colorectal surgeon, family doctor
Common in
Adults after an anal abscess; people with Crohn's disease
Contagious
No
Course
Usually does not close by itself; a specialist can explain the options

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain or swelling near the back passage, worse when sitting
  • Pus, blood or fluid leaking from a small opening in the skin
  • Pain when passing stools

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Most often an earlier abscess near the back passage
  • Bowel conditions such as Crohn's disease
  • An infection, an injury or past surgery in the area

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with chills and severe pain or swelling near the back passage: go to hospital now
  • A hot, red, hard, very painful lump (possible abscess)
  • Heavy bleeding from the back passage
  • Stools that leak, or you cannot control wind or stools
  • Fluid or pus that keeps coming for more than a few weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See a doctor early; this does not usually settle without a specialist
  • Keep the area clean and dry; wash gently with warm water
  • Eat fibre and drink water to keep stools soft
  • Use a soft pad for leaking fluid and change it often

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
