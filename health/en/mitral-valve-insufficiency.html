Mitral valve insufficiency

Another name for a mitral valve that does not close properly and leaks

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

The valve between two heart chambers does not shut tightly, so some blood flows back. This can leave the heart working harder over time.

  • Valve inside the heart
  • More common with age
  • Tired, short of breath
Which doctor
Heart doctor (cardiologist), family doctor
Common in
Older adults; people with other heart problems
Contagious
No
Course
Often slow to change; some people have no problems for years

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A stretched heart or weakened heart muscle
  • Damage after a heart attack or a heart infection
  • Past rheumatic fever, or a valve that was different from birth

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, or sudden trouble breathing: call 999
  • Fainting or nearly fainting
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • A fast, irregular heartbeat that does not settle
  • Swollen legs or breathlessness getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Mitral valve insufficiency?

Another name for a mitral valve that does not close properly and leaks. The valve between two heart chambers does not shut tightly, so some blood flows back. This can leave the heart working harder over time.

What are the common signs of Mitral valve insufficiency?

Short of breath on stairs · Tired all the time · Racing, pounding heart · Swollen legs and ankles

Why does Mitral valve insufficiency happen?

Getting older

How can I look after myself with Mitral valve insufficiency?

See your doctor for check-ups · Keep a healthy weight · Stop smoking

Can an AI Body Master session help with Mitral valve insufficiency?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Mitral valve insufficiency and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
