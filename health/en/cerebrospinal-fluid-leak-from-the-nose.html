Cerebrospinal fluid leak from the nose

Clear, watery fluid from the brain's cushion leaking out through the nose

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

The clear fluid that cushions the brain and spine leaks through a small opening and drips from the nose. It is rare and needs a doctor to check it.

  • Nose and base of skull
  • After a head injury or surgery
  • Clear fluid dripping
Which doctor
Go to the hospital; ear, nose and throat doctor or brain surgeon
Common in
After a head injury or sinus or brain surgery; sometimes with no clear cause
Contagious
No
Course
Needs a doctor's check; some stop alone, others need a procedure

Common signs

More signs

  • Clear, watery fluid dripping from one side of the nose
  • More fluid when you lean forward or strain
  • A salty or metallic taste in the mouth
  • A headache that is worse sitting up and better lying down

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A growth or a thin spot in the bone at the base of the skull
  • Often it starts with no clear cause

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with a stiff neck, strong headache or confusion: call 999
  • Clear fluid from the nose after a head injury: go to hospital now
  • Fits, drowsiness, vomiting or weakness on one side of the body: call 999
  • Watery fluid from the nose that keeps coming, with no cold or allergy

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • See a doctor as soon as you notice clear fluid; do not wait
  • Try not to blow your nose hard, sneeze with the mouth closed or strain
  • Do not put anything into the nose
  • Follow your doctor's advice on rest and activity

Tell your therapist

  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Epilepsy or fits

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
