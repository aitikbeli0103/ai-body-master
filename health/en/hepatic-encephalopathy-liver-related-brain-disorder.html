Hepatic encephalopathy (liver-related brain disorder)

Confusion and changes in thinking or behaviour caused by a poorly working liver

ConditionLiver, gallbladder & pancreasHead

What it is

When the liver cannot clear waste from the blood well, it can affect the brain. A person may become confused, sleepy or act differently from usual.

  • Brain and liver
  • People with liver disease
  • Confusion, sleepiness
Which doctor
Liver doctor (hepatologist), stomach and gut doctor, hospital doctor
Common in
People with serious long-term liver disease
Contagious
No
Course
It can come and go; it needs a doctor, and early care matters

Common signs

More signs

  • Mood or personality change, restlessness

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Serious or long-term liver disease, such as cirrhosis

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot be woken, fits, or very hard to wake: call 999
  • Sudden confusion in someone with liver disease: go to hospital now
  • Vomiting blood or black stools: go to hospital now
  • Fever, yellow skin or eyes, or a swollen belly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Take your liver medicines exactly as your doctor said
  • Do not drink alcohol
  • Family can watch for sleep or behaviour changes and call the doctor early

Tell your therapist

  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Epilepsy or fits

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Hepatic encephalopathy (liver-related brain disorder)?

Confusion and changes in thinking or behaviour caused by a poorly working liver. When the liver cannot clear waste from the blood well, it can affect the brain. A person may become confused, sleepy or act differently from usual.

What are the common signs of Hepatic encephalopathy (liver-related brain disorder)?

Forgetting things · Sleepy in the daytime · Shaking hands · Slurred or unclear speech

Why does Hepatic encephalopathy (liver-related brain disorder) happen?

Not drinking enough water · Straining on the toilet · Drinking alcohol

How can I look after myself with Hepatic encephalopathy (liver-related brain disorder)?

Drink enough water · Ask your pharmacist
