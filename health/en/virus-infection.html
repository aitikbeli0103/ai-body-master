Virus infection

A virus infection that can show up on the skin, in the blood or all over the body

ConditionInfectionsSkinBloodWhole body

What it is

Some viruses spread through the blood and can cause fever and a rash. Most are mild, but some can be serious.

  • Skin, blood, whole body
  • Children and adults
  • Fever and a rash
Which doctor
Family doctor; infection specialist if severe
Common in
Anyone, especially during outbreaks or after mosquito bites
Contagious
Some types can be
Course
Often settles in about a week or two; some types last longer

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Not being protected by vaccines that are advised

See a doctor first if

  • Bleeding from the gums or nose, black stools or vomiting blood: go to hospital now
  • Severe belly pain, constant vomiting, or cold clammy skin: go to hospital now
  • Trouble breathing, confusion, fits or a stiff neck: call 999
  • A baby or child who is very sleepy, not drinking or very unwell: see a doctor promptly
  • Pregnant and has a fever or rash: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Rest and drink plenty of fluids
  • Prevent mosquito bites: repellent, long sleeves, no standing water at home
  • Stay home while you have a fever or a rash that may spread

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Broken, red or sore skin
