Neutropenia (low neutrophil count)

Too few neutrophils, a type of white blood cell that fights germs

ConditionBloodBlood

What it is

Neutrophils help the body fight germs. When there are too few, infections can start more easily and may become serious quickly.

  • In the blood
  • After some illness or medicine
  • Infections come easily
Which doctor
Family doctor, blood doctor (haematologist)
Common in
People on some medicines or cancer care; after some viral illnesses
Contagious
No, but you catch germs more easily
Course
Depends on the cause; some are short, others need a doctor's follow-up

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Some medicines, including some used in cancer care
  • Viral infections that briefly lower blood counts
  • Problems of the bone marrow, where blood cells are made
  • Low vitamin B12 or folate, or immune conditions; sometimes the cause is not known

See a doctor first if

  • Fever or chills: see a doctor or go to hospital the same day
  • Trouble breathing, confusion or feeling very weak: call 999
  • Sore throat, mouth sores or a cough that does not settle
  • Redness, swelling or pus in a cut or skin wound
  • Pain or burning when passing urine

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep your mouth and skin clean; look after cuts right away
  • Cook food well and wash fruit and vegetables
  • Ask your doctor what fever level means you should call or go in

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • A kidney or liver condition

Questions and answers

What is Neutropenia (low neutrophil count)?

Too few neutrophils, a type of white blood cell that fights germs. Neutrophils help the body fight germs. When there are too few, infections can start more easily and may become serious quickly.

What are the common signs of Neutropenia (low neutrophil count)?

Fever, feeling hot · Shivering with chills · Sore, scratchy throat · Painful mouth sores · Tired all the time

Why does Neutropenia (low neutrophil count) happen?

Some medicines, including some used in cancer care · Viral infections that briefly lower blood counts · Problems of the bone marrow, where blood cells are made · Low vitamin B12 or folate, or immune conditions; sometimes the cause is not known

How can I look after myself with Neutropenia (low neutrophil count)?

Wash your hands often
