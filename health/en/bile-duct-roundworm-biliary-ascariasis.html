Bile duct roundworm (biliary ascariasis)

Roundworms that enter the bile duct and cause sudden upper belly pain

ConditionInfectionsAbdomen

What it is

A roundworm that normally lives in the gut can crawl into the bile duct. This blocks bile and can cause strong, cramping pain high in the belly.

  • Upper belly, right side
  • More common in children
  • Sudden cramping pain
Which doctor
Family doctor; hospital at once for strong pain
Common in
Children and adults in places with unsafe soil, food or water
Contagious
Not directly; eggs in soil, food or dirty hands spread it
Course
Needs a doctor; the worms and the blockage must be dealt with

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Swallowing roundworm eggs from soil, dirty hands or unwashed food
  • Poor sanitation and not washing hands
  • Children playing in soil and putting hands in the mouth
  • A large number of worms in the gut, so one moves into the bile duct

See a doctor first if

  • Strong belly pain that comes in waves: go to hospital now
  • Yellow skin or eyes, dark urine or pale stools
  • Fever with chills
  • Vomiting that does not stop, or vomiting a worm
  • A belly that is hard and swollen, or a child who is very unwell: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
