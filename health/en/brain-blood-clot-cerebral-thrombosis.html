Brain blood clot (cerebral thrombosis)

A blood clot that forms in a blood vessel of the brain and blocks blood flow

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

A clot forms inside a blood vessel in the brain and can block the blood flow. Part of the brain may then get too little blood. It needs urgent care.

  • Blood vessels of the brain
  • More common with age
  • Sudden weakness, speech trouble
Which doctor
Brain and nerve doctor (neurologist), hospital emergency team
Common in
Older adults; people with high blood pressure, diabetes or high cholesterol
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on how fast care starts; recovery varies from person to person

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Blood vessels narrowed by fatty build-up over many years
  • An irregular heartbeat or thick blood in some people

See a doctor first if

  • Face drooping, arm or leg weakness, or speech trouble: call 999 now
  • Sudden loss of vision, or sudden severe headache: go to hospital now
  • Sudden confusion, dizziness or trouble walking: go to hospital now
  • Fainting or a fit: call 999
  • Signs that come and go still need a doctor the same day

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
