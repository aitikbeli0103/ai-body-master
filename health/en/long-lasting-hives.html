Long-lasting hives

Itchy, raised welts that come and go on the skin for more than six weeks

ConditionAllergiesSkin

What it is

Itchy, raised welts keep coming and going for more than six weeks, sometimes with swelling of the eyelids or lips. Each welt usually fades within a day.

  • Skin, anywhere on the body
  • Keeps returning for weeks
  • Itchy, raised welts
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor (dermatologist) or allergy doctor
Common in
Adults, more often women; children can have it too
Contagious
No
Course
Comes and goes for months or years; it often settles in time, but this varies

Common signs

More signs

  • Each welt fades within a day, but new ones keep appearing
  • Sometimes swelling of the eyelids, lips or hands
  • Itching that can disturb sleep and daily life

Why it happens

  • Often no clear cause is found
  • The body's defence system may be involved; it sometimes goes along with thyroid problems
  • Heat, pressure on the skin, stress, infections or some medicines can bring on or worsen hives
  • Food allergy is not a common cause

See a doctor first if

  • Swelling of the lips, tongue or throat, trouble breathing or wheezing: call 999
  • Feeling faint, dizzy or very weak together with the hives: call 999
  • Welts that hurt or burn, last more than a day, or leave bruises or dark marks
  • Fever, joint pain, weight loss without trying, or feeling very unwell with the hives
  • Itching that stops you sleeping, or hives that affect work, school or your mood
  • Skin that is scratched open and becomes hot, swollen or has pus

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
