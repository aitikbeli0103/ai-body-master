Excessive dandruff

Many white or yellowish flakes of skin coming off the scalp

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsHead

What it is

Many white or yellowish flakes of skin come off the scalp and show up in the hair and on the shoulders. The scalp may also itch.

  • The scalp
  • Teens and adults
  • White flakes, itchy scalp
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor (dermatologist) if it keeps coming back
Common in
Teenagers and adults; often worse when stressed or tired
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes over months or years

Common signs

More signs

  • A scalp that feels dry and tight, or oily
  • Red or greasy-looking patches on the scalp
  • Flaky skin on the eyebrows, beard or beside the nose in some people

Why it happens

Other causes

  • An oily, irritated scalp (seborrhoeic dermatitis), a very common cause
  • A yeast that normally lives on the scalp can irritate the skin in some people
  • Washing the hair too rarely, or a reaction to shampoo, hair dye or styling products
  • Other scalp skin conditions such as eczema or psoriasis

See a doctor first if

  • Swelling of the face, lips or throat, or trouble breathing after using a hair product: call 999
  • A scalp that is swollen, painful, oozing or crusty, or smells bad (possible infection)
  • Hair falling out in patches, or bald spots with scaly skin
  • Thick, red or silvery patches that spread beyond the scalp
  • Flakes or itching that do not ease after a few weeks of regular gentle washing

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wash your hair regularly with a gentle shampoo and rinse well
  • Rub the scalp softly with your fingertips, not your nails
  • Ask a pharmacist about an anti-dandruff shampoo, and follow the label
  • Avoid very hot water, and keep hair dye and styling products off the scalp when you can

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
