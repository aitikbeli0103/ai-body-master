Small liver cancer

A liver cancer found while it is still small

ConditionCancer & tumoursAbdomen

What it is

This means a cancer in the liver that is found while it is still small. It often causes no signs, so it is usually found on a check-up in people at risk.

  • The liver
  • Adults with liver disease
  • Often no signs early
Which doctor
Liver specialist (hepatologist), cancer doctor (oncologist)
Common in
People with hepatitis B or C or a scarred liver
Contagious
No, but the hepatitis viruses behind it can spread
Course
Finding it early gives more options; your team can explain

Common signs

More signs

  • A dull ache or fullness in the upper right belly

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-lasting hepatitis B or hepatitis C infection
  • A scarred, hardened liver (cirrhosis)
  • Fatty liver with overweight or diabetes

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood or black stools: go to hospital now
  • Sudden severe belly pain: go to hospital now
  • Confusion or unusual sleepiness: go to hospital now
  • Yellow skin or eyes, or a quickly swelling belly
  • Weight loss or a new ache in the upper right belly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop treatment on your own
  • Ask your team about food and emotional support

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
