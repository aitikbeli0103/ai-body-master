Severe fatty liver

A large amount of fat in the liver that can lead to liver damage

ConditionLiver, gallbladder & pancreasAbdomen

What it is

A lot of fat fills the liver and may cause swelling and scarring over time. It needs a doctor's follow-up, and healthy habits can help protect the liver.

  • Liver, upper right belly
  • Adults, weight or sugar issues
  • Tired, heavy upper belly
Which doctor
Liver or stomach specialist; family doctor
Common in
Adults who are overweight or have diabetes or high blood fats
Contagious
No
Course
Needs regular follow-up; lifestyle changes may help slow it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Very high blood fats
  • Fatty liver left for many years without change

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood or black, tarry stools: go to hospital now
  • Confusion or being very drowsy: go to hospital now
  • Yellow skin or eyes, or very dark urine
  • Swollen belly or legs, or easy bruising
  • Strong belly pain with fever, or weight loss without trying

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Severe fatty liver?

A large amount of fat in the liver that can lead to liver damage. A lot of fat fills the liver and may cause swelling and scarring over time. It needs a doctor's follow-up, and healthy habits can help protect the liver.

What are the common signs of Severe fatty liver?

Tired all the time · Pain in the upper belly · No appetite · Bloated, gassy belly · Swollen legs and ankles

Why does Severe fatty liver happen?

Extra body weight · High blood sugar or blood pressure · Drinking alcohol

How can I look after myself with Severe fatty liver?

See your doctor for check-ups · Keep a healthy weight · Ask your pharmacist
