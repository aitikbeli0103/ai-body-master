Chronic outer ear canal infection (otitis externa)

Long-lasting itchy, sore or inflamed skin inside the ear canal

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

The skin of the ear canal, between the outer ear and the eardrum, stays inflamed. The ear may itch, hurt, feel blocked or leak a little fluid.

  • Outer ear canal
  • Swimmers, ear-bud users
  • Itchy, sore ear
Which doctor
Family doctor or ear, nose and throat doctor (ENT)
Common in
Swimmers, hearing-aid users, and people with skin problems
Contagious
No
Course
Can last or come back for weeks to months; avoiding triggers helps

Common signs

More signs

  • Itching deep in the ear
  • Flaky, red or swollen skin in the ear canal
  • A small amount of watery or yellow fluid

Why it happens

  • Water staying in the ear after swimming or bathing
  • Scratching or cleaning the ear with cotton buds or fingers
  • Hearing aids or earphones that trap heat and moisture
  • Skin problems such as eczema or dandruff-type rashes
  • Diabetes or a weak body defence

See a doctor first if

  • Stiff neck with high fever and severe headache: go to hospital now
  • Face drooping on one side: call 999
  • Very bad ear pain, or pain spreading to the face or jaw, especially with diabetes
  • Swelling or redness of the outer ear or behind the ear
  • Hearing loss or fever with the ear problem

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not put cotton buds, fingers, hairpins or oils in the ear
  • Clean earphones and hearing aids, and rest the ear from them
  • Ask your doctor about earplugs for swimming

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
