Male breast cancer

A cancer that starts in the breast tissue of a man

ConditionCancer & tumoursChest

What it is

Men have a small amount of breast tissue, and cancer can start there too. It is not common, but it should be checked early.

  • Breast tissue, behind the nipple
  • Rare; mostly older men
  • A lump, usually painless
Which doctor
Family doctor, breast surgeon, cancer doctor
Common in
Rare; mostly older men
Contagious
No
Course
Often found as a lump; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

More signs

  • A lump in the breast area, often painless
  • Nipple turning inward, or changes in the skin of the breast
  • Fluid or blood from the nipple
  • A sore or redness on the nipple

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Past radiation to the chest, or liver disease

See a doctor first if

  • Any new lump in the breast area: see a doctor soon, do not wait
  • Fluid or blood from the nipple
  • A nipple that turns inward, or skin that dimples or turns red
  • Chest pain with trouble breathing: call 999
  • Bone pain that keeps getting worse, or weight loss without trying

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop treatment on your own
  • Ask your team about pain, food and emotional support
  • Know your family history and tell your doctor about it
  • Talk to someone you trust; many men feel shy about this and support helps

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A kidney or liver condition

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
