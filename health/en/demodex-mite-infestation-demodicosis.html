Demodex mite infestation (demodicosis)

Too many tiny mites in the hair follicles of the skin, causing itching and redness

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Tiny Demodex mites live on most adults' skin without causing harm. When too many build up, they can cause itching, redness and a rough or pimply rash, often on the face.

  • Face, eyelids and scalp
  • More common with age
  • Itchy, red, rough skin
Which doctor
Skin doctor (dermatologist); eye doctor if the eyelids are affected
Common in
Adults, more often older; people with oily skin, rosacea or weak immunity
Contagious
Yes, mites can pass on by close contact, but most people carry them harmlessly
Course
May come and go and last a long time; a doctor can confirm it and plan care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Most adults carry a few of these mites; trouble starts when too many build up
  • Very oily skin gives the mites more to feed on

See a doctor first if

  • Eye pain, a very red eye, or a sudden change in vision: go to hospital now
  • A hot, swollen, painful rash with pus or fever: see a doctor today
  • Swollen, sore or crusty eyelids, or gritty eyes that last: see a doctor
  • Itching or redness that does not ease after a few weeks of good skin care
  • A baby or child with a new rash around the eyes or face: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wash pillowcases, towels and face cloths often in hot water
  • If your eyelids are crusty, ask your doctor how to clean them safely
  • Do not put oils or home mixtures on your face or eyelids without asking a doctor

What is Demodex mite infestation (demodicosis)?

What are the common signs of Demodex mite infestation (demodicosis)?

Itchy skin · Red rash · Dry, flaky skin · Red, itchy eyes

Why does Demodex mite infestation (demodicosis) happen?

A weak immune system · Long-term use of some medicines · Close contact or sharing items

How can I look after myself with Demodex mite infestation (demodicosis)?

Wash gently and pat dry · Do not share towels, razors or cups
