Lung aspergillosis (fungal infection)

A lung infection caused by Aspergillus mould, mostly in people with weak immunity or old lung damage

ConditionLungs & breathingChest

What it is

Aspergillus mould is common in the air. Most people are fine, but in some people it grows in the lungs and causes cough and breathlessness.

  • Lungs, in the chest
  • Weak immunity or lung disease
  • Long cough, breathless
Which doctor
Lung doctor (respiratory physician) or infection specialist
Common in
People with weak immunity, asthma, old TB scars or other long-term lung disease
Contagious
No, it does not spread from person to person
Course
Needs a specialist's care, often over months; the course depends on the type

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Breathing in Aspergillus mould spores from soil, dust or damp places
  • Old lung damage such as from TB or long-term lung disease
  • Asthma or allergy to the mould in some people

See a doctor first if

  • Coughing up blood, even a little: see a doctor now; a lot of blood: call 999
  • Hard to breathe or chest pain: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Fever that does not go away
  • A cough that keeps going for weeks, or weight loss without trying

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Take the medicine your doctor gives for as long as told, and never stop it yourself
  • Avoid dusty or damp places, compost, and mouldy rooms
