Oral thrush (oral candidiasis)

A yeast infection that leaves white patches in the mouth and on the tongue

ConditionMouth & teethHead

What it is

Oral thrush is an overgrowth of a yeast that normally lives in the mouth. It makes creamy white patches and a sore mouth. It is common in babies and people who use dentures.

  • Tongue, cheeks, roof of mouth
  • Babies, older adults, dentures
  • White patches, sore mouth
Which doctor
Family doctor, dentist; paediatrician for babies
Common in
Babies, older adults, people with dentures, diabetes or a weak immune system
Contagious
Not usually, but it can pass between a baby and a breastfeeding mother
Course
Usually clears with proper care, but can come back if the cause stays

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A yeast called Candida that normally lives in the mouth grows too much
  • Dentures that are not cleaned or do not fit well
  • Diabetes, a dry mouth, smoking, or a weak immune system

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble swallowing, pain when swallowing, or pain in the chest when eating: see a doctor promptly
  • A baby who will not feed, or a baby with fever or very unwell: see a doctor promptly
  • Patches that come back again and again for no clear reason
  • Patches that do not wipe off, or a white or red area that lasts more than two weeks
  • Fever, weight loss or feeling very tired along with the patches

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Brush your teeth and tongue gently twice a day
  • Take dentures out at night and clean them well every day
  • Rinse your mouth with water after using an inhaler with steroids
  • Keep blood sugar well controlled if you have diabetes
