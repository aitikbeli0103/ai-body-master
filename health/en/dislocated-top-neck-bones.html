Dislocated top neck bones

The top two neck bones (atlas and axis) have slipped out of place

ConditionBack & spineWhole body

What it is

The two top neck bones sit just below the skull and allow the head to turn. If they slip, the spinal cord nearby can be squeezed. This is a serious problem that needs a doctor.

  • Top of the neck
  • After a hard injury or illness
  • Neck pain, head hard to turn
Which doctor
Hospital emergency, spine or orthopaedic specialist
Common in
People after a car accident, a fall or a neck injury; some joint conditions
Contagious
No
Course
Needs a doctor's care; how long it takes depends on the cause and the care given

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Joint conditions that loosen the neck ligaments, such as long-term arthritis
  • A throat or neck infection, mostly in children

See a doctor first if

  • Neck injury with numb arms or legs, weakness or trouble breathing: call 999 and keep the neck still
  • Fainting, sudden severe headache, or trouble speaking or swallowing: call 999
  • Severe neck pain after an accident or fall: go to hospital now
  • A head that tilts to one side and will not move
  • Do not twist, pull or force the neck

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Seek a doctor first; this is not a problem to look after on your own
  • Keep the neck still and supported until you are checked
  • Follow your doctor's advice on a neck collar and activity
  • Wear a seat belt in cars and avoid risky falls or sudden neck movements

Tell your therapist

  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Dizzy when turning the head
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Dislocated top neck bones?

The top two neck bones (atlas and axis) have slipped out of place. The two top neck bones sit just below the skull and allow the head to turn. If they slip, the spinal cord nearby can be squeezed. This is a serious problem that needs a doctor.

What are the common signs of Dislocated top neck bones?

Stiff, aching neck · Hard to turn the head · Headache at the back · Tingling down the arm

Why does Dislocated top neck bones happen?

A fall, knock or road accident · Present from birth

How can I look after myself with Dislocated top neck bones?

Seek a doctor first; this is not a problem to look after on your own · Keep the neck still and supported until you are checked · Follow your doctor's advice on a neck collar and activity · Wear a seat belt in cars and avoid risky falls or sudden neck movements
