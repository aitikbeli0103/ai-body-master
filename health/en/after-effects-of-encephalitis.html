After-effects of encephalitis

Lasting problems that can remain after brain swelling from an infection

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

After the brain has been inflamed (encephalitis), some people are left with problems such as tiredness, poor memory, mood change or weakness. They can improve slowly.

  • Brain and nerves
  • After a serious illness
  • Memory and mood change
Which doctor
Brain doctor (neurologist), rehabilitation team
Common in
People who had encephalitis; children and adults
Contagious
No
Course
Differs for each person; many improve slowly over months with support

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The brain was inflamed and some nerve cells were damaged
  • A longer or more serious illness can leave more lasting effects
  • Fits during the illness can add to the problems

See a doctor first if

  • A fit (seizure), or fainting: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Sudden severe headache, high fever or stiff neck
  • Sudden new confusion, drowsiness or weakness on one side
  • Thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999
  • Changes that get worse or stop daily life from working

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is After-effects of encephalitis?

Lasting problems that can remain after brain swelling from an infection. After the brain has been inflamed (encephalitis), some people are left with problems such as tiredness, poor memory, mood change or weakness. They can improve slowly.

What are the common signs of After-effects of encephalitis?

Forgetting things · Foggy, cannot focus · Tired all the time · Short-tempered, on edge · Low mood, no interest · Unsteady when walking

Why does After-effects of encephalitis happen?

Germs (infection)

How can I look after myself with After-effects of encephalitis?

See your doctor for check-ups · Ask your pharmacist · Get good sleep · Talk to family and friends
