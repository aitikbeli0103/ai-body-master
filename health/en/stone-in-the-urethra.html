Stone in the urethra

A hard stone stuck in the tube that carries urine out of the body

ConditionKidneys & urinaryReproductive

What it is

A small stone, usually washed down from the kidney or bladder, gets stuck in the urethra and can block or hurt the flow of urine.

  • Urethra, the urine tube
  • Seen more in men
  • Pain, hard to pass urine
Which doctor
Urologist; go to hospital if you cannot pass urine
Common in
Adults, more often men; people who get kidney stones
Contagious
No
Course
A small stone may pass, but a doctor should check it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Stones that form in the kidney and travel down
  • Stones that form in the bladder
  • A narrow urine tube that catches the stone

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine at all, or a very painful swollen lower belly: go to hospital now
  • Fever with chills, or feeling very unwell
  • Severe pain that does not ease
  • Blood or pus in the urine
  • Do not try to pull the stone out yourself

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Stone in the urethra?

A hard stone stuck in the tube that carries urine out of the body. A small stone, usually washed down from the kidney or bladder, gets stuck in the urethra and can block or hurt the flow of urine.

What are the common signs of Stone in the urethra?

Burning when passing urine · Weak flow or hard to start passing urine · Needing the toilet often · Pink or red urine

Why does Stone in the urethra happen?

Not drinking enough water

How can I look after myself with Stone in the urethra?

See your doctor for check-ups · Drink enough water · Ask your pharmacist
