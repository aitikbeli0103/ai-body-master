Breast lump or nodule

A small lump or round spot in the breast, felt by hand or found on a scan

ConditionWomen's healthChest

What it is

A breast nodule is a small lump or round area in the breast tissue. Many are harmless, but every new lump should be checked by a doctor to find out what it is.

  • In the breast
  • Mostly adult women
  • A lump felt or seen on a scan
Which doctor
Family doctor, breast clinic or breast surgeon
Common in
Adult women of any age; less often in men
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; some are only watched with check-ups, others need more tests

Common signs

More signs

  • A lump or thick spot you can feel in the breast
  • A round or oval spot seen on an ultrasound or mammogram
  • The lump may feel soft, firm, smooth or uneven
  • Often no pain, though some lumps are tender, especially before a period

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Normal changes in breast tissue with the monthly hormone cycle
  • Fluid-filled cysts, or harmless solid lumps such as fibroadenomas
  • Less often, a lump can be breast cancer, which is why every new lump needs checking

See a doctor first if

  • A hot, red, painful breast with fever and chills: see a doctor today, or go to hospital now if very unwell
  • Any new lump, or a lump that feels hard, fixed in place or is growing
  • Blood or clear fluid coming from the nipple on its own
  • Dimpling, puckering, redness or an orange-peel look on the breast skin
  • A nipple that newly turns inward, or a nipple rash that will not clear
  • A lump or swelling in the armpit or near the collarbone

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Learn how your breasts normally look and feel, and report any change
  • Ask your doctor how often you should have a breast scan or mammogram

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy
