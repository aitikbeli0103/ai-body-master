Radial nerve injury (wrist drop)

Damage to the radial nerve of the arm that can leave the wrist and fingers weak

ConditionShoulder, arm & handArms & legs

What it is

The radial nerve runs down the back of the arm to the hand. When it is squeezed, stretched or cut, the wrist may droop and the fingers may be hard to straighten.

  • Nerve in the arm
  • After injury or pressure
  • Weak wrist, may tingle
Which doctor
Family doctor, orthopaedic or nerve (neurology) doctor
Common in
People with an arm bone fracture, a deep cut or long pressure on the arm
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; some nerves recover over weeks to months, some need more care

Common signs

More signs

  • The wrist drops and cannot be lifted
  • Hard to straighten the fingers or thumb

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A broken upper arm bone that bruises or traps the nerve
  • Long pressure on the arm, such as sleeping with the arm hanging over a chair
  • A deep cut, blow or a crutch pressing under the armpit

See a doctor first if

  • A sudden weak or drooping arm with face drooping or speech trouble: call 999
  • Weak wrist after an injury or fall: go to hospital to check for a broken bone
  • A cold, pale or bluish hand, or heavy bleeding: go to hospital now
  • Weakness or numbness that is getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • See a doctor early so the cause can be found
  • Avoid resting your arm on hard edges or leaning on it for long
  • Follow your doctor's advice on a splint and gentle hand movements
  • Be careful with hot things and sharp tools if the hand feels numb

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Weak bones
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
