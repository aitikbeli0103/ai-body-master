Kidney cancer that has spread (metastatic)

Kidney cancer that has spread from the kidney to other parts of the body

ConditionCancer & tumoursAbdomen

What it is

Metastatic kidney cancer means cancer that began in a kidney has spread to other places, such as the lungs, bones or liver. Care is led by a cancer team.

  • Kidney, and where it spread
  • More common in adults
  • Side pain, tiredness
Which doctor
Cancer doctor (oncologist), kidney surgeon (urologist)
Common in
Adults, more often older men; smokers
Contagious
No
Course
Varies from person to person; your cancer team can explain what to expect

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-term kidney failure needing dialysis, or some inherited conditions

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, chest pain or coughing up blood: call 999
  • Heavy blood in the urine or clots that block passing urine: go to hospital now
  • Sudden weakness in the legs, numbness or trouble passing urine or stools: go to hospital now
  • New severe bone pain, or a bone that breaks easily
  • Confusion, bad headache or a fit
  • High fever, or unable to eat or drink

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop treatment or medicine on your own; ask your team first
  • Ask your team about pain, food and tiredness
  • Drink fluids as your doctor advises, as your kidneys may need care

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Weak bones
  • High blood pressure
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Kidney cancer that has spread (metastatic)?

Kidney cancer that has spread from the kidney to other parts of the body. Metastatic kidney cancer means cancer that began in a kidney has spread to other places, such as the lungs, bones or liver. Care is led by a cancer team.

What are the common signs of Kidney cancer that has spread (metastatic)?

Pink or red urine · Pain in the side or loin · Losing weight without trying · Aching bones · Fever, feeling hot

Why does Kidney cancer that has spread (metastatic) happen?

Smoking · Extra body weight · High blood sugar or blood pressure · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Kidney cancer that has spread (metastatic)?

See your doctor for check-ups · Talk to family and friends
