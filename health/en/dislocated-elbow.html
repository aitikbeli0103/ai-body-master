Dislocated elbow

The elbow joint is pushed out of its normal position

ConditionShoulder, arm & handBones & muscles

What it is

The bones of the elbow slip out of place, usually after a fall. The elbow looks out of shape, is very painful and cannot be moved.

  • The elbow joint
  • Usually after a fall
  • Severe pain, odd shape
Which doctor
Emergency department; bone doctor (orthopaedic surgeon)
Common in
Children and adults after a fall onto the hand or elbow
Contagious
No
Course
Needs a doctor to put it back; recovery takes weeks and stiffness may linger

Common signs

More signs

  • The elbow looks bent, swollen or out of shape
  • Cannot bend or straighten the arm

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A sudden strong pull on a child's arm
  • Loose joints or earlier elbow injuries

See a doctor first if

  • Hand that is cold, pale, blue or numb: go to hospital now
  • Heavy bleeding or bone through the skin: call 999
  • A bent or deformed elbow: go to hospital, do not force it back
  • A child who is crying, will not use the arm or holds it still
  • Cannot feel or move the fingers

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep the arm still in the position it is in, supported with a cloth or sling
  • Do not try to push the joint back yourself
  • Go to hospital as soon as possible
  • Follow your doctor's advice on when to start moving it

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
