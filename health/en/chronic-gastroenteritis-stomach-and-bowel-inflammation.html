Chronic gastroenteritis (stomach and bowel inflammation)

Long-lasting upset and inflammation of the stomach and bowels, with belly pain, bloating or loose stools

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

The stomach and bowels stay irritated or inflamed for a long time. Belly pain, bloating, nausea or loose stools may come and go, and some days are better than others.

  • Stomach and bowels
  • Comes and goes over time
  • Belly pain and bloating
Which doctor
Family doctor; stomach and gut doctor (gastroenterologist)
Common in
Anyone; more likely after repeated gut infections or with food intolerance
Contagious
Some types can be, if an infection is the cause (via food, water or dirty hands)
Course
Often comes and goes over a long time; a doctor can look for the cause and guide care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Germs or parasites in the gut that last or keep coming back
  • The H. pylori germ in the stomach
  • Food that does not agree with you, or often eating very spicy or oily food

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood, or black or bloody stools: go to hospital now
  • Very weak, dizzy or confused, with a very dry mouth or very little urine: go to hospital now
  • Severe belly pain, a hard swollen belly, or a high fever
  • Vomiting or diarrhoea that will not stop, or you cannot keep fluids down
  • Losing weight without trying, or symptoms lasting more than a few weeks
  • A baby, young child or older person with vomiting or diarrhoea: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Eat small, regular, light meals, and chew slowly
  • Drink enough fluids, especially when stools are loose or you are vomiting
  • Note which foods upset you and eat less of them

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Chronic gastroenteritis (stomach and bowel inflammation)?

Long-lasting upset and inflammation of the stomach and bowels, with belly pain, bloating or loose stools. The stomach and bowels stay irritated or inflamed for a long time. Belly pain, bloating, nausea or loose stools may come and go, and some days are better than others.

What are the common signs of Chronic gastroenteritis (stomach and bowel inflammation)?

Cramping belly pain · Bloated, gassy belly · Feeling sick, nausea · Loose, frequent stools · No appetite

Why does Chronic gastroenteritis (stomach and bowel inflammation) happen?

Smoking · Stress

How can I look after myself with Chronic gastroenteritis (stomach and bowel inflammation)?

Wash your hands often · Ask your pharmacist
