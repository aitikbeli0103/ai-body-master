Lung cancer

A cancer that starts in the cells of the lungs and may cause cough and breathlessness

ConditionCancer & tumoursChest

What it is

Lung cancer starts when cells in the lungs grow out of control and form a growth. It may cause no signs at first, and smoking is the main risk.

  • Lungs, in the chest
  • Smokers, but not only them
  • Lasting cough, short breath
Which doctor
Lung doctor (respiratory physician), cancer doctor (oncologist), chest surgeon
Common in
Adults; most often smokers and ex-smokers, but also people who never smoked
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the type and how early it is found; your cancer team can explain

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Some people who never smoked get it, and often the cause is not known

See a doctor first if

  • Coughing up a lot of blood, sudden bad breathlessness or severe chest pain: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness, trouble speaking or a fit: call 999
  • Fever or chills during cancer treatment: call your cancer team or go to hospital now
  • A cough that lasts for weeks or changes, or any blood in the phlegm: see a doctor soon
  • New or worsening breathlessness, wheezing or chest pain, or a hoarse voice that lasts
  • Losing weight without trying, or new bone pain

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Lung cancer?

A cancer that starts in the cells of the lungs and may cause cough and breathlessness. Lung cancer starts when cells in the lungs grow out of control and form a growth. It may cause no signs at first, and smoking is the main risk.

What are the common signs of Lung cancer?

Cough with phlegm · Short of breath on stairs · Sharp pain when breathing in · Wheezing breath · Losing weight without trying · Tired all the time

Why does Lung cancer happen?

Smoking · Smoke, haze and strong smells · Dust or chemicals at work · Runs in the family

How can I look after myself with Lung cancer?

Follow your care team's plan · Stop smoking · Rest well · Talk to family and friends

Can an AI Body Master session help with Lung cancer?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Lung cancer and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
