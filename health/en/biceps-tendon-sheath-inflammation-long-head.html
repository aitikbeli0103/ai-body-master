Biceps tendon sheath inflammation (long head)

Swelling of the sheath around the long biceps tendon at the front of the shoulder

ConditionBones, joints & spineHead

What it is

The long head of the biceps tendon runs through a groove at the front of the shoulder. When its sheath is inflamed, lifting or reaching can hurt.

  • Front of the shoulder
  • Overhead workers, sports people
  • Aching shoulder on lifting
Which doctor
Family doctor; bone or sports doctor, physiotherapist
Common in
Adults who lift or reach overhead a lot; older adults
Contagious
No
Course
Often settles over weeks with rest and care; can come back

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Other shoulder problems, such as a sore rotator cuff
  • Wear of the tendon with age

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, pain spreading to the jaw or arm, or sweating and breathlessness: call 999
  • A sudden pop, a new bulge in the upper arm, or sudden weakness
  • A hot, red, swollen shoulder with fever
  • Cannot lift the arm after a fall or injury
  • Numbness or tingling down the arm, or pain that wakes you at night

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep the shoulder moving gently so it does not stiffen
  • Fix your posture and sleep without lying on the sore side
  • Return to activity slowly; ask a physiotherapist for safe exercises

Tell your therapist

  • Recent shoulder injury or surgery
  • Hot, red or swollen shoulder
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
