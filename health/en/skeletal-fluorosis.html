Skeletal fluorosis

Bone and joint stiffness and pain caused by taking in too much fluoride over many years

ConditionBones, joints & spineBones & muscles

What it is

Fluoride in small amounts protects teeth. Taking in too much for many years can build up in the bones, making them dense but brittle, and the joints stiff and painful.

  • Bones, spine and joints
  • After years of high fluoride
  • Stiff, aching bones and joints
Which doctor
Family doctor, bone and joint doctor (orthopaedic or rheumatology)
Common in
People who long drink or eat things with very high fluoride
Contagious
No
Course
Builds up slowly over years; lowering the fluoride intake is important

Common signs

More signs

  • Less ability to bend or move the spine
  • Tingling or numbness in the arms or legs
  • Marked or stained teeth (dental fluorosis) in some people

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Eating food grown or cooked with high-fluoride water
  • Work with fluoride in factories or mines
  • Weak kidneys, because they clear fluoride more slowly

See a doctor first if

  • Weakness or numbness in the legs, trouble walking or loss of bladder or bowel control: go to hospital now
  • A fall or knock followed by severe pain, or a limb that looks bent
  • Bone pain that is getting worse or wakes you at night
  • Stomach pain, vomiting or weakness after drinking suspect water
  • Children with bent legs or marked teeth

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Find out whether your drinking water is high in fluoride and ask your local health office
  • Use a safe water source if advised
  • Stay gently active to keep joints moving
  • Follow your doctor's advice and do not take supplements unless advised

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
