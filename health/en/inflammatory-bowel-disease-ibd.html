Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD)

Long-lasting swelling of the gut lining that comes and goes, such as Crohn's disease or ulcerative colitis

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

In IBD the lining of the gut becomes swollen and sore for a long time, with flare-ups and quieter periods. It needs a doctor's long-term care.

  • Bowel
  • Often starts young
  • Belly pain, loose stools
Which doctor
Gut doctor (gastroenterologist), family doctor
Common in
Often begins in young adults, but can start at any age
Contagious
No
Course
Long-term, with flare-ups and quiet times; a doctor guides the care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body's defence system may react too strongly in the gut

See a doctor first if

  • Severe belly pain with a swollen belly or repeated vomiting: go to hospital now
  • Heavy bleeding from the bottom, dizziness or fainting: call 999
  • High fever with bad belly pain
  • Signs of dehydration: very dry mouth, little urine, great weakness
  • Blood in stools, or losing weight without trying

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD)?

Long-lasting swelling of the gut lining that comes and goes, such as Crohn's disease or ulcerative colitis. In IBD the lining of the gut becomes swollen and sore for a long time, with flare-ups and quieter periods. It needs a doctor's long-term care.

What are the common signs of Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD)?

Cramping belly pain · Loose, frequent stools · Losing weight without trying · Tired all the time

Why does Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) happen?

The cause is not fully known · Runs in the family · Smoking

How can I look after myself with Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD)?

See your doctor for check-ups · Note down your symptoms · Talk to family and friends

Can an AI Body Master session help with Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD)?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
