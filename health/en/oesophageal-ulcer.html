Oesophageal ulcer

A sore on the inner lining of the food pipe

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

A raw sore forms on the lining of the food pipe (oesophagus). It can make swallowing painful and cause burning pain behind the breastbone.

  • Food pipe, behind the chest
  • Adults, mostly
  • Burning, painful swallowing
Which doctor
Family doctor, then a stomach and bowel doctor (gastroenterologist)
Common in
Adults with acid reflux; also people who drink alcohol or smoke
Contagious
No
Course
Often settles with the right care, but needs a doctor to find the cause

Common signs

More signs

  • Heartburn or sour liquid coming up

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Alcohol and smoking
  • Some infections, or a weak immune system

See a doctor first if

  • Severe chest pain, sweating or trouble breathing: call 999, it may not be the food pipe
  • Vomiting blood, or black or tarry stools: go to hospital now
  • You cannot swallow food or liquid
  • Pain that lasts more than a few weeks, or weight loss

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Take pills sitting or standing up with a full glass of water
  • Cut down on alcohol, very spicy or sour foods, and stop smoking
  • Do not stop or change your medicine without asking your doctor

Tell your therapist

  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A heart condition
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Oesophageal ulcer?

A sore on the inner lining of the food pipe. A raw sore forms on the lining of the food pipe (oesophagus). It can make swallowing painful and cause burning pain behind the breastbone.

What are the common signs of Oesophageal ulcer?

Burning behind the breastbone · Hard or painful to swallow · Feeling sick, nausea

Why does Oesophageal ulcer happen?

Acid coming up from the stomach · Not drinking enough water

How can I look after myself with Oesophageal ulcer?

Eat small, regular meals
