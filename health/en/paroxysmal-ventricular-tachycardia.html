Paroxysmal ventricular tachycardia

Sudden spells of a very fast heartbeat that start in the lower chambers of the heart

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

In paroxysmal ventricular tachycardia, the lower chambers of the heart suddenly beat very fast for a time. It can be serious and needs a doctor's care.

  • Lower chambers of the heart
  • Often with heart disease
  • Sudden, very fast heartbeat
Which doctor
Heart doctor (cardiologist)
Common in
People with heart disease or a past heart attack
Contagious
No
Course
Needs a heart specialist's follow-up; the plan depends on the cause

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Damage to the heart muscle, such as after a heart attack
  • Other heart disease or a weak heart
  • Imbalance of body salts such as potassium
  • Sometimes no clear cause is found

See a doctor first if

  • Fainting, collapse or a heartbeat that will not slow down: call 999
  • Chest pain or severe trouble breathing with a racing heart: call 999
  • A person who is not responding or not breathing normally: call 999
  • Dizziness, cold sweat or feeling about to faint
  • Any spell of racing heart, even if it settles: see a doctor soon

Questions and answers

What is Paroxysmal ventricular tachycardia?

Sudden spells of a very fast heartbeat that start in the lower chambers of the heart. In paroxysmal ventricular tachycardia, the lower chambers of the heart suddenly beat very fast for a time. It can be serious and needs a doctor's care.

What are the common signs of Paroxysmal ventricular tachycardia?

Racing, pounding heart · Feeling faint · Short of breath on stairs · Tight, heavy chest

Why does Paroxysmal ventricular tachycardia happen?

A side effect of some medicines

How can I look after myself with Paroxysmal ventricular tachycardia?

See your doctor for check-ups · Take medicine as your doctor says · Stop smoking
