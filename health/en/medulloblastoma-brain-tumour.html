Medulloblastoma (brain tumour)

A fast-growing brain tumour at the back of the brain, mostly in children

ConditionCancer & tumoursWhole body

What it is

Medulloblastoma is a cancer that starts in the lower back part of the brain, which helps with balance. It is seen mostly in children.

  • Back of the brain
  • Mostly in children
  • Headache, unsteady walk
Which doctor
Child cancer doctor, brain surgeon (neurosurgeon)
Common in
Mostly children; rare in adults
Contagious
No
Course
Needs a specialist team; they can explain what to expect

Common signs

More signs

  • Headaches, often worse in the morning
  • A child who is unusually tired or irritable

Why it happens

Other causes

  • It starts from cells that did not grow in the usual way in early life
  • It is not caused by anything a parent did or did not do

See a doctor first if

  • A fit (seizure), or a child who is hard to wake: call 999
  • Headache with repeated vomiting: go to hospital now
  • Sudden trouble walking, speaking or seeing
  • A child who is very unwell: see a doctor promptly
  • Headaches or balance problems that keep getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop treatment on your own
  • Ask the team about pain, food, school and emotional support for the child and family

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Epilepsy or fits
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Weak leg or foot

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
