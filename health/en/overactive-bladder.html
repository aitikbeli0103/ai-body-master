Overactive bladder

A bladder that suddenly feels the need to pass urine, often with little warning

ConditionKidneys & urinaryPelvis

What it is

The bladder muscle squeezes at the wrong time, so you feel a sudden strong urge to pass urine, often many times a day and night.

  • Bladder
  • More common with age
  • Sudden urge to pass urine
Which doctor
Family doctor, urologist; gynaecologist for women
Common in
Adults, more often as we get older; men and women
Contagious
No
Course
Often long term, but daily habits and a doctor's advice can help a lot

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Diabetes, nerve problems or weak pelvic muscles

See a doctor first if

  • Blood in the urine
  • Burning when passing urine, or fever and back pain: see a doctor promptly
  • Cannot pass urine, or the lower belly is swollen and painful: go to hospital now
  • New leg weakness or numbness with bladder trouble
  • Very thirsty with weight loss

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Overactive bladder?

A bladder that suddenly feels the need to pass urine, often with little warning. The bladder muscle squeezes at the wrong time, so you feel a sudden strong urge to pass urine, often many times a day and night.

What are the common signs of Overactive bladder?

Needing the toilet often · Waking at night to pass urine · Leaking when coughing

Why does Overactive bladder happen?

The cause is not fully known · Drinking alcohol · An enlarged prostate

How can I look after myself with Overactive bladder?

Cut down on alcohol · Keep a healthy weight

Can an AI Body Master session help with Overactive bladder?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Overactive bladder and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
