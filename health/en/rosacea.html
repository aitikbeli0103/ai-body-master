Rosacea

A long-term skin condition that causes facial redness, small visible blood vessels and sometimes pimple-like bumps

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Rosacea mostly affects the cheeks, nose, chin and forehead, causing redness that comes and goes, and sometimes bumps, stinging skin or sore eyes.

  • Cheeks, nose, chin, forehead
  • Adults, often in middle age
  • Red, warm, stinging skin
Which doctor
Skin doctor (dermatologist); eye doctor if the eyes are sore
Common in
Adults, usually from early middle age; can run in families
Contagious
No
Course
Long-term; flares come and go, and a doctor can help you manage it

Common signs

Types of red veins on the face

Why red veins show

Red veins can be inherited, or come from wrong skincare, irregular routines, diet, medicine or illness.

3 stages of sensitive skin

Sensitive skin or allergy?

  • Sensitive skin over-reacts to weather, cosmetics or cleansers
  • An allergy is the body reacting to one substance (food, medicine, pollen): find it and see a doctor

Barrier care tips

  • Wash with water or a gentle cleanser; pat dry, don't rub
  • Avoid soap, acids, alcohol and fragrance on the face
  • No scrubs or face massage while the skin is sensitive
  • Repair takes about 1 to 6 months; be patient
  • Use sunscreen and moisturiser daily; avoid saunas and very hot water

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Stress, strong emotions, hard exercise, and some face products or medicines can also be triggers

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden loss of vision or severe eye pain: go to hospital now
  • A red, painful eye, blurred vision, or eyes that hurt in bright light: see an eye doctor promptly
  • Skin that is very painful, hot and swollen, or has pus, or a fever: see a doctor promptly
  • Skin on the nose or cheeks getting thick, lumpy or bumpy: see a doctor
  • Facial redness with joint pain, fever or feeling very tired: see a doctor to look for other causes
  • Flare-ups that will not settle, or that hurt your confidence, sleep or daily life: see a doctor

Questions and answers

What is Rosacea?

A long-term skin condition that causes facial redness, small visible blood vessels and sometimes pimple-like bumps. Rosacea mostly affects the cheeks, nose, chin and forehead, causing redness that comes and goes, and sometimes bumps, stinging skin or sore eyes.

What are the common signs of Rosacea?

Red rash · Pimples and breakouts · Red, itchy eyes · Dry, gritty eyes

Why does Rosacea happen?

The cause is not fully known · Runs in the family · Too much sun · Drinking alcohol

How can I look after myself with Rosacea?

Protect yourself from the sun · Cut down on alcohol · Wash gently and pat dry · Ask your pharmacist

Can an AI Body Master session help with Rosacea?
