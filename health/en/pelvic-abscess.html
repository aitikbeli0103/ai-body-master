Pelvic abscess

A pocket of pus in the lower belly, usually after an infection

ConditionWomen's healthPelvis

What it is

A pelvic abscess is a collection of pus among the organs of the lower belly. It is serious and needs a doctor promptly.

  • Lower belly
  • After infection or surgery
  • Fever and lower belly pain
Which doctor
Go to hospital: gynaecologist or surgeon
Common in
Women after a pelvic infection, surgery or childbirth
Contagious
No, but some infections that lead to it can pass on through sex
Course
Needs hospital care; recovery depends on how early it is treated

Common signs

More signs

  • Lower belly pain that is steady or getting worse

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Pelvic infection that was not treated fully
  • After surgery, a miscarriage or childbirth
  • Infection of the bowel, such as appendicitis

See a doctor first if

  • High fever, shaking chills, fast heartbeat or feeling faint: go to hospital now
  • Sudden severe belly pain or a hard, swollen belly: call 999
  • Vomiting that does not stop, or you cannot drink
  • Foul discharge with lower belly pain
  • Pain after surgery, childbirth or a miscarriage that keeps getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not wait at home: get medical care promptly
  • Finish any medicine your doctor gives you exactly as told
  • Rest and drink enough fluids once your doctor agrees

A pocket of pus in the lower belly, usually after an infection. A pelvic abscess is a collection of pus among the organs of the lower belly. It is serious and needs a doctor promptly.

What are the common signs of Pelvic abscess?

Fever, feeling hot · Unusual discharge or itching down below · Pain during or after sex · Feeling sick, nausea

Why does Pelvic abscess happen?

Germs (infection)
