Frontal sinusitis (forehead sinuses)

Swelling of the sinuses behind the forehead, causing headache above the eyes and a blocked nose

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

The frontal sinuses are air spaces in the forehead above the eyes. When they swell, there can be a dull ache or pressure across the forehead, often worse when bending forward.

  • Forehead, above the eyes
  • Often follows a cold
  • Forehead pressure, headache
Which doctor
Family doctor, ear, nose and throat doctor (ENT)
Common in
Adults and older children, often after a cold or with allergies
Contagious
Not itself, but the cold germs behind it can spread
Course
Often eases within days to a couple of weeks; see a doctor if not

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A cold or flu that makes the lining of the nose swell and blocks drainage
  • A bend in the nose wall or growths in the nose in some people

See a doctor first if

  • Swelling or a soft puffy lump on the forehead: go to hospital now
  • Swelling or redness around the eye, double vision or vision change
  • Severe headache with a stiff neck, confusion or high fever: call 999
  • A child with a swollen forehead or fever: see a doctor promptly
  • Symptoms that last more than a couple of weeks or keep coming back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Breathe warm steam, such as in a warm shower
  • Place a warm, damp cloth over the forehead and nose
  • Stay away from smoke and known allergy triggers
