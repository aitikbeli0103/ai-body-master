Acute pharyngolaryngitis (throat and voice box)

A sore, swollen throat and voice box, often with a cold

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

Both the throat and the voice box are swollen at the same time, so swallowing hurts and the voice turns hoarse. It is usually caused by a virus.

  • Throat and voice box
  • Any age
  • Sore throat, hoarse voice
Which doctor
Family doctor; ear, nose and throat doctor if it lasts
Common in
Anyone, especially with colds, smoking or dusty air
Contagious
Yes, if caused by a virus; it spreads by droplets
Course
Often settles within about a week

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Dry or very cold air
  • Shouting or talking too much

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, noisy breathing or blue lips: call 999
  • Drooling, or unable to swallow your saliva: go to hospital now
  • A child who is very unwell or has noisy breathing: see a doctor promptly
  • A high fever that lasts more than a few days
  • Hoarseness lasting more than about three weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Acute pharyngolaryngitis (throat and voice box)?

A sore, swollen throat and voice box, often with a cold. Both the throat and the voice box are swollen at the same time, so swallowing hurts and the voice turns hoarse. It is usually caused by a virus.

What are the common signs of Acute pharyngolaryngitis (throat and voice box)?

Sore, scratchy throat · Hoarse, weak voice · Dry, tickly cough · Fever, feeling hot · Swollen glands in the neck

Why does Acute pharyngolaryngitis (throat and voice box) happen?

Germs (infection) · Smoking

How can I look after myself with Acute pharyngolaryngitis (throat and voice box)?

Drink enough water · Stop smoking · Wash your hands often · Rest well · Ask your pharmacist
