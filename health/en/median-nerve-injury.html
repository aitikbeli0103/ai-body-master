Median nerve injury

Damage to the nerve that gives feeling and movement to the thumb side of the hand

ConditionShoulder, arm & handArms & legs

What it is

The median nerve runs from the neck down the arm to the hand. When it is cut, squeezed or stretched, the thumb and first fingers may lose feeling or strength.

  • Arm to thumb side of hand
  • After a cut, break or squeeze
  • Numb, tingling, weak grip
Which doctor
Family doctor first; nerve or hand specialist (neurologist, hand surgeon)
Common in
People with wrist or arm cuts and breaks; heavy hand users
Contagious
No
Course
Nerves heal slowly, over weeks to months or longer; some changes may stay

Common signs

Why it happens

  • A cut at the wrist, forearm or elbow
  • A broken or dislocated bone near the wrist or elbow
  • Long pressure on the nerve, for example at the wrist
  • Swelling, a tight cast or a hard knock

See a doctor first if

  • A deep cut with heavy bleeding: call 999
  • Sudden numbness or weakness with a cold, pale or blue hand: go to hospital now
  • Sudden weakness of the face, arm or leg or trouble speaking: call 999
  • Cannot make a fist, pinch or feel the fingers
  • Numbness, burning or weakness that keeps getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Get any wrist or arm injury looked at early, before the nerve is damaged more
  • Avoid long pressure on the wrist, and rest the hand
  • Take care with hot or sharp objects if you cannot feel your fingers
  • Follow the advice of your doctor or therapist on gentle hand exercises

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
