Arachnoid cyst

A fluid-filled sac that forms in the thin layers covering the brain

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

An arachnoid cyst is a sac of clear fluid between the brain and its thin covering. Many are small, cause no problem and are found by chance on a scan.

  • Around the brain
  • Often found by chance
  • Often no signs at all
Which doctor
Brain doctor (neurologist) or brain surgeon
Common in
Any age; often found in children and young adults
Contagious
No
Course
Many stay the same for years; your doctor may suggest check-up scans

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • It is not caused by anything you did

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden severe headache, fits, or fainting: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • A head knock followed by headache, vomiting or drowsiness
  • A headache that keeps getting worse, or vision or balance change
  • A baby or child whose head looks bigger or who is very unwell: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask your doctor whether any sport or activity needs care
  • Protect your head: wear a helmet when riding and a seat belt in the car

Tell your therapist

  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Dizzy when turning the head
  • Epilepsy or fits
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine
