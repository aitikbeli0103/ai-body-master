Tongue cancer

A cancer that starts in the cells of the tongue

ConditionCancer & tumoursHead

What it is

Abnormal cells in the tongue grow out of control, often first showing as a sore or lump on the tongue that does not go away.

  • Tongue and mouth
  • Linked to smoking and alcohol
  • A sore or lump on the tongue
Which doctor
Family doctor or dentist first; then an ear, nose and throat or cancer specialist
Common in
Adults, more often older people; people who smoke, chew tobacco or drink a lot
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on where it is and its stage; your doctors can explain what to expect

Common signs

More signs

  • A lump, a thick area, or a red or white patch on the tongue
  • Pain or numbness in the tongue, or bleeding that is not from a bite
  • A lump in the neck, a sore throat that lasts, or pain in one ear

Why it happens

Other causes

  • HPV, a common virus, is linked to cancers at the back of the tongue and throat

See a doctor first if

  • Bleeding from the mouth that will not stop, or trouble breathing or swallowing: go to hospital now
  • If you are having cancer treatment and get a fever, chills or severe pain: call your care team or go to hospital
  • A sore, lump or patch on the tongue that has not gone away in three weeks
  • Numbness of the tongue, or pain in the tongue or one ear with no clear cause
  • A new lump in the neck, or a hoarse voice that lasts
  • Losing weight without trying, or finding it harder and harder to eat

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask your care team about pain, eating, swallowing and speech support
  • Keep your mouth and tongue clean, and see a dentist as your team advises

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy
  • Fever or an active infection

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Tongue cancer?

A cancer that starts in the cells of the tongue. Abnormal cells in the tongue grow out of control, often first showing as a sore or lump on the tongue that does not go away.

What are the common signs of Tongue cancer?

Painful mouth sores · Hard or painful to swallow

Why does Tongue cancer happen?

Smoking · Drinking alcohol · Getting older

How can I look after myself with Tongue cancer?

Follow your care team's plan · Stop smoking · Talk to a counsellor

Can an AI Body Master session help with Tongue cancer?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Tongue cancer and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
