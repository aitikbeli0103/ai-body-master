Refractive error (short or long sight, astigmatism)

The eye does not focus light sharply, so things look blurry at a distance, up close, or both

ConditionEyesHead

What it is

Short sight, long sight and astigmatism happen when the eye bends light a little too much or too little. Glasses or lenses from an eye professional can often make vision clear.

  • The eye's focusing
  • Any age, children too
  • Blurry vision
Which doctor
Eye doctor (ophthalmologist) or optometrist
Common in
Very common, in children and adults
Contagious
No
Course
Often changes slowly over time; regular eye tests help keep glasses right

Common signs

Why it happens

  • The shape of the eye or the front of the eye, often passed down in families
  • Lots of close work such as reading or screens may play a part
  • Less time outdoors in childhood may add to short sight
  • The eye's focusing power changes naturally with age

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden loss of vision or a sudden change in vision: go to hospital now
  • Flashes of light, many new floating spots, or a dark curtain in your sight
  • A painful, red eye with headache or feeling sick
  • A child who squints, sits very close to the screen, or cannot see the board
  • Headaches or tired eyes that keep coming back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Have your eyes tested regularly, and children too
  • Wear your glasses or lenses as advised
  • Rest your eyes often when reading or using a screen
  • Spend some time outdoors each day, especially children
  • Use good lighting for reading

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Refractive error (short or long sight, astigmatism)?

The eye does not focus light sharply, so things look blurry at a distance, up close, or both. Short sight, long sight and astigmatism happen when the eye bends light a little too much or too little. Glasses or lenses from an eye professional can often make vision clear.

What are the common signs of Refractive error (short or long sight, astigmatism)?

Blurry vision · Tired, strained eyes · Headache across the forehead

Why does Refractive error (short or long sight, astigmatism) happen?

The shape of the eye or the front of the eye, often passed down in families · Lots of close work such as reading or screens may play a part · Less time outdoors in childhood may add to short sight · The eye's focusing power changes naturally with age

How can I look after myself with Refractive error (short or long sight, astigmatism)?

Have your eyes tested regularly, and children too · Wear your glasses or lenses as advised · Rest your eyes often when reading or using a screen · Spend some time outdoors each day, especially children · Use good lighting for reading
