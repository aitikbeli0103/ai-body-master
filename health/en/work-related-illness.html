Work-related illness

Health problems caused or made worse by the work a person does

ConditionWork & surroundingsWhole body

What it is

A work-related illness is a health problem that comes from the job, such as aches from poor posture, breathing problems from dust or fumes, hearing loss from noise, or stress.

  • Depends on the job
  • People at work
  • Aches, cough, tired eyes
Which doctor
Family doctor; an occupational health doctor
Common in
Workers in offices, factories, building sites, farms and kitchens
Contagious
Usually no; some jobs raise the risk of catching infections
Course
Often improves when the cause at work is reduced; some need long-term care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Poor posture, long sitting or repeating the same movement
  • Loud noise, heavy lifting or long hours

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, severe trouble breathing, fainting or a serious injury: call 999
  • Contact with a chemical in the eyes or on the skin with burning or swelling: go to hospital now
  • Numbness, weakness or pain that spreads down an arm or leg
  • A cough or breathing trouble that lasts weeks
  • Feeling very low, hopeless, or thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Tell your employer or safety officer about the problem
  • Use the protective gear for your job, such as masks, gloves and ear protection
  • Set up your desk, chair and screen so you sit comfortably
  • Take short breaks to stretch and rest your eyes
  • See a doctor and say what your job is

Tell your therapist

  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Recent shoulder injury or surgery
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Work-related illness?

Health problems caused or made worse by the work a person does. A work-related illness is a health problem that comes from the job, such as aches from poor posture, breathing problems from dust or fumes, hearing loss from noise, or stress.

What are the common signs of Work-related illness?

Stiff, aching neck · Wheezing breath · Tired, strained eyes · Ringing in the ears · Itchy skin

Why does Work-related illness happen?

Smoke, haze and strong smells · Stress

How can I look after myself with Work-related illness?

Tell your employer or safety officer about the problem · Use the protective gear for your job, such as masks, gloves and ear protection · Set up your desk, chair and screen so you sit comfortably · Take short breaks to stretch and rest your eyes · See a doctor and say what your job is

Can an AI Body Master session help with Work-related illness?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Work-related illness and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
