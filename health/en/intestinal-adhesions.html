Intestinal adhesions

Bands of scar-like tissue that make parts of the bowel stick together

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

Bands of scar-like tissue can form inside the belly and make loops of bowel stick together. Many people feel nothing, but some get pain or a blocked bowel.

  • Inside the belly
  • Often after belly surgery
  • Cramping belly pain
Which doctor
Family doctor, general surgeon, gut doctor
Common in
People who have had belly surgery, infection or inflammation
Contagious
No
Course
Adhesions usually stay once formed; many cause no problem

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Past belly or pelvic surgery is the most common cause
  • Past infection or inflammation in the belly
  • Some gut diseases, or a past belly injury

See a doctor first if

  • Severe belly pain with repeated vomiting and a swollen belly: go to hospital now
  • Not able to pass wind or stools
  • Fever with belly pain, or a hard, tender belly
  • Vomiting that is green, brown or smells like stool
  • Belly pain that keeps coming back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Tell every doctor about any past belly surgery
  • Drink enough water and eat fibre if your doctor says it is fine
  • Do not use laxatives without asking a doctor or pharmacist

Tell your therapist

  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Fever or an active infection
