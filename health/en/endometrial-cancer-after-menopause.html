Endometrial cancer after menopause

Cancer of the lining of the womb that is found after the periods have stopped

ConditionCancer & tumoursReproductive

What it is

This is cancer that starts in the lining of the womb. After menopause, any bleeding from the vagina is not normal and should always be checked by a doctor.

  • The womb
  • Women after menopause
  • Bleeding or lower belly ache
Which doctor
Gynaecologist, cancer doctor (oncologist), family doctor
Common in
Women after menopause, more often as they get older
Contagious
No
Course
Often found early because of bleeding; your cancer care team can explain what to expect

Common signs

More signs

  • Any bleeding from the vagina after menopause, even a little
  • Watery, pink or smelly discharge

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A long time of high oestrogen in the body without enough balance
  • Never having had children, or a family history of womb or bowel cancer

See a doctor first if

  • Any bleeding from the vagina after menopause: see a doctor soon, do not wait
  • Heavy bleeding with dizziness or fainting: go to hospital now
  • Severe pain in the lower belly that comes on suddenly: go to hospital now
  • Smelly or bloody discharge that does not go away
  • Weight loss without trying, or new pain in the pelvis that lasts

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
