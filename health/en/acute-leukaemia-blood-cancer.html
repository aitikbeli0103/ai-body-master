Acute leukaemia (blood cancer)

A fast-growing cancer of the blood and bone marrow, where abnormal white blood cells crowd out healthy ones

ConditionCancer & tumoursBlood

What it is

Acute leukaemia is a cancer that starts in the bone marrow, where abnormal white blood cells grow fast and crowd out healthy blood cells, so it needs prompt medical care.

  • Blood and bone marrow
  • Can affect any age
  • Tired, bruising, infections
Which doctor
Blood specialist (haematologist) at a hospital; see a doctor promptly
Common in
Children and adults; the type often depends on age
Contagious
No
Course
Comes on over days to weeks; prompt care from a specialist team is important

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Earlier cancer treatment, such as chemotherapy or radiotherapy, can raise the risk
  • Heavy exposure to radiation or to some chemicals, such as benzene
  • Some blood disorders and inherited conditions can raise the risk

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy bleeding that will not stop, or blood in vomit, stools or urine: go to hospital now
  • Trouble breathing, chest pain, fainting, confusion or a fit: call 999
  • Sudden weakness on one side, face drooping or speech trouble: call 999
  • Fever or shivering with chills, especially during cancer care: go to hospital straight away
  • Easy bruising, tiny red spots on the skin, nosebleeds or bleeding gums
  • Feeling very tired, pale or breathless for no clear reason, or bone pain that lasts

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop or change your treatment or medicines on your own; ask your doctor or pharmacist
  • Ask your care team about pain, food and emotional support
  • Take care to avoid cuts and knocks, as you may bruise or bleed easily; ask your team for advice

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
