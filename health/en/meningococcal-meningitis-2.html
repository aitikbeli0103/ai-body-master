Meningococcal meningitis

A serious bacterial infection that swells the layers around the brain

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

A germ called meningococcus can infect the layers around the brain and spinal cord. It can get worse in hours and is a medical emergency.

  • Head, neck and blood
  • Spreads by close contact
  • Fever, headache, rash
Which doctor
Go to hospital at once; infection specialist
Common in
Babies, young children, teenagers, young adults and people in crowded places
Contagious
Yes, through close contact and droplets
Course
Needs hospital care right away; early care gives the best chance of a good recovery

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • It passes on through coughs, sneezes, kissing and sharing cups
  • A weak immune system or no spleen

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with a bad headache, stiff neck or confusion: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • A rash that does not fade when pressed with a glass: call 999
  • Fits, hard to wake, very floppy, or cold hands and feet with a fever: call 999
  • A baby with fever who cries oddly, will not feed or has a bulging soft spot: go to hospital now
  • You lived with or were close to someone who has it: tell a doctor quickly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
