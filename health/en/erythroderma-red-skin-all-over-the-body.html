Erythroderma (red skin all over the body)

Redness and flaking of the skin over most of the body

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Most of the skin on the body turns red, dry and flaky, and it may itch. You can feel cold, shaky or unwell, so it needs a doctor's care soon.

  • Skin over most of the body
  • Needs a doctor's care soon
  • Red, flaky, itchy, chilly
Which doctor
Skin doctor (dermatologist); hospital if very unwell
Common in
Adults; people with eczema or psoriasis, or after a new medicine
Contagious
No, but rare causes such as scabies can spread
Course
Depends on the cause; often weeks to months, with a doctor's follow-up

Common signs

Why it happens

  • A long-term skin problem such as eczema or psoriasis getting much worse
  • A skin reaction to a medicine, which a doctor needs to check
  • Other skin diseases, allergic skin reactions, or rarely scabies
  • Rarely, a problem of the blood or lymph system, which a doctor can check for
  • Sometimes no cause is found

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, chest pain, a very fast heartbeat or fainting: call 999
  • Red, flaky skin over most of the body: see a doctor the same day
  • Fever, shaking chills, confusion or feeling very weak: go to hospital now
  • Skin that oozes, blisters, hurts a lot or smells bad (possible infection)
  • Passing very little urine, dizziness or a very dry mouth (signs of dehydration)
  • Redness that started soon after a new medicine: ask your doctor or pharmacist right away

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep warm with a light blanket if you feel chilly
  • Drink fluids regularly, unless your doctor has limited them

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • A heart condition

  • Pacemaker or metal implant

What is Erythroderma (red skin all over the body)?

Redness and flaking of the skin over most of the body. Most of the skin on the body turns red, dry and flaky, and it may itch. You can feel cold, shaky or unwell, so it needs a doctor's care soon.

What are the common signs of Erythroderma (red skin all over the body)?

Red rash · Dry, flaky skin · Itchy skin · Shivering with chills · Fever, feeling hot

Why does Erythroderma (red skin all over the body) happen?

A long-term skin problem such as eczema or psoriasis getting much worse · A skin reaction to a medicine, which a doctor needs to check · Other skin diseases, allergic skin reactions, or rarely scabies · Rarely, a problem of the blood or lymph system, which a doctor can check for · Sometimes no cause is found

How can I look after myself with Erythroderma (red skin all over the body)?

Keep the skin moisturised · Wear loose cotton clothes · Ask your pharmacist
