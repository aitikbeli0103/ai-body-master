Vaginal cyst

A small fluid-filled lump in or near the wall of the vagina

ConditionWomen's healthPelvisReproductive

What it is

A vaginal cyst is a small closed sac under the skin of the vagina. Most are small, soft and slow-growing, and many cause no trouble at all.

  • Wall of the vagina
  • Adult women
  • A small, soft lump
Which doctor
Women's doctor (gynaecologist), family doctor
Common in
Women of childbearing age; after childbirth or vaginal injury
Contagious
No
Course
Often stays small and may need only a check-up; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

More signs

  • A small, smooth lump felt in the vagina
  • A feeling of pressure or fullness

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Childbirth or a small tear or cut in the vagina
  • A gland or duct that gets blocked and fills with fluid
  • Past surgery or injury in the area

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy bleeding from the vagina: go to hospital now
  • Fever with a hot, red, very painful lump (it may be infected)
  • A lump that grows quickly or feels hard
  • Pain that makes it hard to walk, sit or pass urine
  • Any new lump after the change of life (menopause)

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep the area clean with plain water and mild, unscented soap
  • Wear loose, cotton underwear
