Acne during periods

Pimples that appear or get worse in the days before or during the period

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsReproductiveSkin

What it is

Many women get pimples, or see them get worse, in the days before and during their period, often on the chin, jawline and cheeks.

  • Chin, jawline and cheeks
  • Linked to the monthly cycle
  • Pimples, sometimes tender
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor or gynaecologist if it keeps returning
Common in
Girls and women of any age with periods, most often teens and young adults
Contagious
No
Course
Often settles after the period; it may come back each month

Why care for acne early

Most severe acne started as mild acne that was left alone. Marks and scars take longer to fade, so start caring early.

Marks left after acne

Ways that make acne worse

  • Squeezing pimples: it pushes germs deeper
  • Using steroid creams for a long time: acne comes back when you stop
  • Strong acid peels at home: skin becomes sensitive
  • Gua sha or scrubbing over pimples: it spreads the inflammation

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Oil and dead skin block the pores, and skin bacteria can then cause red pimples
  • Heavy make-up, sweat, and touching or picking at the skin
  • Sometimes a hormone condition such as polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)

See a doctor first if

  • Swelling of the face, lips or tongue, or trouble breathing after using a product or medicine: call 999
  • Thoughts of harming yourself, or feeling very low or hopeless before your period: see a doctor now or call 999
  • Very irregular or missing periods, with extra facial hair, quick weight gain or thinning hair
  • Sudden, severe breakouts with fever or feeling very unwell
  • Deep, painful lumps, scarring, or no improvement after several weeks of simple care
  • You are pregnant or may be: ask your doctor before using any acne product or medicine

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wash your face gently twice a day with a mild cleanser; do not scrub
  • Choose oil-free skincare and make-up, and remove make-up before bed
  • Note when pimples show up in your cycle; it helps your doctor
  • Get regular sleep and make time to relax; for creams or medicines, ask your doctor or pharmacist
