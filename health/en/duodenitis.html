Duodenitis

Inflammation (swelling and irritation) of the lining of the duodenum, the first part of the small bowel

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

The lining of the duodenum becomes red and sore. Often it causes an upset stomach, nausea or a dull ache in the upper belly, and sometimes nothing at all.

  • Upper belly
  • Often mild, can flare up
  • Upset stomach, nausea
Which doctor
Family doctor, stomach and bowel doctor (gastroenterologist)
Common in
Adults; people who drink heavily, smoke or use some pain medicines for long
Contagious
Some types can be, such as those caused by H. pylori or other infections
Course
Often eases when the cause is found and avoided, but it can come back

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Alcohol, smoking, or some pain-relief medicines used for a long time
  • Extra stomach acid washing into the duodenum
  • Long-term bowel conditions such as Crohn's disease or coeliac disease

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood, or black, sticky or bloody stools: go to hospital now
  • Sudden, very severe belly pain or a hard belly: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Fainting, or feeling very weak, pale and dizzy: go to hospital now
  • Repeated vomiting, or signs of lack of fluid such as a very dry mouth and little urine
  • Pain with a high fever, or diarrhoea that is bloody or lasts for days
  • Weight loss you cannot explain, or symptoms that last for weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See your doctor to find the cause, especially if the pain keeps coming back
  • Avoid alcohol and smoking, which can irritate the lining
  • Drink enough water, and wash your hands before eating to lower the risk of infection

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Duodenitis?

Inflammation (swelling and irritation) of the lining of the duodenum, the first part of the small bowel. The lining of the duodenum becomes red and sore. Often it causes an upset stomach, nausea or a dull ache in the upper belly, and sometimes nothing at all.

What are the common signs of Duodenitis?

Pain in the upper belly · Feeling sick, nausea · Bloated, gassy belly · No appetite

Why does Duodenitis happen?

Germs (infection) · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Duodenitis?

Eat small, regular meals · Ask your pharmacist
