Swollen kidney (hydronephrosis)

A kidney that swells because urine cannot drain away properly

ConditionKidneys & urinaryAbdomen

What it is

Urine backs up into the kidney because something slows or blocks its way out, so the kidney swells. Finding the cause early matters.

  • Kidney and urine tubes
  • Any age, even before birth
  • Side or back pain
Which doctor
Family doctor; urologist or kidney doctor
Common in
People with stones, an enlarged prostate or pregnancy; some babies
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; clearing the blockage early helps protect the kidney

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Stones in the kidney or urine tubes
  • A narrowing of the urine tube, or pressure from a growth

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine, or very little urine with vomiting: go to hospital now
  • Severe pain in the side with fever and chills
  • Confusion, severe weakness or trouble breathing
  • A baby or child who is feverish, not feeding or very unwell: see a doctor promptly
  • Blood in the urine

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
