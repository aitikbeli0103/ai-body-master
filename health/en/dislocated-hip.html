Dislocated hip

The ball of the thigh bone slips out of the hip socket

ConditionPelvis, hip, knee & legArms & legs

What it is

The top of the thigh bone comes out of its socket in the hip. It is an emergency that needs a hospital, usually after a hard fall, a car crash or a hard knock.

  • Hip joint
  • After a hard fall or crash
  • Severe hip pain
Which doctor
Emergency doctor, then bone doctor (orthopaedic surgeon)
Common in
People in road accidents or hard falls; some who have had a hip replacement
Contagious
No
Course
Needs a doctor to put it back quickly; recovery takes weeks to months

Common signs

More signs

  • The leg looks shorter or is turned in or out
  • Numbness or tingling in the leg or foot

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A hip replacement can slip out more easily, especially soon after surgery

See a doctor first if

  • Suspected hip dislocation: go to hospital now or call 999
  • A leg that looks shorter, twisted or bent out of shape
  • Cannot move the leg, or a foot that is numb, pale, cold or blue
  • Heavy swelling, or pain after a serious crash or fall

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Do not try to put the joint back yourself
  • Keep the person still and do not let them walk
  • Do not give food or drink in case surgery is needed
  • After hospital care, follow your doctor's advice on rest and walking aids

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Dislocated hip?

What are the common signs of Dislocated hip?

Pain in the hip · Cannot put weight on the leg · Swelling and bruising

Why does Dislocated hip happen?

A fall, knock or road accident · An injury or fall · Sports and hard knocks
