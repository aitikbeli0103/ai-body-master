Sinoatrial block (heartbeat signal block)

The signal from the heart's natural pacemaker is delayed or blocked, so a beat may be missed

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsBlood

What it is

The electrical signal that starts each heartbeat sometimes does not leave its starting point. The heart may pause briefly or beat slowly.

  • Heart's signal system
  • More common with age
  • Pauses, dizziness
Which doctor
Heart doctor (cardiologist), family doctor
Common in
Older adults; people with other heart disease
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; some people have no problems, others need a doctor to follow it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Other heart disease, or past heart surgery
  • In fit, young people it can be a normal finding at rest

See a doctor first if

  • Fainting or nearly fainting: go to hospital now
  • Chest pain or sudden trouble breathing: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Repeated dizzy spells or a very slow pulse with tiredness

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Sinoatrial block (heartbeat signal block)?

The signal from the heart's natural pacemaker is delayed or blocked, so a beat may be missed. The electrical signal that starts each heartbeat sometimes does not leave its starting point. The heart may pause briefly or beat slowly.

What are the common signs of Sinoatrial block (heartbeat signal block)?

Feeling faint · Dizzy when standing up · Tired all the time · Racing, pounding heart

Why does Sinoatrial block (heartbeat signal block) happen?

Getting older · A side effect of some medicines

How can I look after myself with Sinoatrial block (heartbeat signal block)?

See your doctor for check-ups · Ask your pharmacist · Stop smoking
