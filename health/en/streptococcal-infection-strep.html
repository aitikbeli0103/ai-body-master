Streptococcal infection (strep)

Infection by strep bacteria, most often a sore throat or skin infection

ConditionInfectionsWhole body

What it is

Strep is a group of bacteria that can infect the throat or skin, and sometimes other parts of the body.

  • Throat or skin, sometimes more
  • Common in school children
  • Fever and sore throat
Which doctor
Family doctor; children see a paediatrician
Common in
Children and teenagers; people who live or work closely together
Contagious
Yes, by droplets from coughs and sneezes, and by touch
Course
Often gets better within days to a week; a doctor decides if medicine is needed

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Strep bacteria passed on by coughs, sneezes or close contact
  • Sharing cups, spoons or towels with someone who is sick
  • Cuts or insect bites on the skin that let the germ in

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, drooling, or cannot swallow: go to hospital now
  • High fever with a stiff neck, confusion or very drowsy: call 999
  • A red, hot, fast-spreading patch of skin, or a sandpaper-like rash
  • Weeks later: swollen painful joints, dark urine or puffy face
  • A baby or child who is very unwell: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Rest and drink plenty of fluids
  • If a doctor gives medicine, take all of it as told; ask your doctor or pharmacist about pain or fever relief
  • Stay home until you feel better and the doctor says it is fine

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • A heart condition
  • A kidney or liver condition

Questions and answers

What is Streptococcal infection (strep)?

Infection by strep bacteria, most often a sore throat or skin infection. Strep is a group of bacteria that can infect the throat or skin, and sometimes other parts of the body.

What are the common signs of Streptococcal infection (strep)?

Sore, scratchy throat · Fever, feeling hot · Swollen glands in the neck · Shivering with chills · Headache across the forehead · Red rash

Why does Streptococcal infection (strep) happen?

Crowded places and droplets

How can I look after myself with Streptococcal infection (strep)?

Wash your hands often · Do not share towels, razors or cups
