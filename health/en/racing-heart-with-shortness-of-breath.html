Racing heart with shortness of breath

Feeling the heart beat fast or hard, together with feeling short of breath

SymptomHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

Your heart races or pounds and you also feel short of breath. After hard exercise this is normal, but if it happens at rest or on little effort, see a doctor.

  • Chest
  • More often with age
  • Racing, hard to breathe
Which doctor
Family doctor; heart or lung doctor if it keeps happening
Common in
Anyone; more often with heart or lung problems, anaemia or anxiety
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; a doctor should find out why

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Hard exercise, a fright, or strong feelings
  • Anxiety or a panic attack
  • Anaemia, fever, or an overactive thyroid
  • A heart rhythm problem, heart weakness or lung disease such as asthma
  • A blood clot in the lungs, which is an emergency

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden or severe trouble breathing: call 999
  • Chest pain or pressure, fainting, or cold sweat: call 999
  • Blue or grey lips, or you cannot speak a full sentence
  • One leg newly swollen or painful, or coughing up blood
  • Waking at night short of breath, or swollen ankles

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Stop what you are doing, sit down and breathe slowly
  • Build activity up slowly, as your doctor allows

Tell your therapist

  • A heart condition
  • High blood pressure
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Related conditions

  • Variant angina (Prinzmetal's angina)
  • Viral myocarditis (heart muscle inflammation)
  • Sick sinus syndrome
  • Incomplete right bundle branch block
  • Unstable angina
  • Varicose veins in the leg (great saphenous vein)
  • Low blood pressure
  • Arteriovenous fistula (AV fistula)
