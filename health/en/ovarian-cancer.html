Ovarian cancer

A cancer that starts in one or both ovaries, the organs that make eggs

ConditionCancer & tumoursReproductive

What it is

A cancer that starts in the ovaries, which make eggs and female hormones. Early signs can be vague and easy to mistake for common stomach upsets.

  • Ovaries, in the lower belly
  • Most often after menopause
  • Bloating, lower belly ache
Which doctor
Gynaecologist or women's cancer doctor (gynaecological oncologist)
Common in
Women, most often after menopause
Contagious
No
Course
Early signs are easy to miss; your cancer team can explain what to expect

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A close family member with ovarian or breast cancer, or an inherited gene change such as BRCA
  • Endometriosis (womb-lining tissue growing outside the womb) may raise the risk a little

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden severe belly pain, especially with fever or vomiting: go to hospital now
  • A hard swollen belly with repeated vomiting and no wind or stools: go to hospital now
  • A painful swollen leg, chest pain or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Heavy vaginal bleeding: go to hospital now; any bleeding after menopause: see a doctor soon
  • Bloating, feeling full quickly or belly or pelvic pain on most days for more than three weeks
  • Losing weight without trying, or needing the toilet much more often than usual

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask your team about pain, food and emotional support
  • Tell your doctor if close relatives had ovarian, breast or bowel cancer; ask if genetic advice is right for you

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Pregnancy
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Ovarian cancer?

A cancer that starts in one or both ovaries, the organs that make eggs. A cancer that starts in the ovaries, which make eggs and female hormones. Early signs can be vague and easy to mistake for common stomach upsets.

What are the common signs of Ovarian cancer?

Bloated, gassy belly · Dragging ache in the lower belly · No appetite · Needing the toilet often · Tired all the time

Why does Ovarian cancer happen?

The cause is not fully known · Getting older · Extra body weight

How can I look after myself with Ovarian cancer?

Follow your care team's plan · Eat small, regular meals · Ask your pharmacist

Can an AI Body Master session help with Ovarian cancer?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Ovarian cancer and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
