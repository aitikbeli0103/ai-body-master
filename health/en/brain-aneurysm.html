Brain aneurysm

A weak, bulging spot in the wall of a blood vessel in the brain

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

A part of a brain artery wall becomes weak and bulges out like a small balloon. Many are never noticed, but a burst one is an emergency.

  • A blood vessel in the brain
  • Adults, more in middle age
  • Often no signs at all
Which doctor
Brain doctor (neurologist), brain surgeon
Common in
Adults, more often women and those with high blood pressure
Contagious
No
Course
Often stays quiet for years; a doctor decides if it needs watching or care

Common signs

More signs

  • A large one may press on nearby parts and cause pain behind or above one eye
  • A sudden, very severe headache if it leaks or bursts

Why it happens

See a doctor first if

  • A sudden, very severe headache: call 999 now
  • Fainting, fits or becoming hard to wake: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Headache with stiff neck, vomiting or dislike of light: go to hospital now
  • New double vision or a drooping eyelid

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Brain aneurysm?

A weak, bulging spot in the wall of a blood vessel in the brain. A part of a brain artery wall becomes weak and bulges out like a small balloon. Many are never noticed, but a burst one is an emergency.

What are the common signs of Brain aneurysm?

Often no signs at first · Double or changed vision

Why does Brain aneurysm happen?

The cause is not fully known · High blood sugar or blood pressure · Smoking · Runs in the family · Present from birth

How can I look after myself with Brain aneurysm?

Check your blood pressure at home · Stop smoking · See your doctor for check-ups

Can an AI Body Master session help with Brain aneurysm?
