Sigmoid colon cancer

Cancer in the lower, S-shaped part of the large bowel

ConditionCancer & tumoursAbdomen

What it is

The sigmoid colon is the last curved part of the large bowel before the rectum. Cancer here often starts as a small growth called a polyp.

  • Lower left belly
  • More common after middle age
  • Bowel changes, blood in stool
Which doctor
Bowel specialist (gastroenterologist), surgeon, cancer doctor
Common in
Adults, more often after middle age
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the stage; the cancer care team can explain what to expect

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Polyps in the bowel, or a family history of bowel cancer
  • Long-lasting bowel inflammation, such as colitis
  • Lots of red or processed meat, little fibre, being overweight or inactive

See a doctor first if

  • Severe belly pain with a swollen belly, vomiting, and not able to pass wind or stool: go to hospital now
  • Blood in the stool, or black stools
  • A change in bowel habit that lasts more than a few weeks
  • Weight loss without trying, or looking pale and very tired
  • A feeling that the bowel never empties, or pain in the lower left belly that stays

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Questions and answers

What is Sigmoid colon cancer?

Cancer in the lower, S-shaped part of the large bowel. The sigmoid colon is the last curved part of the large bowel before the rectum. Cancer here often starts as a small growth called a polyp.

What are the common signs of Sigmoid colon cancer?

Hard to pass stools · Loose, frequent stools · Cramping belly pain · Losing weight without trying · Tired all the time

Why does Sigmoid colon cancer happen?

Getting older · Smoking

How can I look after myself with Sigmoid colon cancer?

See your doctor for check-ups · Drink enough water · Stop smoking
