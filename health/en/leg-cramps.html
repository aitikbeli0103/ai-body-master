Leg cramps

A sudden, painful tightening of a leg muscle that you cannot control

ConditionBones, joints & spineBones & muscles

What it is

A leg muscle, often the calf, suddenly tightens and hurts for a short time. It often happens at night or after a lot of activity.

  • Calf, thigh or foot
  • Common in older adults
  • Sudden tight pain
Which doctor
Family doctor if it keeps coming back
Common in
Older adults, pregnant women, active people, night-time sleepers
Contagious
No
Course
Usually eases within minutes; it may come back from time to time

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Tired muscles after a lot of walking, standing or exercise
  • Sitting or lying in one position for a long time
  • Sometimes a nerve, circulation or other health problem

See a doctor first if

  • Cramps with chest pain or trouble breathing: call 999
  • One leg suddenly swollen, red, warm and painful: go to hospital now
  • Cramps that are very frequent or wake you up most nights
  • Leg weakness, numbness or muscle wasting with the cramps
  • Cramps that began after starting a new medicine

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Gently stretch the muscle and walk around when a cramp starts
  • Warm the muscle with a warm towel, not too hot
  • Stretch your calves gently before bed and after exercise

Tell your therapist

  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

What are the common signs of Leg cramps?

Cramps in the calf · Heavy, tired legs · Legs feel weak

Why does Leg cramps happen?

Not drinking enough water · Pregnancy

How can I look after myself with Leg cramps?

Drink enough water · Ask your pharmacist
