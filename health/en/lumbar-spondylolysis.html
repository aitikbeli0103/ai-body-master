Lumbar spondylolysis

A small crack or weak spot in a bone of the lower spine

ConditionBones, joints & spineLower back

What it is

A tiny crack or stress break can form in a small bridge of bone at the back of a lower spine bone. It can cause lower back pain, especially when bending backwards.

  • Lower back
  • Young athletes, active people
  • Pain on bending back
Which doctor
Orthopaedic doctor, sports doctor or family doctor
Common in
Teens and young adults in sports; some older adults
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; a doctor can explain what to expect and when to return to sport

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Repeated bending backwards, such as in gymnastics, diving or cricket
  • Heavy training without enough rest
  • Often there is no clear single cause

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine or stools, or numbness around the groin or bottom: go to hospital now
  • Weakness, numbness or tingling in a leg
  • Back pain after a fall or hard knock: go to hospital
  • Back pain with fever, or severe pain at night
  • Back pain in a child or teenager that lasts more than a few days

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Rest from the sport or activity that makes the pain worse
  • Return to sport slowly and only when your doctor agrees
  • Follow the exercise advice from your doctor or physiotherapist

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
