Autoimmune hepatitis

Long-term liver inflammation caused by the body's own defence system

ConditionLiver, gallbladder & pancreasAbdomen

What it is

The body's defence system mistakenly attacks the liver cells and causes inflammation. It is not caused by a virus or by alcohol.

  • The liver
  • More often in women
  • Tired, aching joints
Which doctor
Liver doctor (hepatologist) or stomach and liver doctor
Common in
Women more than men; can start at any age
Contagious
No
Course
A long-term condition; a doctor follows it over time and explains the options

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The defence system attacks the liver by mistake
  • It may run in families or go with other autoimmune conditions
  • Sometimes an infection or a medicine may set it off

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood, black stools or confusion: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Yellow skin or eyes that get worse
  • A swollen belly or swollen legs
  • Fever, new belly pain or dark urine
  • Easy bruising or bleeding

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Autoimmune hepatitis?

Long-term liver inflammation caused by the body's own defence system. The body's defence system mistakenly attacks the liver cells and causes inflammation. It is not caused by a virus or by alcohol.

What are the common signs of Autoimmune hepatitis?

Tired all the time · Yellow skin and eyes · Itchy skin · Pain in the upper belly · Aching all over

Why does Autoimmune hepatitis happen?

The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Autoimmune hepatitis?

Keep your regular tests · Take medicine as your doctor says
