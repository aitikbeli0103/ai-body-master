Costochondritis (inflamed rib cartilage)

Soreness and swelling of the cartilage that joins the ribs to the breastbone

ConditionBones, joints & spineChestBones & muscles

What it is

The cartilage where the ribs meet the breastbone becomes sore and tender, which can cause chest pain that is often worse with pressing or deep breaths.

  • Front of the chest
  • Adults, any age
  • Sore spot when pressed
Which doctor
Family doctor first; emergency doctor if chest pain is sudden
Common in
Adults, after a cough, strain or injury; often the cause is not known
Contagious
No
Course
Usually eases by itself over weeks, but can come and go for a while

Common signs

More signs

  • Sharp, aching or pressure-like pain at the front of the chest
  • A tender spot you can find by pressing on the ribs near the breastbone
  • Pain that may spread a little along the ribs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Strain from heavy coughing, lifting or hard exercise
  • A recent chest cold or breathing infection

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain with trouble breathing, sweating, or pain spreading to the arm, neck or jaw: call 999
  • Any new chest pain that you are not sure about: have it checked by a doctor now
  • Fever, or a red, hot, swollen spot on the chest
  • Coughing up blood
  • Pain that is getting worse or lasts for many weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • A warm towel on the sore area may feel comfortable
  • Breathe slowly and gently; avoid holding your breath
  • Return to exercise slowly as the pain settles

Tell your therapist

  • A heart condition
  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Weak bones

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
