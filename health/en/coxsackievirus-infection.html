Coxsackievirus infection

A common virus infection, often with fever, sore throat and mouth sores or rash

ConditionInfectionsWhole body

What it is

Coxsackieviruses are common germs, mostly in children. They often cause a mild illness like hand, foot and mouth disease, and most people get better with rest and fluids.

  • Whole body
  • Mostly young children
  • Fever, sore mouth
Which doctor
Family doctor; child doctor (paediatrician) for children
Common in
Babies and young children; adults can catch it too
Contagious
Yes, through saliva, coughs, sores and stools
Course
Usually better in about a week or so

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Catching the virus from saliva, sneezes or coughs of a sick person
  • Touching blisters or objects with the virus, then touching the mouth
  • Poor hand washing, especially after changing nappies

See a doctor first if

  • Stiff neck, severe headache or very sleepy and hard to wake: go to hospital now
  • Trouble breathing, fast breathing or chest pain: call 999
  • A fit, fainting, or limbs that cannot move properly: go to hospital now
  • Not drinking, no urine for many hours, or a dry mouth: see a doctor promptly
  • A baby or young child that looks very unwell, or a fever lasting more than three days

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Coxsackievirus infection?

A common virus infection, often with fever, sore throat and mouth sores or rash. Coxsackieviruses are common germs, mostly in children. They often cause a mild illness like hand, foot and mouth disease, and most people get better with rest and fluids.

What are the common signs of Coxsackievirus infection?

Fever, feeling hot · Sore, scratchy throat · Painful mouth sores · Red rash · No appetite

Why does Coxsackievirus infection happen?

Crowded places and droplets

How can I look after myself with Coxsackievirus infection?

Rest well · Drink enough water · Wash your hands often · Ask your pharmacist

Can an AI Body Master session help with Coxsackievirus infection?
