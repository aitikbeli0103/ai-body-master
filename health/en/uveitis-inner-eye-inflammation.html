Uveitis (inner eye inflammation)

Inflammation of the middle layer of the eye (the uvea)

ConditionEyesHead

What it is

Uveitis is swelling inside the eye. It can make the eye red, sore and blurry, and it needs to be checked by an eye doctor soon.

  • Inside the eye
  • Any age, often adults
  • Red, sore, blurry eye
Which doctor
Eye doctor (ophthalmologist) as soon as possible
Common in
Adults of any age; some people with body-wide inflammatory conditions
Contagious
No, but some causes are infections
Course
Can settle or come back; follow-up with an eye doctor matters

Common signs

More signs

  • Eye pain or a dull ache

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Body-wide inflammatory or autoimmune conditions
  • An eye injury

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden loss of vision or sudden blurring: go to hospital now
  • A red, painful eye that is sensitive to light: see an eye doctor the same day
  • An eye injury or something in the eye
  • Many new floating spots or flashes of light
  • Eye symptoms that keep coming back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See an eye doctor early; do not wait for it to pass
  • Wear sunglasses in bright light
  • Do not rub the eye

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pregnancy
