Aortic dissection

A tear in the inner wall of the aorta, which is a life-threatening emergency

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

The inner layer of the aorta tears, and blood flows into the wall. It usually causes sudden, severe pain. Call 999 at once.

  • Chest, back, belly
  • More common in older adults
  • Sudden tearing pain
Which doctor
Emergency department; heart or vascular surgeon
Common in
Adults with high blood pressure; also older adults
Contagious
No
Course
An emergency that needs hospital care at once

Common signs

More signs

  • Sudden, very strong pain in the chest or upper back
  • Pain that feels like tearing and may move down the back

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A weak aortic wall from age or from birth

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden, severe pain in the chest, back or belly: call 999 now
  • Fainting, cold sweat or a very fast weak pulse: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Trouble breathing or sudden weakness in the legs
  • Do not drive yourself; wait for the ambulance

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Aortic dissection?

A tear in the inner wall of the aorta, which is a life-threatening emergency. The inner layer of the aorta tears, and blood flows into the wall. It usually causes sudden, severe pain. Call 999 at once.

What are the common signs of Aortic dissection?

Feeling faint · Weakness on one side of the body

Why does Aortic dissection happen?

High blood sugar or blood pressure · Smoking · An injury or fall · Runs in the family

How can I look after myself with Aortic dissection?

Check your blood pressure at home · Stop smoking · Avoid heavy lifting · See your doctor for check-ups
