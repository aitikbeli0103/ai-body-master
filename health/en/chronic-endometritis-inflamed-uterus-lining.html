Chronic endometritis (inflamed uterus lining)

Long-lasting, low-level inflammation of the inner lining of the womb

ConditionWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

The lining of the womb (the endometrium) stays inflamed for a long time, often because of germs. Some women have no signs at all.

  • Womb lining
  • Women of child-bearing age
  • Spotting or dull ache
Which doctor
Gynaecologist
Common in
Women with past womb infection, or who struggle to conceive
Contagious
Some causes can spread through sex
Course
Needs a doctor's check; it often does not go away by itself

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Germs reaching the womb, sometimes from an infection passed on by sex
  • An infection after childbirth, miscarriage or a womb procedure
  • Having a coil (IUD) fitted in the womb

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy bleeding that soaks a pad in an hour, or fainting: go to hospital now
  • Fever with severe belly pain or foul-smelling discharge: go to hospital now
  • Pregnant or possibly pregnant with bleeding or belly pain: go to hospital now
  • Trouble getting pregnant or repeated miscarriage
  • Bleeding after sex or after menopause

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • See a gynaecologist; this needs tests that only a doctor can arrange
  • Do not buy antibiotics or other medicine yourself; ask your doctor or pharmacist
  • Use condoms and ask your partner to be checked if a doctor advises
  • Keep the genital area clean; avoid douching

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Questions and answers

What is Chronic endometritis (inflamed uterus lining)?

Long-lasting, low-level inflammation of the inner lining of the womb. The lining of the womb (the endometrium) stays inflamed for a long time, often because of germs. Some women have no signs at all.

What are the common signs of Chronic endometritis (inflamed uterus lining)?

Bleeding between periods · Dragging ache in the lower belly · Unusual discharge or itching down below · Pain during or after sex

Why does Chronic endometritis (inflamed uterus lining) happen?

The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Chronic endometritis (inflamed uterus lining)?

See a gynaecologist; this needs tests that only a doctor can arrange · Do not buy antibiotics or other medicine yourself; ask your doctor or pharmacist · Use condoms and ask your partner to be checked if a doctor advises · Keep the genital area clean; avoid douching
