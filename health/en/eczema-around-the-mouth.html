Eczema around the mouth

Inflamed, red, dry and itchy skin around the mouth

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsHead

What it is

Eczema around the mouth is inflamed skin near the lips that turns red, dry, scaly and itchy or sore. It often follows lip licking, drooling or irritation.

  • Skin around the mouth
  • Children and adults
  • Red, dry, itchy skin
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor (dermatologist); paediatrician for a child
Common in
Children who lick their lips or drool; people with sensitive skin
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes; it can improve when the trigger is found and avoided

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Licking the lips often, or drooling, which irritates the skin
  • Irritants such as toothpaste, lip products, make-up, or acidic and spicy food on the skin
  • Allergy to something that touches the skin, such as a cosmetic, a metal or a food
  • A tendency to eczema or sensitive skin, often with dry skin elsewhere

See a doctor first if

  • Swelling of the lips, face or tongue, or trouble breathing after food or a product: call 999
  • A baby or young child with the rash who has a fever, is very unwell or is not feeding well: see a doctor promptly
  • Oozing, yellow or honey-coloured crusts, pus, or a fever: it may be infected
  • Small, painful blisters or sores in a cluster, which can be a cold sore
  • The rash spreads fast, or spreads to the eyes, face or body
  • No improvement after a few weeks, or it keeps coming back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Rinse your mouth well after brushing, and wipe toothpaste and food off the skin
  • Avoid scented lip balms and cosmetics, and very spicy or sour food touching the skin, until it settles
