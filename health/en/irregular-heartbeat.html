Irregular heartbeat

The heart beats too fast, too slow or in an uneven pattern

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

The heart's own electrical signals are off, so the heartbeat may feel fast, slow, skipping or fluttering. Some are harmless, and some need a doctor.

  • Chest and heart
  • Any age; more with age
  • Racing or skipping beat
Which doctor
Family doctor, heart doctor (cardiologist)
Common in
Anyone; more often in older adults and people with heart disease
Contagious
No
Course
Varies: some come and go, some need long-term care from a doctor

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • An overactive thyroid, or low salts in the blood

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, fainting or trouble breathing with a fast heartbeat: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • A racing heart that does not settle after a few minutes of rest
  • Feeling very dizzy or about to faint
  • A heartbeat that is often uneven, even if you feel well

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Irregular heartbeat?

The heart beats too fast, too slow or in an uneven pattern. The heart's own electrical signals are off, so the heartbeat may feel fast, slow, skipping or fluttering. Some are harmless, and some need a doctor.

What are the common signs of Irregular heartbeat?

Racing, pounding heart · Feeling faint · Short of breath on stairs · Tight, heavy chest · Tired all the time

Why does Irregular heartbeat happen?

High blood sugar or blood pressure · Smoking · A side effect of some medicines

How can I look after myself with Irregular heartbeat?

See your doctor for check-ups · Cut down on alcohol · Get good sleep

Can an AI Body Master session help with Irregular heartbeat?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Irregular heartbeat and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
