Spinal cord vascular malformation

A rare tangle or abnormal link of blood vessels on or in the spinal cord

ConditionBrain & nervesBack

What it is

Some blood vessels of the spinal cord form abnormally. This can press on or disturb the cord, and may cause back pain, weakness or numbness.

  • Spine and spinal cord
  • Rare; can start at any age
  • Back pain, weak or numb legs
Which doctor
Neurologist or neurosurgeon, family doctor first
Common in
Rare; found in adults and sometimes in children
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; it needs a doctor's checks and follow-up

Common signs

More signs

  • Numbness or tingling in the legs or body
  • Trouble passing urine or stools
  • Symptoms may come and go, or slowly get worse

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Some are linked to other rare conditions in the family
  • Rarely follow an injury or an operation on the spine

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden weakness or loss of feeling in the legs: go to hospital now
  • Sudden severe back pain: go to hospital now
  • Cannot pass urine or stools, or leaking without control: go to hospital now
  • Slowly worsening leg weakness or numbness
  • Numbness in the groin or inner thighs

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
