Long-lasting cough

A cough that keeps going for many weeks and does not clear up by itself

ConditionLungs & breathingChest

What it is

A cough that lasts for weeks is a sign that something keeps irritating the airways. It is worth finding out why.

  • Throat, airways and lungs
  • Can affect any adult
  • Cough for weeks
Which doctor
Family doctor; lung doctor if it carries on
Common in
Smokers, people with allergy, asthma or acid reflux
Contagious
Some causes can be, such as TB or a lingering infection
Course
Depends on the cause; finding the cause is the first step

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Allergy, asthma or a blocked, dripping nose

See a doctor first if

  • Hard to breathe or chest pain: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Coughing up blood
  • Losing weight without trying, or night sweats
  • Fever that keeps coming back
  • A cough that lasts more than a few weeks, or a hoarse voice that will not clear

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Sip warm water through the day
  • Keep the air at home clean and not too dry
  • See a doctor to find the cause; ask before using cough medicine

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection

Questions and answers

What is Long-lasting cough?

A cough that keeps going for many weeks and does not clear up by itself. A cough that lasts for weeks is a sign that something keeps irritating the airways. It is worth finding out why.

What are the common signs of Long-lasting cough?

Dry, tickly cough · Cough with phlegm · Sore, scratchy throat · Hoarse, weak voice

Why does Long-lasting cough happen?

Smoking · Acid coming up from the stomach · Germs (infection)

How can I look after myself with Long-lasting cough?

Stop smoking

Can an AI Body Master session help with Long-lasting cough?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Long-lasting cough and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
