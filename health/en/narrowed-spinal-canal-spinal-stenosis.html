Narrowed spinal canal (spinal stenosis)

Narrowing of the space in the spine that can squeeze the nerves

ConditionBones, joints & spineBones & muscles

What it is

The channel inside the spine that holds the spinal cord and nerves becomes narrower, so the nerves can be squeezed. This can cause back or leg pain, numbness or weakness.

  • Lower back or neck
  • More common with age
  • Leg pain, numbness
Which doctor
Family doctor, spine or bone doctor (orthopaedic), nerve doctor
Common in
Older adults; people with long-term back wear and tear
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes on slowly over time; a doctor can explain what to expect

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Bony growths or thickened ligaments in the spine
  • A bulging disc
  • An old spine injury, or a spine that was narrow from birth

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden weakness in both legs, or loss of control of the bladder or bowel: go to hospital now
  • Numbness around the groin or inner thighs: go to hospital now
  • Leg weakness or numbness that is getting worse
  • Back pain after a fall or injury
  • Back pain with fever or weight loss you cannot explain

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
