Excessive sweating on the forehead

Beads of sweat that keep forming on the forehead and run down the face

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsHead

What it is

The forehead sweats a lot, with beads of sweat forming and sometimes running into the eyes. It can happen with heat, spicy food or worry, or for no clear reason.

  • Forehead and face
  • In heat or with spicy food
  • Beads of sweat running down
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor (dermatologist) if it persists
Common in
Anyone; more often in hot weather, with spicy food or at menopause
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes with heat, food and feelings

Common signs

More signs

  • A damp hairline or a wet collar
  • Red, flushed skin on the face, in some people
  • Sweating on the scalp, cheeks, nose or upper lip as well
  • Make-up, sunscreen or glasses sliding off the face

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Hot, humid weather, hot rooms or exercise
  • Hot or spicy food and drinks, caffeine or alcohol
  • Stress, nervousness or embarrassment
  • Sometimes an overactive thyroid, diabetes, an infection with fever, or a medicine

See a doctor first if

  • Sweating with chest pain or pressure, pain in the jaw or arm, or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Cold sweat with fainting, confusion or feeling very weak: go to hospital now
  • Sweating with a high fever, a stiff neck or a very bad headache: go to hospital now
  • Soaking night sweats or weight loss without trying
  • A racing heart, shaking, always feeling hot, or a lump in the neck along with the sweating
  • Sweating that started after a new medicine: ask your doctor or pharmacist

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep cool with a fan or air-conditioning, and carry a small towel or tissues
  • Slow, calm breathing may help when worry brings on the sweating
