Hypertrophic pharyngitis (thickened throat lining)

Long-lasting throat irritation where the throat lining becomes thick and bumpy

ConditionEars, nose & throatNeck

What it is

The lining at the back of the throat is irritated for a long time and thickens, so the throat often feels dry, sore or as if something is stuck.

  • Back of the throat
  • Smokers and talkers
  • Dry, scratchy throat
Which doctor
Family doctor, ear-nose-throat doctor
Common in
Smokers, people in dusty air, heavy voice users, and those with reflux
Contagious
No
Course
Often lasts a long time and may come and go, especially if the cause continues

Common signs

Why it happens

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing or noisy breathing: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Trouble swallowing, or drooling because you cannot swallow
  • Coughing up blood, or a lump in the neck
  • Hoarse voice or sore throat lasting more than a few weeks, or weight loss without trying

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Questions and answers

What is Hypertrophic pharyngitis (thickened throat lining)?

Long-lasting throat irritation where the throat lining becomes thick and bumpy. The lining at the back of the throat is irritated for a long time and thickens, so the throat often feels dry, sore or as if something is stuck.

What are the common signs of Hypertrophic pharyngitis (thickened throat lining)?

Sore, scratchy throat · Hoarse, weak voice · Dry, tickly cough

Why does Hypertrophic pharyngitis (thickened throat lining) happen?

Smoking · Germs (infection) · Acid coming up from the stomach · Dry air and air-conditioning

How can I look after myself with Hypertrophic pharyngitis (thickened throat lining)?

Stop smoking · Drink enough water · Ask your pharmacist
