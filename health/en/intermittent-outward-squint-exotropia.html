Intermittent outward squint (exotropia)

An eye that drifts outwards at times, often when tired or looking far away

ConditionEyesHead

What it is

One eye sometimes turns outwards while the other looks straight ahead. It often shows when a person is tired, daydreaming or looking into the distance, and it comes and goes.

  • Eye muscles and eye alignment
  • Often starts in childhood
  • One eye drifts out at times
Which doctor
Eye doctor (ophthalmologist); for a child, a children's eye doctor
Common in
Children, but it can also be found in adults
Contagious
No
Course
Can come and go for years; an eye doctor can explain how it is followed up

Common signs

More signs

  • One eye turns outwards at times, mostly when tired or looking far
  • Closing or squinting one eye in bright sunlight
  • The eyes look straight at other times

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Weak balance between the muscles that move the eyes
  • Needing glasses, or a difference in sight between the two eyes

See a doctor first if

  • A sudden new squint or double vision in an adult, especially with headache or weakness: go to hospital now
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or trouble speaking together with eye changes: call 999
  • A child whose eye turns out more and more often, or who seems to see poorly
  • Eye strain or headache that keeps coming back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Have your child's eyes checked early; do not wait for it to pass
  • Wear glasses as the eye doctor advises
  • Take regular rests during reading and screen time
  • Get enough sleep, as tiredness can make it show more

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Epilepsy or fits

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
