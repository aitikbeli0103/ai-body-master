Essential tremor

Shaking of the hands, head or voice that is often worse when you use them

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

A common movement condition that causes a steady shake, mostly in the hands. It is usually not dangerous but can make eating, writing or holding a cup hard.

  • Hands, sometimes head
  • More common in later life
  • Shaky when using hands
Which doctor
Family doctor, brain and nerve doctor (neurologist)
Common in
Adults, more in older people; often runs in families
Contagious
No
Course
Usually lasts for life and may slowly increase; doctors can suggest ways to manage it

Common signs

More signs

  • A shaky voice or a nodding head
  • Shaking that is worse with stress, tiredness or caffeine
  • The shaking is usually less when the hands are resting

Why it happens

Other causes

  • It often runs in families

See a doctor first if

  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble that comes on suddenly: call 999
  • A shake that starts suddenly or gets worse quickly
  • Shaking with stiffness, slow movement or trouble walking
  • Shaking after starting a new medicine: ask your doctor
  • Shaking that stops you eating, writing or working

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Use a heavier cup or pen, or a cup with two handles
  • Ask your doctor before drinking alcohol to calm the shake

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Essential tremor?

Shaking of the hands, head or voice that is often worse when you use them. A common movement condition that causes a steady shake, mostly in the hands. It is usually not dangerous but can make eating, writing or holding a cup hard.

What are the common signs of Essential tremor?

Shaking hands

Why does Essential tremor happen?

The cause is not fully known · Stress · A side effect of some medicines

How can I look after myself with Essential tremor?

Cut down on coffee and tea · Get good sleep · Rest the sore joint
