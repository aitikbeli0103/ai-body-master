Broken upper-arm bone

A break in the long bone of the upper arm between the shoulder and the elbow

ConditionShoulder, arm & handArms & legs

What it is

The humerus is the long bone of the upper arm. A fall, a hard blow or an accident can break it, causing pain, swelling and an upper arm that looks bent and cannot be used.

  • Upper arm
  • After a fall or a hard blow
  • Pain, swelling, weak arm
Which doctor
Bone doctor (orthopaedic), emergency department
Common in
Older adults after falls; younger people after accidents
Contagious
No
Course
Often mends in weeks to months; a doctor will explain what to expect

Common signs

More signs

  • An upper arm that looks bent, short or out of shape

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Weak bones (osteoporosis) in older adults, so a small fall can be enough
  • Sometimes a bone weakened by another illness (less common)

See a doctor first if

  • A bent or deformed upper arm after a fall or accident: go to hospital
  • A hand that is numb, pale, cold or blue: go to hospital now
  • Not able to lift the wrist or fingers, which may mean a nerve is hurt
  • Bone showing, or bleeding that will not stop: call 999
  • Chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting or a head knock after the injury: call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep the arm still and supported against your body, for example in a sling
  • Do not try to straighten the arm or push the bone back
  • Take off rings and watches early, before swelling starts

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Recent shoulder injury or surgery
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
