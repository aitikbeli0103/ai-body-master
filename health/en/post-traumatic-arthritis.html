Post-traumatic arthritis

Joint wear and pain that develops after an earlier joint injury

ConditionBones, joints & spineBones & muscles

What it is

After a joint is hurt, for example by a fracture or a torn ligament, the smooth cartilage inside can wear down over time. The joint may then become painful, stiff and swollen.

  • Any joint that was hurt
  • After an old injury
  • Aching, stiff, swollen joint
Which doctor
Family doctor, orthopaedic doctor, rheumatologist, physiotherapist
Common in
People who once had a joint fracture, ligament tear or hard knock
Contagious
No
Course
Usually slow over years; care can help you stay active

Common signs

More signs

  • Stiffness, especially after rest or in the morning
  • Swelling that comes and goes
  • A grinding or clicking feeling when the joint moves

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A past fracture that reached into the joint
  • A torn ligament or damaged cartilage
  • A joint that came out of place (dislocation)
  • Often it appears years after the injury

See a doctor first if

  • A joint that is suddenly very painful, hot, red and swollen, with fever: go to hospital
  • A joint that locks, gives way, or cannot take weight after a new fall
  • A bent or deformed limb, or pale, cold or numb skin below the joint
  • Pain that wakes you at night or keeps getting worse
  • Pain that stops you doing daily tasks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Stay gently active; walking and swimming are kind to joints
  • Rest when the joint is sore, then start again slowly

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Hot, red or swollen shoulder
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Post-traumatic arthritis?

Joint wear and pain that develops after an earlier joint injury. After a joint is hurt, for example by a fracture or a torn ligament, the smooth cartilage inside can wear down over time. The joint may then become painful, stiff and swollen.

What are the common signs of Post-traumatic arthritis?

Aching, swollen joints · Less movement in the joint

Why does Post-traumatic arthritis happen?

Extra body weight

How can I look after myself with Post-traumatic arthritis?

Keep a healthy weight · Wear comfortable, supportive shoes · Ask your pharmacist
