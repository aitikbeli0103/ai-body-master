Acute middle ear infection (otitis media)

An infection of the space behind the eardrum

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

Germs get into the space behind the eardrum, often after a cold. It causes ear pain and sometimes fever, and is very common in young children.

  • Middle part of the ear
  • Common in young children
  • Ear pain, muffled hearing
Which doctor
Family doctor, paediatrician or ear, nose and throat doctor
Common in
Babies and young children; also adults after a cold
Contagious
No, but the cold that started it can spread
Course
Often improves within a few days, but a doctor should check it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A cold or sore throat that spreads to the ear
  • A blocked tube between the ear and the throat
  • Allergies or a blocked nose

See a doctor first if

  • A baby or young child with ear pain and a fever: see a doctor promptly
  • Swelling, redness or pain behind the ear: go to hospital
  • A stiff neck, strong headache or confusion: call 999
  • Pus or blood from the ear, or sudden hearing loss
  • Pain that has not improved after two to three days

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

What are the common signs of Acute middle ear infection (otitis media)?

Fever, feeling hot · Hard to hear people · Ringing in the ears · Room feels like spinning

Why does Acute middle ear infection (otitis media) happen?

Smoke, haze and strong smells

How can I look after myself with Acute middle ear infection (otitis media)?
