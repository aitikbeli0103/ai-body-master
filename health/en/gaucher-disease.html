Gaucher disease

A rare inherited condition where a missing enzyme lets fatty substances build up in the spleen, liver and bones

ConditionBloodBlood

What it is

A missing enzyme lets fatty substances build up in cells. The spleen and liver can grow bigger, and blood and bones can be affected.

  • Spleen, liver and bones
  • Inherited, rare
  • Tired, bone pain
Which doctor
Specialist in inherited conditions, blood doctor
Common in
Born with it; signs may start in childhood or later
Contagious
No, it is inherited
Course
Lifelong; a specialist team plans care and follow-up

Common signs

More signs

  • Slow growth in children

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The changed gene makes too little of a certain enzyme
  • Parents are often carriers without any signs
  • Nothing a person did causes it

See a doctor first if

  • Bleeding that will not stop: go to hospital now
  • Sudden severe belly pain, especially on the left side
  • Sudden bone pain with fever, or a bone that may be broken
  • Trouble breathing: call 999
  • A child who is not growing well or is very unwell: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask your specialist about the treatment plan; never stop any medicine on your own
  • Stay gently active and eat a balanced diet
  • Families may ask about genetic counselling

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pregnancy
  • A kidney or liver condition

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
