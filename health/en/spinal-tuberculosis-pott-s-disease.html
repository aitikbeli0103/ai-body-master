Spinal tuberculosis (Pott's disease)

TB germ infection of the spine bones that can cause back pain and a bent back

ConditionInfectionsBack

What it is

The germ that causes TB, usually spread from the lungs, settles in the bones of the spine and slowly causes pain and weakness of the back.

  • Bones of the spine
  • Slow, over weeks to months
  • Ongoing back pain
Which doctor
Family doctor, then TB or infection doctor or spine specialist
Common in
People with TB elsewhere in the body, weak immunity or crowded living
Contagious
Usually not by itself, but TB in the lungs can spread by coughing
Course
Needs a long course of medicine from a doctor; follow-up is important

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The TB germ travels from the lungs or other parts through the blood to the spine

See a doctor first if

  • New weakness or numbness in the legs, or trouble walking: go to hospital now
  • Cannot control urine or stools: go to hospital now
  • Back pain with fever, night sweats or weight loss
  • Back pain that wakes you at night or keeps getting worse
  • A new hump or bend in the spine

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Spinal tuberculosis (Pott's disease)?

TB germ infection of the spine bones that can cause back pain and a bent back. The germ that causes TB, usually spread from the lungs, settles in the bones of the spine and slowly causes pain and weakness of the back.

What are the common signs of Spinal tuberculosis (Pott's disease)?

Lower back pain · Hard to bend or lift · Fever, feeling hot · Soaking sweats at night · Losing weight without trying · Tired all the time

Why does Spinal tuberculosis (Pott's disease) happen?

A weak immune system · Close contact or sharing items · Crowded places and droplets

How can I look after myself with Spinal tuberculosis (Pott's disease)?

Take medicine as your doctor says · See your doctor for check-ups
