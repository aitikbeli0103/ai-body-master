Impetigo

A skin infection that causes red sores with honey-coloured crusts, mostly in children

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Impetigo is a common skin infection caused by germs (bacteria). It makes red sores that burst and form golden-yellow crusts, often around the nose and mouth.

  • Skin, often face and hands
  • Mostly young children
  • Itchy sores with crusts
Which doctor
Family doctor or children's doctor; skin doctor if it keeps returning
Common in
Young children, especially in warm, humid weather; also adults with broken skin
Contagious
Yes, it spreads by touching the sores or sharing towels and clothes
Course
Often improves within a few weeks with a doctor's advice; it can return

Common signs

More signs

  • Sores with honey-coloured or yellow-brown crusts
  • Often around the nose and mouth, but also on the hands, arms and legs
  • Sometimes swollen glands near the sores

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Cuts, scratches, insect bites, eczema or other broken skin let germs in
  • Scratching itchy skin can spread it to other parts of the body

See a doctor first if

  • Very unwell with a high fever, drowsiness or confusion: go to hospital now
  • A baby or young child with sores: see a doctor promptly, especially a newborn
  • Fever, or redness around the sores that spreads, feels hot or becomes more painful
  • Puffy face or ankles, or dark, tea-coloured urine after a skin infection
  • Sores that do not improve after a few days of the care your doctor advised
  • Diabetes or a weak immune system: see a doctor early, even for a few sores

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
