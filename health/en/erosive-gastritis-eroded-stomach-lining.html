Erosive gastritis (eroded stomach lining)

Inflamed stomach lining with small raw patches (erosions) that can cause upper belly pain

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

The lining that protects the stomach is irritated and has small worn spots, so stomach acid can sting it and cause burning pain, nausea or bloating.

  • Lining of the stomach
  • Mostly adults
  • Burning pain in the upper belly
Which doctor
Family doctor; a stomach and gut specialist (gastroenterologist)
Common in
Adults who drink alcohol or smoke, or often take some pain-killers
Contagious
Some types can be: the stomach germ H. pylori can pass between people
Course
Often eases once the cause is found and avoided; it can come back

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Drinking a lot of alcohol, or smoking
  • Severe illness, injury or major surgery that puts the body under strain
  • Bile washing back into the stomach, or sometimes no clear cause

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood or brown 'coffee-ground' vomit, or black sticky stools: go to hospital now
  • Sudden, severe belly pain or a hard, board-like belly: call 999
  • Upper belly or chest pain with sweating, trouble breathing, or pain in the arm or jaw: call 999
  • Feeling faint, very pale, cold and clammy, or a racing heart: go to hospital now
  • Vomiting that does not stop, trouble swallowing, or losing weight without trying
  • Pain that keeps coming back or lasts more than two weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Cut down on alcohol and stop smoking; avoid very spicy, sour or oily food
  • Eat slowly and chew well; avoid lying down right after eating
  • Make time to relax and sleep well, as stress can make the stomach feel worse

Tell your therapist

  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
