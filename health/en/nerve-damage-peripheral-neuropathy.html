Nerve damage (peripheral neuropathy)

Damage to the nerves outside the brain and spinal cord, often in the hands and feet

ConditionBrain & nervesWhole body

What it is

The nerves that carry feeling and movement signals to the hands, feet and body are damaged, so you may feel tingling, numbness, burning or weakness.

  • Hands and feet
  • Often linked to diabetes
  • Tingling, numb or burning
Which doctor
Family doctor, nerve specialist (neurologist)
Common in
People with diabetes, older adults, heavy drinkers
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; a doctor can explain what to expect

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Low vitamin levels, kidney or thyroid problems

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden weakness, face drooping or trouble speaking: call 999
  • Weakness that spreads quickly over days, or trouble breathing or swallowing: go to hospital now
  • A foot sore, cut or blister that does not heal
  • Falls, or the feet or hands losing feeling
  • Numbness or burning that keeps getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Check your feet every day for cuts, blisters or redness
  • Wear soft, well-fitting shoes and do not walk barefoot
  • Keep blood sugar well controlled if you have diabetes
  • Test bath water with your elbow, not your hands or feet

Tell your therapist

  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Broken, red or sore skin

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Nerve damage (peripheral neuropathy)?

Damage to the nerves outside the brain and spinal cord, often in the hands and feet. The nerves that carry feeling and movement signals to the hands, feet and body are damaged, so you may feel tingling, numbness, burning or weakness.

What are the common signs of Nerve damage (peripheral neuropathy)?

Numb, tingling feet · Tingling in the hand · Legs feel weak · Unsteady when walking

Why does Nerve damage (peripheral neuropathy) happen?

High blood sugar or blood pressure · Drinking alcohol · Germs (infection) · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Nerve damage (peripheral neuropathy)?

Cut down on alcohol
