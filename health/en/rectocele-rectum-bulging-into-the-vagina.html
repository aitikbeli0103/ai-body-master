Rectocele (rectum bulging into the vagina)

The rectum bulges forward into the back wall of the vagina

ConditionStomach & intestinesPelvis

What it is

The wall between the rectum and the vagina becomes weak, so the rectum pushes forward into the vagina. It can make passing stool feel hard or incomplete.

  • Lower belly and vagina
  • Women, often after childbirth
  • A bulge or hard to empty bowel
Which doctor
Gynaecologist, colorectal surgeon, family doctor
Common in
Women who have had children; older women
Contagious
No
Course
Often slowly gets worse over time; mild cases may cause few problems

Common signs

More signs

  • A bulge or pressure felt in the vagina
  • Pressing on the vagina wall with a finger to help stool out

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Childbirth, especially large babies or several births
  • Previous pelvic surgery

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass stool or wind, with a swollen belly or vomiting: go to hospital now
  • Heavy bleeding from the vagina or anus
  • Blood in the stool, or a change in bowel habit that lasts weeks
  • Cannot pass urine, or leaking urine or stool
  • Severe pain, or fever with pain in the lower belly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Eat plenty of fibre and drink enough water to keep stools soft
  • Do not strain; give yourself time on the toilet
  • Ask your doctor about pelvic floor exercises
  • Tell your doctor about any worry, even if it feels awkward
