Chronic fatigue

Deep tiredness that lasts for months and does not get better with rest

ConditionWhole body & generalWhole body

What it is

Chronic fatigue is a deep tiredness that lasts a long time and is not fixed by rest. It can make daily tasks hard, and a doctor should look for other causes.

  • Whole body
  • Adults, often long-term
  • Very tired, even after rest
Which doctor
Family doctor; sometimes a specialist
Common in
Adults, more often middle-aged, and women
Contagious
No
Course
Can last months or longer; many people slowly improve with support

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • It may start after a viral illness or a long period of stress
  • Other illnesses such as low blood count or thyroid problems can feel the same, so a check-up matters

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, trouble breathing or fainting: call 999
  • Weight loss you cannot explain, night sweats or fever that keeps coming back
  • Tiredness that came on suddenly or keeps getting worse
  • Feeling very low or hopeless, or thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999
  • Tiredness so strong that you cannot work or look after yourself

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep a regular time to sleep and wake up
  • Pace yourself: do a little, then rest, and avoid pushing too hard on good days
  • Try gentle activity such as short walks, as your doctor agrees
  • Talk to someone you trust about how you feel

Tell your therapist

  • High blood pressure

  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine

What are the common signs of Chronic fatigue?

Tired all the time · Sleepy in the daytime · Foggy, cannot focus · Aching all over · Forgetting things · Cannot fall asleep

Why does Chronic fatigue happen?

The cause is not fully known · Too little sleep

How can I look after myself with Chronic fatigue?

Eat healthy, balanced meals
