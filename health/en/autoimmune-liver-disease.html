Autoimmune liver disease

A group of long-term liver conditions where the body's defences attack the liver or bile tubes

ConditionLiver, gallbladder & pancreasAbdomen

What it is

In these conditions the body's own defence system damages the liver cells or the small tubes that carry bile. They develop slowly and need long-term follow-up.

  • The liver and bile tubes
  • Often in middle age
  • Tired, itchy skin
Which doctor
Liver doctor (hepatologist) or stomach and liver doctor
Common in
Adults, more often women; may go with other autoimmune conditions
Contagious
No
Course
Long-term and usually slow; regular check-ups help the doctor guide care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The defence system attacks the liver or bile tubes by mistake
  • It may run in families or go with other autoimmune conditions
  • An infection or something in the surroundings may set it off in some people

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood, black stools or confusion: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Yellow skin or eyes that get worse
  • Fever with shaking chills or new belly pain
  • A swollen belly or swollen legs
  • Very bad itching that stops you sleeping

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
