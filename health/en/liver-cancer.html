Liver cancer

A cancer that starts in the cells of the liver, in the upper right belly

ConditionCancer & tumoursAbdomen

What it is

Liver cancer starts when cells in the liver grow out of control. It often develops in a liver already damaged by long-term disease, and may cause no signs early on.

  • Liver, upper right belly
  • Mostly adults
  • Often no signs at first
Which doctor
Liver doctor (hepatologist), cancer doctor (oncologist) or surgeon
Common in
Adults with long-term liver disease, hepatitis B or C, or cirrhosis
Contagious
No, but hepatitis B and C viruses, a cause of liver cancer, can spread
Course
Depends on size, stage and how well the liver works; your team can explain

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-term hepatitis B or hepatitis C infection
  • Cirrhosis (scarring of the liver) from any cause
  • Fatty liver, linked to being overweight or having diabetes
  • Food spoiled by mould (aflatoxin) and smoking; sometimes no clear cause is found

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood, or black or bloody stools: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Confusion, or being very sleepy and hard to wake: go to hospital now
  • Sudden severe belly pain, or a swollen belly with fever: go to hospital now
  • Yellow skin or eyes, dark urine or pale stools: see a doctor soon
  • Pain or a lump under the right ribs, or a swollen belly or legs
  • Losing weight without trying, no appetite or feeling very tired

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Liver cancer?

A cancer that starts in the cells of the liver, in the upper right belly. Liver cancer starts when cells in the liver grow out of control. It often develops in a liver already damaged by long-term disease, and may cause no signs early on.

What are the common signs of Liver cancer?

Pain in the upper belly · No appetite · Losing weight without trying · Feeling sick, nausea · Yellow skin and eyes · Tired all the time

Why does Liver cancer happen?

Drinking alcohol

How can I look after myself with Liver cancer?

Follow your care team's plan · Cut down on alcohol · Ask your pharmacist · Keep your regular tests
