Tonsil stones (tonsilloliths)

Small white or yellow hard lumps that form in the pits of the tonsils

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

Bits of food, mucus and dead cells can get stuck in the small pits of the tonsils and harden into small stones. They are usually harmless but can smell bad.

  • Pits of the tonsils
  • Teens and adults
  • Bad breath, stuck feeling
Which doctor
Family doctor, dentist, or ear-nose-throat doctor
Common in
Teens and adults, especially after repeated throat infections
Contagious
No
Course
They may come and go; often harmless but can return

Common signs

More signs

  • Small white or yellow spots you can see on the tonsils
  • A feeling that something is stuck at the back of the throat

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Food, mucus and dead cells collect in the pits of the tonsils
  • Tonsils with deep pits or that were often infected

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing or swallowing, or drooling: go to hospital now
  • Fever with a very sore throat or one-sided throat swelling
  • Bleeding from the tonsil or coughing up blood
  • One tonsil much bigger than the other
  • Stones that keep coming back or bad breath that will not go away

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Brush your teeth and tongue gently, and clean between teeth
  • Gargle with warm water, especially after meals
  • Do not dig at the stones with sharp tools or fingers

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pregnancy
