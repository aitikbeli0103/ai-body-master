Cavitary lung TB (tuberculosis)

Lung TB where the germ has made hollow spaces (cavities) in the lung

ConditionInfectionsChest

What it is

In this form of lung TB, the germ damages part of the lung and leaves hollow spaces. It often causes a long cough with phlegm and can spread to others easily.

  • The lungs
  • Can spread to others
  • Long cough with phlegm
Which doctor
Chest doctor (respiratory), infection doctor
Common in
People near someone with active TB; weak defences; diabetes
Contagious
Yes, through coughing; see a doctor soon
Course
Needs months of medicine and check-ups from a doctor

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Breathing in the TB germ from a person with active TB
  • Lung TB that was not found or not treated for a long time
  • Weak body defences, diabetes or poor nutrition
  • Smoking and heavy drinking

See a doctor first if

  • Coughing up a lot of blood or any blood: go to hospital now
  • Severe trouble breathing, blue lips or chest pain: call 999
  • A cough lasting more than two or three weeks
  • Fever, night sweats and weight loss together
  • A child or older person with a long cough: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Take all your medicine every day as your doctor says, even when you feel better
  • Cover your mouth when coughing and wear a mask near others
  • Keep rooms well aired and sleep in a separate room if your doctor advises
  • Eat well, rest and do not smoke
  • Ask people you live with to get checked

  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin

Questions and answers

What is Cavitary lung TB (tuberculosis)?

Lung TB where the germ has made hollow spaces (cavities) in the lung. In this form of lung TB, the germ damages part of the lung and leaves hollow spaces. It often causes a long cough with phlegm and can spread to others easily.

What are the common signs of Cavitary lung TB (tuberculosis)?

Cough with phlegm · Fever, feeling hot · Soaking sweats at night · Losing weight without trying · Tired all the time · Sharp pain when breathing in

Why does Cavitary lung TB (tuberculosis) happen?

Breathing in the TB germ from a person with active TB · Lung TB that was not found or not treated for a long time · Weak body defences, diabetes or poor nutrition · Smoking and heavy drinking

How can I look after myself with Cavitary lung TB (tuberculosis)?
