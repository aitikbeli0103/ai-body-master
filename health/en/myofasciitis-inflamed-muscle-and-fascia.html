Myofasciitis (inflamed muscle and fascia)

Sore, tender muscles and the thin sheet around them, often with tight spots

ConditionBones, joints & spineBones & muscles

What it is

The muscles and the thin layer wrapped around them (the fascia) become sore and tight, often in the neck, shoulders or back. It is often linked to strain or poor posture.

  • Neck, shoulders, back
  • Adults, desk and manual work
  • Tender, tight muscle spots
Which doctor
Family doctor, physiotherapist or rehabilitation doctor
Common in
Adults who sit long, repeat movements or carry heavy loads
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes over weeks to months; many people improve with care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Straining the muscles by repeating the same movement
  • Sitting or standing in a poor posture for long hours
  • Cold air or sleeping in an awkward position

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, trouble breathing or pain spreading to the jaw or arm: call 999
  • Pain with fever, a hot red swollen area, or after a fall or accident
  • Weakness or numbness in an arm or leg
  • Severe pain that wakes you at night, or pain with weight loss
  • Pain that lasts for weeks without getting better

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do gentle stretches and light walking
  • Set up your chair and screen at a comfortable height

Tell your therapist

  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Recent back injury or surgery

  • Pregnancy

Questions and answers

What is Myofasciitis (inflamed muscle and fascia)?

Sore, tender muscles and the thin sheet around them, often with tight spots. The muscles and the thin layer wrapped around them (the fascia) become sore and tight, often in the neck, shoulders or back. It is often linked to strain or poor posture.

What are the common signs of Myofasciitis (inflamed muscle and fascia)?

Stiff, aching neck · Pain in the shoulder · Ache between the shoulder blades · Lower back pain · Aching all over

Why does Myofasciitis (inflamed muscle and fascia) happen?

Stress · An injury or fall

How can I look after myself with Myofasciitis (inflamed muscle and fascia)?

Take breaks from sitting · A warm cloth on the sore area · Get good sleep
