Gallbladder inflammation

Sudden swelling and irritation of the gallbladder, usually from a gallstone

ConditionLiver, gallbladder & pancreasAbdomen

What it is

A gallstone often blocks the exit of the gallbladder, which then swells and becomes inflamed. It causes pain in the upper right belly and needs prompt medical care.

  • Gallbladder, upper right belly
  • Adults, more often women
  • Strong pain and fever
Which doctor
Family doctor today; hospital or surgeon
Common in
Adults with gallstones
Contagious
No
Course
Needs a doctor; it can become serious if left alone

Common signs

Why it happens

  • A gallstone blocking the gallbladder outlet
  • Thick bile (sludge) building up
  • Infection in the bile
  • Being very ill, for example in hospital

See a doctor first if

  • Severe pain in the upper right belly that lasts for hours: go to hospital
  • Fever with chills
  • Confusion, fainting or a very fast heartbeat: call 999
  • Yellow skin or eyes
  • Vomiting that will not stop

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Avoid heavy or fatty meals while you are in pain
  • Take small sips of fluids and avoid alcohol
  • Follow your doctor's plan and go to follow-up visits

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A heart condition

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
