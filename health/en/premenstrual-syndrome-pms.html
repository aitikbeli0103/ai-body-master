Premenstrual syndrome (PMS)

Mood and body changes that come in the days before a period

ConditionWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

Some women feel moody, bloated, tired or sore in the week or so before the period. It usually eases once the period starts.

  • Whole body, before a period
  • Women who have periods
  • Mood swings and bloating
Which doctor
Family doctor, gynaecologist (women's doctor)
Common in
Women in their childbearing years
Contagious
No
Course
Comes and goes with the monthly cycle; the strength differs from month to month

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Stress, poor sleep and little exercise may make it feel worse
  • Family members with the same problem

See a doctor first if

  • Thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999
  • Mood changes so strong that work, family or daily life is affected
  • Very heavy bleeding or severe pain with your periods
  • Symptoms that stay after the period ends
  • Symptoms that start for the first time after age 40 or suddenly change

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep regular sleep times and gentle exercise such as walking
  • Note your symptoms on a calendar to see the pattern
  • Plan lighter days before the period and talk to people you trust
  • For pain medicine or supplements, ask your doctor or pharmacist

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Premenstrual syndrome (PMS)?

Mood and body changes that come in the days before a period. Some women feel moody, bloated, tired or sore in the week or so before the period. It usually eases once the period starts.

What are the common signs of Premenstrual syndrome (PMS)?

Mood swings before the period · Bloated, gassy belly · Short-tempered, on edge · Tired all the time · Headache across the forehead

Why does Premenstrual syndrome (PMS) happen?

Hormone changes · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Premenstrual syndrome (PMS)?

Cut down on coffee and tea

Can an AI Body Master session help with Premenstrual syndrome (PMS)?
