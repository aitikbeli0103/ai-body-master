Inflamed bone lining (periostitis)

Pain and soreness in the thin layer that covers a bone

ConditionBones, joints & spineBones & muscles

What it is

The thin lining around a bone becomes irritated, often after too much strain, so the bone feels sore and tender to touch.

  • Along a bone, often the shin
  • After running or hard activity
  • Sore, tender bone
Which doctor
Family doctor, bone doctor (orthopaedic) or sports doctor
Common in
Runners, dancers, active people; anyone who suddenly does more
Contagious
No
Course
Often eases with rest and a slow return to activity, but can come back

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain when you press on the bone
  • Pain that is worse during or just after activity
  • Mild swelling over the sore spot

Why it happens

  • Doing too much, too fast, such as a sudden jump in walking or running
  • Hard ground or worn-out, unsupportive shoes
  • Flat feet or tight calf muscles
  • Sometimes an injury or, less often, an infection of the bone

See a doctor first if

  • A hot, red, very painful or swollen bone with fever: go to hospital now
  • A bent or deformed limb, or you cannot put weight on it after a fall
  • A foot or leg that turns numb, pale or cold
  • Pain at rest or at night, or pain that keeps getting worse
  • Pain that does not ease after a few weeks of rest

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Rest from the activity that hurts, and choose gentle options like swimming
  • Go back to activity slowly, adding a little each week
  • Stretch the calves gently and avoid hard ground for a while

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
