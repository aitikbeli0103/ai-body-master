Pica (eating non-food items)

A long-lasting urge to eat things that are not food, like soil, chalk or ice

ConditionEating & drinkingWhole body

What it is

A person keeps eating things that are not food, such as soil, chalk, paper or lots of ice. It can be harmful, and there is often a reason behind it.

  • Whole body
  • Children, pregnancy, anyone
  • Craving non-food items
Which doctor
Family doctor; a paediatrician for a child
Common in
Young children, pregnant women, some with other health needs
Contagious
No
Course
Often settles once the cause is found; a doctor can guide you

Common signs

More signs

  • Eating non-food items for more than a month
  • Strong cravings for ice, soil, chalk, starch or paper

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Low iron or other low minerals; this is common in pregnancy
  • In young children it can be part of how they explore
  • Some developmental or mental health conditions

See a doctor first if

  • Swallowed something sharp, a battery, a magnet or a poison: go to hospital now
  • Severe belly pain, repeated vomiting or a swollen belly
  • Not able to pass stool, or black or bloody stools
  • A child who chews paint, old pipes or soil: see a doctor promptly about lead and other poisons
  • Pregnancy with this habit: tell your doctor early

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Tell your doctor honestly; there is nothing to be ashamed of
  • Keep harmful items out of reach, especially from young children
  • Be kind, not angry, with a child; gently offer a safe distraction
