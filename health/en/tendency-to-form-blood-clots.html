Tendency to form blood clots

A tendency for the blood to form clots more easily than usual

ConditionBloodBlood

What it is

Some people are born with, or develop, changes in the blood that make clots form more easily. Many never have a clot, but the risk is higher.

  • Blood and blood vessels
  • Often runs in families
  • May show as a clot
Which doctor
Blood doctor (haematologist), family doctor
Common in
People with a family history of clots; some women in pregnancy
Contagious
No
Course
Usually lifelong; a doctor can explain how to lower the risk

Common signs

More signs

  • Sudden breathlessness or chest pain
  • More than one clot, or a clot at a young age

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Some immune conditions can make blood clot more easily
  • Smoking, being overweight and some hormone medicines add to the risk

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden chest pain or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • A swollen, painful, warm leg or arm, especially on one side: go to hospital now
  • Coughing blood or sudden severe belly pain
  • Pregnant or planning a pregnancy: tell your doctor early

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
