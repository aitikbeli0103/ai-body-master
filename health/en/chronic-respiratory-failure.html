Chronic respiratory failure

When the lungs cannot bring in enough oxygen or clear carbon dioxide over a long time

ConditionLungs & breathingChest

What it is

In chronic respiratory failure the lungs, over weeks to months, cannot take in enough oxygen or clear enough carbon dioxide. It needs regular care from a doctor.

  • Lungs and breathing
  • Long-term lung disease
  • Breathless, tired, drowsy
Which doctor
Chest doctor (respiratory physician)
Common in
People with long-term lung disease such as COPD, and some nerve or muscle diseases
Contagious
No
Course
Long-term; needs regular care and watching

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-term lung disease such as COPD, scarring of the lungs or severe asthma
  • Weak breathing muscles or nerve disease

See a doctor first if

  • Severe breathlessness, blue lips or confusion: call 999
  • Very sleepy, hard to wake, or morning headaches
  • Fever with more or coloured phlegm, which may be an infection
  • Swollen legs or quick weight gain
  • Breathing that is slowly getting worse over days

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Chronic respiratory failure?

When the lungs cannot bring in enough oxygen or clear carbon dioxide over a long time. In chronic respiratory failure the lungs, over weeks to months, cannot take in enough oxygen or clear enough carbon dioxide. It needs regular care from a doctor.

What are the common signs of Chronic respiratory failure?

Short of breath on stairs · Tired all the time · Sleepy in the daytime · Cough with phlegm · Wheezing breath · Feeling faint

Why does Chronic respiratory failure happen?

Smoking · Extra body weight

How can I look after myself with Chronic respiratory failure?

Stop smoking · Get the recommended vaccines
