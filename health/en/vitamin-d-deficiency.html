Vitamin D deficiency

Not enough vitamin D, which can weaken the bones and muscles

ConditionEating & drinkingWhole body

What it is

Vitamin D helps the body take in calcium for strong bones. Low levels are common and may cause tiredness, bone and muscle aches, or weak bones over time.

  • Bones and muscles
  • Indoor workers, older adults
  • Aches, tiredness, weakness
Which doctor
Family doctor; an endocrinologist if needed
Common in
People who stay indoors or cover up, older adults, babies fed only breast milk
Contagious
No
Course
Often improves with doctor-guided changes; blood tests can check it

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Spending little time in safe daytime sun, even in a sunny country
  • Covering the skin, using sunscreen all the time or having darker skin
  • Getting older, or being overweight
  • Gut, kidney or liver illness, or some medicines
  • A diet with few foods that contain vitamin D

See a doctor first if

  • A fall with bone pain or a limb you cannot use: go to hospital now
  • Muscle spasms or fits, or tingling around the mouth and hands
  • Bone pain that does not go away, or trouble climbing stairs or standing up
  • A baby or child with bowed legs, late walking, or poor growth: see a doctor promptly
  • Pregnancy with bone pain or muscle weakness

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask your doctor about a blood test before taking any supplement
  • Spend a short time outdoors in the early morning or late afternoon, without burning
  • Eat foods such as oily fish, eggs and vitamin D added milk, plus calcium-rich foods

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Vitamin D deficiency?

Not enough vitamin D, which can weaken the bones and muscles. Vitamin D helps the body take in calcium for strong bones. Low levels are common and may cause tiredness, bone and muscle aches, or weak bones over time.

What are the common signs of Vitamin D deficiency?

Tired all the time · Aching bones · Low mood, no interest · Often no signs at first

Why does Vitamin D deficiency happen?

Spending little time in safe daytime sun, even in a sunny country · Covering the skin, using sunscreen all the time or having darker skin · Getting older, or being overweight · Gut, kidney or liver illness, or some medicines · A diet with few foods that contain vitamin D

How can I look after myself with Vitamin D deficiency?

Stay active every day
