Cracked rib

A small crack in a rib bone, usually after a fall, knock or hard cough

ConditionChest & ribsAbdomen

What it is

A rib has a small crack but has not broken apart. It hurts most when you breathe in deeply, cough, laugh or twist the body.

  • Side of the chest
  • After a fall or knock
  • Sharp pain on breathing in
Which doctor
Family doctor; emergency department after a hard knock
Common in
People after a fall, sports knock or accident; older adults with weak bones
Contagious
No
Course
Usually mends slowly over several weeks; pain is often worse at first

Common signs

More signs

  • Sharp pain at one spot on the chest wall
  • Pain when you press on the rib or twist the body
  • Bruising or mild swelling over the rib
  • Taking shallow breaths to avoid the pain

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Very strong or long-lasting coughing
  • Weak bones in older people can crack more easily

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, or feeling more and more short of breath: call 999
  • Chest pain that spreads to the arm, jaw or back, or with sweating: call 999
  • Coughing up blood, or lips turning blue
  • A hard knock to the chest or a road accident: go to hospital for a check
  • Fever with cough and phlegm in the days after the injury
  • Pain in the upper belly or left shoulder tip after a knock

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Take slow, deep breaths often, and cough gently while hugging a pillow to the chest
  • Do not wrap the chest tightly, as it can make breathing shallow
  • For pain medicine, ask your doctor or pharmacist

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • A heart condition

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
