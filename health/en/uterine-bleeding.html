Uterine bleeding

Blood coming from the womb outside the normal period or too heavy

SymptomWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

Bleeding that is heavier, longer or at a different time than your usual period. It has many causes, so it is worth telling a doctor.

  • Womb and lower belly
  • Women of any age
  • Bleeding from the vagina
Which doctor
Gynaecologist (women's doctor), family doctor
Common in
Teens, women near menopause, and women after menopause
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; a doctor can find the reason

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Fibroids, polyps or a thick womb lining
  • Infection, a coil (IUD), or some medicines such as blood thinners
  • Pregnancy problems; less often, cancer, so any bleeding after menopause needs a check

See a doctor first if

  • Soaking a pad in an hour or less, dizziness or fainting: go to hospital now
  • Bleeding or severe belly pain in pregnancy or a possible pregnancy: go to hospital now
  • Any bleeding after menopause
  • Bleeding after sex or between periods that keeps happening
  • Fever, bad-smelling discharge, or feeling very weak and pale

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Rest, and drink enough water
  • Eat regular meals with iron-rich foods such as greens, beans and lean meat
  • Do not use any herbal or hormone product without asking a doctor

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • A heart condition
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
