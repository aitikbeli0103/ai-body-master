Kidney cancer

Cancer that starts in the kidneys, the two organs that clean the blood and make urine

ConditionCancer & tumoursAbdomen

What it is

Cells in a kidney grow out of control and form a tumour; early on there are often no signs, and it is sometimes found by chance on a scan done for another reason.

  • The kidneys, in the back
  • Mostly older adults
  • Often no signs early on
Which doctor
Family doctor first; then a urologist or a cancer specialist (oncologist)
Common in
Mostly older adults; more often in men and in smokers
Contagious
No
Course
Differs from person to person; your care team explains the plan and what to expect

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-term kidney failure, or some inherited conditions
  • Long contact with some chemicals at work, and older age

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, chest pain or coughing up blood: call 999
  • Cannot pass urine, or heavy blood in the urine with clots: go to hospital now
  • Sudden severe pain in the side or back: go to hospital now
  • Blood in the urine, even once or without pain: see a doctor promptly
  • A lump or a lasting ache in the side or lower back
  • Weight loss without trying, new bone pain, or a swollen, painful leg

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Kidney cancer?

Cancer that starts in the kidneys, the two organs that clean the blood and make urine. Cells in a kidney grow out of control and form a tumour; early on there are often no signs, and it is sometimes found by chance on a scan done for another reason.

What are the common signs of Kidney cancer?

Pink or red urine · Pain in the side or loin · Losing weight without trying · Pale, tired look · Often no signs at first

Why does Kidney cancer happen?

The cause is not fully known · Smoking · Extra body weight

How can I look after myself with Kidney cancer?

Follow your care team's plan · Ask your pharmacist · Stop smoking

Can an AI Body Master session help with Kidney cancer?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Kidney cancer and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
