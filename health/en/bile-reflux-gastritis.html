Bile reflux gastritis

Stomach lining irritated by bile (a digestive fluid) that flows back from the small intestine

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

Bile, a digestive fluid made by the liver, flows back into the stomach. It can irritate the stomach lining and cause upper belly pain, nausea and a bitter taste.

  • Stomach and upper belly
  • Often after stomach surgery
  • Burning pain, bitter taste
Which doctor
Family doctor, or a stomach and gut doctor (gastroenterologist)
Common in
People who had stomach surgery; it can also happen without surgery
Contagious
No
Course
Can last a long time and come and go; your doctor can explain how to manage it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Stomach surgery, especially when the valve at the stomach exit was removed or bypassed
  • A valve at the stomach exit that does not work well
  • Gallbladder removal may play a part in some people

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain with sweating, breathlessness, or pain spreading to the arm or jaw: call 999
  • Vomiting blood, or black, tarry stools: go to hospital now
  • Sudden severe belly pain, or a hard, very tender belly: go to hospital now
  • Vomiting that will not stop, or you cannot keep fluids down
  • Trouble swallowing, or losing weight without trying
  • Symptoms that last for weeks, keep coming back or get worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Eat smaller, more frequent meals and avoid very fatty or heavy meals
  • Do not lie down straight after eating; raise the head of your bed a little
  • Note which foods and habits make it worse and tell your doctor

Tell your therapist

  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine
