Nasal polyps

Soft, painless swellings that grow inside the nose or sinuses

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

Soft, round growths form on the lining of the nose or sinuses after long-lasting swelling there. They are not cancer, but they can block the nose and smell.

  • Inside the nose and sinuses
  • Mostly adults
  • Blocked nose, poor smell
Which doctor
ENT doctor, family doctor
Common in
Adults with allergies, asthma or long-term sinus trouble
Contagious
No
Course
Often long-lasting and may come back; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

More signs

  • A heavy feeling in the face or forehead
  • Snoring, or breathing through the mouth

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-lasting swelling of the lining of the nose and sinuses
  • Allergies and asthma

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden change in vision, double vision or swelling around the eye: go to hospital now
  • Fever with a severe headache or stiff neck: go to hospital now
  • Nosebleeds that are heavy or keep coming back
  • A growth on only one side of the nose, or a lump on the face
  • Trouble breathing through the nose that does not get better

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See a doctor for a proper look inside the nose
  • Try to avoid what triggers your allergies, such as dust and smoke
  • Rinse the nose with clean salt water if your doctor agrees

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • High blood pressure
  • Blood-thinning medicine
