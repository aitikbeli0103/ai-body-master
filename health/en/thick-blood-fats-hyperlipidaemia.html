Thick blood fats (hyperlipidaemia)

Too much fat in the blood, such as cholesterol

SymptomHeart & blood vesselsBlood

What it is

There is more fat in the blood than is healthy. It usually causes no signs, but over the years it can narrow blood vessels, so a blood test is the way to know.

  • In the blood
  • More common with age
  • Often no signs
Which doctor
Family doctor; heart or hormone doctor if needed
Common in
Adults, people with diabetes, overweight, or a family history
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long term; food, activity and checks help keep it under watch

Common signs

More signs

  • Yellowish bumps on the eyelids or skin can appear in some people
  • Over years it can lead to chest pain or other heart and vessel problems

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Smoking and too much alcohol

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain or pressure, or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Severe pain in the belly with high blood fats: go to hospital now
  • Leg pain when walking that eases with rest
  • A family history of early heart disease and no check yet

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Have a blood test as your doctor advises
  • Eat more vegetables, fruit and whole grains; less fried and fatty food
  • Be active most days, such as brisk walking

Tell your therapist

  • High blood pressure
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine
