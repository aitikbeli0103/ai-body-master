Chronic sore throat (simple pharyngitis)

Mild, long-lasting throat irritation with dryness, itch and a need to clear the throat

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

This is a milder form of long-lasting throat inflammation. The throat feels dry, itchy or slightly sore, and the lining may look a little red, but it is not a sudden infection.

  • Back of the throat
  • Adults, often smokers
  • Dry, itchy throat
Which doctor
Family doctor; ear, nose and throat doctor (ENT) if it lasts
Common in
Smokers, people in dusty or air-conditioned places, and heavy talkers
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes for weeks or months; usually eases when triggers are avoided

Common signs

More signs

  • A feeling that something is stuck in the throat
  • The need to clear the throat again and again
  • Mild soreness, but usually no high fever

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Too much talking, shouting or singing

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, drooling, or not able to open the mouth: call 999
  • Trouble swallowing, or food getting stuck
  • A lump in the neck, or blood in the spit
  • Losing weight without trying
  • Throat problems or a hoarse voice that last for weeks and do not ease

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Sip water often through the day
  • Keep the air-conditioning gentle and the room not too dry
  • Rest the voice and avoid clearing the throat hard

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • A severe allergy
