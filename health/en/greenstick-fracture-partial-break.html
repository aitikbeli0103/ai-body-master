Greenstick fracture (partial break)

A child's bone that bends and cracks on one side without breaking through

ConditionAny other bone or soft-tissue injuryBones & muscles

What it is

Children's bones are softer and can bend like a young green twig. In a greenstick fracture the bone cracks on one side and bends on the other.

  • Forearm, wrist, lower leg
  • Young children
  • Pain and a bent limb
Which doctor
Children's doctor (paediatrician) or bone doctor; hospital for an X-ray
Common in
Children, often after a fall onto an outstretched hand
Contagious
No
Course
Children's bones often mend well over weeks; a doctor guides the care

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain and tenderness in the arm or leg after a fall
  • Swelling, and sometimes a slight bend in the limb
  • Crying or guarding the arm when it is touched or moved

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Children's bones are softer and bend before they break
  • Accidents in the home, in cars or on bicycles

See a doctor first if

  • A bent limb, bone through the skin, or heavy bleeding: go to hospital now
  • The child will not use the limb or put weight on it: see a doctor promptly
  • Numb, pale, cold or blue fingers or toes
  • Severe pain, heavy swelling, or the child is very unwell or drowsy after a fall
  • A young child with unexplained injuries: speak to a doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Keep the arm or leg still and supported, and see a doctor promptly
  • Do not try to straighten the limb
  • Follow the doctor's advice on the cast and on when the child can play again
  • Keep a cast dry and clean, and ask the doctor about any itching or smell

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Fever or an active infection

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
