Menopausal depression

Low mood that starts or gets worse around menopause

ConditionTold by a doctor: moodHead

What it is

Around menopause, changes in the body, sleep and life can bring low mood, tearfulness or loss of interest that lasts for weeks. It is common, and a doctor can help.

Questions and answers

What is Menopausal depression?

Low mood that starts or gets worse around menopause. Around menopause, changes in the body, sleep and life can bring low mood, tearfulness or loss of interest that lasts for weeks. It is common, and a doctor can help.

What are the common signs of Menopausal depression?

Low mood, no interest · Sudden hot flushes · Cannot fall asleep · Short-tempered, on edge · Worried and on edge

Why does Menopausal depression happen?

Hormone changes · Stress

How can I look after myself with Menopausal depression?

Talk to family and friends · Get good sleep · Stay active every day

Can an AI Body Master session help with Menopausal depression?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Menopausal depression and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
