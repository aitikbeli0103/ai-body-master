Anaemia

Not enough healthy red blood cells or haemoglobin to carry oxygen

ConditionBloodBlood

What it is

The blood does not carry enough oxygen around the body. This can make you tired, pale and short of breath.

  • In the blood
  • Common in women
  • Tired and pale
Which doctor
Family doctor; blood doctor (haematologist) if needed
Common in
Women with heavy periods, pregnant women, children, older adults
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; many types can be managed once found

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Not enough iron in the body
  • Not enough vitamin B12 or folate
  • Blood loss, such as heavy periods or bleeding in the gut

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, severe trouble breathing or fainting: call 999
  • Black or bloody stools, or vomiting blood: go to hospital now
  • Very heavy periods or bleeding that does not stop
  • Feeling very tired, dizzy or breathless with small tasks
  • Anaemia in pregnancy, or in a baby or child: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Anaemia?

Not enough healthy red blood cells or haemoglobin to carry oxygen. The blood does not carry enough oxygen around the body. This can make you tired, pale and short of breath.

What are the common signs of Anaemia?

Tired all the time · Pale, tired look · Short of breath on stairs · Racing, pounding heart · Feeling faint · Cold hands and feet

Why does Anaemia happen?

Other long-term illnesses · Runs in the family

How can I look after myself with Anaemia?

See your doctor for check-ups · Eat healthy, balanced meals · Ask your pharmacist · Rest well

Can an AI Body Master session help with Anaemia?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Anaemia and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
