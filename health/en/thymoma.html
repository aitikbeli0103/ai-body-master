Thymoma

A growth in the thymus, a small gland behind the breastbone

ConditionCancer & tumoursChest

What it is

A thymoma is a tumour that starts in the thymus, a small gland in the middle of the chest. It often grows slowly and is sometimes found by chance on a chest scan.

  • Middle of the chest
  • Mostly middle-aged and older
  • Often no signs at first
Which doctor
Family doctor first; chest specialist, chest surgeon, cancer doctor
Common in
Adults, more often middle-aged and older
Contagious
No
Course
Often slow-growing; your care team can explain what to expect

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • It is linked with some autoimmune conditions, such as myasthenia gravis
  • Past radiation to the chest may be a factor
  • It is not caused by anything you did

See a doctor first if

  • Severe trouble breathing or sudden chest pain: call 999
  • Trouble swallowing or choking on food or drink, or a weak voice: go to hospital now
  • Swelling of the face, neck or arms, or enlarged veins in the neck or chest
  • Drooping eyelids, double vision or weakness that gets worse through the day
  • A cough that lasts for weeks, or weight loss without trying

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

What is Thymoma?

What are the common signs of Thymoma?

Found on a scan · Tight, heavy chest · Short of breath on stairs · Double or changed vision

Why does Thymoma happen?

The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Thymoma?

See your doctor for check-ups · Rest well · Ask your pharmacist · Talk to family and friends
