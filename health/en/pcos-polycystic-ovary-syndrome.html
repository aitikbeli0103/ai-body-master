PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome)

A hormone condition in women that can cause irregular periods, extra hair and acne

ConditionWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

In PCOS the hormones are out of balance, so the ovaries may not release an egg every month, and periods can be irregular, with acne or extra hair.

  • Ovaries and hormones
  • Women of child-bearing age
  • Irregular or missed periods
Which doctor
Family doctor, women's doctor (gynaecologist), hormone doctor (endocrinologist)
Common in
Women in their child-bearing years, often starting in the teens or twenties
Contagious
No
Course
Long-term; signs can change over the years, and regular check-ups help

Common signs

More signs

  • Extra hair on the face, chest or belly

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body may not use insulin well, which can push the ovaries to make more male-type hormones
  • Having family members with PCOS or diabetes makes it more likely

See a doctor first if

  • Very heavy bleeding that soaks pads quickly, or bleeding with dizziness or fainting: go to hospital now
  • Sudden, severe pain in the lower belly, especially with fever, vomiting or fainting: go to hospital now
  • You are or may be pregnant and have bleeding or severe belly pain: go to hospital now
  • Periods that stop for many months, or bleeding that goes on for a long time or comes between periods: see a doctor
  • Very thirsty, passing a lot of urine, very tired or blurry vision (could be high blood sugar): see a doctor soon
  • Low mood that does not lift, or thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now, or call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Move your body on most days, for example brisk walking, as far as you are able
  • If you are overweight, slow and steady weight loss may help your periods and blood sugar; avoid crash diets
  • Write your periods on a calendar, and tell your doctor your goals, such as regular periods or having a baby

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • High blood pressure
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Questions and answers

What is PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome)?

A hormone condition in women that can cause irregular periods, extra hair and acne. In PCOS the hormones are out of balance, so the ovaries may not release an egg every month, and periods can be irregular, with acne or extra hair.

What are the common signs of PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome)?

Period comes late or early · Pimples and breakouts · Hair coming out · Gaining weight easily · Trouble getting pregnant

Why does PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome) happen?

The cause is not fully known · Hormone changes · Extra body weight

How can I look after myself with PCOS (polycystic ovary syndrome)?

Eat healthy, balanced meals · Keep your regular tests · Stop smoking
