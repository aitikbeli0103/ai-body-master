Pancreatic cancer

A cancer that starts in the pancreas, the organ behind the stomach that makes digestive juices and insulin

ConditionCancer & tumoursAbdomen

What it is

Cells in the pancreas grow out of control and form a tumour. It is often found late, because early signs are mild or easy to mistake for stomach trouble.

  • Behind the stomach
  • More common with age
  • Upper belly or back pain
Which doctor
Family doctor first, then a digestive or cancer specialist
Common in
Mostly older adults; smoking, long-term diabetes or a family history raise the chance
Contagious
No
Course
It can spread early, so a cancer team plans the care that suits each person

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-lasting inflammation of the pancreas (pancreatitis), long-term diabetes or being very overweight
  • Heavy drinking over many years, mainly because it can inflame the pancreas

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood, black stools, fainting or very bad belly pain: go to hospital now
  • Yellow skin or eyes, especially with dark urine, pale stools or fever: see a doctor today
  • Upper belly or back pain that keeps coming back or wakes you at night
  • Weight loss you cannot explain, or no appetite for weeks
  • New diabetes, or blood sugar that suddenly becomes hard to control

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop or change treatment or medicine on your own; ask your doctor or pharmacist
  • Ask your team about pain, eating and where to get emotional support
  • Ask to see a dietitian, because eating well can be hard

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Pancreatic cancer?

A cancer that starts in the pancreas, the organ behind the stomach that makes digestive juices and insulin. Cells in the pancreas grow out of control and form a tumour. It is often found late, because early signs are mild or easy to mistake for stomach trouble.

What are the common signs of Pancreatic cancer?

Pain in the upper belly · Yellow skin and eyes · Losing weight without trying · No appetite · Tired all the time

Why does Pancreatic cancer happen?

The cause is not fully known · Smoking · Runs in the family

How can I look after myself with Pancreatic cancer?

Follow your care team's plan · Stop smoking

Can an AI Body Master session help with Pancreatic cancer?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Pancreatic cancer and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
