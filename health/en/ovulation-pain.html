Ovulation pain

One-sided lower belly pain around the middle of the monthly cycle

SymptomWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

Some women feel a short ache or twinge on one side of the lower belly around the time an egg is released from the ovary.

  • Lower belly, one side
  • Mid-cycle
  • Dull ache or twinge
Which doctor
Family doctor, gynaecologist if it is strong or keeps changing
Common in
Women who still have periods
Contagious
No
Course
Usually mild and short, often gone within a day or two

Common signs

More signs

  • It comes around the middle of the cycle
  • The side may change from one month to the next

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The ovary stretching and releasing an egg
  • A little fluid or blood from the egg sac irritating the belly lining

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden severe belly pain, fainting or feeling very weak: go to hospital now
  • Pain with a missed period or a positive pregnancy test: go to hospital now
  • Fever, vomiting or foul-smelling discharge with the pain
  • Heavy bleeding, or pain that lasts for days or gets worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
