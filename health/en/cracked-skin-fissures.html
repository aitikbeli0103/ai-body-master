Cracked skin (fissures)

Dry or thick skin that splits into thin lines or deeper cracks, often on the hands, fingers or feet

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Very dry or thick skin can split into thin lines or deeper cracks (fissures). They can sting, itch or bleed, and often appear where skin bends or rubs, such as the fingers and heels.

  • Hands, fingers, feet, lips
  • Dry air, frequent washing
  • Stinging, tight, dry skin
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor (dermatologist) if it keeps coming back
Common in
Anyone; more with dry weather, wet work, frequent hand washing or older age
Contagious
No, but a skin infection behind it, such as a fungus, can spread
Course
Often eases with steady skin care; cracks that keep returning need a doctor's check

Common signs

More signs

  • Thin lines or deeper splits, often over joints, fingertips and heels
  • Stinging or pain when the skin is touched, stretched or washed
  • Cracks at the corners of the mouth or on the lips

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Washing hands often, wet work, or contact with soaps, cleaning products or chemicals
  • Eczema, psoriasis or a fungal skin infection
  • Less often, conditions such as diabetes or an underactive thyroid

See a doctor first if

  • Red, hot, swollen or painful skin with pus or fever: see a doctor the same day
  • Cracks on the hands or feet of someone with diabetes, numbness or poor blood flow: see a doctor promptly
  • Deep cracks that bleed easily, keep widening or do not improve with regular skin care
  • A crack or sore that does not close, or a patch that grows, bleeds or looks different
  • A rash over a large area, blisters, or cracks that began after a new medicine or product

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wear gloves for washing-up, cleaning or other wet work
  • Keep showers short and lukewarm, and avoid strong air-conditioning blowing on the skin
