Dislocated knee

The knee joint is knocked out of its normal position

ConditionPelvis, hip, knee & legArms & legs

What it is

The thigh bone and the shin bone move out of line at the knee. It is a serious injury because nearby blood vessels and nerves can be hurt, so go to hospital at once.

  • Knee joint
  • After a strong force
  • Severe pain and swelling
Which doctor
Emergency doctor, then bone doctor (orthopaedic surgeon)
Common in
People in road accidents, bad falls or hard sports injuries
Contagious
No
Course
Needs urgent care; recovery usually takes months and a doctor guides it

Common signs

More signs

  • The knee looks out of shape or bent the wrong way
  • Numbness or tingling below the knee
  • A foot that feels cold or looks pale

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A bad fall from a height
  • People who are very overweight can be hurt even by a small fall

See a doctor first if

  • Suspected knee dislocation: go to hospital now or call 999
  • A foot that is cold, pale, blue or has no pulse
  • Numbness, or cannot move the foot or toes
  • A bent or deformed knee, or a wound over the joint

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Do not try to straighten or push the knee back yourself
  • Keep the leg still, and support it in the position found
  • Do not give food or drink in case surgery is needed
  • After hospital care, follow your doctor's advice on rest, supports and walking aids

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Dislocated knee?

The knee joint is knocked out of its normal position. The thigh bone and the shin bone move out of line at the knee. It is a serious injury because nearby blood vessels and nerves can be hurt, so go to hospital at once.

What are the common signs of Dislocated knee?

Swollen, stiff knee · Cannot put weight on the leg

Why does Dislocated knee happen?

A fall, knock or road accident · Sports and hard knocks

How can I look after myself with Dislocated knee?
