Kidney damage from high uric acid

Kidney harm that can follow long-standing high uric acid in the blood

ConditionBlood sugar, fats, uric acid & thyroidLower back

What it is

When uric acid stays high for a long time, tiny crystals can build up in the kidneys and may slowly make them work less well.

  • Kidneys, lower back
  • Often with gout or stones
  • Often quiet at first
Which doctor
Family doctor, kidney doctor (nephrologist)
Common in
Adults with long-standing gout or high uric acid
Contagious
No
Course
Often slow over years; regular checks can follow it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Uric acid that stays high for years
  • Uric acid kidney stones

See a doctor first if

  • Very little or no urine passed for a day: go to hospital now
  • Sudden severe pain in the side or back, with vomiting or fever: go to hospital
  • Blood in the urine
  • New swelling of the legs or face, or breathlessness
  • A joint that is suddenly hot, red and very painful

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Kidney damage from high uric acid?

Kidney harm that can follow long-standing high uric acid in the blood. When uric acid stays high for a long time, tiny crystals can build up in the kidneys and may slowly make them work less well.

What are the common signs of Kidney damage from high uric acid?

Lower back pain · Swollen legs and ankles · Puffy face in the morning · Tired all the time · Waking at night to pass urine

Why does Kidney damage from high uric acid happen?

High blood sugar or blood pressure · Not drinking enough water

How can I look after myself with Kidney damage from high uric acid?

See your doctor for check-ups · Drink enough water · Eat healthy, balanced meals
