Blood disease

A general name for illnesses of the blood or the organs that make blood

ConditionBloodBlood

What it is

This covers many conditions of the red cells, white cells, platelets or clotting. The signs and care depend on the type, so a doctor's tests are needed.

  • Blood and bone marrow
  • Many different types
  • Tired, pale, bruises
Which doctor
Family doctor, blood doctor (haematologist)
Common in
Anyone, at any age; it depends on the type
Contagious
Mostly no
Course
Very different for each type; a doctor can explain yours

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Not enough iron or vitamins
  • Infections, immune problems or some medicines
  • Cancers of the blood or bone marrow

See a doctor first if

  • Bleeding that will not stop, or vomiting blood: call 999
  • Chest pain, trouble breathing or fainting: call 999
  • Fever or shaking chills: go to hospital the same day
  • Unexplained weight loss, night sweats or swollen glands
  • A baby or child who is pale, very tired or bruising easily

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Blood disease?

A general name for illnesses of the blood or the organs that make blood. This covers many conditions of the red cells, white cells, platelets or clotting. The signs and care depend on the type, so a doctor's tests are needed.

What are the common signs of Blood disease?

Tired all the time · Pale, tired look · Bruises easily · Fever, feeling hot · Short of breath on stairs

Why does Blood disease happen?

The cause is not fully known · Runs in the family

How can I look after myself with Blood disease?

See your doctor for check-ups · Keep your regular tests · Ask your pharmacist · Wash your hands often

Can an AI Body Master session help with Blood disease?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Blood disease and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
