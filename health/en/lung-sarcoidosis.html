Lung sarcoidosis

A condition where small clumps of inflamed cells form in the lungs and sometimes other organs

ConditionLungs & breathingHead

What it is

In sarcoidosis the body's defence cells gather in small clumps, most often in the lungs. Many people have mild signs and some get better on their own.

  • Lungs, sometimes more
  • Often adults, mid-life
  • Dry cough, tired
Which doctor
Lung doctor (respiratory specialist)
Common in
Adults, often between early adulthood and middle age
Contagious
No
Course
Varies: some people improve on their own, others need long-term check-ups

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body's defence system seems to over-react to something
  • It may run in some families

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, fainting or a racing or uneven heartbeat: call 999
  • Sudden severe trouble breathing, or blue lips: call 999
  • Eye pain, redness or blurry vision: see a doctor promptly
  • Coughing up blood, or breathlessness getting worse
  • Strong thirst, passing a lot of urine, or new lumps in the skin

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Lung sarcoidosis?

A condition where small clumps of inflamed cells form in the lungs and sometimes other organs. In sarcoidosis the body's defence cells gather in small clumps, most often in the lungs. Many people have mild signs and some get better on their own.

What are the common signs of Lung sarcoidosis?

Dry, tickly cough · Short of breath on stairs · Tired all the time · Tight, heavy chest · Red rash · Red, itchy eyes

Why does Lung sarcoidosis happen?

The cause is not fully known · Germs (infection)

How can I look after myself with Lung sarcoidosis?

See your doctor for check-ups · Stop smoking · Ask your pharmacist
