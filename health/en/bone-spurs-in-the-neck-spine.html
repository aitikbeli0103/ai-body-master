Bone spurs in the neck spine

Small bony growths on the neck bones that can narrow the space for nerves

ConditionBones, joints & spineNeckBones & muscles

What it is

With age, the neck bones can grow small bony edges. They may stiffen the neck and sometimes press on a nerve, causing arm or hand symptoms.

  • Neck bones
  • Middle age and older
  • Stiff, aching neck
Which doctor
Family doctor, bone or spine doctor, nerve doctor
Common in
Adults in middle age and older; people with desk work or old neck injuries
Contagious
No
Course
Spurs stay, but the aching often comes and goes; a doctor can guide you

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Years of poor posture, such as looking down at a phone or desk
  • Family history; sometimes the cause is not clear

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden face drooping, arm weakness or trouble speaking: call 999
  • Arm or hand weakness or numbness that is getting worse
  • Clumsy hands, trouble walking, or losing control of urine or stools
  • Neck pain after a fall or accident
  • Neck pain with fever, or a very stiff neck with severe headache

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep a good posture; raise your phone and screen to eye level
  • Use a pillow that keeps your neck in line with your spine
  • Avoid sudden, forceful twisting or cracking of the neck

Tell your therapist

  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Dizzy when turning the head
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
