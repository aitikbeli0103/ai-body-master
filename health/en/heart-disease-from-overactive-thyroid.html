Heart disease from overactive thyroid

Heart strain and uneven heartbeat caused by too much thyroid hormone

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsNeckChest

What it is

When the thyroid gland makes too much hormone, the heart can beat faster and harder. Over time this may strain the heart and cause an uneven heartbeat.

  • Thyroid in the neck
  • The heart is affected
  • Racing, pounding heart
Which doctor
Hormone doctor (endocrinologist), heart doctor, family doctor
Common in
People with an overactive thyroid, more often women; older adults
Contagious
No
Course
Often eases when the thyroid problem is looked after by a doctor

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • An overactive thyroid that is not yet controlled
  • Graves' disease, thyroid lumps or thyroid swelling
  • Taking too much thyroid medicine; ask your doctor or pharmacist

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain or sudden trouble breathing: call 999
  • Fainting, or a very fast, uneven heartbeat that does not settle: go to hospital now
  • High fever, confusion or severe restlessness with a racing heart: call 999
  • Swollen legs, or waking at night short of breath
  • Losing weight without trying, with shaking hands or heavy sweating

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
