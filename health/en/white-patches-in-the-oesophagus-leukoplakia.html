White patches in the oesophagus (leukoplakia)

Flat white patches on the lining of the food pipe, often found during a camera check

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

Some of the lining of the food pipe (oesophagus) becomes thicker and looks white. It often causes no feeling at all, so a doctor usually finds it by looking inside.

  • Food pipe, behind the chest
  • Often found by chance
  • Often no feeling at all
Which doctor
Stomach and gut doctor (gastroenterologist), family doctor
Common in
Adults; more often in people who smoke or drink alcohol
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; a doctor usually advises regular check-ups to keep an eye on it

Common signs

More signs

  • Mild discomfort when swallowing

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-term irritation of the food pipe lining
  • Smoking and drinking alcohol
  • Very hot food or drinks taken often

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Vomiting blood, or black stools: go to hospital now
  • Food gets stuck, or you cannot swallow even water
  • Weight loss without trying
  • Pain when swallowing that does not go away

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself
