Silent coronary heart disease

Narrowed heart arteries that cause little or no warning signs

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

The blood vessels that feed the heart are narrowed, yet the person may feel nothing or only mild discomfort. It is often found at a check-up.

  • Heart arteries
  • Often found at check-ups
  • Few or no signs
Which doctor
Heart doctor (cardiologist), family doctor
Common in
Older adults; people with diabetes, high blood pressure or high cholesterol
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long term; healthy habits and regular doctor visits matter

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • High cholesterol building up inside the artery walls

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, pressure or tightness, especially with sweating or feeling sick: call 999
  • Sudden trouble breathing or fainting: call 999
  • Pain spreading to the arm, jaw or back: go to hospital now
  • Getting breathless or tired on less and less effort

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
