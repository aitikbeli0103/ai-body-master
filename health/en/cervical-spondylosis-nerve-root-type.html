Cervical spondylosis (nerve root type)

Neck bone wear that irritates a nerve root and causes pain or tingling into the arm

ConditionBones, joints & spineNeckBones & muscles

What it is

Wear in the neck bones and discs can press on a nerve where it leaves the spine, causing neck pain and pain, tingling or weakness that runs down one arm.

  • Neck, down one arm
  • Adults, more with age
  • Pain and tingling
Which doctor
Family doctor, spine or bone doctor (orthopaedic), nerve doctor
Common in
Adults in middle age and older; people with long hours of desk or phone use
Contagious
No
Course
Often eases over weeks to months, though it can come back; a doctor can guide you

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Bone spurs or a bulging disc pressing on a nerve root
  • Long periods of poor posture, such as looking down at a phone

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden weakness in the face or arm, or trouble speaking: call 999
  • Chest pain or pain spreading from the chest to the arm or jaw: call 999
  • Arm or hand weakness that is getting worse, or dropping things
  • Numbness or weakness in the legs, or trouble with urine or stools: go to hospital now
  • Severe pain after a fall or neck injury, or pain with fever

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Keep a good posture and raise your phone or screen closer to eye level
  • Take short breaks to move your neck gently if it is comfortable
  • Use a pillow that keeps your neck in a natural line while you sleep
  • A warm towel on the neck may feel soothing; avoid forceful pulling or twisting

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Dizzy when turning the head
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
