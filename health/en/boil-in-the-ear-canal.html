Boil in the ear canal

A painful, infected lump in the skin of the outer ear canal

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

A germ infects a hair root in the skin of the ear canal and forms a sore lump. The ear hurts, especially when you touch it or chew.

  • Outer ear canal
  • After ear picking or water
  • Ear pain, worse on touch
Which doctor
Family doctor; ear, nose and throat doctor
Common in
Adults who pick their ears or swim; people with diabetes
Contagious
Not usually, but keep towels and ear items to yourself
Course
Often settles in about a week or two, but see a doctor

Common signs

More signs

  • Redness or swelling at the ear opening
  • Sometimes a small amount of fluid from the ear

Why it happens

  • Small scratches from picking the ear with fingers or cotton buds
  • Water staying in the ear after swimming or bathing
  • Hearing aids or earphones that irritate the skin
  • Diabetes can make skin infections more likely

See a doctor first if

  • Redness and swelling spreading behind or around the ear
  • Fever, or severe ear pain that keeps getting worse
  • Weakness of the face or a drooping face: call 999
  • Diabetes with an ear infection: see a doctor promptly
  • Hearing loss or fluid, pus or blood from the ear

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
