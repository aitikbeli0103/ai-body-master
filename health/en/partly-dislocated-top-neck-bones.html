Partly dislocated top neck bones

The top two neck bones slip out of their normal position

ConditionBack & spineNeck

What it is

The first two bones of the neck, just under the skull, are held by ligaments. When these loosen or are hurt, the bones can shift and put pressure on nearby nerves.

  • Top of the neck
  • After a neck injury
  • Neck pain, stiff neck
Which doctor
Orthopaedic or spine doctor; emergency department after an injury
Common in
People after falls or accidents; some people with joint disease or from birth
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; a doctor decides the care after an X-ray or scan

Common signs

More signs

  • Feeling that the head is heavy or unsteady

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Joint diseases such as rheumatoid arthritis that loosen the ligaments
  • An infection in the throat or neck, mostly in children

See a doctor first if

  • Neck pain after a fall or accident: keep the head still and call 999
  • New numbness, tingling or weakness in the arms or legs: go to hospital now
  • Trouble breathing or swallowing, or losing control of urine or stools: call 999
  • Fainting, severe dizziness or blurred vision when the head moves
  • Neck pain with fever or a child who cannot turn the head after a throat infection

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Follow your doctor's advice about a neck collar and rest
  • Support your head when you lie down and use a firm, comfortable pillow

Tell your therapist

  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Dizzy when turning the head
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Weak bones
