Achilles tendonitis

Swelling and soreness of the Achilles tendon at the back of the ankle

ConditionBones, joints & spineBones & muscles

What it is

The Achilles tendon joins the calf to the heel bone. When it is overused it can become inflamed, painful and tender to touch.

  • Back of the ankle
  • Active people, sudden effort
  • Pain above the heel
Which doctor
Family doctor; sports doctor, physiotherapist
Common in
Runners, ball players, weekend sports people
Contagious
No
Course
Usually settles over weeks with rest and care; may come back

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Sudden increase in exercise, running or jumping
  • Tight calf muscles
  • Old or worn shoes, or hard running surfaces
  • Some medicines or long-term illness can raise the risk

See a doctor first if

  • A sudden snap with sharp pain and you cannot rise on your toes: go to hospital
  • A red, hot and very swollen ankle or calf
  • Cannot put weight on the foot
  • Fever with a painful, swollen ankle
  • Pain that keeps getting worse despite rest

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Warm up and stretch the calf gently before exercise
  • Go back to sport slowly; ask a physiotherapist for a plan

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Achilles tendonitis?

Swelling and soreness of the Achilles tendon at the back of the ankle. The Achilles tendon joins the calf to the heel bone. When it is overused it can become inflamed, painful and tender to touch.

What are the common signs of Achilles tendonitis?

Heel pain on first steps · Painful ankle · Hard to walk far

Why does Achilles tendonitis happen?

Sudden increase in exercise, running or jumping · Tight calf muscles · Old or worn shoes, or hard running surfaces · Some medicines or long-term illness can raise the risk

How can I look after myself with Achilles tendonitis?

Rest the sore joint · Wear comfortable, supportive shoes
