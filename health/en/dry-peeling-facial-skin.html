Dry, peeling facial skin

Dry skin on the face that feels tight and peels in flakes

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsHeadSkin

What it is

The skin on the face loses moisture and becomes tight, rough and flaky, often around the nose, cheeks, eyebrows or forehead. Air-conditioning, sun and face products are common triggers.

  • Face, nose and cheeks
  • Can happen at any age
  • Tight, flaky, sore skin
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor (dermatologist) if it keeps coming back
Common in
Anyone; more common with dry or sensitive skin
Contagious
No
Course
Often settles with gentle care and less irritation; it may come back

Common signs

More signs

  • Fine flakes or peeling that may show up under make-up
  • Skin that feels sore after washing the face
  • Peeling after sunburn or after using strong face products

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Washing the face too often or too hard, or using hot water
  • New or strong face products, make-up, scrubs or peels, or an allergy to one of them
  • Skin conditions such as eczema, or flaking by the nose and eyebrows (seborrhoeic dermatitis)

See a doctor first if

  • Swelling of the face, lips or tongue, or trouble breathing after a new product: call 999
  • A painful blistering rash on one side of the face, especially near the eye: see a doctor now
  • Skin near the eyes that is very swollen or painful, or any change in your vision
  • Oozing, yellow crusts, pus or a fever with the rash
  • Peeling that is not better after a few weeks of gentle care

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Why does Dry, peeling facial skin happen?

How can I look after myself with Dry, peeling facial skin?
