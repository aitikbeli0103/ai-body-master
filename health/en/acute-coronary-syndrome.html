Acute coronary syndrome

A sudden drop in blood flow to the heart muscle, which is a medical emergency

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

This is the name for sudden problems that cut blood flow to the heart, such as a heart attack. It needs emergency care at once.

  • Chest, heart
  • More common with age
  • Chest pressure or pain
Which doctor
Emergency department; heart doctor (cardiologist)
Common in
Adults with high blood pressure, diabetes, smoking or high cholesterol
Contagious
No
Course
Needs urgent hospital care; recovery differs for each person

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A narrowed or blocked blood vessel feeding the heart
  • Fatty build-up in the blood vessels over many years

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, pressure or tightness lasting more than a few minutes: call 999 now
  • Pain spreading to the arm, neck, jaw or back
  • Sudden trouble breathing, cold sweat or feeling faint
  • Feeling very sick or vomiting together with chest discomfort
  • Do not drive yourself; wait for the ambulance

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Acute coronary syndrome?

A sudden drop in blood flow to the heart muscle, which is a medical emergency. This is the name for sudden problems that cut blood flow to the heart, such as a heart attack. It needs emergency care at once.

What are the common signs of Acute coronary syndrome?

Tight, heavy chest · Short of breath on stairs · Feeling sick, nausea · Sweating a lot · Feeling faint

Why does Acute coronary syndrome happen?

High blood sugar or blood pressure · Smoking · Runs in the family

How can I look after myself with Acute coronary syndrome?

Ask your pharmacist · Stop smoking · See your doctor for check-ups

Can an AI Body Master session help with Acute coronary syndrome?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Acute coronary syndrome and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
