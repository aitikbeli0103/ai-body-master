Kidney failure

A serious state where the kidneys can no longer clean the blood well enough on their own

ConditionKidneys & urinaryAbdomen

What it is

Waste and extra water build up in the body because the kidneys are working very little. It needs close care from a kidney team, who can explain the options.

  • Both kidneys
  • After long-term kidney disease
  • Swelling, tired, sick
Which doctor
Kidney specialist (nephrologist) and kidney care team
Common in
People with long-term kidney disease, diabetes or high blood pressure
Contagious
No
Course
Needs long-term care; the kidney team plans what is best

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-term kidney disease that has got worse over time
  • Some medicines or poisons that harm the kidneys

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, chest pain, a fit, fainting or deep confusion: call 999
  • Passing very little or no urine
  • Fast weight gain or fast swelling of the legs, face or belly
  • Vomiting that does not stop, or very bad itching with great tiredness
  • Missing a planned kidney treatment visit

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Follow your kidney team's plan and never stop a treatment on your own
  • Ask the team about the right amount of fluid, salt and food for you
  • Do not take painkillers, herbal products or supplements without asking them first

Tell your therapist

  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A heart condition
  • High blood pressure
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Kidney failure?

A serious state where the kidneys can no longer clean the blood well enough on their own. Waste and extra water build up in the body because the kidneys are working very little. It needs close care from a kidney team, who can explain the options.

What are the common signs of Kidney failure?

Puffy face in the morning · Swollen legs and ankles · Tired all the time · Feeling sick, nausea · No appetite · Short of breath on stairs

Why does Kidney failure happen?

High blood sugar or blood pressure · Not drinking enough water

How can I look after myself with Kidney failure?

Rest well · Talk to family and friends

Can an AI Body Master session help with Kidney failure?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Kidney failure and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
