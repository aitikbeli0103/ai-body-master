Stomach lymphoma

A cancer of the lymph tissue found in the wall of the stomach

ConditionCancer & tumoursAbdomen

What it is

Stomach lymphoma starts in the immune (lymph) tissue in the stomach wall. It is different from the more common stomach cancers that start in the stomach lining.

  • Stomach, upper belly
  • Mostly adults
  • Stomach pain, poor appetite
Which doctor
Family doctor first; stomach doctor (gastroenterologist), blood and cancer doctor
Common in
Adults, more often middle-aged and older
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the type and stage; your care team can explain

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A long-lasting stomach infection with a germ called H. pylori is a known factor for some types
  • Some autoimmune conditions

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood, or black, sticky stools: go to hospital now
  • Severe belly pain, a hard swollen belly, or fainting: go to hospital now
  • Vomiting often, or cannot keep food or drink down
  • Weight loss without trying, or feeling full after a few bites
  • Night sweats, fever or swollen glands that last

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop or change treatment on your own
  • Ask your team about food, pain and nausea; ask a pharmacist before any medicine
  • Share how you feel with someone you trust

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
