Chronic rhinitis (blocked or runny nose)

Long-lasting inflammation inside the nose with blockage, runny nose or sneezing

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

The lining of the nose stays swollen or irritated for a long time. The nose is often blocked or runny, and there may be sneezing or a drip at the back of the throat.

  • Inside the nose
  • Adults and children
  • Blocked, runny nose
Which doctor
Family doctor or ear, nose and throat doctor (ENT)
Common in
People with allergies, asthma or who live with dust and haze
Contagious
No, unless it is a cold
Course
Often long-lasting and may come and go; avoiding triggers helps

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Changes in weather or air-conditioning that is too cold
  • Using some nose sprays for too long (ask your doctor or pharmacist)

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing or swelling of the face or lips: call 999
  • Stiff neck with high fever and severe headache: go to hospital now
  • Swelling around the eye, double vision or a change in sight
  • Heavy or repeated nosebleeds, or blood-stained discharge from one side only
  • A blocked nose that does not improve for weeks, or a child who breathes only through the mouth

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Find and avoid your triggers, such as dust, smoke and strong smells
  • Wash bedding often and keep the bedroom clean and dust-free
  • Wear a mask in haze or dusty places

Tell your therapist

  • A severe allergy
  • Pregnancy
  • High blood pressure

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Chronic rhinitis (blocked or runny nose)?

Long-lasting inflammation inside the nose with blockage, runny nose or sneezing. The lining of the nose stays swollen or irritated for a long time. The nose is often blocked or runny, and there may be sneezing or a drip at the back of the throat.

What are the common signs of Chronic rhinitis (blocked or runny nose)?

Blocked nose · Sneezing, runny nose · Sore, scratchy throat · Cannot fall asleep

Why does Chronic rhinitis (blocked or runny nose) happen?

Dust, pollen or pets · Dry air and air-conditioning · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Chronic rhinitis (blocked or runny nose)?

Drink enough water · Ask your pharmacist
