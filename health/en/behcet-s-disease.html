Behcet's disease

A rare illness with repeated mouth sores, eye inflammation and skin problems

ConditionAutoimmune & rheumaticWhole body

What it is

Blood vessels in different parts of the body become inflamed, causing sores in the mouth, red eyes and skin spots. It comes and goes.

  • Mouth, eyes, skin
  • Often young adults
  • Repeated mouth sores
Which doctor
Rheumatologist; eye doctor for the eyes
Common in
Young adults; seen more along the old Silk Road regions
Contagious
No
Course
Comes in flares and calm times; it is followed long term

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The immune system causes inflammation in blood vessels
  • Certain genes may make some people more likely to get it
  • An infection or other trigger may start a flare in some people

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden loss or blurring of vision, or a painful red eye: go to hospital now
  • Face drooping, arm weakness, speech trouble or a sudden bad headache: call 999
  • A swollen, painful leg, or sudden chest pain or short breath: call 999
  • Severe belly pain or blood in the stools
  • Sores that are very painful or do not heal over weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep seeing your specialist and an eye doctor regularly
  • Use a soft toothbrush and avoid very hot, spicy or sharp foods in flares
  • Rest, and handle stress with calm activities you enjoy
  • Note when flares happen to share with your doctor

Tell your therapist

  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pregnancy
  • Broken, red or sore skin

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Behcet's disease?

A rare illness with repeated mouth sores, eye inflammation and skin problems. Blood vessels in different parts of the body become inflamed, causing sores in the mouth, red eyes and skin spots. It comes and goes.

What are the common signs of Behcet's disease?

Painful mouth sores · Red rash · Red, itchy eyes · Blurry vision

Why does Behcet's disease happen?

The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Behcet's disease?

Take medicine as your doctor says
