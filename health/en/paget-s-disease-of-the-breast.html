Paget's disease of the breast

A rare skin change of the nipple that may be linked to breast cancer

ConditionWomen's healthSkin

What it is

Paget's disease of the breast shows up as red, flaky or crusty skin on the nipple, and it needs a doctor's check because it can go with a breast cancer.

  • Nipple and the skin around it
  • Mostly in older women
  • Red, scaly, itchy skin
Which doctor
Family doctor, breast surgeon, skin doctor
Common in
Mostly women, rarely men; more often after middle age
Contagious
No
Course
Does not clear up on its own; needs a doctor's check and follow-up

Common signs

More signs

  • Itching, burning or tingling of the nipple
  • A flat or turned-in nipple, or fluid coming out
  • Sometimes a lump in the same breast

Why it happens

Other causes

  • It is often linked to a breast cancer inside the same breast

See a doctor first if

  • A nipple rash or sore that does not heal after a few weeks: see a doctor soon
  • A new lump in the breast or under the arm
  • Bloody or other fluid from the nipple
  • A nipple that turns in, or the breast skin that dimples or changes shape
  • Unexplained weight loss or bone pain

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not wait: ask a doctor to look at any nipple skin change
  • Do not use creams on it without asking your doctor or pharmacist
  • Ask your team about pain, food and emotional support

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
