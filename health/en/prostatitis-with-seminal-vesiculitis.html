Prostatitis with seminal vesiculitis

Swelling and soreness of the prostate and the small glands beside it

ConditionMen's healthReproductive

What it is

The prostate and the seminal vesicles, the small glands that help make semen, are inflamed together. This can bring pelvic pain and urine or sex problems.

  • Prostate and nearby glands
  • Adult men
  • Pelvic ache, urine trouble
Which doctor
Family doctor or urologist
Common in
Adult men; more often with a past urine infection or long sitting
Contagious
Usually not, but some causes of infection can pass on in sex
Course
Can settle with care but may return; a doctor guides what to do

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain when semen comes out, or blood-tinged semen

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Germs from the urine or bowel reaching the prostate
  • Some infections passed on in sex
  • Long sitting, or holding urine for long times

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine at all: go to hospital now
  • High fever with shaking chills, or feeling confused or very weak: go to hospital now
  • Severe pain in the pelvis or scrotum
  • Blood in the urine, or lots of blood in the semen
  • Symptoms that last more than a few weeks or keep returning

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Take a warm bath to ease pelvic aching
  • Limit alcohol and very spicy food if they make it worse

What is Prostatitis with seminal vesiculitis?

Swelling and soreness of the prostate and the small glands beside it. The prostate and the seminal vesicles, the small glands that help make semen, are inflamed together. This can bring pelvic pain and urine or sex problems.

What are the common signs of Prostatitis with seminal vesiculitis?

Ache or bulge in the groin · Burning when passing urine · Shivering with chills

Why does Prostatitis with seminal vesiculitis happen?

An enlarged prostate

How can I look after myself with Prostatitis with seminal vesiculitis?
