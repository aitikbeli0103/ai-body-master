Inflammation around the anus (perianal)

Redness, swelling and soreness of the skin around the anus

ConditionStomach & intestinesPelvis

What it is

The skin and tissue around the anus become red, sore or itchy. It has several possible causes, so a doctor should look if it does not settle.

  • Skin around the anus
  • Adults and children
  • Itch, soreness, swelling
Which doctor
Family doctor, colorectal or general surgeon
Common in
Adults and children; people with loose stools or skin problems
Contagious
Some types can be
Course
Depends on the cause; mild cases often settle in days to weeks

Common signs

More signs

  • Redness and soreness around the anus
  • Swelling or a tender lump near the anus
  • Pain when passing stools or sitting
  • Sometimes a little discharge or wetness

Why it happens

  • Loose or frequent stools that irritate the skin
  • Wiping too hard, harsh soap or wet wipes
  • Skin problems, allergy or a skin infection
  • Piles, small tears, or infection of a small gland near the anus

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with a hot, very painful swelling near the anus: go to hospital
  • Pus, a foul smell, or a lump that keeps coming back
  • Blood or mucus in the stools, or long-lasting loose stools
  • Weight loss without trying
  • No better after about a week

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wear loose, cotton underwear
  • Eat fibre and drink water to keep stools soft
  • Do not scratch; ask a pharmacist before using any cream

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
