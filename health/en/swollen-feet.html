Swollen feet

Puffiness in one or both feet, often from fluid build-up

SymptomHeart & blood vesselsArms & legs

What it is

The feet look larger or puffy and shoes may feel tight. It can be harmless after a long day, but it can also be a sign of a health problem.

  • One or both feet
  • Can happen to anyone
  • Puffy, tight shoes
Which doctor
Family doctor
Common in
Pregnancy, older adults, long standing, travel, hot weather
Contagious
No
Course
Often eases with rest; see a doctor if it stays or keeps returning

Common signs

More signs

  • Shoes, socks or straps feel tight
  • Skin looks shiny or stretched
  • Swelling is often worse by evening

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Standing, sitting or travelling for a long time
  • Hot weather or too much salty food

See a doctor first if

  • Swelling with chest pain or trouble breathing: call 999
  • One foot or leg suddenly swollen, red, warm or painful: go to hospital now
  • Swelling after an injury, or with fever
  • If you have diabetes: any swelling, sore or colour change on the foot
  • Swelling that keeps coming back or does not go down

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Rest with your feet raised above heart level for a while
  • Move and stretch your ankles often when sitting
  • Wear loose, comfortable shoes
  • Check your feet daily if you have diabetes

Tell your therapist

  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A heart condition
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Puffiness in one or both feet, often from fluid build-up. The feet look larger or puffy and shoes may feel tight. It can be harmless after a long day, but it can also be a sign of a health problem.

What are the common signs of Swollen feet?

Why does Swollen feet happen?

Pregnancy · Germs (infection) · A side effect of some medicines
