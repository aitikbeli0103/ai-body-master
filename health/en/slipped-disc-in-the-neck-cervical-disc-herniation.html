Slipped disc in the neck (cervical disc herniation)

A disc in the neck bulges out and may press on a nerve, causing neck and arm symptoms

ConditionBones, joints & spineNeckBones & muscles

What it is

A soft disc between the bones of the neck pushes out of place. It may press on a nerve and cause neck pain, or pain, tingling or weakness in the shoulder, arm or hand.

  • Neck, may reach the arm
  • Adults, more with age
  • Neck pain, tingling arm
Which doctor
Family doctor, orthopaedic or spine doctor
Common in
Adults who work long hours at a desk or phone, and older people
Contagious
No
Course
Often eases over weeks to months; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Poor posture, such as looking down at a phone for long hours
  • A neck injury, a sudden jerk or a whiplash
  • Smoking and a weak neck and shoulder muscles; sometimes the cause is not known

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden weakness in the arm or leg, a drooping face or trouble speaking: call 999
  • Clumsy hands, trouble walking or losing control of your bladder or bowel: go to hospital now
  • Neck pain after a fall or accident
  • Neck pain with fever, or with a stiff neck and headache
  • Arm weakness or numbness that keeps getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep your screen at eye level and take breaks from the phone
  • Change position often; do gentle neck and shoulder movements
  • Use a pillow that keeps your neck in a neutral position
  • Avoid sudden, forceful twisting or pulling of the neck

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Dizzy when turning the head
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
