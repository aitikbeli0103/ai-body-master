Benign lung tumour

A non-cancerous growth in the lung or airway that is usually found on a scan

SymptomLungs & breathingChest

What it is

A benign lung tumour is a lump in the lung that is not cancer. It often causes no signs and is found by chance on a chest scan.

  • Inside the lung
  • Often found by chance
  • Often no signs at all
Which doctor
Chest doctor (respiratory physician), family doctor
Common in
Adults of many ages; often found during a scan for another reason
Contagious
No
Course
Often stays the same for a long time; a doctor decides if it needs watching

Common signs

More signs

  • A cough that does not go away, if the lump presses on an airway
  • Chest infections that keep coming back in the same place

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Some are small growths of normal lung or airway tissue

See a doctor first if

  • Coughing up blood: see a doctor now
  • Severe trouble breathing or chest pain: call 999
  • A cough that lasts more than a few weeks
  • Weight loss you cannot explain, or a lump that is growing
  • Fever with chest infections that keep returning

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.
