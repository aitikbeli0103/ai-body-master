Invasive breast cancer

Breast cancer that has grown out of the ducts or glands where it began into nearby breast tissue

ConditionCancer & tumoursChest

What it is

Cells in the milk ducts or milk glands of the breast grow out of control and spread into nearby breast tissue; it is the most common kind of breast cancer.

  • The breast and armpit
  • More common in women
  • A lump or skin changes
Which doctor
Family doctor first; then a breast surgeon or cancer specialist (oncologist)
Common in
Mostly women, more often with age; men can also be affected, rarely
Contagious
No
Course
Differs from person to person; your care team explains the plan and what to expect

Common signs

More signs

  • Dimpling, puckering, redness or scaly skin on the breast or nipple
  • A nipple that turns inward, or fluid from the nipple, sometimes with blood

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Gene changes that run in some families
  • Hormone factors, such as periods that started early or menopause that came late
  • Drinking alcohol, being overweight after menopause, and little exercise

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden trouble breathing or chest pain: call 999
  • Fever or shaking chills during cancer care: go to hospital now
  • A new lump or thickening in the breast or armpit: see a doctor promptly
  • Blood-stained nipple fluid, a nipple turning inward, or dimpled, red breast skin
  • A swollen, red, warm or painful arm or leg: see a doctor the same day
  • New bone pain, a cough that will not go away, or weight loss without trying

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Follow the plan from your cancer care team and keep all appointments
  • Learn how your breasts normally look and feel, and tell a doctor about any change
  • Ask your team about pain, food, exercise and emotional support
  • Ask your team before using any supplement, herbal product or hormone product
  • Never stop treatment on your own; talk to your team first

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Weak bones
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
