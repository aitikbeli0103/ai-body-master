Broken finger

A crack or break in one of the small bones of the finger

ConditionShoulder, arm & handArms & legsBones & muscles

What it is

A finger bone can crack or break after a knock, a fall, a crush or a sports injury. The finger becomes painful, swollen and hard to move, and may look bent.

  • Finger and hand
  • After a knock or fall
  • Pain, swelling, stiffness
Which doctor
Bone doctor (orthopaedic), hand doctor, emergency department
Common in
Anyone; common in sports, work injuries and falls
Contagious
No
Course
Usually takes some weeks to mend; the finger may feel stiff for a while

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain right away, worse when you touch or move the finger
  • A finger that looks bent, crooked or shorter than normal
  • Cannot bend or straighten the finger fully, or hard to grip

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A finger caught in a door or crushed by a heavy object
  • Weak bones can break more easily, even from a small knock

See a doctor first if

  • A finger that is bent, crooked or out of place: go to hospital
  • A finger that turns pale, cold, blue or numb
  • An open cut, or bone showing through the skin: go to hospital now
  • Heavy swelling, or several fingers or the hand hurt after a crush
  • Redness, warmth, pus or fever in the days after the injury

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Remove rings from the hurt finger early, before it swells
  • Keep the hand raised above the heart to ease swelling
  • Do not pull or force the finger straight; keep it still and get it checked

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
