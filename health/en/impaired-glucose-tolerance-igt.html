Impaired glucose tolerance (IGT)

Blood sugar stays higher than normal for a while after a sugary drink in a test

ConditionBlood sugar, fats, uric acid & thyroidWhole body

What it is

In a glucose tolerance test, the blood sugar goes higher than normal after a sugary drink, but not high enough to be called diabetes. It is a warning sign that the body is handling sugar less well.

  • Blood sugar
  • Found on a sugar drink test
  • Often no signs

See a doctor first if

  • Very thirsty, passing lots of urine, or losing weight without trying
  • Vomiting, stomach pain, fast breathing or very sleepy: go to hospital now
  • Blurred vision or cuts that heal slowly
  • Chest pain or stroke signs (face drooping, arm weakness, speech trouble): call 999

What are the common signs of Impaired glucose tolerance (IGT)?

Why does Impaired glucose tolerance (IGT) happen?
