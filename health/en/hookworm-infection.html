Hookworm infection

A gut infection with small worms that enter through the skin of the feet

ConditionInfectionsWhole body

What it is

Tiny worm larvae in soil can enter through bare skin, then live in the gut and take a little blood. Over time this can make a person pale and tired.

  • Gut
  • Walking barefoot on soil
  • Pale and tired
Which doctor
Family doctor
Common in
People who go barefoot on soil; children, farm and estate workers
Contagious
Not directly from person to person; it spreads through soil with waste in it
Course
A doctor can confirm it with a stool test and check for low blood

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Walking barefoot on soil with human waste in it
  • Poor toilets and poor sewage
  • Working or playing in wet soil without shoes or gloves
  • Eating vegetables that were not washed well

See a doctor first if

  • Feeling faint, breathless at rest, or chest pain with a pale look: go to hospital now
  • Black stools or blood in the stools
  • A child who is very pale, tired or not growing well: see a doctor promptly
  • A pregnant woman who is very tired and pale
  • Ongoing belly pain, loose stools or weight loss

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wear shoes or slippers outdoors, and gloves for soil work
  • Wash vegetables well and cook food fully
  • Eat regular, balanced meals; ask your doctor or pharmacist before taking any medicine or iron
