Abdominal aortic aneurysm

A swollen, weak bulge in the main blood vessel that runs through the belly

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

The wall of the large blood vessel in the belly (the aorta) becomes weak and bulges out. It often gives no signs until it is found on a scan.

  • Large blood vessel in the belly
  • Older adults, more in men
  • Often no signs
Which doctor
Family doctor, vascular surgeon or heart specialist
Common in
Older men, smokers, people with high blood pressure
Contagious
No
Course
Often found by chance and watched by a doctor over time; follow your doctor's advice

Common signs

More signs

  • A pulsing feeling in the belly, like a heartbeat
  • A dull ache in the belly, back or side that does not go away

Why it happens

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden, severe pain in the belly, back or side: call 999
  • Fainting, cold sweat, very pale skin or a racing heart with belly pain: call 999
  • Pain in the chest with trouble breathing: call 999
  • A new pulsing lump in the belly
  • A cold, painful or pale leg or foot

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Abdominal aortic aneurysm?

A swollen, weak bulge in the main blood vessel that runs through the belly. The wall of the large blood vessel in the belly (the aorta) becomes weak and bulges out. It often gives no signs until it is found on a scan.

What are the common signs of Abdominal aortic aneurysm?

Often no signs at first · Found on a scan

Why does Abdominal aortic aneurysm happen?

Smoking · High blood sugar or blood pressure · Getting older · Runs in the family

How can I look after myself with Abdominal aortic aneurysm?

See your doctor for check-ups · Stop smoking · Check your blood pressure at home · Avoid heavy lifting
