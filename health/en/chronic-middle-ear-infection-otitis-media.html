Chronic middle ear infection (otitis media)

Middle ear infection that lasts a long time or keeps coming back

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

The space behind the eardrum stays inflamed or infected for a long time. The ear may leak fluid, hearing may be reduced, and the eardrum can be damaged.

  • Middle ear
  • Often starts in childhood
  • Discharge, less hearing
Which doctor
Ear, nose and throat doctor (ENT); family doctor first
Common in
Children, and adults who had many ear infections before
Contagious
No, but a cold that starts it can spread
Course
Can last or return for months; a doctor can explain what to expect

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Colds, allergies or a blocked nose that stop the ear tube from draining
  • A hole or damage in the eardrum

See a doctor first if

  • Stiff neck with high fever and severe headache: go to hospital now
  • Face drooping on one side: call 999
  • Swelling, redness or pain behind the ear
  • Sudden loss of hearing, or strong spinning dizziness with vomiting
  • A baby or child with ear pain and fever: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not put cotton buds, fingers or oils into the ear
  • Keep children away from cigarette smoke
  • Have your hearing checked if it seems to be changing

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Chronic middle ear infection (otitis media)?

Middle ear infection that lasts a long time or keeps coming back. The space behind the eardrum stays inflamed or infected for a long time. The ear may leak fluid, hearing may be reduced, and the eardrum can be damaged.

What are the common signs of Chronic middle ear infection (otitis media)?

Hard to hear people · Ringing in the ears · Room feels like spinning

Why does Chronic middle ear infection (otitis media) happen?

Germs (infection) · Smoke, haze and strong smells

How can I look after myself with Chronic middle ear infection (otitis media)?

Keep the ear dry · Ask your pharmacist
