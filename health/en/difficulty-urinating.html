Difficulty urinating

Trouble passing urine, with a weak flow, straining or pain

SymptomKidneys & urinaryAbdomen

What it is

Urine does not come out easily. The flow may be weak or stop and start, you may need to strain, or it may hurt. It has many possible causes.

  • Bladder and urine pipe
  • Any age, more in older men
  • Weak flow, straining
Which doctor
Family doctor; a urine doctor (urologist) if it continues
Common in
Older men; people with urine infections, stones or nerve problems
Contagious
No, though an infection behind it may be
Course
Depends on the cause; it is best checked early

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A stone or a narrow urine pipe
  • Urine infection and swelling
  • Nerve problems, for example from diabetes or a back problem

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine at all, with a painful full lower belly: go to hospital now
  • Fever, shaking chills, or pain in the back or side
  • Blood in the urine
  • Weak legs, numbness in the groin, or loss of control of bowel or bladder: go to hospital now
  • Difficulty in a child, or in pregnancy: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

What is Difficulty urinating?

What are the common signs of Difficulty urinating?

Burning when passing urine · Needing the toilet often · Waking at night to pass urine · Dragging ache in the lower belly · Fever, feeling hot

Why does Difficulty urinating happen?

An enlarged prostate · A side effect of some medicines
