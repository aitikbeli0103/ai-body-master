Lung abscess

A pocket of pus that forms inside the lung after an infection

ConditionLungs & breathingChest

What it is

Germs destroy a small area of lung tissue, and a pocket filled with pus forms. It needs a doctor's care, as it can be serious.

  • Inside the lung
  • Weak or unwell people
  • Fever, smelly phlegm
Which doctor
Lung doctor (respiratory physician) or hospital doctor
Common in
People who drink heavily, have bad teeth, or have trouble swallowing or fits
Contagious
No, but the first infection can sometimes spread
Course
Needs medical care, often for weeks; a doctor will follow it closely

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Weak immunity, or something blocking an airway

See a doctor first if

  • Hard to breathe or chest pain: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Coughing up a lot of blood
  • High fever with shaking chills
  • Foul-smelling phlegm that keeps coming
  • Losing weight without trying, or feeling very weak or confused

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Lung abscess?

A pocket of pus that forms inside the lung after an infection. Germs destroy a small area of lung tissue, and a pocket filled with pus forms. It needs a doctor's care, as it can be serious.

What are the common signs of Lung abscess?

Fever, feeling hot · Cough with phlegm · Soaking sweats at night · Losing weight without trying · Sharp pain when breathing in

Why does Lung abscess happen?

Germs (infection) · Drinking alcohol · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Lung abscess?

See your doctor for check-ups
