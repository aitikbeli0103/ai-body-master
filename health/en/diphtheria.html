Diphtheria

A serious germ infection of the throat and nose that can make breathing difficult

ConditionInfectionsNeckWhole body

What it is

A germ makes a poison in the throat or nose, causing sore throat, fever, swollen neck glands and a thick grey coating that can block breathing, so it needs hospital care at once.

  • Throat, nose and neck
  • At risk if not vaccinated
  • Sore throat and fever
Which doctor
Hospital emergency department; infectious disease doctor
Common in
People without all their vaccines and boosters; crowded living places
Contagious
Yes, through coughs and sneezes, and by touching skin sores or shared items
Course
Can get serious quickly and needs urgent hospital care

Common signs

More signs

  • A thick grey-white coating on the tonsils or throat
  • Sometimes a blood-stained nose discharge, or skin sores that are slow to get better

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Some people carry the germ without feeling ill and can still pass it on
  • Missing childhood vaccines or the booster doses that protect against it
  • Crowded places and close contact with a sick person

See a doctor first if

  • Hard to breathe or swallow, noisy breathing, or blue lips: call 999 now
  • Sore throat with fever, swollen neck glands or a grey-white coating in the throat: go to hospital now
  • A sick child or baby with a sore throat and trouble breathing or feeding: go to hospital now
  • Chest pain, a fast or uneven heartbeat, feeling faint or very weak: call 999
  • Close contact with someone who has diphtheria: see a doctor or the health office early
  • New weakness, trouble speaking or swallowing, or blurry vision after a throat illness: go to hospital

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Go to hospital quickly with these signs, and tell the staff if you were near someone sick
  • Keep away from others and cover coughs and sneezes until a doctor says it is safe
  • Follow the health team's advice if you were in contact with a sick person

Questions and answers

What is Diphtheria?

A serious germ infection of the throat and nose that can make breathing difficult. A germ makes a poison in the throat or nose, causing sore throat, fever, swollen neck glands and a thick grey coating that can block breathing, so it needs hospital care at once.

What are the common signs of Diphtheria?

Sore, scratchy throat · Swollen glands in the neck · Hoarse, weak voice

Why does Diphtheria happen?

Germs (infection) · Crowded places and droplets

How can I look after myself with Diphtheria?

Wash your hands often · Get the recommended vaccines
