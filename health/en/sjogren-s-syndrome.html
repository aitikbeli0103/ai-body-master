Sjögren's syndrome

A long-term illness that dries out the eyes and mouth

ConditionAutoimmune & rheumaticWhole body

What it is

The body's defence system affects the glands that make tears and saliva, so the eyes and mouth feel dry and gritty. It can also cause tiredness and joint aches.

  • Eyes, mouth, joints
  • Often women over middle age
  • Dry eyes and dry mouth
Which doctor
Joint doctor (rheumatologist), eye doctor, dentist
Common in
Adults, mostly women past middle age
Contagious
No
Course
Long term; the dryness can be eased with good daily care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body's defence system attacks the glands that make tears and saliva
  • It can come alone or together with another joint or defence-system illness

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden loss of vision, or a red, painful eye: go to hospital now
  • A new lump or swelling in the cheek, jaw or neck that stays
  • Weakness, numbness or trouble walking
  • Many new tooth holes, mouth sores or trouble swallowing
  • Long-lasting fever, weight loss or a new cough

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Use eye drops made for dry eyes, as your doctor or pharmacist advises
  • Sip water often and keep your mouth moist
  • Avoid smoke, dry air and long hours on a screen

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • A severe allergy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Sjögren's syndrome?

A long-term illness that dries out the eyes and mouth. The body's defence system affects the glands that make tears and saliva, so the eyes and mouth feel dry and gritty. It can also cause tiredness and joint aches.

What are the common signs of Sjögren's syndrome?

Dry, gritty eyes · Dry mouth · Tired all the time · Aching all over · Stiff, swollen fingers

Why does Sjögren's syndrome happen?

Runs in the family · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Sjögren's syndrome?

Regular dentist check-ups
