Obsessive cleanliness (germ phobia)

A strong need to stay clean, with fear of germs, that takes up a lot of the day

ConditionTold by a doctor: anxiety, fears & stressWhole body

What it is

Some people feel very afraid of dirt or germs and wash or clean again and again. It can make daily life tiring and stressful.

  • Mind and whole body
  • Can start at any age
  • Worry, then repeated washing
Which doctor
Family doctor, psychiatrist or clinical psychologist
Common in
Teens and adults; often starts in young adulthood
Contagious
No
Course
Can come and go with stress; support from a doctor can help many people

Common signs

More signs

  • Strong fear of germs, dirt or being contaminated
  • Washing hands or cleaning again and again
  • Avoiding touching doors, money or other people
  • Feeling upset if things are not clean or in order

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Anxiety, and a tendency to worry a lot

See a doctor first if

  • Thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999
  • Skin that is cracked, bleeding, red or infected from washing
  • Cleaning takes up hours of the day or stops you working or going out
  • Feeling very low, hopeless or unable to cope
  • Using strong chemicals on your skin or body

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Talk to someone you trust about how you feel
  • Ask your doctor about talking therapy and other support
  • Try slow breathing when the urge to wash comes

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
