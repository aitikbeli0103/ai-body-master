Familial high cholesterol (hypercholesterolaemia)

A condition passed down in families that causes very high cholesterol from birth

ConditionBlood sugar, fats, uric acid & thyroidChest

What it is

The body clears cholesterol poorly because of a gene passed down in the family, so blood cholesterol stays high from a young age, often with no signs.

  • Blood and blood vessels
  • Runs in families
  • Often no signs
Which doctor
Family doctor, heart doctor (cardiologist), hormone doctor
Common in
People with family members who have very high cholesterol or early heart disease
Contagious
No
Course
Lifelong, but a doctor can guide you on keeping cholesterol in check

Common signs

More signs

  • Yellowish fatty lumps on the tendons, such as the back of the heel or hands
  • Yellowish patches around the eyelids
  • A grey-white ring around the coloured part of the eye at a young age

Why it happens

  • A gene passed down from one or both parents
  • Each child of an affected parent may carry the gene
  • Food and lifestyle make it worse but are not the main cause
  • Other causes of high cholesterol may also exist; a doctor can check

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, pressure or tightness, or pain spreading to the arm or jaw: call 999
  • Sudden face drooping, arm weakness or trouble speaking: call 999
  • Short of breath, faint or cold sweats: go to hospital now
  • Pain in the calves when walking that eases with rest
  • Close family with early heart disease: ask for a cholesterol check

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
