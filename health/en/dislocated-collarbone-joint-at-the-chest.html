Dislocated collarbone joint at the chest

The joint where the collarbone meets the breastbone slips out of place

ConditionChest & ribsNeckChest

What it is

The inner end of the collarbone moves out of its joint with the breastbone, at the front and top of the chest. It usually follows a strong force or fall.

  • Top of the chest, near the neck
  • After a strong fall or knock
  • Pain and a lump at the joint
Which doctor
Emergency department; bone doctor (orthopaedic surgeon)
Common in
Young people in sports or road accidents; rare in general
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on how far the bone has moved; a doctor decides the plan

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain and swelling where the collarbone meets the breastbone
  • A visible lump, or the joint looks different from the other side
  • More pain when you move the arm, lie flat or breathe in deeply

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A fall onto the shoulder or an outstretched arm
  • Loose joints in some people can make it easier

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing or a feeling of choking: call 999
  • Trouble swallowing, a changed voice or a noisy breath: go to hospital now
  • Injury from a road accident or a very hard blow: go to hospital now
  • A numb, pale, cold or very weak arm or hand
  • A big swelling at the front of the chest or neck
  • Chest pain, or pain that keeps getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Get it checked by a doctor first, as an X-ray or scan is often needed
  • Do not try to push the bone back yourself

Tell your therapist

  • Recent shoulder injury or surgery
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
