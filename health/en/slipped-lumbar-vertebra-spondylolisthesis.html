Slipped lumbar vertebra (spondylolisthesis)

A bone in the lower spine slides forward over the bone below it

ConditionBones, joints & spineLower backBones & muscles

What it is

One bone of the lower spine slips forward over the one below. This may cause lower back pain, stiffness, or leg pain when standing or walking.

  • Lower back, maybe the legs
  • Older adults, some athletes
  • Aching lower back
Which doctor
Family doctor, orthopaedic or spine doctor
Common in
Older adults, and young people in sports that bend the back
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; many people cope well, and a doctor can advise on the options

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A weakness in the bone from birth or a small crack from repeated strain

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot control your bladder or bowel, or numbness around the groin or buttocks: go to hospital now
  • A leg that is getting weak or numb
  • Back pain after a fall or accident
  • Back pain with fever, or unexplained weight loss
  • Pain that is severe or keeps you from walking

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep gently active; short walks are usually fine
  • Strengthen your belly and back muscles gently, as advised by a professional

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy
