Colon polyp

A small growth on the lining of the colon (large bowel)

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

A colon polyp is a small lump that sticks out from the lining of the colon. It often causes no signs and is found during a bowel check. A doctor may remove it, as some polyps can change over time.

  • Lining of the colon
  • Common as people get older
  • Often found by a bowel check
Which doctor
Family doctor; a bowel specialist for a scope test (colonoscopy)
Common in
Adults, more often as they get older; sometimes in several family members
Contagious
No
Course
Many stay small and quiet for a long time; checks help a doctor decide what to do

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Changes in the cells of the bowel lining as we get older
  • Polyps or bowel cancer in close family members
  • Long-lasting inflammation of the bowel

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy bleeding from the bottom, or fainting: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Strong belly pain with a swollen belly, vomiting, or no stool or wind: go to hospital now
  • Blood in the stool that is new or keeps coming back
  • A change in your bowel habit that lasts more than a few weeks
  • Losing weight without trying, or feeling very tired and pale
  • Polyps or bowel cancer in close family: ask your doctor when to start bowel checks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Why does Colon polyp happen?
