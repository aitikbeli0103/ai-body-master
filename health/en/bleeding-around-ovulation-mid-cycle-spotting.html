Bleeding around ovulation (mid-cycle spotting)

Light bleeding or spotting that happens around the time of ovulation, between periods

ConditionWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

Some women notice a little pink or brown blood in the middle of the cycle, around the time an egg is released. It is often light and short.

  • Womb and ovaries
  • Women of child-bearing age
  • Light spotting mid-cycle
Which doctor
Gynaecologist or family doctor
Common in
Women who still have periods; more noticeable around ovulation
Contagious
No
Course
Usually lasts a day or two and can come and go from month to month

Common signs

  • A few drops of pink or brown blood in the middle of the cycle
  • Light bleeding that lasts only a day or two
  • A mild one-sided ache low in the belly at the same time
  • Clear, stretchy discharge around the same days

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Changes in hormones after starting, stopping or missing birth control
  • Other causes such as infection, polyps or fibroids, which a doctor can check

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy bleeding that soaks a pad in an hour, or dizziness and fainting: go to hospital now
  • Bleeding with severe belly pain, especially if you might be pregnant: go to hospital now
  • Bleeding after sex, or bleeding that keeps coming back every month
  • Bleeding with fever or foul-smelling discharge
  • Any bleeding after menopause

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Use a pad or panty liner rather than a tampon for light spotting
  • Rest, sleep well and keep stress low
  • Have your regular cervical check as advised by your doctor

Tell your therapist

  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine
