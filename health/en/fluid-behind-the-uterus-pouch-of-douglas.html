Fluid behind the uterus (pouch of Douglas)

A small amount of fluid in the space behind the womb

SymptomWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

The pouch of Douglas is a small space behind the womb. A little fluid there is common and often normal, but a larger amount needs a doctor to find the reason.

  • Behind the womb
  • Women, seen on a scan
  • Often no feeling
Which doctor
Women's doctor (gynaecologist)
Common in
Women of child-bearing age; often found on a scan
Contagious
No, but an infection that causes it may spread
Course
A small amount often goes away by itself; a doctor decides if more checks are needed

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Normal fluid released around the time of ovulation
  • Pelvic infection or inflammation
  • Endometriosis or a burst ovarian cyst
  • Less often, other illnesses in the belly; a doctor needs to check

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden severe belly pain, fainting or a swollen hard belly: call 999
  • Pain with a high fever
  • Pregnancy with bleeding or pain on one side: go to hospital now
  • Pain that keeps getting worse, or a belly that keeps swelling

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
