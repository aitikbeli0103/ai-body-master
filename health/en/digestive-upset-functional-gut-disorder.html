Digestive upset (functional gut disorder)

The stomach and bowel do not work smoothly, causing belly upset, bloating and stool changes, with no clear damage found

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

Tests often show no clear damage in the stomach or bowel, yet they do not work smoothly. This can cause belly pain, bloating, feeling sick, or loose or hard stools that come and go.

  • Stomach and bowel
  • Often linked to stress
  • Bloating, pain, stool changes
Which doctor
Family doctor; a gut doctor (gastroenterologist) if it continues
Common in
Adults and teenagers; often linked with stress or irregular meals
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes for a long time; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Irregular meals, eating too fast, or foods that do not suit your gut
  • Often no single cause is found

See a doctor first if

  • Severe belly pain that does not ease, a hard swollen belly, or repeated vomiting: go to hospital now
  • Upper belly pain with chest pain, trouble breathing or sweating: call 999
  • Vomiting blood or passing black stools: go to hospital now
  • Losing weight without trying, fever, or belly pain or diarrhoea that wakes you at night: see a doctor soon
  • Signs of dehydration, such as a very dry mouth, little urine or dizziness: see a doctor today
  • Blood in the stools, or new bowel changes lasting weeks, especially in an older adult: see a doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Eat regular meals slowly, and in moderate amounts
  • Notice which foods upset you and cut down on those, without following extreme diets
  • Talk to someone you trust about stress, and tell your doctor if it keeps coming back

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Fever or an active infection

What are the common signs of Digestive upset (functional gut disorder)?

Cramping belly pain · Bloated, gassy belly · Pain in the upper belly · Feeling sick, nausea · Loose, frequent stools · Hard to pass stools

Why does Digestive upset (functional gut disorder) happen?

Stress · Germs (infection) · Drinking alcohol

How can I look after myself with Digestive upset (functional gut disorder)?

Cut down on coffee and tea · Get good sleep
