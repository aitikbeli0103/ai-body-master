Essential thrombocythaemia (high platelet count)

A blood condition where the bone marrow makes too many platelets, the cells that help blood clot

ConditionBloodBlood

What it is

The bone marrow makes more platelets than the body needs. This can make clots more likely, and sometimes bleeding.

  • Blood and bone marrow
  • Often found in adults
  • Headache, tingling hands
Which doctor
Blood doctor (haematologist), family doctor
Common in
Adults, more often middle-aged and older people
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long term; many people are monitored with regular check-ups

Common signs

More signs

  • A full feeling in the upper left belly

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Often a change in the genes of blood-making cells in the bone marrow
  • It is usually not passed down in families
  • Smoking, high blood pressure and high cholesterol raise the chance of clots

See a doctor first if

  • Face drooping, arm weakness or trouble speaking: call 999
  • Chest pain or sudden trouble breathing: call 999
  • Sudden loss or change of vision, or a very bad headache
  • A swollen, painful, warm leg or arm
  • Bleeding that will not stop, or black stools

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Essential thrombocythaemia (high platelet count)?

A blood condition where the bone marrow makes too many platelets, the cells that help blood clot. The bone marrow makes more platelets than the body needs. This can make clots more likely, and sometimes bleeding.

What are the common signs of Essential thrombocythaemia (high platelet count)?

Often no signs at first · Tired all the time · Tingling in the hand · Bruises easily

Why does Essential thrombocythaemia (high platelet count) happen?

The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Essential thrombocythaemia (high platelet count)?

See your doctor for check-ups · Stop smoking · Ask your pharmacist
