Hand injury

Any cut, crush, burn or break to the hand or fingers

ConditionShoulder, arm & handArms & legs

What it is

The hand has many small bones, nerves and tendons, so even a small injury can affect how well it works. Early care matters.

  • Hand and fingers
  • Work, kitchen, home
  • Pain, bleeding, swelling
Which doctor
Emergency department; hand or bone doctor (orthopaedic, plastic)
Common in
Workers, home cooks, drivers and children; after accidents
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on how deep the injury is; a doctor can explain what to expect

Common signs

More signs

  • Cuts, bleeding or torn skin on the hand
  • A finger that looks bent or cannot move

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Knives, glass, machines or tools at work or at home
  • Doors, car doors or heavy objects crushing the hand
  • Hot oil, water or sparks causing burns

See a doctor first if

  • Bleeding that does not stop after firm pressure for ten minutes: call 999
  • A finger or part of it cut off: go to hospital now (see the lost finger page)
  • Fingers that are pale, cold, blue or numb
  • A deep cut, a bent finger or a bone that may be broken
  • Redness, pus, fever or red streaks up the arm after the injury

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Press firmly with a clean cloth and keep the hand raised above the heart
  • Take off rings and watches early before the swelling starts
  • Rinse small dirty cuts with clean running water
  • Cover with a clean dressing and let a doctor look at it
  • Ask your doctor about a tetanus shot if the wound was dirty

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Numb or tingling arm

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Hand injury?

Any cut, crush, burn or break to the hand or fingers. The hand has many small bones, nerves and tendons, so even a small injury can affect how well it works. Early care matters.

What are the common signs of Hand injury?

Swelling and bruising · Tingling in the hand · Weak, clumsy grip

Why does Hand injury happen?

An injury or fall

How can I look after myself with Hand injury?

Press firmly with a clean cloth and keep the hand raised above the heart · Take off rings and watches early before the swelling starts · Rinse small dirty cuts with clean running water · Cover with a clean dressing and let a doctor look at it · Ask your doctor about a tetanus shot if the wound was dirty
