Idiopathic pulmonary haemosiderosis

A rare lung condition with repeated bleeding in the lungs, mostly in children

ConditionLungs & breathingChest

What it is

A rare condition where small amounts of blood leak into the lungs again and again, and the cause is not known. A specialist doctor is needed.

  • Lungs
  • Rare, mostly in children
  • Cough, pale, tired
Which doctor
Lung doctor (respiratory specialist); children's doctor for a child
Common in
Mostly children, but adults can get it; it is rare
Contagious
No
Course
Long-term and may come and go; it needs ongoing care from a specialist

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body's defence system may attack the lungs, but this is not fully understood
  • It can be linked to some other conditions, which a doctor will check
  • Not caused by anything a parent or patient did

See a doctor first if

  • Coughing up blood, or a lot of blood: call 999
  • Severe trouble breathing, blue lips or fainting: call 999
  • A child who is very pale, floppy, or breathing fast: go to hospital now
  • A cough that keeps coming back with tiredness and paleness
  • Poor growth or weight in a child

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

What are the common signs of Idiopathic pulmonary haemosiderosis?

The cause is not fully known
