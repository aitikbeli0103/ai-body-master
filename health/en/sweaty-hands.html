Sweaty hands

Hands that are damp or dripping with sweat, even when it is not hot

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsArms & legs

What it is

The palms sweat much more than usual, so the hands feel wet and slippery, and it often gets worse with nerves.

  • Palms of the hands
  • Often starts when young
  • Wet, slippery hands
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor (dermatologist) if it is troublesome
Common in
Children, teens and adults; often runs in families
Contagious
No
Course
Often lasts for years and can come and go; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

More signs

  • Wet marks on paper, and phones or tools that slip
  • Often worse when nervous, excited or in hot weather
  • Hands that feel cold and clammy
  • Feeling shy about shaking hands

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Nervousness, stress or strong emotions make it worse
  • Hot or humid weather can make it worse
  • Sometimes linked to other medical problems or to some medicines

See a doctor first if

  • Cold, clammy sweat with chest pain or pressure, or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Cold, clammy sweat with fainting, confusion or a racing heart: go to hospital now
  • Sweating that starts suddenly in an adult, or comes with fever, night sweats or weight loss
  • Sweating with shaking, a racing heart, or feeling hot all the time
  • Skin on the hands that is cracked, red, sore or looks infected
  • Sweating that gets in the way of work, school or daily tasks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Use an antiperspirant on clean, dry palms; a pharmacist can suggest a suitable one
  • Carry a small towel or tissues for wiping your hands
  • Try slow, calm breathing when you feel nervous

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
