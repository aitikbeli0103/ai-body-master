Incomplete right bundle branch block

A small change on a heart tracing (ECG) showing a slight delay in the heart's electrical signal on the right side

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsBlood

What it is

The heart's electrical signal travels a little more slowly down the right side. It is often seen in healthy people and usually causes no signs, but your doctor decides if any more checks are needed.

  • Seen on a heart tracing
  • Often found by chance
  • Usually no signs
Which doctor
Family doctor; heart doctor (cardiologist) if needed
Common in
Adults and children; often found at a check-up
Contagious
No
Course
Often stays the same for years; your doctor will say if it needs follow-up

Common signs

More signs

  • It is found on a heart tracing (ECG) done for another reason
  • Fainting or a racing heartbeat only if another heart problem is also present
  • Tiredness or breathlessness only if a heart or lung problem is also present

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Often it is just a normal variation in a healthy heart
  • Sometimes it goes with a lung problem or strain on the right side of the heart

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, severe trouble breathing or fainting: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • A very fast or very slow heartbeat with dizziness
  • New breathlessness or swollen ankles
  • A family history of heart disease and you feel unwell: tell your doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
