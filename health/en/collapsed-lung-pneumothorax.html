Collapsed lung (pneumothorax)

Air leaking into the space around the lung so the lung cannot open fully

ConditionLungs & breathingChest

What it is

Air gets between the lung and the chest wall and the lung shrinks back. It often brings sudden chest pain and breathlessness and needs a doctor quickly.

  • One side of the chest
  • Tall, thin people, smokers
  • Sudden sharp pain
Which doctor
Emergency doctor, chest doctor (respirologist)
Common in
Tall, thin young men; smokers; people with lung disease
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on its size and cause; it needs a doctor's care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A weak spot on the lung that bursts, often with no clear reason
  • A hit or injury to the chest
  • Lung diseases such as asthma or long-term lung disease
  • Some medical procedures, or changes in air pressure

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden chest pain with trouble breathing: call 999 now
  • Blue lips, fainting or confusion: call 999
  • Breathing that is getting worse or a very fast heartbeat
  • Chest pain after an injury to the chest: go to hospital now
  • It has happened before and the pain comes back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

Questions and answers

What is Collapsed lung (pneumothorax)?

Air leaking into the space around the lung so the lung cannot open fully. Air gets between the lung and the chest wall and the lung shrinks back. It often brings sudden chest pain and breathlessness and needs a doctor quickly.

What are the common signs of Collapsed lung (pneumothorax)?

Sharp pain when breathing in · Short of breath on stairs · Dry, tickly cough · Racing, pounding heart

Why does Collapsed lung (pneumothorax) happen?

Smoking

How can I look after myself with Collapsed lung (pneumothorax)?

Stop smoking · See your doctor for check-ups
