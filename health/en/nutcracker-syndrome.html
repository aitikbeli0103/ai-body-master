Nutcracker syndrome

A vein that carries blood from the left kidney is squeezed between two blood vessels

ConditionKidneys & urinaryAbdomen

What it is

A vein from the left kidney gets squeezed between two larger blood vessels, like a nut in a nutcracker. Some people have no signs; others have pain or blood in the urine.

  • Left kidney vein
  • Often young and slim adults
  • Side pain, blood in urine
Which doctor
Urologist or kidney doctor; family doctor first
Common in
Often found in slim young adults; also seen in children
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; some people improve over time and some need a doctor's follow-up

Common signs

More signs

  • Protein in the urine found on a test

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The vein is squeezed by nearby blood vessels
  • Being very slim or losing weight quickly may play a part
  • Growth spurts in teenagers

See a doctor first if

  • Strong side or belly pain with vomiting or fever: go to hospital now
  • Fainting or feeling about to faint
  • Heavy blood in the urine, or clots
  • Blood in the urine that keeps coming back
  • Swelling of the legs, or very little urine

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

What are the common signs of Nutcracker syndrome?

Why does Nutcracker syndrome happen?

The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Nutcracker syndrome?
