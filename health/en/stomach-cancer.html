Stomach cancer

Cancer that starts in the lining of the stomach

ConditionCancer & tumoursAbdomen

What it is

Cells in the lining of the stomach grow out of control; it often takes years to develop and may cause few signs at first.

  • In the upper belly
  • More common in older adults
  • Upper belly discomfort
Which doctor
Family doctor first; then a stomach doctor (gastroenterologist) and cancer doctor
Common in
Mostly older adults, and more often in men
Contagious
No
Course
Often slow at first, but it can spread; your cancer team can explain what to expect

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A long-lasting stomach infection with a germ called H. pylori
  • Long-lasting stomach inflammation, or an old stomach operation

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood, or black, sticky, bad-smelling stools: go to hospital now
  • Severe belly pain, or vomiting that will not stop: go to hospital now
  • Fainting, or looking very pale and short of breath: go to hospital now
  • Trouble swallowing, or food that keeps coming back up
  • Indigestion, a full feeling or belly pain that lasts for weeks
  • Losing weight without trying, or loss of appetite for weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Questions and answers

What is Stomach cancer?

Cancer that starts in the lining of the stomach. Cells in the lining of the stomach grow out of control; it often takes years to develop and may cause few signs at first.

What are the common signs of Stomach cancer?

Pain in the upper belly · Burning behind the breastbone · Feeling sick, nausea · No appetite · Losing weight without trying · Bloated, gassy belly

Why does Stomach cancer happen?

Smoking · Salty, smoked or preserved food · Getting older

How can I look after myself with Stomach cancer?

Follow your care team's plan · Eat small, regular meals · Stop smoking · Talk to family and friends

Can an AI Body Master session help with Stomach cancer?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Stomach cancer and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
