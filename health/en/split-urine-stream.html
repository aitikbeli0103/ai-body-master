Split urine stream

Urine that sprays or splits in two instead of one steady stream

SymptomKidneys & urinaryAbdomen

What it is

The urine stream divides or sprays sideways. It is often harmless, but it can also show a narrowing or blockage along the urine tube.

  • Bladder and urine tube
  • Seen more in men
  • Weak or split stream
Which doctor
Family doctor; urologist if it stays
Common in
Men with a big prostate; anyone with a narrow urine tube
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; a one-off split is often harmless

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A few drops at the tip of the urine opening
  • Infection or a small stone

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine at all, or a painful swollen lower belly: go to hospital now
  • Fever with chills together with urine problems
  • Blood in the urine
  • A stream that keeps getting weaker or slower
  • Pain when passing urine, or leaking after you finish

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

What is Split urine stream?

Urine that sprays or splits in two instead of one steady stream. The urine stream divides or sprays sideways. It is often harmless, but it can also show a narrowing or blockage along the urine tube.

What are the common signs of Split urine stream?

Needing the toilet often · Waking at night to pass urine · Burning when passing urine

Why does Split urine stream happen?

An enlarged prostate · An injury or fall

How can I look after myself with Split urine stream?
