Myocardial bridge (artery tunnelled in heart muscle)

A heart artery that runs through the heart muscle instead of on top of it

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

A small part of a heart artery is tucked inside the heart muscle. Many people have no problems, but some feel chest discomfort.

  • Artery in the heart wall
  • Present from birth
  • Chest discomfort
Which doctor
Heart doctor (cardiologist), family doctor
Common in
Born with it; often found by chance on a heart scan
Contagious
No
Course
Many people never have symptoms; a doctor can explain what applies to you

Common signs

Why it happens

  • It is a difference in how the artery formed before birth
  • It is usually not caused by anything you did
  • Symptoms may come when the heart beats fast, such as in exercise
  • Other heart artery problems can happen at the same time

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain that is strong, lasts, or comes with sweating: call 999
  • Fainting or nearly fainting
  • Sudden trouble breathing: call 999
  • A fast heartbeat that does not settle
  • New chest pain on effort, even if mild

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

More ways to look after yourself

  • Stop and rest if chest discomfort starts
