High homocysteine (hyperhomocysteinaemia)

A higher than usual level of homocysteine, a substance in the blood, found on a blood test

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsBlood

What it is

Homocysteine is a natural substance made by the body. A high level on a blood test can go with a higher chance of problems in the blood vessels, so doctors like to look into it.

  • In the blood
  • Found on a blood test
  • Usually no signs
Which doctor
Family doctor; heart doctor or blood doctor if needed
Common in
Adults; more likely with low vitamin intake, kidney problems or smoking
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; your doctor can recheck the level over time

Common signs

More signs

  • Signs of a blood clot or narrowed blood vessels in some people
  • Often found when checking someone with heart disease risk

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Not enough folate (folic acid), vitamin B12 or vitamin B6 in the diet
  • Kidney problems or an underactive thyroid

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden chest pain or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • One leg suddenly swollen, red, warm or painful: go to hospital now
  • Numbness, tingling or weakness that keeps getting worse
  • You are pregnant and were told your level is high

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
