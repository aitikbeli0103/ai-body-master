Proctitis (rectal inflammation)

Inflammation of the lining of the last part of the bowel, the rectum

ConditionStomach & intestinesPelvis

What it is

The lining of the rectum, the last part of the bowel, becomes sore and inflamed. It can cause pain, a feeling of needing the toilet, and mucus or blood in the stool.

  • Lower belly and back passage
  • Many possible causes
  • Pain and urgent toilet need
Which doctor
Family doctor, stomach and bowel doctor (gastroenterologist)
Common in
Adults; people with bowel inflammation, some infections, or past pelvic radiation
Contagious
Some causes can be
Course
Depends on the cause; a doctor needs to find it first

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain or a full feeling in the back passage
  • A frequent, urgent need to pass stool, even when little comes out
  • Mucus, pus or blood in the stool

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Bowel inflammation that lasts a long time, such as Crohn's disease or colitis
  • Infections, including some that spread through sex
  • Radiation to the pelvis for cancer care
  • Often the cause needs tests to find out

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy bleeding from the back passage, or feeling faint: go to hospital now
  • Severe belly pain or a hard, swollen belly: go to hospital now
  • Fever with chills, or feeling very unwell
  • Blood or mucus in the stool that lasts more than a few days
  • Losing weight without trying

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
