Bladder carcinoma in situ

Early cancer cells found only in the inner lining of the bladder

ConditionCancer & tumoursPelvis

What it is

Cancer cells are found only on the inner surface of the bladder and have not grown into the deeper wall. It needs a specialist's care and regular check-ups.

  • Inner lining of the bladder
  • Mostly in adults
  • Burning, urgent urine
Which doctor
Urologist; cancer specialist (oncologist) if needed
Common in
Adults, more often in smokers and people exposed to some chemicals
Contagious
No
Course
It can come back, so regular check-ups matter; your doctor will explain

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain in the lower belly

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-term bladder irritation or infections

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine, or severe pain in the lower belly: go to hospital now
  • Blood or clots in the urine
  • Fever with chills and back or side pain
  • Weight loss you cannot explain
  • Urine signs that come back after treatment

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Questions and answers

What is Bladder carcinoma in situ?

Early cancer cells found only in the inner lining of the bladder. Cancer cells are found only on the inner surface of the bladder and have not grown into the deeper wall. It needs a specialist's care and regular check-ups.

What are the common signs of Bladder carcinoma in situ?

Pink or red urine · Burning when passing urine · Needing the toilet often · Often no signs at first

Why does Bladder carcinoma in situ happen?

Smoking · Dust or chemicals at work · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Bladder carcinoma in situ?

See your doctor for check-ups · Stop smoking

Can an AI Body Master session help with Bladder carcinoma in situ?
