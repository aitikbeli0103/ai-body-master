Tobacco or nicotine dependence

A strong craving and habit of using tobacco or nicotine that is hard to stop

ConditionSmokingChestWhole body

What it is

Nicotine makes the body and mind used to it, so going without can bring cravings and feeling edgy. Support from a doctor can make stopping easier.

  • Lungs and whole body
  • Adults and teenagers
  • Strong urge to smoke
Which doctor
Family doctor, quit-smoking clinic
Common in
People who smoke, vape or chew tobacco; often starts when young
Contagious
No, but second-hand smoke can harm other people
Course
Cravings often ease over weeks after stopping; many people need several tries

Common signs

More signs

  • A strong urge to smoke or vape, often after meals or when stressed
  • Smoking soon after waking up
  • Finding it hard to stop even when you want to

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Nicotine is a habit-forming substance
  • Starting at a young age
  • Low mood or anxiety can make stopping harder

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, trouble breathing, face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Coughing up blood: go to hospital now
  • A cough that lasts more than a few weeks, or losing weight without trying
  • Feeling very low or thoughts of harming yourself while quitting: see a doctor now or call 999
  • You are pregnant and still smoking: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Pick a quit day and tell family and friends
  • Ask your doctor or pharmacist about quit-smoking support
  • Avoid triggers; sip water or go for a walk when a craving comes
  • Throw away cigarettes, vapes and lighters
  • If you slip, try again; many people need several tries

Tell your therapist

  • A heart condition
  • High blood pressure
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Tobacco or nicotine dependence?

Stress · Smoke, haze and strong smells
