Still's disease

A rare inflammatory illness with fever, rash and joint pain

ConditionAutoimmune & rheumaticSkin

What it is

Still's disease is a rare illness in which the body's defence system causes inflammation, leading to repeated high fevers, a skin rash and sore joints.

  • Skin and joints
  • Often starts in young adults
  • Fever, rash, sore joints
Which doctor
Rheumatologist (joint and immune doctor), family doctor first
Common in
Young adults, but it can start at any age
Contagious
No
Course
Can come in flare-ups or last a long time; a doctor can plan follow-up

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body's defence system becomes overactive and causes inflammation
  • It is not caused by something you did or ate
  • Some people may have a trigger such as an infection

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing or chest pain: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • A fever that lasts many days with no clear reason
  • Confusion, severe weakness or fainting
  • Joint pain or swelling that keeps coming back or is getting worse
  • A new rash together with a high fever

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
