High uric acid

Higher-than-normal uric acid in the blood, often with no signs

ConditionBlood sugar, fats, uric acid & thyroidAbdomen

What it is

Uric acid is a waste the body makes and passes out in urine, and when the level in the blood stays high it can lead to gout or kidney stones in some people.

  • Level of uric acid in the blood
  • Men, and women after menopause
  • Often no signs; found on a test
Which doctor
Family doctor; joint doctor (rheumatologist) or kidney doctor
Common in
Men, and women after menopause; people who are overweight or have family members with gout
Contagious
No
Course
Often long-term; levels change with food, weight, water intake and medicines, so check-ups help

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body makes too much uric acid, or the kidneys do not remove enough of it
  • Uric acid is made when the body breaks down purines, found in some foods and drinks
  • Lots of red meat, organ meats, seafood, beer or other alcohol, and sugary drinks
  • Kidney problems, or some medicines such as water pills; ask your doctor, and do not stop any medicine yourself

See a doctor first if

  • A hot, red, swollen joint with fever or chills: go to hospital the same day
  • Severe pain in the side or back that comes in waves, with vomiting, fever or blood in the urine: go to hospital now
  • Sudden, severe joint pain and swelling: see a doctor to find out the cause
  • Passing much less urine than usual, or swelling of the face or legs: see a doctor soon
  • Joint pain that keeps coming back, or hard lumps near the joints: see a doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Limit beer and other alcohol, and sugary drinks
  • Eat less red meat, organ meats (such as liver) and seafood; eat more vegetables and whole grains
  • Be active on most days, for example walking

Tell your therapist

  • A kidney or liver condition
  • High blood pressure
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A heart condition
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
