Gallbladder cancer

Cancer that starts in the gallbladder, the small pouch under the liver that stores bile

ConditionCancer & tumoursAbdomen

What it is

Gallbladder cancer is not common. It often causes no signs early on, so it is frequently found late or by chance, for example during a scan or gallbladder surgery.

  • Gallbladder, under the liver
  • Older adults; more in women
  • Often no signs early on
Which doctor
Family doctor, then a liver and bile specialist, surgeon or cancer doctor
Common in
Mostly older adults, more often women; people with long-term gallstones
Contagious
No
Course
Often found late as early signs are few; your cancer team can explain more

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Gallstones or long-term gallbladder inflammation (most people with gallstones never get this cancer)
  • A hard, calcified gallbladder wall, or large polyps in the gallbladder
  • Being very overweight

See a doctor first if

  • Yellow skin or eyes with fever, shivering and belly pain: go to hospital now
  • Severe belly pain that does not ease, or vomiting that will not stop: go to hospital now
  • Yellow skin or eyes, dark urine, pale stools or itching all over: see a doctor soon
  • Pain in the upper right belly that keeps coming back or lasts for weeks
  • Weight loss, no appetite or feeling very tired with no clear reason
  • A lump you can feel in the upper belly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask your doctor whether any gallstones need follow-up
  • If cancer is found, follow your cancer team's plan and keep all appointments
  • If rich or fatty food upsets you, eat smaller, lighter meals; a dietitian can advise you
  • Never stop treatment on your own; tell your team about pain, itching or low mood

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Fever or an active infection

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Gallbladder cancer?

Cancer that starts in the gallbladder, the small pouch under the liver that stores bile. Gallbladder cancer is not common. It often causes no signs early on, so it is frequently found late or by chance, for example during a scan or gallbladder surgery.

What are the common signs of Gallbladder cancer?

Pain in the upper belly · Yellow skin and eyes · Itchy skin · Feeling sick, nausea · No appetite · Losing weight without trying

Why does Gallbladder cancer happen?

Getting older · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Gallbladder cancer?

Keep a healthy weight
