Long-sightedness (hyperopia)

An eye focus problem where close things look blurry but far things are clearer

ConditionEyesHead

What it is

The eye focuses light slightly behind the retina, so reading and close work can look blurry and make the eyes tired.

  • Both eyes, often
  • Children and older adults
  • Blurry close-up vision
Which doctor
Eye doctor (ophthalmologist) or optometrist
Common in
Children, and adults as they get older
Contagious
No
Course
Often lasts for life but glasses can help; a child may grow out of mild cases

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The eyeball is a little short, or the front of the eye is a little flat
  • Sometimes it is linked to another eye or health problem

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden loss of vision or sudden new blurring: go to hospital now
  • A painful, red eye with headache or feeling sick: go to hospital now
  • Flashes of light or many new floating spots
  • A child who squints, rubs the eyes a lot or struggles at school
  • Headaches or tired eyes that keep coming back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Have your eyes checked regularly, and your children's too
  • Wear the glasses or contact lenses your eye doctor suggests
  • Use good light for reading and take short breaks from close work
  • Keep the screen at a comfortable distance

Tell your therapist

  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Long-sightedness (hyperopia)?

An eye focus problem where close things look blurry but far things are clearer. The eye focuses light slightly behind the retina, so reading and close work can look blurry and make the eyes tired.

What are the common signs of Long-sightedness (hyperopia)?

Tired, strained eyes · Blurry vision · Headache across the forehead

Why does Long-sightedness (hyperopia) happen?

Runs in the family · Getting older

How can I look after myself with Long-sightedness (hyperopia)?

Have your eyes checked regularly, and your children's too · Wear the glasses or contact lenses your eye doctor suggests · Use good light for reading and take short breaks from close work · Keep the screen at a comfortable distance

Can an AI Body Master session help with Long-sightedness (hyperopia)?
