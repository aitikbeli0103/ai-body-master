Hip joint tuberculosis (TB)

A slow bone and joint infection of the hip caused by TB germs

ConditionInfectionsArms & legs

What it is

TB germs can spread from another part of the body to the hip joint. The hip slowly becomes painful and stiff, and walking gets harder.

  • Hip joint
  • Children and adults
  • Hip pain, limping
Which doctor
Bone doctor (orthopaedic), chest or infection doctor
Common in
Children and young adults; people with weak body defences
Contagious
The hip itself rarely spreads, but TB in the lungs can
Course
Develops slowly over months; it needs a doctor's long treatment plan

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • TB germs that travel through the blood from the lungs or elsewhere
  • Living closely with someone who has active TB
  • Weak body defences, such as from diabetes, HIV or poor nutrition

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden weakness or numbness in the legs, or trouble passing urine: go to hospital now
  • Cannot put any weight on the leg, or the leg looks bent or shorter
  • Coughing blood, or a long cough with fever and night sweats
  • A child who limps, has hip pain or will not walk: see a doctor promptly
  • A hip that is hot, red or swollen with fever

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Hip joint tuberculosis (TB)?

A slow bone and joint infection of the hip caused by TB germs. TB germs can spread from another part of the body to the hip joint. The hip slowly becomes painful and stiff, and walking gets harder.

What are the common signs of Hip joint tuberculosis (TB)?

Pain in the hip · Hard to walk far · Soaking sweats at night · Losing weight without trying · Fever, feeling hot · Tired all the time

Why does Hip joint tuberculosis (TB) happen?

Long-term use of some medicines

How can I look after myself with Hip joint tuberculosis (TB)?

Take medicine as your doctor says · Rest the sore joint · Eat healthy, balanced meals · See your doctor for check-ups
