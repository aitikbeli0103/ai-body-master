Mental disorder

A health condition that changes mood, thinking or behaviour and makes daily life harder

ConditionTold by a doctor: otherWhole body

What it is

A broad term for health conditions that change how a person feels, thinks or acts, such as depression or anxiety. They are real health problems, not weakness.

  • Mind, mood and behaviour
  • Can affect any age
  • Changes in mood or thinking
Which doctor
Family doctor, psychiatrist, psychologist or counsellor
Common in
Anyone of any age; it is common and not a sign of weakness
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; the right support helps many people, and some need long-term care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Often a mix of causes, and sometimes the cause is not known
  • Long-term stress, trauma, loss, loneliness, or money and work worries
  • Long-term illness, pain, poor sleep, or alcohol and drug use
  • Big life changes, or hard experiences in childhood

See a doctor first if

  • Thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Thoughts of harming someone else, or voices telling you to do so: call 999
  • Being very confused, or seeing or hearing things that are not there: see a doctor today
  • Not eating, drinking or sleeping for days, or not able to look after yourself
  • Feeling hopeless, or low mood or worry that lasts for weeks and spoils daily life

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Limit alcohol and avoid drugs; they can make things worse
  • See a doctor early, and keep your appointments
  • If you take medicine, ask your doctor or pharmacist before changing or stopping it

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Epilepsy or fits
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
