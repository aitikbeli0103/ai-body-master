Erythema nodosum (tender red lumps)

Tender red lumps on the skin, usually on the shins, caused by inflammation under the skin

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Erythema nodosum is inflammation in the fat layer under the skin. It causes tender red or purple lumps, most often on the shins (the front of the lower legs).

  • Usually on the shins
  • More common in women
  • Tender, warm red lumps
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor or another specialist to look for the cause
Common in
Women more than men; most often young and middle-aged adults
Contagious
No, but some infections that set it off can spread
Course
Often fades over several weeks; some take longer and it can come back

Common signs

More signs

  • The skin over the lumps feels warm, tight and sore
  • As the lumps fade they look like bruises: blue, purple or yellow-green
  • Lumps may also appear on the thighs or arms

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-term inflammatory illnesses, for example bowel disease

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain or trouble breathing: call 999
  • One leg that is red, hot, swollen and painful (it could be a clot or an infection): see a doctor today
  • High fever, shaking chills or feeling very unwell: see a doctor today
  • Lumps that open, leak pus or turn black: see a doctor promptly
  • A cough that will not go away, coughing blood, weight loss, night sweats or bloody diarrhoea: see a doctor soon
  • Lumps that last more than several weeks or keep coming back: see a doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See a doctor to look for the cause; the lumps can be linked to another illness
  • Do not rub or massage the lumps

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
