Dislocated shoulder

The ball at the top of the arm bone slips out of its socket in the shoulder

ConditionShoulder, arm & handChestBones & muscles

What it is

After a fall or a hard hit, the top of the arm bone can pop out of the shoulder socket, which is very painful and needs a doctor's care straight away.

  • Shoulder joint
  • After a fall or a hard hit
  • Sharp pain, arm hard to move
Which doctor
Hospital emergency doctor, then a bone doctor (orthopaedic doctor)
Common in
Active young people in sports; older adults after a fall
Contagious
No
Course
Needs a doctor to put it back; recovery often takes weeks, and it can happen again

Common signs

More signs

  • Sudden, severe pain in the shoulder
  • The shoulder looks square or out of shape, or has a bump

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Loose shoulder joints, or a past dislocation, make it more likely

See a doctor first if

  • Bone sticking out of the skin, or heavy bleeding: call 999
  • After a big fall or road accident, or with pain in the neck, head or chest: call 999
  • Shoulder pain with chest pain, trouble breathing or a cold sweat: call 999
  • Shoulder looks out of shape, or you cannot move the arm after a fall or hit: go to hospital now
  • Hand or fingers numb, tingling, pale, blue or cold: go to hospital now
  • A shoulder that keeps slipping out, or pain and swelling that get worse after it was put back: see your doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not try to push the shoulder back into place yourself
  • Keep the arm still and support it against your body in the most comfortable position
  • After the doctor has put it back, follow their advice on the sling, rest and gentle exercises

Tell your therapist

  • Recent shoulder injury or surgery
  • Hot, red or swollen shoulder
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Weak bones
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

What are the common signs of Dislocated shoulder?

Why does Dislocated shoulder happen?

An injury or fall · Sports and hard knocks · A fall, knock or road accident
