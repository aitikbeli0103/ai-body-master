Chronic appendicitis

Long-lasting, mild inflammation of the appendix, a small pouch on the lower right of the belly

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

The appendix is a small pouch off the large bowel, low on the right side of the belly. When it stays mildly inflamed, you may feel a dull ache there that comes and goes for weeks or months.

  • Lower right belly
  • Comes and goes for weeks
  • Dull ache or cramps
Which doctor
Family doctor; surgeon or stomach and gut doctor
Common in
Can affect any age, including teens and adults
Contagious
No
Course
Pain may come and go for weeks or months; a doctor can check with tests

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The opening of the appendix gets blocked, for example by a small hard lump of stool
  • Swelling of the appendix wall after an infection
  • A blockage that comes and goes may let the inflammation return

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden, severe belly pain, especially on the lower right: go to hospital now
  • Belly pain with fever, vomiting, or a hard belly that hurts to touch: go to hospital now
  • Pain that gets worse when you cough, walk or jump
  • Feeling faint, a very fast heartbeat, or cold, clammy skin
  • Belly pain in a child or in someone who is pregnant: see a doctor promptly
  • Belly pain that keeps coming back or lasts more than a few days

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Eat fibre-rich food such as fruit, vegetables and whole grains to keep stools soft
  • Rest when the pain is bad; note when it comes, how long it lasts and what makes it worse
