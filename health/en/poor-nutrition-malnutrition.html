Poor nutrition (malnutrition)

The body does not get enough food or nutrients to stay strong and healthy

ConditionEating & drinkingWhole body

What it is

Malnutrition means the body does not get the right amount of food or nutrients. A person can be too thin, or even look well fed but still lack vitamins and minerals.

  • Whole body
  • Children and older adults
  • Weak, thin, tired
Which doctor
Family doctor; a dietitian
Common in
Young children, older adults, people with long illness
Contagious
No
Course
Often improves with proper food and care from a doctor

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Not enough food or too little variety in the diet
  • Trouble chewing, swallowing or low appetite, often in older people
  • Long illness, gut problems or cancer that stop the body using food well
  • Low mood, living alone or too little money for food

See a doctor first if

  • Fainting, confusion or too weak to stand: go to hospital now
  • Swollen feet, legs or face
  • A child who is not growing, very sleepy or not eating or drinking: see a doctor promptly
  • Losing weight without trying, or constant vomiting or diarrhoea
  • Cannot eat or swallow properly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

More ways to look after yourself
