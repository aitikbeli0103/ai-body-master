Buerger's disease (thromboangiitis obliterans)

Swelling and blocking of small blood vessels in the hands and feet, linked to tobacco

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsArms & legs

What it is

Small arteries and veins in the hands and feet swell and become blocked, so less blood gets through. It is strongly linked to tobacco use.

  • Hands, feet, fingers, toes
  • Linked to tobacco
  • Cold, painful fingers/toes
Which doctor
Vascular surgeon or family doctor
Common in
Mostly younger to middle-aged adults who use tobacco
Contagious
No
Course
Long-lasting; stopping all tobacco is the most important step

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • It is more often seen in men, but women can get it too

See a doctor first if

  • A finger or toe that turns black, or has a sore that will not heal
  • A hand or foot that is suddenly cold, pale, very painful or numb: go to hospital now
  • A red, hot, swollen area or pus: this may be an infection
  • Pain in the hands or feet that wakes you at night
  • Sudden chest pain or trouble breathing: call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Stop all tobacco, including chewing tobacco and vaping; ask your doctor for help
  • Keep hands and feet warm and wear soft, well-fitting shoes
  • Check your fingers and toes every day for cuts or sores
  • Protect your skin from cuts, burns and very cold

Tell your therapist

  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Buerger's disease (thromboangiitis obliterans)?

Swelling and blocking of small blood vessels in the hands and feet, linked to tobacco. Small arteries and veins in the hands and feet swell and become blocked, so less blood gets through. It is strongly linked to tobacco use.

What are the common signs of Buerger's disease (thromboangiitis obliterans)?

Cold hands and feet · Cramps in the calf · Cuts heal slowly · Hard to walk far

Why does Buerger's disease (thromboangiitis obliterans) happen?

The cause is not fully known · Smoking · Smoke, haze and strong smells

How can I look after myself with Buerger's disease (thromboangiitis obliterans)?
