Urinary incontinence in older adults

Passing urine by accident in older age, when the bladder cannot hold it

ConditionKidneys & urinaryReproductive

What it is

Urine leaks out before the person can reach the toilet, or when coughing or laughing. It is common in older age but is not something you must just accept.

  • Bladder and pelvic floor
  • More common with age
  • Leaking urine
Which doctor
Family doctor, geriatrician, urologist
Common in
Older adults, especially women and men with prostate problems
Contagious
No
Course
Often improves with the right care; a doctor can find the cause

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Childbirth in the past, or menopause in women

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden leaking with weakness, numbness or trouble speaking: call 999
  • Not able to pass urine, or a painful full bladder
  • Fever, burning or blood in the urine
  • Leaking with new back pain or loss of bowel control
  • New confusion in an older person

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Go to the toilet at regular times, before you feel desperate
  • Ask about pelvic floor exercises
  • Keep a clear, safe path to the toilet, with a light at night
  • Keep skin clean and dry, and ask about suitable pads

Tell your therapist

  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pregnancy
  • High blood pressure
  • A heart condition

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Urinary incontinence in older adults?

Passing urine by accident in older age, when the bladder cannot hold it. Urine leaks out before the person can reach the toilet, or when coughing or laughing. It is common in older age but is not something you must just accept.

What are the common signs of Urinary incontinence in older adults?

Leaking when coughing · Needing the toilet often · Waking at night to pass urine

Why does Urinary incontinence in older adults happen?

Getting older · An enlarged prostate · A side effect of some medicines
