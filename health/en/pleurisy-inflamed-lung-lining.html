Pleurisy (inflamed lung lining)

Swollen, sore lining around the lungs that hurts when you breathe in

ConditionLungs & breathingChest

What it is

The two thin layers around the lungs become sore and rub together, causing a sharp pain in the chest when you breathe in or cough.

  • Side of the chest
  • Can happen to any adult
  • Sharp pain when breathing
Which doctor
Family doctor, lung doctor (respiratory physician)
Common in
Adults after a chest infection or other lung problem
Contagious
Not itself, but some infections behind it can spread
Course
Often settles when the cause is looked after; see a doctor

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Pneumonia or other bacterial infections, including TB
  • A blood clot in the lung
  • Autoimmune conditions, such as lupus or rheumatoid arthritis

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain with sweating, pain spreading to the arm or jaw, or feeling faint: call 999
  • Sudden severe trouble breathing, or blue lips: call 999
  • Coughing up blood
  • A swollen, painful leg together with chest pain
  • High fever or shaking chills
  • Chest pain that lasts more than a day or two

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Lie on the side that hurts if it eases the pain
  • Hold a pillow against your chest when you cough
  • Drink enough water and do not smoke

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • A heart condition
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Cancer or cancer treatment

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Pleurisy (inflamed lung lining)?

Swollen, sore lining around the lungs that hurts when you breathe in. The two thin layers around the lungs become sore and rub together, causing a sharp pain in the chest when you breathe in or cough.

What are the common signs of Pleurisy (inflamed lung lining)?

Sharp pain when breathing in · Dry, tickly cough · Short of breath on stairs · Fever, feeling hot

Why does Pleurisy (inflamed lung lining) happen?

Germs (infection) · An injury or fall

How can I look after myself with Pleurisy (inflamed lung lining)?

Rest well · Ask your pharmacist
