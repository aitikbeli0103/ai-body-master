Alzheimer's disease

A brain disease that slowly affects memory, thinking and daily life

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

Over time, the brain's cells slowly lose their work. This brings memory loss and trouble thinking, and later makes daily tasks harder. Care and support can make life easier.

  • Brain
  • More common with age
  • Forgetting, confusion
Which doctor
Family doctor, brain doctor (neurologist) or elderly care doctor
Common in
Mostly people over 65; sometimes younger
Contagious
No
Course
Usually changes slowly over years; care and support help daily life

Common signs

Why it happens

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden confusion, drowsiness, or face drooping and weak arm: call 999
  • A fall, head knock, or sudden worsening: go to hospital
  • Wandering and getting lost, or leaving the stove on
  • Not eating or drinking, or signs of low mood or thoughts of self-harm
  • Memory problems that stop daily life from working: see a doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Alzheimer's disease?

A brain disease that slowly affects memory, thinking and daily life. Over time, the brain's cells slowly lose their work. This brings memory loss and trouble thinking, and later makes daily tasks harder. Care and support can make life easier.

What are the common signs of Alzheimer's disease?

Forgetting things · Foggy, cannot focus · Short-tempered, on edge · Low mood, no interest · Cannot fall asleep

Why does Alzheimer's disease happen?

The cause is not fully known · Getting older · Runs in the family · Smoking

How can I look after myself with Alzheimer's disease?

Stay active every day · Make the home safe from falls

Can an AI Body Master session help with Alzheimer's disease?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Alzheimer's disease and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
