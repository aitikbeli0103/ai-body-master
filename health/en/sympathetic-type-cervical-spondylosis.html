Sympathetic-type cervical spondylosis

Neck joint wear that may upset the nerves and blood flow, causing dizziness and other body symptoms

ConditionBones, joints & spineNeck

What it is

This is a name used for neck wear and tear that comes with a mix of symptoms such as dizziness, headache, blurry vision, ringing in the ears or a racing heart, along with neck stiffness.

  • Neck, with head symptoms
  • Adults, more with age
  • Dizzy, headache, tired eyes
Which doctor
Family doctor first; then a spine, ear-nose-throat or heart doctor if needed
Common in
Adults with neck wear and tear, long desk or phone use
Contagious
No
Course
Symptoms can come and go; other causes should be checked by a doctor first

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Poor posture, such as looking down for long hours
  • Not fully understood; the same symptoms can have other causes, so a doctor should check

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden dizziness with a drooping face, arm weakness, trouble speaking or double vision: call 999
  • Chest pain, trouble breathing or fainting: call 999
  • Sudden loss of vision or hearing: go to hospital now
  • A sudden severe headache, or neck stiffness with fever
  • Clumsy hands, trouble walking, or losing control of your bladder or bowel

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep your screen at eye level and take regular breaks from the phone
  • Do gentle neck and shoulder movements, and avoid sudden jerks of the neck
  • Stand up slowly if you feel dizzy, and sit down if it starts

Tell your therapist

  • Dizzy when turning the head
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • A heart condition
  • High blood pressure
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
