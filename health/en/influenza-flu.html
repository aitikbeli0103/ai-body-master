Influenza (flu)

A viral infection of the nose, throat and lungs that spreads easily and brings sudden fever and aches

ConditionInfectionsWhole body

What it is

The flu is caused by influenza viruses; it usually starts suddenly with fever, body aches and tiredness, and it can feel much worse than a common cold.

  • Nose, throat and lungs
  • Spreads from person to person
  • Sudden fever and body aches
Which doctor
Family doctor or clinic; hospital if very unwell
Common in
Anyone; can be more serious in young children, older adults and pregnant women
Contagious
Yes, by coughs, sneezes, close contact and touching things the virus is on
Course
Most people feel better within a week or two; the cough and tiredness can last longer

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Touching a surface with the virus on it and then your eyes, nose or mouth can also spread it
  • A person can spread the virus from about a day before signs start

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, chest pain that does not go away, or blue or grey lips or face: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Confusion, being very hard to wake, fainting or a fit: call 999
  • A baby or child who breathes fast, is very sleepy, or will not feed or drink: go to hospital now
  • Signs of dehydration: you cannot keep fluids down, pass very little urine, or feel dizzy when you stand
  • A very high fever that will not come down, or any fever for more than three days (it could be another illness, such as dengue)
  • Getting better, then suddenly worse again with a new fever, cough or trouble breathing
  • If you are older, pregnant, or have a long-term illness or weak immunity: see a doctor early, even if the signs are mild

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Stay home until you feel better and have no fever, so you do not spread it
  • Flu is caused by a virus, so antibiotics do not help; take them only if your doctor prescribes them

Questions and answers

What is Influenza (flu)?

A viral infection of the nose, throat and lungs that spreads easily and brings sudden fever and aches. The flu is caused by influenza viruses; it usually starts suddenly with fever, body aches and tiredness, and it can feel much worse than a common cold.

What are the common signs of Influenza (flu)?

Fever, feeling hot · Shivering with chills · Aching all over · Headache across the forehead · Dry, tickly cough · Sore, scratchy throat

Why does Influenza (flu) happen?

Germs (infection) · Crowded places and droplets · Close contact or sharing items · Pregnancy

How can I look after myself with Influenza (flu)?

Rest well · Wash your hands often · Ask your pharmacist · Get the recommended vaccines
