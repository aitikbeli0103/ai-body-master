Panic disorder

Sudden waves of strong fear with a pounding heart and fast breathing

ConditionTold by a doctor: anxiety, fears & stressWhole body

What it is

A panic attack is a sudden rush of fear that peaks within minutes. When attacks keep coming back and you worry about the next one, a doctor may call it panic disorder.

  • Chest, breathing, whole body
  • Often starts in early adulthood
  • Sudden strong fear
Which doctor
Family doctor, psychiatrist or clinical psychologist
Common in
Teens and adults; more often in women
Contagious
No
Course
Attacks usually fade within minutes; support from a doctor can help many people

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Too much coffee or tea, little sleep, or smoking may make attacks more likely

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, trouble breathing or fainting, especially the first time: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999
  • Pain spreading to the arm or jaw, or weakness on one side of the body: call 999
  • Attacks that keep coming back or make you afraid to go out
  • Using alcohol or other substances to cope

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Breathe in slowly through the nose and out slowly through the mouth
  • Remind yourself that an attack passes, usually within minutes
  • Talk to someone you trust and tell your doctor

Tell your therapist

  • A heart condition
  • High blood pressure
  • Pregnancy

Questions and answers

What is Panic disorder?

Sudden waves of strong fear with a pounding heart and fast breathing. A panic attack is a sudden rush of fear that peaks within minutes. When attacks keep coming back and you worry about the next one, a doctor may call it panic disorder.

What are the common signs of Panic disorder?

Racing, pounding heart · Short of breath on stairs · Sweating a lot · Tight, heavy chest · Feeling faint

Why does Panic disorder happen?

The cause is not fully known · Stress · Runs in the family

How can I look after myself with Panic disorder?

Cut down on coffee and tea · Get good sleep

Can an AI Body Master session help with Panic disorder?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Panic disorder and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
