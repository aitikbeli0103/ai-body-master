Loose rectal lining (rectal mucosal prolapse)

The inner lining of the back passage slips down and may show outside the anus

ConditionStomach & intestinesPelvis

What it is

The soft inner lining of the rectum becomes loose and slides down, sometimes showing at the anus, especially when straining on the toilet.

  • Back passage
  • Older adults, long straining
  • Feels like a lump
Which doctor
Family doctor; colorectal surgeon
Common in
Adults who strain a lot; older people
Contagious
No
Course
Often slowly gets worse if straining continues; a doctor can advise

Common signs

More signs

  • A soft lump coming out of the anus when passing stool
  • A feeling that the bowel is not fully empty

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long, repeated diarrhoea
  • Sitting a long time on the toilet

See a doctor first if

  • The lump comes out and cannot go back, becomes very painful or turns dark: go to hospital now
  • Heavy bleeding from the back passage
  • Blood in the stool that keeps happening, or a change in bowel habit
  • Leaking stool or being unable to hold it in
  • Fever with pain around the anus

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Eat more fibre such as vegetables, fruit and whole grains
  • Do not strain, and do not sit long on the toilet
  • Keep the area clean and dry, and wash gently
  • Ask your doctor about gentle exercises for the muscles of the pelvis

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
