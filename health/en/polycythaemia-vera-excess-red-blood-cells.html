Polycythaemia vera (excess red blood cells)

A blood condition in which the body makes too many red blood cells

ConditionBloodBloodWhole body

What it is

The bone marrow makes too many red blood cells, so the blood becomes thick. This can slow blood flow and raise the chance of clots.

  • In the blood and bone marrow
  • Mostly middle-aged and older
  • Headache, itch, flushing
Which doctor
Blood doctor (haematologist)
Common in
Mostly adults over middle age
Contagious
No
Course
Long-term; doctors usually follow it with regular blood tests

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Often a change in a gene in the bone marrow cells, not usually inherited
  • Other conditions can also raise red blood cells and need to be checked

See a doctor first if

  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Chest pain or sudden trouble breathing: call 999
  • One leg suddenly swollen, red and painful: go to hospital
  • Sudden vision change or severe headache: go to hospital now
  • Black stools or bleeding that does not stop
