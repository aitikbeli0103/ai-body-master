Alcohol-related brain disease (encephalopathy)

Brain problems linked to heavy, long-term drinking of alcohol

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

Long, heavy drinking can harm the brain and nerves. This may bring confusion, poor memory, unsteady walking and trouble thinking clearly.

  • Brain and nerves
  • Long, heavy drinking
  • Confusion, poor memory
Which doctor
Family doctor, brain doctor (neurologist) or mental health doctor
Common in
People who drink heavily for a long time, or who eat poorly
Contagious
No
Course
Some changes may improve if drinking stops with medical help; some can last

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Poor eating, which can lead to a lack of vitamin B1
  • A weak liver can let harmful substances reach the brain
  • Stopping drinking suddenly after heavy use can bring on dangerous reactions

See a doctor first if

  • Fits, severe shaking, seeing things that are not there: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Sudden confusion, drowsiness, or cannot be woken: call 999
  • Vomiting blood or black stools: go to hospital now
  • Yellow skin or eyes, or a swollen belly
  • Thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Talk to your doctor before cutting down; stopping suddenly can be unsafe
  • Ask about support to stop drinking, from your doctor or a support group

Questions and answers

What is Alcohol-related brain disease (encephalopathy)?

Brain problems linked to heavy, long-term drinking of alcohol. Long, heavy drinking can harm the brain and nerves. This may bring confusion, poor memory, unsteady walking and trouble thinking clearly.

What are the common signs of Alcohol-related brain disease (encephalopathy)?

Confused or muddled · Forgetting things · Unsteady when walking · Numb, tingling feet · Foggy, cannot focus

Why does Alcohol-related brain disease (encephalopathy) happen?

Drinking alcohol

How can I look after myself with Alcohol-related brain disease (encephalopathy)?

Eat healthy, balanced meals · Ask your pharmacist · See your doctor for check-ups
