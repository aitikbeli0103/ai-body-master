Atrophic vaginitis in older women

Dry, thin and sore vaginal lining in older women after menopause

ConditionWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

After menopause the body makes less oestrogen, so the vaginal lining of older women can become thin, dry and easily sore or infected. Care and a doctor's advice can make life more comfortable.

  • Vagina and urine passage
  • Older women
  • Dry, burning, sore
Which doctor
Women's doctor (gynaecologist), family doctor
Common in
Women some years after menopause
Contagious
No
Course
Often lasts without care; many women feel more comfortable with the right help

Common signs

More signs

  • A dry, tight or burning feeling in the vagina
  • Itching or soreness around the opening

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Not having sex for a long time can make the dryness feel worse
  • Removal of the ovaries or some cancer treatments

See a doctor first if

  • Any bleeding after menopause: see a doctor
  • Fever, strong lower belly pain or feeling confused: go to hospital
  • Smelly or bloody discharge
  • Blood in the urine or burning when passing urine
  • A lump or sore that does not heal

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
