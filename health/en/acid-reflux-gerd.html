Acid reflux (GERD)

Stomach acid flows back up into the food pipe and causes a burning feeling in the chest

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

Stomach acid and food flow back up into the food pipe (oesophagus), which can cause a burning feeling behind the breastbone, a sour taste in the mouth, or a sore throat.

  • Chest, throat, upper belly
  • After meals, lying down
  • Burning chest, sour taste
Which doctor
Family doctor; a gut specialist if it continues
Common in
Adults of any age; more with extra weight, pregnancy or smoking
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes; habits help, see a doctor if it keeps coming back

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The valve at the bottom of the food pipe is weak or opens at the wrong time
  • Big or fatty meals, spicy food, coffee, fizzy drinks, alcohol or chocolate can set it off
  • A hiatal hernia, where part of the stomach slips up into the chest
  • Lying down soon after eating, or taking some medicines

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain with sweating, breathlessness or pain spreading to the arm or jaw (may be the heart): call 999
  • Vomiting blood, or black or bloody stools: go to hospital now
  • Food sticking or pain when you swallow: see a doctor soon
  • Weight loss without trying, repeated vomiting, or looking pale and tired: see a doctor soon
  • Heartburn that keeps coming back for weeks, or is new for an older adult: see a doctor
  • A cough, wheeze or hoarse voice that lasts for weeks: see a doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wait a few hours after eating before you lie down
  • Raise the head of your bed a little, with blocks under the bed legs or a wedge pillow
  • Stop smoking, and ask your doctor or pharmacist before using any medicine for heartburn

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • A heart condition
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

What are the common signs of Acid reflux (GERD)?

Burning behind the breastbone · Sore, scratchy throat · Hoarse, weak voice · Dry, tickly cough · Feeling sick, nausea

Why does Acid reflux (GERD) happen?

Extra body weight

How can I look after myself with Acid reflux (GERD)?

Eat small, regular meals · Keep a healthy weight
