Body lice infestation

A skin problem caused by tiny lice that live in clothes and bedding and bite the skin to feed

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Body lice infestation happens when tiny lice live in clothes and bedding and bite the skin, causing strong itching and a red rash.

  • Skin under clothes
  • Spreads by shared clothes
  • Strong itching, red bumps
Which doctor
Family doctor or skin doctor (dermatologist)
Common in
People in crowded places or who cannot wash and change clothes often
Contagious
Yes, through close contact and shared clothes, bedding or towels
Course
Usually settles once clothes and bedding are well washed; it can come back

Common signs

More signs

  • Scratch marks, scabs or sore, broken skin
  • Skin that turns thick, rough or darker after a long time
  • Tiny insects or small white eggs (nits) in the seams of clothes

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Body lice live in clothes and bedding and bite the skin to feed
  • More likely when people live very close together or cannot wash and change clothes often
  • Wearing the same clothes for many days without washing makes it easier for them to stay
  • Anyone who comes into contact can get them; there is no need to feel ashamed

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with shaking chills, bad headache, confusion or feeling very ill: go to hospital now
  • Spreading redness, pus, warmth or red streaks around scratched skin: see a doctor today
  • Lice in a baby, a young child or a pregnant woman: see a doctor before using anything on the skin
  • Itching or rash that does not get better after clothes and bedding are washed well
  • Not sure what is causing the itch: a doctor can check, as other skin problems look similar

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Bathe, put on clean clothes, and wash used clothes, bedding and towels in hot water, then dry them well
  • Check people you live closely with, and wash everyone's clothes and bedding at the same time

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Pregnancy
