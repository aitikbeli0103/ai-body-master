Acute bone infection from the blood (osteomyelitis)

A serious infection of the bone that reaches it through the blood

ConditionBones, joints & spineBones & muscles

What it is

Germs in the blood can settle in a bone and cause infection, pain and swelling. It needs a doctor quickly, and is more often seen in children.

  • A bone, often in leg or arm
  • More often in children
  • Fever and bone pain
Which doctor
Go to a doctor or hospital promptly; bone or infection specialist
Common in
Children; also people with diabetes or weak defences
Contagious
No, it does not pass from person to person
Course
Needs prompt medical care; recovery takes weeks and the doctor guides it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • They may come from a skin wound, a boil or another infection
  • A recent knock or injury to the bone may play a part

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with bone pain, swelling or not using a limb: go to hospital now
  • A baby or child who is very unwell, drowsy or will not move a limb
  • Fast breathing, confusion or feeling faint: call 999
  • Redness spreading, or a wound that leaks pus near the bone
  • Pain that does not ease, or a limb that looks bent

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • See a doctor early; do not wait for it to pass by itself
  • Take the medicines your doctor gives exactly as told, until finished
  • Rest the sore limb and avoid putting weight on it until the doctor says it is safe
  • Keep skin cuts clean and covered; manage diabetes well

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Acute bone infection from the blood (osteomyelitis)?

A serious infection of the bone that reaches it through the blood. Germs in the blood can settle in a bone and cause infection, pain and swelling. It needs a doctor quickly, and is more often seen in children.

What are the common signs of Acute bone infection from the blood (osteomyelitis)?

Fever, feeling hot · Aching bones · Red, warm, swollen skin · Cannot put weight on the leg · Tired all the time

Why does Acute bone infection from the blood (osteomyelitis) happen?

Germs (infection) · High blood sugar or blood pressure
