Brain infection (intracranial)

Infection inside the skull, such as in the brain or the layers around it

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

Germs such as viruses, bacteria or fungi get into the brain or the layers covering it and cause swelling and illness. It needs urgent care.

  • Brain and its coverings
  • Babies and elderly most at risk
  • Fever, headache, stiff neck
Which doctor
Emergency doctor first; then an infection or brain specialist
Common in
Babies, older adults, and people with weak immunity or an ear or sinus infection
Contagious
Some types can be
Course
Needs hospital care; recovery depends on the germ and how early care starts

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Bacteria, which can be more serious
  • Fungi or parasites, mostly in people with weak immunity
  • A spread from an ear, sinus or tooth infection, or after a head injury or surgery

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with severe headache and stiff neck: go to hospital now
  • Confusion, hard to wake or a fit: call 999
  • A baby or child who is very sleepy, floppy or not feeding: see a doctor promptly or go to hospital now
  • A purple-red rash that does not fade when pressed: go to hospital now

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
