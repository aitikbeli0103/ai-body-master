Gynaecological problems

A general name for problems of the womb, ovaries, vagina and nearby parts

ConditionWomen's healthPelvisReproductive

What it is

This covers many problems of a woman's reproductive parts, such as infections, period problems and growths. A doctor can find out which one it is.

  • Lower belly, womb, vagina
  • Women of any age
  • Pain, discharge, bleeding
Which doctor
Gynaecologist, family doctor
Common in
Women of all ages, most often in the childbearing years
Contagious
Some types can be, such as infections passed on by sex
Course
Depends on the cause; many can be managed well when checked early

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Infections from germs, yeast or sex
  • Growths such as cysts or fibroids

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden, severe lower belly pain, fainting or very heavy bleeding: go to hospital now
  • Pregnant, or may be pregnant, with pain or bleeding: go to hospital now
  • Bleeding after the periods have stopped for good, or bleeding after sex
  • Fever with lower belly pain or smelly discharge
  • A new lump or swelling in the lower belly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep the private area clean and dry; wear loose, breathable underwear
  • Practise safe sex, and tell your doctor about any unusual signs early
  • Do not use harsh washes inside the vagina

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Questions and answers

What is Gynaecological problems?

A general name for problems of the womb, ovaries, vagina and nearby parts. This covers many problems of a woman's reproductive parts, such as infections, period problems and growths. A doctor can find out which one it is.

What are the common signs of Gynaecological problems?

Dragging ache in the lower belly · Unusual discharge or itching down below · Cramps during the period · Pain during or after sex

Why does Gynaecological problems happen?

Hormone changes · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Gynaecological problems?

See your doctor for check-ups
