Psoriasis

A long-term condition where skin renews itself too fast, causing red, scaly and sometimes itchy patches

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Psoriasis happens when the body's defences make skin cells grow too fast, so they pile up as red, scaly patches; it has several types and often comes and goes.

  • Skin, nails and joints
  • Any age; runs in families
  • Red, scaly, itchy patches
Which doctor
Skin doctor (dermatologist); joint specialist (rheumatologist) if joints hurt
Common in
Any age, most often adults; often runs in families
Contagious
No
Course
Long-term; it comes and goes in flare-ups, and a doctor can help manage it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The immune system (the body's defence) is overactive and makes skin cells grow too fast
  • It is not caused by germs or poor hygiene, and it cannot be caught from someone else

See a doctor first if

  • Red, hot skin over much of the body, or many pus spots, with fever or feeling very unwell: go to hospital now
  • Painful, stiff or swollen joints, or swollen fingers or toes, especially stiff in the morning: see a doctor soon
  • Skin that is very sore, hot, swollen, oozing or has pus may be infected: see a doctor promptly
  • A red, painful eye with blurred vision or dislike of light: see a doctor promptly
  • Patches that spread fast, cover a large area, or stop you sleeping or working: see a doctor
  • Feeling very low, or thoughts of harming yourself: talk to a doctor now, or call 999

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep baths and showers short and lukewarm, use a mild wash, and pat the skin dry
  • Find your triggers, such as stress, smoking or alcohol, and cut back where you can

Questions and answers

What is Psoriasis?

A long-term condition where skin renews itself too fast, causing red, scaly and sometimes itchy patches. Psoriasis happens when the body's defences make skin cells grow too fast, so they pile up as red, scaly patches; it has several types and often comes and goes.

What are the common signs of Psoriasis?

Red rash · Dry, flaky skin · Itchy skin · Brittle, ridged nails · Stiff, swollen fingers

Why does Psoriasis happen?

Runs in the family · Stress · Smoking

How can I look after myself with Psoriasis?

Keep the skin moisturised · Do not squeeze or pick at it · Ask your pharmacist
