Hole in the heart (atrial septal defect)

A hole in the wall between the two upper chambers of the heart, present from birth

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

Some babies are born with a hole in the wall between the heart's two upper chambers. Small holes may cause no trouble, while larger ones can make the heart work harder over time.

  • Inside the heart
  • Present from birth
  • May tire you out
Which doctor
Heart doctor (cardiologist); children's heart doctor for a child
Common in
Born with it; sometimes only found in adult life
Contagious
No
Course
Small holes may stay the same; others need heart checks and a doctor's plan

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The heart does not fully form while the baby grows in the womb

See a doctor first if

  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Chest pain, fainting or severe trouble breathing: call 999
  • Blue or grey lips or skin: go to hospital now
  • A baby or child who feeds poorly, breathes fast or is very tired: see a doctor promptly
  • A fast, irregular heartbeat or swollen ankles

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
