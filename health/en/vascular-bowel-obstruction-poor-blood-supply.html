Vascular bowel obstruction (poor blood supply)

A blocked bowel caused by poor blood supply to the bowel wall

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

When the blood vessels that feed the bowel are blocked or squeezed, the bowel stops moving and can be badly harmed. It is a serious emergency.

  • Belly and bowel
  • Mostly older adults
  • Severe belly pain
Which doctor
Go to hospital at once; surgeon
Common in
Older adults; people with heart or blood vessel disease
Contagious
No
Course
Can worsen within hours; it needs hospital care

Common signs

More signs

  • Strong belly pain that does not ease

Why it happens

  • A blood clot in a vessel that feeds the bowel
  • Hardened or narrowed blood vessels
  • A bowel that twists or is squeezed, for example in a hernia
  • Heart rhythm problems or very low blood pressure

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden, severe belly pain: go to hospital now or call 999
  • Repeated vomiting, or vomit that is green or has blood
  • A very swollen belly with no wind or stool passing
  • Fainting, cold clammy skin, fast heartbeat or fever
  • Blood in the stool

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not wait at home; go to hospital or call 999
  • Do not eat, drink or take medicine until a doctor says so
  • Do not use laxatives or heat on the belly

A blocked bowel caused by poor blood supply to the bowel wall. When the blood vessels that feed the bowel are blocked or squeezed, the bowel stops moving and can be badly harmed. It is a serious emergency.

Why does Vascular bowel obstruction (poor blood supply) happen?

A blood clot in a vessel that feeds the bowel · Hardened or narrowed blood vessels · A bowel that twists or is squeezed, for example in a hernia · Heart rhythm problems or very low blood pressure
