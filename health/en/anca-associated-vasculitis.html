ANCA-associated vasculitis

Inflammation of small blood vessels that can affect the lungs, kidneys and nerves

ConditionAutoimmune & rheumaticBlood

What it is

The immune system wrongly attacks small blood vessels, so organs such as the kidneys and lungs may not get enough blood. It needs a specialist.

  • Small blood vessels
  • More common in older adults
  • Tired, fever, aches
Which doctor
Rheumatologist or kidney doctor
Common in
Mostly middle-aged and older adults
Contagious
No
Course
Needs long-term follow-up; it can flare and settle

Common signs

More signs

  • Cough, coughing blood or short breath

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The immune system makes antibodies that attack blood vessel walls
  • Genes and the environment may both play a part
  • Some medicines or infections may set it off in a few people

See a doctor first if

  • Coughing up blood or sudden trouble breathing: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Blood in the urine, very little urine or sudden swelling
  • Sudden loss of vision, or a painful red eye
  • Fever or new symptoms while taking immune-lowering medicine

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is ANCA-associated vasculitis?

Inflammation of small blood vessels that can affect the lungs, kidneys and nerves. The immune system wrongly attacks small blood vessels, so organs such as the kidneys and lungs may not get enough blood. It needs a specialist.

What are the common signs of ANCA-associated vasculitis?

Tired all the time · Blocked nose · Pink or red urine · Numb, tingling feet

Why does ANCA-associated vasculitis happen?

The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with ANCA-associated vasculitis?

Take medicine as your doctor says · Wash your hands often · Stop smoking

Can an AI Body Master session help with ANCA-associated vasculitis?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure ANCA-associated vasculitis and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
