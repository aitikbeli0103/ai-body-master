Retained baby teeth

A baby tooth that stays in place after the adult tooth has started to come in

ConditionMouth & teethHead

What it is

A baby tooth does not fall out when it should, so the adult tooth may come in beside it or behind it, making a double row of teeth.

  • Teeth and jaw
  • Children and teens
  • A loose or crowded tooth
Which doctor
Dentist, children's dentist
Common in
Children and teens while the adult teeth are coming in
Contagious
No
Course
A dentist can check whether it will fall out by itself or needs help

Common signs

  • A baby tooth that stays when the adult tooth is already showing
  • Two rows of teeth, one in front of the other
  • Crowded or crooked adult teeth
  • Food trapped between the teeth, or sore gums

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The adult tooth is not pushing the baby tooth out in the usual way
  • The adult tooth grows in a slightly different place
  • Sometimes the adult tooth is missing or stuck in the jaw

See a doctor first if

  • Swelling of the face or gum with fever: see a doctor promptly
  • A child with a lot of pain or who cannot eat
  • Pus, a bad taste or a pimple on the gum
  • A baby tooth that stays well after the adult tooth has come in

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not pull the tooth at home
  • Brush twice a day, including around the extra tooth
  • Cut down on sweet drinks and snacks

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • A severe allergy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
