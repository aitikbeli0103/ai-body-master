ACL (knee ligament) injury

A stretched or torn ligament deep inside the knee that keeps it steady

ConditionPelvis, hip, knee & legBones & muscles

What it is

The anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) helps hold the knee steady. A twist or sudden stop can stretch or tear it, and the knee may swell and feel as if it gives way.

  • Inside the knee
  • Football, badminton, jumping
  • Swelling, knee gives way
Which doctor
Bone specialist (orthopaedic) or sports doctor; physiotherapist
Common in
Active people in sports with twisting and jumping; both men and women
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; a doctor and physiotherapist can explain the options and the time needed

Common signs

  • A pop or a tearing feeling at the moment of injury
  • Swelling of the knee within hours
  • Pain, and trouble bending or straightening the knee fully
  • The knee feels loose or gives way when you stand or turn

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Stopping suddenly, changing direction or twisting with the foot planted
  • Landing badly from a jump
  • Other knee injuries often happen at the same time

See a doctor first if

  • You cannot put weight on the leg at all: go to hospital
  • The knee looks out of shape or you cannot straighten it (locked)
  • A foot that is pale, cold, blue or numb: go to hospital now
  • A painful, swollen calf, or a hot red knee with fever
  • The knee keeps giving way in daily life

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Use crutches or a knee support if your doctor advises
  • Do guided leg-strengthening exercises with a physiotherapist

Tell your therapist

  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
