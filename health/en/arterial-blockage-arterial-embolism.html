Arterial blockage (arterial embolism)

A sudden blockage of an artery by a clot or other material that has travelled in the blood

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

A clot or other material travels through the blood and gets stuck in an artery, cutting off blood to the body part beyond it. It is often an emergency.

  • An artery anywhere in the body
  • Often with heart rhythm issues
  • Sudden pain, pale, cold limb
Which doctor
Emergency doctor first, then a vascular surgeon or heart doctor
Common in
Often people with an uneven heartbeat, heart disease or hardened arteries
Contagious
No
Course
Needs urgent care; how it goes depends on which artery and how fast help comes

Common signs

  • Sudden pain in an arm or leg
  • A limb that turns pale, blue or cold
  • Numbness, tingling or weakness in the limb
  • No pulse that can be felt in the affected limb
  • Sudden belly pain or other sudden signs if another organ is affected

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A clot that forms in the heart, often with an uneven heartbeat
  • A piece that breaks off from a hardened artery wall
  • Heart valve problems or a recent heart attack

See a doctor first if

  • A sudden pale, cold, painful or numb arm or leg: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Sudden severe belly pain: go to hospital now
  • Sudden chest pain or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Sudden loss of vision in one eye: go to hospital now

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
