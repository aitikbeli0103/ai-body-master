Lung bulla (large air pocket)

A large air-filled pocket in the lung that has formed where lung tissue has been damaged

ConditionLungs & breathingChest

What it is

A bulla is a large air pocket in the lung. Many people feel nothing, but a big one can make breathing harder or may burst.

  • Inside the lung
  • Linked to smoking
  • Breathless, chest pain
Which doctor
Lung doctor (respiratory specialist), family doctor
Common in
Long-term smokers; people with long-term lung disease; some tall, thin young adults
Contagious
No
Course
Often stays the same for a long time; a doctor can watch it with scans

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long-term lung damage such as emphysema

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden sharp chest pain with sudden trouble breathing: call 999
  • Blue or grey lips, or you cannot speak a full sentence: call 999
  • Coughing up blood
  • Fever with coloured phlegm, or breathing getting worse over days
  • Plans to fly, dive or climb to high places: ask your doctor first

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
