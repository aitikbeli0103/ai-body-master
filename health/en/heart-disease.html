Heart disease

A general term for problems that affect the heart or the vessels feeding it

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

Heart disease means the heart does not work as well as it should. It may be from narrowed heart vessels, weak heart muscle, a faulty valve or an uneven heartbeat.

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain or pressure, sweating or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Fainting: go to hospital now
  • A very fast or uneven heartbeat that does not settle: go to hospital now
  • Waking at night short of breath, or swollen legs that keep getting worse
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or trouble speaking: call 999

Questions and answers

What is Heart disease?

A general term for problems that affect the heart or the vessels feeding it. Heart disease means the heart does not work as well as it should. It may be from narrowed heart vessels, weak heart muscle, a faulty valve or an uneven heartbeat.

What are the common signs of Heart disease?

Tight, heavy chest · Short of breath on stairs · Racing, pounding heart · Swollen legs and ankles · Tired all the time

Why does Heart disease happen?

High blood sugar or blood pressure · Smoking · Runs in the family

How can I look after myself with Heart disease?

Take medicine as your doctor says · Stop smoking · Eat less salt

Can an AI Body Master session help with Heart disease?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Heart disease and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
