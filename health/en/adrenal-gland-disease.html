Adrenal gland disease

Problems of the two small glands above the kidneys that make hormones for stress, salt and blood pressure

ConditionBlood sugar, fats, uric acid & thyroidLower back

What it is

The adrenal glands sit above the kidneys. When they make too much or too little hormone, you may feel very tired, weak, or have blood pressure changes.

  • Above the kidneys
  • Adults, many causes
  • Tired and weak
Which doctor
Hormone doctor (endocrinologist), family doctor
Common in
Adults of any age; some types are rare
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the type; many can be managed with a doctor's care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Small growths on the gland, usually not cancer
  • The body's own defences attacking the gland

See a doctor first if

  • Fainting, confusion or severe weakness: call 999
  • Severe belly pain with vomiting and feeling very unwell: go to hospital now
  • Very high blood pressure with bad headache or chest pain: call 999
  • You take steroid medicine and become very ill or vomit: see a doctor now
  • Tiredness and weakness lasting weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Adrenal gland disease?

Problems of the two small glands above the kidneys that make hormones for stress, salt and blood pressure. The adrenal glands sit above the kidneys. When they make too much or too little hormone, you may feel very tired, weak, or have blood pressure changes.

What are the common signs of Adrenal gland disease?

Tired all the time · Dizzy when standing up · No appetite · Feeling sick, nausea · Pain in the side or loin

Why does Adrenal gland disease happen?

Long-term use of some medicines · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Adrenal gland disease?

Keep your regular tests · Eat healthy, balanced meals · Drink enough water

Can an AI Body Master session help with Adrenal gland disease?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Adrenal gland disease and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
