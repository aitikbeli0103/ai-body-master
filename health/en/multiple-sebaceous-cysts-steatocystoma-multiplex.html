Multiple sebaceous cysts (steatocystoma multiplex)

Many small, smooth, oily lumps under the skin, often on the chest and arms

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsChest

What it is

Many small cysts filled with an oily material form under the skin. They are usually harmless and painless, but they tend to stay once they appear.

  • Chest, arms, armpits and neck
  • Often starts in the teens
  • Many small, smooth lumps
Which doctor
Skin doctor (dermatologist), family doctor
Common in
Teenagers and young adults; often runs in families
Contagious
No
Course
Usually lasts for years and new ones can appear; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

More signs

  • Skin-coloured, yellowish or slightly bluish bumps
  • Often on the chest, upper arms, armpits and neck
  • Usually painless, but a cyst can become red and sore

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The oil glands in the skin form small sacs that fill with oil
  • It often starts in the teens, when oil glands are more active

See a doctor first if

  • A lump that becomes red, hot, swollen and very painful, or leaks pus
  • Spreading redness or red streaks, or fever: see a doctor today
  • A lump that grows quickly, feels hard, bleeds or does not move under the skin
  • A new lump that looks different from the others
  • Lumps that keep getting infected or leave scars, or that you find upsetting

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wear loose, soft clothes so the lumps are not rubbed
  • Take photos now and then so a doctor can see if anything changes
  • If the lumps bother you, ask a skin doctor about your options
