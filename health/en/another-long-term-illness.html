Another long-term illness

A health problem that lasts for months or years and needs regular care

ConditionWhole body & generalWhole body

What it is

A long-term illness is a health problem that stays for a long time or keeps coming back. Many people live well with one by following their doctor's care plan.

  • Whole body
  • Needs regular check-ups
  • Lasts months or years
Which doctor
Family doctor first; a specialist if needed
Common in
Anyone, more often with age
Contagious
Usually no; it depends on the illness
Course
Varies by illness; regular care can help keep it steady

Common signs

More signs

  • Daily life may need some changes

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Often more than one reason, and sometimes not known

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting, or face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Symptoms that suddenly get worse
  • Fever that does not go away, or weight loss you cannot explain
  • New symptoms that you do not understand
  • Feeling very low, or thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

What are the common signs of Another long-term illness?

Why does Another long-term illness happen?

Getting older · Runs in the family · Smoking · An injury or fall

How can I look after myself with Another long-term illness?

See your doctor for check-ups · Ask your pharmacist · Eat healthy, balanced meals · Note down your symptoms
