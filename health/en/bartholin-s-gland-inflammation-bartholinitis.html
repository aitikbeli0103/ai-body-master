Bartholin's gland inflammation (bartholinitis)

Swelling and soreness of a small gland beside the opening of the vagina

ConditionWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

The Bartholin glands sit on each side of the vaginal opening and make a little moisture. If a gland gets blocked or infected, it can swell into a sore lump.

  • Beside the vaginal opening
  • Women in their active years
  • Sore, swollen lump
Which doctor
Women's doctor (gynaecologist), family doctor
Common in
Women in their active years; less common after menopause
Contagious
Some causes can be, such as infections passed on by sex
Course
Can settle in days to weeks; it may come back, so tell your doctor

Common signs

More signs

  • A tender lump on one side of the vaginal opening
  • Pain when walking, sitting or during sex
  • Redness and warmth around the lump

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A blocked gland that fills with fluid
  • Germs from skin or gut, or from infections spread by sex

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with chills or feeling very unwell: go to hospital now
  • Very strong pain, or you cannot sit or walk
  • Redness that spreads, or a lump that keeps getting bigger
  • Pain or swelling when you are pregnant
  • A new lump after menopause, or a lump that keeps coming back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Soak in a shallow bath of warm water a few times a day
  • Wear loose cotton underwear and keep the area clean and dry
  • See a doctor if it is not getting better after a few days
