Coronary heart disease

Narrowing of the blood vessels that feed the heart muscle, so less blood reaches the heart

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

Fatty deposits slowly build up inside the arteries that feed the heart. This can cause chest pain or breathlessness, and can lead to a heart attack.

  • Heart and chest
  • More common with age
  • Chest pressure or tightness
Which doctor
Heart doctor (cardiologist), family doctor
Common in
Older adults; smokers; people with high blood pressure, diabetes or high cholesterol
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long-term; healthy habits and regular check-ups can help keep it steady

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Fatty build-up (plaque) slowly narrows the arteries that feed the heart
  • Too much alcohol and long-term stress may add to the risk

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain or pressure lasting more than a few minutes, or spreading to the arm, jaw or back: call 999
  • Sudden breathlessness, cold sweat, feeling sick or faint, even without chest pain: call 999
  • Fainting, or a racing or uneven heartbeat with dizziness: call 999
  • Chest pain at rest, or coming more often or more easily than before: go to hospital today
  • Swollen ankles, or breathlessness when lying flat: see a doctor soon
