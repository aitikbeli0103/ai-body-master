Luteinised unruptured follicle syndrome (LUFS)

An egg grows in its sac but the sac does not open, so the egg is not released

ConditionWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

The ovary prepares an egg and the body acts as if ovulation happened, but the egg stays trapped. Periods can look normal, yet pregnancy may be harder.

  • Ovary
  • Women trying to conceive
  • Normal-looking periods
Which doctor
Gynaecologist, fertility specialist
Common in
Women who find it hard to get pregnant
Contagious
No
Course
Can come and go from cycle to cycle; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Endometriosis or scarring in the pelvis
  • Some fertility medicines or older age may play a part

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden strong belly pain with dizziness or fainting: call 999
  • Heavy bleeding that soaks a pad every hour
  • Pelvic pain with fever
  • Trying to get pregnant for a long time without success
  • Pain that gets worse with each period

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
