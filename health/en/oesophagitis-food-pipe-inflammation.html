Oesophagitis (food pipe inflammation)

Irritation and swelling of the lining of the food pipe

ConditionStomach & intestinesChestAbdomen

What it is

The lining of the food pipe (oesophagus) becomes red and sore. It often causes heartburn and a sore feeling when swallowing.

  • Food pipe, chest to upper belly
  • Common in adults
  • Heartburn, sore swallowing
Which doctor
Family doctor, or a stomach and bowel doctor (gastroenterologist)
Common in
Adults with reflux; people who smoke or drink alcohol
Contagious
No
Course
Often improves when the cause is dealt with; it can come back

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Alcohol, smoking, very hot drinks or spicy food
  • Some pills irritating the lining
  • Some infections, allergies, or a weak immune system

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain with sweating, trouble breathing or pain spreading to the arm or jaw: call 999
  • Vomiting blood, or black or tarry stools: go to hospital now
  • Food getting stuck, or you cannot swallow
  • Losing weight without trying, or heartburn that lasts for weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Stay upright for a while after meals
  • Cut down on alcohol, fatty or spicy food, and stop smoking
  • Take pills with a full glass of water, and ask your doctor or pharmacist about reflux medicine

Tell your therapist

  • A heart condition
  • High blood pressure
  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine
