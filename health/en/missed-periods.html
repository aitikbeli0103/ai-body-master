Missed periods

A period that does not come when you expect it

SymptomWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

A missed period is when your period is late or does not come. The most common reason is pregnancy, but stress, weight change and hormones can also be the cause.

  • Womb and ovaries
  • Women of all ages
  • Period is late or absent
Which doctor
Family doctor, gynaecologist
Common in
Women of childbearing age
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; a doctor can find out why

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Stress, a big change in weight, or very hard exercise
  • Polycystic ovaries or a thyroid problem
  • Breastfeeding, menopause coming near, or some contraception

See a doctor first if

  • Missed period with strong one-sided belly pain, shoulder pain, dizziness or fainting: call 999
  • Pregnant with bleeding or strong pain: go to hospital now
  • No period for three months or more
  • Milky fluid from the nipples, or bad headaches with vision changes
  • Very heavy bleeding when the period does come

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
