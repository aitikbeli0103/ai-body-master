Hydrocephalus (water on the brain)

Too much fluid collecting inside the brain, which raises the pressure in the head

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

The clear fluid that normally cushions the brain builds up in spaces inside it. This can press on the brain and needs a doctor's care.

  • Inside the head
  • Babies and older adults
  • Headache, sickness, confusion
Which doctor
Brain doctor (neurologist) or brain surgeon (neurosurgeon)
Common in
Babies, and some older adults or people after head injury
Contagious
No
Course
Needs a doctor's care; many people need ongoing follow-up

Common signs

More signs

  • Headache, often worse in the morning
  • In babies: a fast-growing head, a bulging soft spot, being very sleepy

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The fluid is made faster than it drains away, or its flow is blocked

See a doctor first if

  • Very bad headache with repeated vomiting or confusion: go to hospital now
  • Very sleepy, hard to wake, a fit or passing out: call 999
  • A baby with a bulging soft spot, a fast-growing head or poor feeding: see a doctor promptly
  • Sudden loss of vision or new weakness on one side: call 999
  • If you have a shunt (drain tube): headache, fever or redness along the tube

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
