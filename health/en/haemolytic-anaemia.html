Haemolytic anaemia

Anaemia where red blood cells are broken down faster than the body can make new ones

ConditionBloodBlood

What it is

Red blood cells are destroyed earlier than normal, so the body has too few to carry oxygen. This can cause tiredness, paleness and yellow skin.

  • Red blood cells
  • Any age, many causes
  • Tired, pale, breathless
Which doctor
Family doctor, blood doctor (haematologist)
Common in
Any age; some types are inherited, others appear later in life
Contagious
No, though an infection behind it may spread
Course
Depends on the cause; some are short, some long term

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The immune system attacking the body's own red blood cells
  • Infections, and some medicines
  • Some blood and lymph conditions

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain or trouble breathing at rest: call 999
  • Fainting, confusion or a very fast heartbeat: go to hospital now
  • Dark tea-coloured urine with yellow skin or eyes
  • Fever with shivering, or pain in the upper left belly
  • A baby or child who looks pale or yellow and is very unwell: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
