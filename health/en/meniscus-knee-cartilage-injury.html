Meniscus (knee cartilage) injury

A tear or wear in the C-shaped cushion inside the knee

ConditionPelvis, hip, knee & legWhole body

What it is

The meniscus is a rubbery cushion between the thigh bone and shin bone. A twist or wear over time can tear it, causing knee pain, swelling and catching.

  • Inside the knee
  • After a twist or with age
  • Pain, swelling, catching
Which doctor
Bone and joint doctor (orthopaedic), family doctor
Common in
Sports players; older adults with worn knees
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; some settle with rest, others need a doctor's advice

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A sudden twist of the knee while the foot is planted

See a doctor first if

  • A knee that is locked and cannot fully straighten
  • Cannot put weight on the leg, or the knee looks bent or out of shape: go to hospital
  • Numb, pale or cold foot: go to hospital now
  • Heavy swelling soon after an injury
  • Fever with a hot, red knee

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Meniscus (knee cartilage) injury?

A tear or wear in the C-shaped cushion inside the knee. The meniscus is a rubbery cushion between the thigh bone and shin bone. A twist or wear over time can tear it, causing knee pain, swelling and catching.

What are the common signs of Meniscus (knee cartilage) injury?

Knee pain on stairs · Swollen, stiff knee · Hard to walk far

Why does Meniscus (knee cartilage) injury happen?

Heavy or repeated work · Getting older · Sports and hard knocks

How can I look after myself with Meniscus (knee cartilage) injury?

Rest the sore joint · Avoid heavy lifting
