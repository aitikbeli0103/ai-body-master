Lupus (lupus erythematosus)

A group of autoimmune conditions where the body attacks its own tissues, the best known being lupus

ConditionAutoimmune & rheumaticWhole body

What it is

Lupus is an autoimmune condition. The body's defence system wrongly attacks healthy tissue, and this can cause a rash, joint pain, tiredness and sometimes problems in organs.

  • Skin, joints and organs
  • More often in women
  • Tired, rash, sore joints
Which doctor
Rheumatologist; skin doctor; family doctor
Common in
More often women, often from young adulthood
Contagious
No
Course
Long-term; it often flares and settles, and needs regular doctor care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The immune system attacks the body's own healthy tissue
  • Genes, hormones and the environment may play a part
  • Sunlight, infections and some medicines can trigger a flare

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Stroke signs such as face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble, or a fit: call 999
  • Swollen face or legs, or foamy or dark urine: see a doctor soon
  • Fever, a new rash or a sudden bad headache: see a doctor soon
  • Pregnancy, or planning a pregnancy: talk to your doctor first

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Lupus (lupus erythematosus)?

A group of autoimmune conditions where the body attacks its own tissues, the best known being lupus. Lupus is an autoimmune condition. The body's defence system wrongly attacks healthy tissue, and this can cause a rash, joint pain, tiredness and sometimes problems in organs.

What are the common signs of Lupus (lupus erythematosus)?

Tired all the time · Red rash · Fever, feeling hot · Hair coming out · Painful mouth sores · Aching all over

Why does Lupus (lupus erythematosus) happen?

The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Lupus (lupus erythematosus)?

See your doctor for check-ups · Protect yourself from the sun · Rest well · Wash your hands often · Ask your pharmacist
