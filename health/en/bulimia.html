Bulimia

An eating problem with episodes of overeating followed by ways to get rid of the food

ConditionTold by a doctor: eatingWhole body

What it is

In bulimia, a person eats a large amount in a short time, feels out of control, then tries to make up for it, for example by vomiting. It is a health problem, and help is available.

  • Mind, eating and stomach
  • More often young women
  • Guilt and shame about food
Which doctor
Family doctor, psychiatrist, dietitian
Common in
Teenagers and young adults, but anyone can be affected
Contagious
No
Course
It can last a long time without help; many people recover with steady support

Common signs

More signs

  • Eating large amounts quickly and feeling unable to stop
  • Going to the toilet right after meals, or vomiting on purpose
  • Worry about weight and body shape most of the time
  • Feeling ashamed and hiding eating habits

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Often a mix of feelings, family and social pressure about body shape
  • Low self-esteem, stress or past hard experiences
  • Strict dieting can start the cycle

See a doctor first if

  • Fainting, chest pain, a very fast or uneven heartbeat, or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Vomiting blood, or black stools: go to hospital now
  • Thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life: see a doctor now or call 999
  • Muscle cramps, severe weakness or confusion from vomiting or misuse of laxatives or water pills
  • Purging or binging that happens often and you cannot stop: see your doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Tell a doctor or someone you trust; you do not have to cope alone
  • Try to eat regular meals and avoid long gaps between meals
  • After vomiting, rinse your mouth with water and wait before brushing teeth
  • Avoid using laxatives or water pills unless your doctor says so
  • Be kind to yourself; setbacks happen on the way to recovery

Tell your therapist

  • A heart condition
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Pregnancy
  • High blood pressure

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
