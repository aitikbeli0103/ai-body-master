Knee ligament injury

A stretched or torn band that holds the knee bones together

ConditionPelvis, hip, knee & legArms & legs

What it is

Ligaments are strong bands that keep the knee steady. A sudden twist or knock can stretch or tear one, so the knee feels painful, swollen or wobbly.

  • Inside the knee
  • Often from sport or a twist
  • Knee feels wobbly
Which doctor
Bone doctor (orthopaedic) or sports doctor; physiotherapist
Common in
Football, badminton and basketball players; people after a twisting fall
Contagious
No
Course
Mild sprains often settle in weeks; a full tear can take months and needs a doctor's plan

Common signs

Why it happens

  • A sudden twist of the knee with the foot planted
  • Landing badly after a jump
  • A hard knock to the side of the knee
  • Stopping or changing direction suddenly while running
  • Weak muscles around the knee or tiredness

See a doctor first if

  • A foot that is numb, pale, cold or blue: go to hospital now
  • Cannot put weight on the leg, or the knee looks bent or out of shape: go to hospital
  • A pop or snap at the time, followed by quick swelling
  • The knee gives way or locks
  • Fever with a hot, red knee, or a swollen, painful calf

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask your doctor or physiotherapist about a knee support and safe strengthening exercises

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Knee ligament injury?

A stretched or torn band that holds the knee bones together. Ligaments are strong bands that keep the knee steady. A sudden twist or knock can stretch or tear one, so the knee feels painful, swollen or wobbly.

What are the common signs of Knee ligament injury?

Swollen, stiff knee · Knee pain on stairs · Hard to walk far

Why does Knee ligament injury happen?

A sudden twist of the knee with the foot planted · Landing badly after a jump · A hard knock to the side of the knee · Stopping or changing direction suddenly while running · Weak muscles around the knee or tiredness

How can I look after myself with Knee ligament injury?

Rest the sore joint · A cold pack on the sore spot · Rest with your legs raised
