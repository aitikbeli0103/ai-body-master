Balance disorder (vestibular dysfunction)

Trouble with the inner ear and brain system that keeps you steady

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

The parts of the inner ear and brain that tell you where your body is are not working well. You may feel dizzy, spinning or unsteady on your feet.

  • Inner ear and brain
  • More common with age
  • Dizzy, spinning, unsteady
Which doctor
Family doctor; ear-nose-throat or nerve specialist
Common in
Adults, more often older people
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; often comes and goes, and a doctor can look for the cause

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A head injury or a virus affecting the balance nerve
  • Low blood pressure, some medicines, or poor blood flow

See a doctor first if

  • Dizziness with face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Dizziness with chest pain, fainting or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Sudden severe headache, double vision or sudden hearing loss: go to hospital now
  • Falls, or dizziness that keeps coming back
  • Dizziness that begins after a new medicine: ask your doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Sit or lie down when you feel dizzy, and get up slowly
  • Do not drive or climb when dizzy

Tell your therapist

  • Dizzy when turning the head
  • High blood pressure
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Balance disorder (vestibular dysfunction)?

Trouble with the inner ear and brain system that keeps you steady. The parts of the inner ear and brain that tell you where your body is are not working well. You may feel dizzy, spinning or unsteady on your feet.

What are the common signs of Balance disorder (vestibular dysfunction)?

Room feels like spinning · Dizzy when standing up · Unsteady when walking · Ringing in the ears · Hard to hear people

Why does Balance disorder (vestibular dysfunction) happen?

Germs (infection) · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Balance disorder (vestibular dysfunction)?

Make the home safe from falls · Drink enough water · Stay active every day
