Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS)

A disease of the nerves that control muscles, causing growing weakness

ConditionBrain & nervesWhole body

What it is

The nerve cells that move the muscles slowly stop working. Muscles become weak and thin, which can affect walking, using the hands, speaking, swallowing and breathing.

  • Muscles all over the body
  • Usually adults
  • Growing muscle weakness
Which doctor
Brain doctor (neurologist) and a care team
Common in
Mostly adults in middle or older age
Contagious
No
Course
Usually gets worse over time; the care team can help you stay comfortable

Common signs

More signs

  • Muscle twitching, cramps or stiffness
  • Thinner muscles and tiredness

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A small number of cases run in families
  • Nerve cells that control muscles slowly break down
  • Age and some genes may raise the chance

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing or a blue tinge to the lips: call 999
  • Choking when eating or drinking, or food going down the wrong way
  • Fever, cough and chest pain, which may mean a chest infection
  • A fall or a new sudden weakness: go to hospital
  • Low mood or thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Stay in touch with your neurologist and care team
  • Ask about help with swallowing, speaking, walking aids and breathing
  • Eat slowly, in small bites, sitting upright
  • Lean on family, friends and support groups

Tell your therapist

  • Weak leg or foot
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A heart condition
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS)?

A disease of the nerves that control muscles, causing growing weakness. The nerve cells that move the muscles slowly stop working. Muscles become weak and thin, which can affect walking, using the hands, speaking, swallowing and breathing.

What are the common signs of Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS)?

Weak, clumsy grip · Legs feel weak · Slurred or unclear speech

Why does Amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS) happen?

The cause is not fully known
