Intercostal neuritis (inflamed rib nerves)

Swelling and irritation of the nerves between the ribs, causing chest wall pain

ConditionBrain & nervesHeadChest

What it is

The nerves that run between the ribs become inflamed, often after an infection or injury. This can cause burning or stabbing pain around the chest.

  • Along the ribs
  • Adults of any age
  • Burning or stabbing pain
Which doctor
Family doctor; brain and nerve doctor if it lasts
Common in
Adults; often after shingles, an infection or a chest injury
Contagious
Usually No; shingles blisters can spread the virus to people who never had chickenpox
Course
Often settles over weeks to months, depending on the cause

Common signs

More signs

  • Burning, aching or stabbing pain along a rib
  • Skin over the area that is very tender to touch

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Other infections or a recent illness
  • A chest or rib injury, or past chest surgery
  • Sometimes the cause is not found

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain with trouble breathing, sweating, or pain spreading to the arm or jaw: call 999
  • Any new chest pain you are not sure about: have it checked by a doctor first
  • A blistering rash on the chest: see a doctor early, as early care can help
  • A rash near the eye, or fever with a bad headache
  • Pain that lasts long after the rash has gone, or new weakness

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep any blisters clean and dry, and do not scratch them
  • If there are blisters, keep away from babies, pregnant women and people who are unwell
  • For pain medicine, ask your doctor or pharmacist

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • A heart condition

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
