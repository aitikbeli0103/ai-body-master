Lymph node inflammation (lymphadenitis)

Swollen, tender lymph glands, usually because of an infection nearby

ConditionBloodBlood

What it is

Lymph nodes are small glands that help fight germs. When they react to an infection they can swell and feel sore, for example in the neck, armpit or groin.

  • Neck, armpit or groin
  • Any age, often children
  • Sore, swollen lump
Which doctor
Family doctor
Common in
Children and adults, often after a throat, skin or tooth infection
Contagious
The gland itself is not, but the infection behind it may be
Course
Often settles within a few weeks once the infection clears; a doctor should check it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Throat, ear, tooth or skin infections close to the gland
  • Less often, deeper infections such as tuberculosis
  • Rarely, immune illnesses or cancers that need a doctor's check

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing or swallowing, or a swollen throat or tongue: call 999
  • A high fever, or a stiff neck with a bad headache: go to hospital now
  • A red, hot, quickly growing swelling, or pus
  • A hard, painless lump, or a lump that grows or lasts more than a few weeks
  • Weight loss, night sweats or tiredness with no clear reason

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
