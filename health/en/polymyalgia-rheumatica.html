Polymyalgia rheumatica

An inflammatory condition causing pain and morning stiffness in the shoulders and hips

ConditionAutoimmune & rheumaticBones & muscles

What it is

Polymyalgia rheumatica causes aching and stiffness in the shoulders, neck and hips, worst in the morning. It mostly affects people over fifty.

  • Shoulders, neck and hips
  • Mostly people over fifty
  • Aching, stiff in the morning
Which doctor
Family doctor; rheumatologist
Common in
Older adults, usually over fifty, more often women
Contagious
No
Course
Often lasts months to a few years, and a doctor guides the care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The immune system may become overactive and inflame the tissues around the joints
  • Genes and an earlier infection may play a part

See a doctor first if

  • New headache with scalp pain, jaw pain when chewing, or sudden vision change: go to hospital now
  • Stroke signs such as face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Fever, night sweats or losing weight without trying
  • Chest pain or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Stiffness or pain so bad that you cannot dress or get out of bed

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • A warm shower or warm pack in the morning may help
  • Take care not to fall; ask your doctor before changing any medicine

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • High blood pressure
  • Recent shoulder injury or surgery
  • A heart condition
