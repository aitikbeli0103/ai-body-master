Cervical spinal stenosis (narrowed canal)

Narrowing of the space in the neck bones that can press on the spinal cord or nerves

ConditionBones, joints & spineNeck

What it is

The channel inside the neck bones becomes narrower, which can squeeze the spinal cord or nearby nerves and cause neck, arm or balance problems.

  • Neck bones
  • More common with age
  • Neck ache, tingling
Which doctor
Family doctor, spine or bone doctor (orthopaedic), nerve doctor
Common in
Middle-aged and older adults; people born with a narrow spinal canal
Contagious
No
Course
Often changes slowly over months or years; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Bony growths (spurs) or thickened ligaments in the neck

See a doctor first if

  • Weakness or numbness in both arms or both legs that is getting worse: see a doctor now
  • New trouble holding your urine or stools: go to hospital now
  • Walking that is becoming unsteady, or frequent falls
  • A fall or injury to the neck followed by new weakness or numbness: go to hospital now
  • Clumsy hands, dropping things, or trouble with buttons

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Use a supportive pillow and take breaks from phone or computer use
  • Walk gently and carefully; take care on stairs and wet floors
  • Ask your doctor before any neck exercises or stretching

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Cervical spinal stenosis (narrowed canal)?

Narrowing of the space in the neck bones that can press on the spinal cord or nerves. The channel inside the neck bones becomes narrower, which can squeeze the spinal cord or nearby nerves and cause neck, arm or balance problems.

What are the common signs of Cervical spinal stenosis (narrowed canal)?

Stiff, aching neck · Tingling down the arm · Weak, clumsy grip · Unsteady when walking

Why does Cervical spinal stenosis (narrowed canal) happen?

Getting older · Present from birth · An injury or fall

How can I look after myself with Cervical spinal stenosis (narrowed canal)?

Avoid heavy lifting
