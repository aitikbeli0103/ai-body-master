Sterile prostatitis (no bacteria found)

Long-lasting prostate and pelvic pain, with no germs found in tests

ConditionMen's healthPelvis

What it is

The prostate area is sore or uncomfortable, but urine and prostate fluid tests show no germs. It can last a long time and tends to come and go.

  • Pelvis, groin, behind scrotum
  • Adult men, any age
  • Dull ache that comes and goes
Which doctor
Family doctor or urologist
Common in
Adult men, including younger men; often with stress or long sitting
Contagious
No
Course
Often long-lasting with good and bad spells; many men cope well with a doctor's help

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain or discomfort when sitting for long
  • Pain after sex or when semen comes out
  • Feeling low or tense because of the long pain

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Tight or tired muscles in the pelvic floor
  • A past infection or injury in the area
  • Long sitting, cycling, or holding urine

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine at all: go to hospital now
  • Fever with chills, or very severe pain
  • Blood in the urine or in the semen
  • Weight loss or a new lump you cannot explain
  • Thoughts of harming yourself because of the pain: see a doctor now or call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Take a warm bath to relax the pelvic muscles
  • Do gentle walking and stretching, and try slow, deep breathing

Tell your therapist
