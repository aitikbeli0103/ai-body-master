Left anterior fascicular block

A slowed electrical signal in one branch of the heart, found on a heart tracing (ECG)

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

This is a pattern seen on a heart tracing (ECG), where the signal moves more slowly through one small part of the heart. Many people feel nothing at all.

  • Heart's electrical system
  • More often in older adults
  • Often no feeling at all
Which doctor
Family doctor, heart doctor (cardiologist)
Common in
Older adults; people with high blood pressure or heart disease
Contagious
No
Course
Often stays the same for years; a doctor may check it from time to time

Common signs

More signs

  • If another heart problem is present, you may feel tired or short of breath
  • Sometimes a racing or skipping heartbeat from another cause

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Some heart surgery or heart muscle problems

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, fainting or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Repeated dizziness or near-fainting
  • A very slow, very fast or uneven heartbeat that you can feel

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.
