Blisters at the corners of the mouth

Small fluid-filled blisters that appear at the corners of the mouth

ConditionMouth & teethHead

What it is

Small blisters can appear at the corner of the mouth. They may sting or tingle first, then break open and crust over.

  • Corner of the mouth
  • Can happen at any age
  • Tingling, stinging
Which doctor
Family doctor; dentist if the mouth is also sore
Common in
Anyone; more often with stress, illness or strong sun
Contagious
Yes, if caused by a virus; it spreads by close contact
Course
Often clears up by itself in one to two weeks

Common signs

More signs

  • Tingling or burning before the blisters show
  • Blisters that break and form a yellow-brown crust
  • Soreness when opening the mouth wide

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Sometimes cracks or irritation at the mouth corners

See a doctor first if

  • Swelling of the face or lips with trouble breathing: call 999
  • Blisters near the eye, or eye pain or redness: see a doctor now
  • A baby or young child with blisters around the mouth: see a doctor promptly
  • Blisters with high fever, or spreading widely
  • Not better after two weeks, or coming back often
  • Weak body defences (for example from illness or some medicines)

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
