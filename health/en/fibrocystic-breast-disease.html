Fibrocystic breast disease

Common, non-cancer changes in the breast that can cause lumps and soreness

ConditionWomen's healthChest

What it is

The breast tissue reacts to monthly hormone changes, so the breasts can feel lumpy or sore; any new lump should still be checked.

  • The breasts
  • Women who still have periods
  • Lumpy, tender breasts
Which doctor
Family doctor; breast clinic or breast surgeon for lumps
Common in
Women who still have periods; less common after menopause
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes with the monthly cycle; usually eases after menopause

Common signs

  • Breasts that feel lumpy or thick, often on both sides
  • Soreness, especially when touched, often worse before a period
  • Fullness or swelling of the breasts before a period
  • Smooth, round lumps that move under the skin and may change in size
  • Symptoms often ease once the period starts

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Breast tissue reacts to these hormones, so it can swell and form small fluid-filled sacs (cysts)
  • Some hormone medicines can play a part; do not stop any medicine without asking your doctor

See a doctor first if

  • A new lump that does not go away, feels hard, or does not move
  • Skin changes on the breast: dimpling, thickening, redness or an orange-peel look
  • Nipple discharge that is bloody or comes from one breast on its own
  • A new lump or swelling in the armpit, or a nipple that has newly turned inward
  • A red, hot, painful breast with fever, or pain that is severe and getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wear a well-fitting, supportive bra; a soft one at night may feel better
  • Get to know how your breasts normally look and feel, and check them regularly
  • Ask your doctor how often you should have a breast check or scan

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Broken, red or sore skin

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Fibrocystic breast disease?

Common, non-cancer changes in the breast that can cause lumps and soreness. The breast tissue reacts to monthly hormone changes, so the breasts can feel lumpy or sore; any new lump should still be checked.

What are the common signs of Fibrocystic breast disease?

Breasts that feel lumpy or thick, often on both sides · Soreness, especially when touched, often worse before a period · Fullness or swelling of the breasts before a period · Smooth, round lumps that move under the skin and may change in size · Symptoms often ease once the period starts

Why does Fibrocystic breast disease happen?

Hormone changes · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Fibrocystic breast disease?
