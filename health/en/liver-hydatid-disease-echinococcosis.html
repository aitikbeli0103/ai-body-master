Liver hydatid disease (echinococcosis)

A liver cyst caused by the larvae of a small tapeworm

ConditionLiver, gallbladder & pancreasAbdomen

What it is

Hydatid disease happens when tiny eggs of a dog tapeworm are swallowed and form slow-growing cysts, often in the liver. It needs a doctor's care.

  • Liver, sometimes lungs
  • Contact with dogs or sheep
  • Slow, dull belly ache
Which doctor
Family doctor, then liver or infection specialist
Common in
People who live or work near dogs and farm animals
Contagious
Not from person to person; it comes from tapeworm eggs
Course
Cysts grow slowly over years; a doctor decides the best care

Common signs

More signs

  • A lump in the upper belly in some people

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Swallowing eggs of a dog tapeworm
  • Food or water with the eggs on it
  • It is more likely where people live close to animals and hygiene is hard

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden trouble breathing, swelling of the face, rash or fainting: call 999
  • Sudden strong belly pain: go to hospital now
  • Fever with belly pain and yellow skin
  • A lump in the belly that is getting bigger
  • Long cough or chest pain

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wash hands well after touching dogs or farm animals, and before eating
  • Wash vegetables and fruit and drink safe water
  • Ask a vet about worm care for pet dogs
  • Follow your doctor's advice and keep your appointments
