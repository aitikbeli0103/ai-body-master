Myocarditis (inflamed heart muscle)

Swelling and irritation of the heart muscle, often after an infection

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

The heart muscle becomes inflamed. This can make the heart weaker at pumping blood and may cause chest pain, tiredness or an uneven heartbeat.

  • Heart muscle
  • Any age, often younger
  • Chest pain, tired
Which doctor
Heart doctor (cardiologist); go to hospital if unwell
Common in
Often follows a virus; can affect children and adults
Contagious
Not itself, but some viruses that cause it can spread
Course
Some people recover well; others need longer follow-up with a doctor

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Sometimes bacteria or other germs
  • Sometimes a reaction to a medicine or the body's own defences

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Fainting or nearly fainting
  • A racing, pounding or very uneven heartbeat
  • Chest pain or tiredness after a recent fever or flu
  • A child or baby who is very tired, pale or breathing fast: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Myocarditis (inflamed heart muscle)?

Swelling and irritation of the heart muscle, often after an infection. The heart muscle becomes inflamed. This can make the heart weaker at pumping blood and may cause chest pain, tiredness or an uneven heartbeat.

What are the common signs of Myocarditis (inflamed heart muscle)?

Tight, heavy chest · Short of breath on stairs · Racing, pounding heart · Fever, feeling hot · Tired all the time

Why does Myocarditis (inflamed heart muscle) happen?

Germs (infection) · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Myocarditis (inflamed heart muscle)?

Rest well · See your doctor for check-ups
