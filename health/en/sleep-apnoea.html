Sleep apnoea

Breathing that repeatedly pauses during sleep because the airway narrows

ConditionSleepHead

What it is

In sleep apnoea the airway narrows or closes many times during sleep, so breathing pauses briefly. Sleep is broken, and a person may feel very tired in the day.

  • Throat and airway
  • Adults, often with extra weight
  • Loud snoring, tired by day
Which doctor
Family doctor, sleep doctor or ENT doctor
Common in
Adults, especially with extra weight, and older people
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long-term, but a doctor can help you manage it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The throat muscles relax in sleep and the airway narrows
  • A narrow airway, big tonsils or a blocked nose
  • Alcohol before bed, smoking, and getting older

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, or sudden trouble breathing: call 999
  • Falling asleep while driving: do not drive and see a doctor soon
  • Pauses in breathing seen by others, with choking or gasping
  • Morning headaches, high blood pressure, or an uneven heartbeat
  • Heavy tiredness in the day that affects work or safety

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask your doctor about a sleep check; do not use sleep pills without advice

Questions and answers

What is Sleep apnoea?

Breathing that repeatedly pauses during sleep because the airway narrows. In sleep apnoea the airway narrows or closes many times during sleep, so breathing pauses briefly. Sleep is broken, and a person may feel very tired in the day.

What are the common signs of Sleep apnoea?

Loud snoring, tired by day · Sleepy in the daytime · Wakes up short of breath · Foggy, cannot focus · Short-tempered, on edge

Why does Sleep apnoea happen?

Extra body weight

How can I look after myself with Sleep apnoea?

Keep a healthy weight · Cut down on alcohol

Can an AI Body Master session help with Sleep apnoea?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Sleep apnoea and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
