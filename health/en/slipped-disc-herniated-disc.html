Slipped disc (herniated disc)

A soft disc between the spine bones bulges out and may press on a nerve

ConditionBones, joints & spineBones & muscles

What it is

The soft cushion between two bones of the spine pushes out of place. It may press on a nearby nerve and cause back pain or pain, numbness or weakness in a leg.

  • Back, sometimes down the leg
  • Adults, often middle age
  • Aching or shooting pain
Which doctor
Family doctor, orthopaedic or spine doctor
Common in
Adults who lift heavy things, sit long or are overweight
Contagious
No
Course
Often eases over weeks to months; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Sometimes an injury or a weak back; often the exact cause is not known

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot control your bladder or bowel, or numbness around the groin or buttocks: go to hospital now
  • A leg that is getting weak or a foot that drags
  • Back pain after a fall or accident
  • Back pain with fever, or unexplained weight loss
  • Pain that is severe, keeps getting worse or does not ease with rest

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep gently moving and walking; long bed rest is usually not helpful
  • Learn to lift by bending your knees and keeping the load close

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What are the common signs of Slipped disc (herniated disc)?

Lower back pain · Pain shooting down the leg · Hard to bend or lift · Legs feel weak

Why does Slipped disc (herniated disc) happen?

Wear and tear with age · Heavy or repeated work · Standing or sitting still for long

How can I look after myself with Slipped disc (herniated disc)?

Take breaks from sitting · Keep a healthy weight · Ask your pharmacist
