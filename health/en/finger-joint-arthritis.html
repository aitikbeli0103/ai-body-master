Finger joint arthritis

Wear or inflammation of the small joints of the fingers, causing pain, stiffness and sometimes lumps

ConditionBones, joints & spineArms & legs

What it is

The small joints of the fingers become sore and stiff. They may look swollen or bumpy, and it can be harder to grip, open jars or button clothes.

  • Finger joints
  • More common after middle age
  • Stiff, sore, swollen
Which doctor
Family doctor, rheumatologist (joint doctor) or orthopaedic doctor
Common in
Adults over middle age, more often women; people with hard hand work
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long-term and can flare up and settle; care can help keep the hands useful

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Past finger injuries or years of heavy hand work
  • Some types are caused by the body's own defence system attacking the joints

See a doctor first if

  • A joint that is suddenly red, hot, very swollen and painful, especially with fever
  • A finger injury, or a finger that looks bent or out of place: go to hospital
  • Numbness, or pale, cold or blue fingers
  • Morning stiffness lasting more than an hour, or many joints swollen
  • Hands so painful you cannot hold things or care for yourself

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep the fingers moving gently every day
  • Soak the hands in comfortably warm water before gentle exercises
  • Use jar openers, thick grips and easy-to-use tools
  • Pace your tasks and rest your hands between them

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Numb or tingling arm

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Finger joint arthritis?

Wear or inflammation of the small joints of the fingers, causing pain, stiffness and sometimes lumps. The small joints of the fingers become sore and stiff. They may look swollen or bumpy, and it can be harder to grip, open jars or button clothes.

What are the common signs of Finger joint arthritis?

Stiff, swollen fingers · Stiff back in the morning · Weak, clumsy grip · Wrist pain

Why does Finger joint arthritis happen?

Getting older · Runs in the family · The cause is not fully known
