Depressive state

A low, flat mood that can come with tiredness and loss of interest

ConditionTold by a doctor: moodWhole body

What it is

A depressive state means feeling low, empty or flat for some time, often with tiredness and poor sleep. It can be mild or serious, so it is worth talking to a doctor.

  • Mood and whole body
  • Can affect any adult
  • Low, flat, tired
Which doctor
Family doctor or psychiatrist
Common in
Any adult under stress, loss or long illness
Contagious
No
Course
Often eases with rest, support and care; it can come back

See a doctor first if

  • Thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life: see a doctor now or call 999
  • Not able to work, eat or sleep for many days
  • Hearing or seeing things that are not there
  • Using alcohol or other substances to cope
  • Low mood that lasts more than two weeks

Questions and answers

What is Depressive state?

A low, flat mood that can come with tiredness and loss of interest. A depressive state means feeling low, empty or flat for some time, often with tiredness and poor sleep. It can be mild or serious, so it is worth talking to a doctor.

What are the common signs of Depressive state?

Low mood, no interest · Tired all the time · Cannot fall asleep · Foggy, cannot focus · Short-tempered, on edge

Why does Depressive state happen?

Stress · Too little sleep · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Depressive state?

Rest well

Can an AI Body Master session help with Depressive state?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Depressive state and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
