Duodenal diverticulum

A small pouch that bulges out from the wall of the first part of the small bowel

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

A weak spot lets the wall of the duodenum bulge out into a small pouch. Most people never feel it and it is found by chance, but a few get pain, bloating or other trouble.

  • Upper belly
  • More common with age
  • Often no symptoms
Which doctor
Family doctor, stomach and bowel doctor (gastroenterologist)
Common in
Older adults; often found by chance on a scan or an endoscopy test
Contagious
No
Course
Often stays quiet and causes no trouble; a doctor can tell if it needs a closer look

Common signs

More signs

  • Rarely, fever or sharp pain if the pouch becomes inflamed or bleeds

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Pressure inside the bowel may push the weak spot outward over time
  • Problems start if food or fluid gets stuck in the pouch and it becomes irritated or inflamed

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden, severe belly pain or a hard belly: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Vomiting blood, or black or bloody stools: go to hospital now
  • Severe upper belly pain that goes through to the back: go to hospital now
  • Fever with belly pain: see a doctor or go to hospital the same day
  • Yellow skin or eyes, dark urine or pale stools: see a doctor the same day
  • Repeated vomiting, or you cannot keep food or drink down

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • If it was found by chance and gives no trouble, your doctor may simply keep watch
  • Eat plenty of vegetables and fruit, and drink enough water
  • Limit alcohol and do not smoke
