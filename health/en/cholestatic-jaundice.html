Cholestatic jaundice

Yellow skin and eyes because bile cannot flow out of the liver properly

ConditionLiver, gallbladder & pancreasWhole body

What it is

When bile cannot flow from the liver to the gut, a yellow colour builds up in the body. The skin and eyes turn yellow and the skin may itch.

  • Liver and bile ducts
  • Can happen at any age
  • Yellow skin, itch
Which doctor
Family doctor first; liver or digestive doctor
Common in
Adults with gallstones or liver problems
Contagious
No, but some causes (like hepatitis) can be
Course
Depends on the cause; a doctor needs to find it

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Gallstones blocking the bile tube
  • Inflammation or infection of the liver
  • Some medicines or herbal products
  • A growth pressing on the bile tube
  • Tests are needed to find the cause

See a doctor first if

  • Yellow skin or eyes: see a doctor soon, do not wait
  • Confusion or being very drowsy: call 999
  • Fever with chills and belly pain: go to hospital now
  • Vomiting blood or black stools
  • Dark urine and pale stools that last

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
