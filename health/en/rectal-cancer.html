Rectal cancer

Cancer that starts in the rectum, the last part of the bowel before the back passage

ConditionCancer & tumoursPelvis

What it is

Cells in the lining of the rectum grow out of control; it often starts as a small growth called a polyp that slowly changes over time.

  • Last part of the bowel
  • More common with age
  • Changes in toilet habits
Which doctor
Colorectal surgeon, cancer doctor (oncologist), family doctor
Common in
Mostly adults past middle age; a family history raises the risk
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on its size and spread; your cancer team can explain what to expect

Common signs

More signs

  • Feeling that the bowel has not fully emptied

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Polyps in the bowel, or a close relative with bowel cancer
  • Long-term bowel inflammation, such as ulcerative colitis

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy bleeding from the back passage, or feeling faint: go to hospital now
  • Severe belly pain with a swollen belly, vomiting, or not passing stools or gas: go to hospital now
  • Blood in your stool, or bleeding from the back passage that keeps coming back
  • A change in toilet habits that lasts for more than a few weeks
  • Losing weight without trying, or feeling very tired for weeks
  • A lump or pain in the back passage that does not go away

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop or change your cancer treatment or medicine on your own
  • Ask your team about pain, toilet habits, food and tiredness; help is available
  • Ask your team or a dietitian what foods are best for you

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Rectal cancer?

Cancer that starts in the rectum, the last part of the bowel before the back passage. Cells in the lining of the rectum grow out of control; it often starts as a small growth called a polyp that slowly changes over time.

What are the common signs of Rectal cancer?

Blood on the toilet paper · Changes in toilet habits · Cramping belly pain · Losing weight without trying

Why does Rectal cancer happen?

The cause is not fully known · Getting older · Smoking

How can I look after myself with Rectal cancer?

Follow your care team's plan · Talk to family and friends

Can an AI Body Master session help with Rectal cancer?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Rectal cancer and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
