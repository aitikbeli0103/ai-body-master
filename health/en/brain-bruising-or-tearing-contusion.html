Brain bruising or tearing (contusion)

Bruised or torn areas of brain tissue after a hard knock to the head

ConditionHead & neckHead

What it is

A hard blow to the head can bruise or tear part of the brain. It is a serious injury that needs urgent care in hospital.

  • Inside the head
  • After a fall or accident
  • Headache, confusion
Which doctor
Emergency doctor first; brain surgeon (neurosurgeon)
Common in
People hurt in road accidents, falls or fights
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on how severe it is; it needs hospital care and follow-up

Common signs

More signs

  • Headache that does not go away
  • Weakness or numbness in an arm or leg

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Falls, especially in older people
  • Being hit on the head in a fight or by a falling object

See a doctor first if

  • Fainting, fits, or cannot be woken: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Clear fluid or blood from the nose or ear: go to hospital now
  • Repeated vomiting or a headache that keeps getting worse
  • Any hard knock to the head followed by confusion or sleepiness

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Get medical help straight away after a serious head injury
  • Do not move the person's neck; keep them still until help comes
  • After hospital care, rest and follow your doctor's advice
  • Avoid alcohol and contact sports until your doctor says it is safe
  • Wear a helmet on a motorbike and a seat belt in a car

Tell your therapist

  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Epilepsy or fits
  • Dizzy when turning the head

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
