Myasthenia gravis

A condition where muscles get weak with use and get better with rest

ConditionBrain & nervesWhole body

What it is

The signals between nerves and muscles do not pass well, so muscles tire easily. Drooping eyelids and double vision are common.

  • Nerve-to-muscle signals
  • Any age, adults mostly
  • Weak and tired muscles
Which doctor
Brain and nerve doctor (neurologist)
Common in
Adults of any age; more often young women and older men
Contagious
No
Course
Long-term; signs can come and go, and doctors can help manage them

Common signs

More signs

  • Drooping eyelids

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body's defence system mistakenly attacks the nerve-muscle link
  • A gland in the chest (thymus) is sometimes involved

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, a weak cough or a very weak neck: call 999
  • Choking or being unable to swallow: go to hospital now
  • Sudden worsening of weakness or speech: go to hospital now
  • Fever or infection while having this condition

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Rest during the day and plan hard tasks for when you feel strongest
  • Eat soft foods in small bites if chewing is tiring
  • Tell any doctor or dentist you have this condition

Tell your therapist

Questions and answers

What is Myasthenia gravis?

A condition where muscles get weak with use and get better with rest. The signals between nerves and muscles do not pass well, so muscles tire easily. Drooping eyelids and double vision are common.

What are the common signs of Myasthenia gravis?

Blurry vision · Legs feel weak · Hard or painful to swallow · Tired all the time

Why does Myasthenia gravis happen?

The cause is not fully known · Germs (infection)
