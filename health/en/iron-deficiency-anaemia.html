Iron-deficiency anaemia

Too little iron in the body, so there are fewer healthy red blood cells to carry oxygen

ConditionBloodBlood

What it is

The body needs iron to make red blood cells. When iron runs low, the blood carries less oxygen, so you may feel tired, pale and short of breath.

  • Red blood cells
  • Common in women and children
  • Tired and pale
Which doctor
Family doctor; blood doctor if needed
Common in
Women with heavy periods, pregnant women, young children, people who eat little iron
Contagious
No
Course
Often improves once the cause is found and looked after; a doctor guides this

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Not enough iron in food
  • Heavy or long periods, or pregnancy
  • Slow bleeding in the stomach or gut that you may not see
  • Problems taking in iron from food, such as some gut illnesses
  • Growing fast in childhood

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, trouble breathing or fainting: call 999
  • Black stools, blood in the stools, or vomiting blood: go to hospital now
  • Very heavy periods that soak through pads quickly
  • A racing heart or feeling faint when standing
  • A pregnant woman or young child who looks very pale and tired

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask your doctor before taking iron pills; too much iron can be harmful
  • Fruit with vitamin C at meals can help the body take in iron
  • Get the cause checked by your doctor, and rest when you feel tired

Questions and answers

What is Iron-deficiency anaemia?

Too little iron in the body, so there are fewer healthy red blood cells to carry oxygen. The body needs iron to make red blood cells. When iron runs low, the blood carries less oxygen, so you may feel tired, pale and short of breath.

What are the common signs of Iron-deficiency anaemia?

Pale, tired look · Tired all the time · Short of breath on stairs · Cold hands and feet · Hair coming out · Brittle, ridged nails

Why does Iron-deficiency anaemia happen?

Not enough iron in food · Heavy or long periods, or pregnancy · Slow bleeding in the stomach or gut that you may not see · Problems taking in iron from food, such as some gut illnesses · Growing fast in childhood

How can I look after myself with Iron-deficiency anaemia?

Eat healthy, balanced meals · Cut down on coffee and tea

Can an AI Body Master session help with Iron-deficiency anaemia?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Iron-deficiency anaemia and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
