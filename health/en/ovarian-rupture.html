Ovarian rupture

A cyst or follicle on the ovary bursts, which can cause sudden pain and bleeding

ConditionWomen's healthPelvis

What it is

A fluid-filled cyst or follicle on the ovary can burst. It often causes sudden pain low in the belly. Some cases are mild, but bleeding inside the body can be serious, so get checked.

  • Lower belly, ovary
  • Women of childbearing age
  • Sudden sharp pain
Which doctor
Emergency doctor, gynaecologist
Common in
Women of childbearing age, often mid-cycle or after hard activity
Contagious
No
Course
Mild cases may settle with rest, but heavy bleeding needs urgent care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • An ovarian cyst that grows large and bursts
  • A follicle that bursts at ovulation, sometimes with extra bleeding

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden strong belly pain: go to hospital now
  • Fainting, feeling very dizzy, pale or cold, or a racing heart: call 999
  • Pain with heavy bleeding from the vagina
  • Belly pain when you could be pregnant
  • Belly pain with fever or vomiting

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

What are the common signs of Ovarian rupture?

Why does Ovarian rupture happen?

A fall, knock or road accident · A side effect of some medicines
