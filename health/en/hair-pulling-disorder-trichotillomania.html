Hair-pulling disorder (trichotillomania)

A strong urge to pull out your own hair that is hard to stop

ConditionTold by a doctor: anxiety, fears & stressSkin

What it is

A person feels a strong urge to pull out hair from the scalp, eyebrows or lashes. It is often linked to stress or tension, and it is not a sign of weakness.

  • Scalp, brows, lashes
  • Often starts young
  • Urge to pull hair
Which doctor
Family doctor, psychologist, psychiatrist or skin doctor
Common in
Teens and adults; more often reported in women
Contagious
No
Course
Can come and go for years; support often helps people feel more in control

Common signs

More signs

  • Pulling out hair again and again, often without noticing
  • Feeling tense before pulling and relieved afterwards
  • Feeling ashamed and hiding the hair loss
  • Sometimes playing with or eating the pulled hair

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Often linked to stress, boredom or strong feelings
  • It may come with anxiety or low mood

See a doctor first if

  • Thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999
  • Stomach pain, vomiting or a swollen belly if hair is swallowed: go to hospital
  • Sore, bleeding or infected skin where hair is pulled
  • Hair loss that may have another cause, such as patches appearing suddenly
  • A child or teen who is very upset or avoids school or friends

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Be kind to yourself: it is not your fault
  • Keep your hands busy with a stress ball or fidget toy
  • Notice when the urge starts and take a slow breath
  • Keep nails short and sleep regularly
  • Talk to your doctor about support, for you or your child

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
