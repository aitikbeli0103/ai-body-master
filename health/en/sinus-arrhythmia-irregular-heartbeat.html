Sinus arrhythmia (irregular heartbeat)

A heartbeat that speeds up and slows down with breathing; often normal, especially in the young

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

The heart beats a little faster when you breathe in and a little slower when you breathe out. This is usually a normal pattern.

  • Heart rhythm
  • Common in young people
  • Beat changes with breathing
Which doctor
Family doctor; heart doctor if unsure
Common in
Children, young adults and fit people
Contagious
No
Course
Often needs no care; it is usually found at a check-up

Common signs

More signs

  • A pulse that is faster when you breathe in and slower when you breathe out
  • It is found on a pulse check or heart tracing
  • Sometimes a mild awareness of the heartbeat

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A normal link between breathing and the nerves that control the heart
  • Being young or physically fit

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain or sudden trouble breathing: call 999
  • Fainting or nearly fainting: go to hospital now
  • Racing or uneven heartbeat that does not settle
  • Dizzy spells or unusual tiredness

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Why does Sinus arrhythmia (irregular heartbeat) happen?

The cause is not fully known · A side effect of some medicines
