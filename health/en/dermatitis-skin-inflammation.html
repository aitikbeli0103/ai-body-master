Dermatitis (skin inflammation)

Red, dry, itchy or sore skin caused by inflammation of the skin

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsSkin

What it is

Dermatitis means the skin is inflamed. It can look red, dry and itchy, and sometimes blistered or cracked. There are several types, each with its own triggers.

  • Skin, anywhere on the body
  • Can start at any age
  • Red, dry, itchy skin
Which doctor
Family doctor or skin doctor (dermatologist)
Common in
Anyone; more often in people with allergies, asthma or dry skin
Contagious
No. You cannot catch it from another person
Course
Often comes and goes; may ease when triggers are found and avoided

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Contact with things that irritate the skin, such as harsh soaps, detergents or chemicals
  • An allergy to things like nickel, perfume, rubber or some plants

See a doctor first if

  • Swollen lips, face or tongue, wheezing or trouble breathing after contact with something: call 999
  • Rash over most of the body, or skin that is red, peeling and painful: go to hospital now
  • Fever with painful, hot, swollen skin, pus or yellow crusts: see a doctor today
  • Groups of painful blisters or sores on top of the rash, especially with fever or near the eyes: go to hospital now
  • A baby or child with a widespread or oozing rash: see a doctor promptly
  • Itching that stops you sleeping, or no improvement after a week or two of care: see a doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Bathe in lukewarm water, keep showers short, and use a mild, soap-free wash
  • Notice what makes it worse (soaps, perfume, metals, plants) and avoid it
  • Wear soft cotton next to the skin, and keep your nails short

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • A severe allergy
  • Fever or an active infection

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
