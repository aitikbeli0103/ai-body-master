Kidney sarcoma

A rare cancer that starts in the soft tissue of the kidney

ConditionCancer & tumoursAbdomen

What it is

Kidney sarcoma is a rare cancer that grows from the supporting tissue of the kidney. It is different from the more common kidney cancers.

  • Kidney, in the back of the belly
  • Rare; can affect adults
  • Side pain or a lump
Which doctor
Kidney doctor (urologist), cancer doctor (oncologist)
Common in
Rare; often found in adults
Contagious
No
Course
Differs from person to person; the cancer care team can explain it

Common signs

More signs

  • A lump or fullness in the side or belly

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Past radiation to the belly area may raise the risk

See a doctor first if

  • Blood in the urine: see a doctor soon
  • A new lump in the side or belly
  • Heavy bleeding, fainting or very severe pain: go to hospital now
  • Weight loss without trying or pain that keeps getting worse
  • Fever with trouble breathing: call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Kidney sarcoma?

A rare cancer that starts in the soft tissue of the kidney. Kidney sarcoma is a rare cancer that grows from the supporting tissue of the kidney. It is different from the more common kidney cancers.

What are the common signs of Kidney sarcoma?

Pain in the side or loin · Pink or red urine · Losing weight without trying · No appetite

Why does Kidney sarcoma happen?

The cause is not fully known · Runs in the family · Smoking

How can I look after myself with Kidney sarcoma?

See your doctor for check-ups · Drink enough water · Stop smoking
