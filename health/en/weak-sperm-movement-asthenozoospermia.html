Weak sperm movement (asthenozoospermia)

A low share of sperm that swim well, which can make it harder to start a pregnancy

ConditionMen's healthReproductive

What it is

Sperm need to swim well to reach the egg. In this condition many of them move slowly or weakly. It is found by a semen test, not by feeling.

  • Male reproductive system
  • Men trying for a baby
  • Often no signs at all
Which doctor
Family doctor, urologist or fertility doctor
Common in
Men of child-bearing age; often found when a couple cannot conceive
Contagious
No
Course
Varies with the cause; some men improve and some do not

Common signs

More signs

  • A semen test shows slow or weak sperm movement
  • Sometimes a swelling or ache in the sac, if there is a cause there

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Swollen veins in the sac (varicocele)
  • Past infections of the testicle or the genital tract
  • Smoking, heavy alcohol, being overweight or long stress
  • Too much heat on the groin, or some medicines and chemicals

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden, severe pain in a testicle: go to hospital now
  • A hard lump or growing swelling in a testicle
  • Fever with pain or redness in the sac
  • Blood in the semen or pain when passing urine
  • Trying for a baby for a long time without success: see a doctor together with your partner

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Avoid long, hot baths and keeping a laptop on your lap

A low share of sperm that swim well, which can make it harder to start a pregnancy. Sperm need to swim well to reach the egg. In this condition many of them move slowly or weakly. It is found by a semen test, not by feeling.
