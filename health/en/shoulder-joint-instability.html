Shoulder joint instability

A shoulder joint that is loose and can slip partly or fully out of place

ConditionBones, joints & spineArms & legs

What it is

The shoulder is a very mobile joint held in place by ligaments and muscles. When these are stretched or torn, the shoulder can feel loose, give way or slip out of its socket.

  • Shoulder
  • Often after injury or sport
  • Loose, slipping, painful
Which doctor
Orthopaedic doctor, sports doctor, physiotherapist
Common in
Young active people, sports players, and people who were hurt before
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; strengthening and a doctor's advice often help, but it can recur

Common signs

More signs

  • A feeling that the shoulder is loose or about to slip
  • A clicking, catching or popping feeling

Why it happens

  • A past shoulder dislocation or hard fall
  • Naturally loose joints (very flexible people)
  • Repeated overhead movements, such as swimming or throwing
  • Weak shoulder and shoulder-blade muscles
  • Torn ligaments or damage to the rim of the socket

See a doctor first if

  • Shoulder looks out of place or deformed, or you cannot move it: go to hospital
  • Numbness, pale or cold skin, or a weak pulse in the arm or hand
  • Severe pain after a fall or sports injury
  • A hot, red, swollen shoulder with fever
  • Shoulder that slips out again and again

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask a physiotherapist for safe strengthening exercises
  • Warm up before sport and build up slowly
  • Ask your doctor before using any pain medicine

Tell your therapist

  • Recent shoulder injury or surgery
  • Hot, red or swollen shoulder
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
