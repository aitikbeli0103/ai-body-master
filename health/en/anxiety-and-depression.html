Anxiety and depression

Feeling worried and tense as well as low and tired, often together for weeks or longer

ConditionTold by a doctor: anxiety, fears & stressWhole body

What it is

Some people feel anxious and low at the same time. Worry, poor sleep and loss of interest can build up and affect daily life. It is common, not a weakness, and help works.

  • Mind and whole body
  • Can affect anyone
  • Worry, low mood, tiredness
Which doctor
Family doctor, psychiatrist or psychologist
Common in
Adults of any age, often after long stress, loss or illness
Contagious
No
Course
Often improves with support and care from a doctor; it can come and go

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Often a mix of reasons and the exact cause is not known
  • Poor sleep, loneliness, or a family history of low mood or anxiety

See a doctor first if

  • Thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life: see a doctor now or call 999
  • Hearing or seeing things others do not, or feeling very confused
  • Not eating or sleeping for days, or you cannot look after yourself
  • Low mood or worry that lasts more than two weeks
  • Using alcohol or other substances to cope

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Talk to someone you trust; you do not have to carry it alone
  • Keep a regular time for sleeping, waking and meals
  • Take a gentle walk or light activity each day if you can
  • Do one small thing you used to enjoy, even for a short time
  • Ask your doctor about talking therapy and other support; do not change medicine on your own

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • A heart condition
  • High blood pressure
  • Epilepsy or fits
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Anxiety and depression?

Feeling worried and tense as well as low and tired, often together for weeks or longer. Some people feel anxious and low at the same time. Worry, poor sleep and loss of interest can build up and affect daily life. It is common, not a weakness, and help works.

What are the common signs of Anxiety and depression?

Low mood, no interest · Worried and on edge · Cannot fall asleep · Tired all the time · Foggy, cannot focus · Short-tempered, on edge

Why does Anxiety and depression happen?

Stress · Other long-term illnesses

How can I look after myself with Anxiety and depression?

Talk to someone you trust; you do not have to carry it alone · Keep a regular time for sleeping, waking and meals · Take a gentle walk or light activity each day if you can · Do one small thing you used to enjoy, even for a short time · Ask your doctor about talking therapy and other support; do not change medicine on your own
