Intercostal neuralgia (rib nerve pain)

Sharp or burning pain along the nerves that run between the ribs

ConditionBrain & nervesHeadChest

What it is

A nerve between two ribs becomes irritated, giving sharp, stabbing or burning pain around the chest wall, often on one side.

  • Along the ribs
  • Adults, more with age
  • Sharp, band-like pain
Which doctor
Family doctor; brain and nerve doctor if it lasts
Common in
Adults; also after shingles, chest injury or surgery
Contagious
No
Course
Often eases over weeks, but it depends on the cause

Common signs

More signs

  • Sharp, stabbing or burning pain along one or more ribs
  • Pain that can wrap around from the back to the front
  • Sometimes tingling, numb or very sore skin over the ribs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A nerve squeezed or irritated in the spine or between the ribs
  • Shingles (the nerve pain can stay after the rash heals)
  • A rib or chest injury, or chest surgery
  • Often the cause is not found

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain with trouble breathing, sweating, or pain spreading to the arm or jaw: call 999
  • Any new chest pain you are not sure about: have it checked by a doctor first
  • A painful blistering rash on one side of the chest
  • Coughing blood, fever, or losing weight without trying
  • Pain after a fall or hit to the chest, or weakness in the legs

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Rest, and avoid movements that bring on the pain
  • Breathe slowly and gently; avoid holding your breath
  • For pain medicine, ask your doctor or pharmacist

Tell your therapist

  • A heart condition
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
