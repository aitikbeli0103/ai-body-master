Brain haemorrhage from high blood pressure

Bleeding inside the brain caused by long-standing high blood pressure

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

Over time, high blood pressure can weaken the small blood vessels in the brain. One can burst and bleed. It is an emergency.

  • Deep inside the brain
  • People with high blood pressure
  • Sudden headache, weakness
Which doctor
Emergency doctor first; then a neurologist and family doctor
Common in
Middle-aged and older adults whose blood pressure is not well controlled
Contagious
No
Course
Varies a lot; needs hospital care, and recovery may take weeks to months

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Forgetting or stopping blood pressure medicine without advice

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden severe headache, weakness, drooping face or trouble speaking: call 999 now
  • Fainting, a fit or being hard to wake: call 999
  • Very high blood pressure reading with headache, vomiting or blurred vision: go to hospital now
  • Sudden loss of vision: go to hospital now

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Brain haemorrhage from high blood pressure?

Bleeding inside the brain caused by long-standing high blood pressure. Over time, high blood pressure can weaken the small blood vessels in the brain. One can burst and bleed. It is an emergency.

What are the common signs of Brain haemorrhage from high blood pressure?

Vomiting · Weak arm · Slurred or unclear speech · Confused or muddled · High reading on a blood pressure check

Why does Brain haemorrhage from high blood pressure happen?

High blood sugar or blood pressure · Smoking · Getting older

How can I look after myself with Brain haemorrhage from high blood pressure?

Note down your symptoms · Eat less salt · Stop smoking
