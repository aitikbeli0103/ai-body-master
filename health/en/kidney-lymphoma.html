Kidney lymphoma

Lymphoma, a cancer of the body's germ-fighting cells, that is found in the kidney

ConditionCancer & tumoursAbdomen

What it is

Kidney lymphoma is a cancer of the lymph cells that involves one or both kidneys. It is often part of lymphoma elsewhere in the body, and is cared for by a cancer team.

  • Kidney and lymph glands
  • Mostly adults
  • Fever, night sweats
Which doctor
Blood and cancer doctor (haematologist or oncologist)
Common in
Adults; people with a weak immune system
Contagious
No
Course
Varies by type; your cancer team can explain what to expect

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Some long-lasting infections

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing or chest pain: call 999
  • High fever or shivering, especially during cancer treatment: go to hospital now
  • Heavy blood in the urine, or unable to pass urine: go to hospital now
  • Severe belly or back pain
  • Swollen glands that keep growing, soaking night sweats or losing weight without trying

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Kidney lymphoma?

Lymphoma, a cancer of the body's germ-fighting cells, that is found in the kidney. Kidney lymphoma is a cancer of the lymph cells that involves one or both kidneys. It is often part of lymphoma elsewhere in the body, and is cared for by a cancer team.

What are the common signs of Kidney lymphoma?

Pain in the side or loin · Pink or red urine · Soaking sweats at night · Losing weight without trying · Swollen glands in the neck

Why does Kidney lymphoma happen?

The cause is not fully known · A weak immune system · Getting older

How can I look after myself with Kidney lymphoma?

See your doctor for check-ups · Wash your hands often · Rest well · Talk to family and friends
