Heart palpitations

Feeling your heart beat fast, hard, or skip a beat

SymptomHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

Palpitations are the feeling that your heart is pounding, fluttering or skipping. They are often harmless, but sometimes they point to a heart rhythm problem.

  • Chest and throat
  • Can happen to anyone
  • Pounding or fluttering
Which doctor
Family doctor, heart doctor (cardiologist)
Common in
Anyone; often with stress, caffeine or poor sleep
Contagious
No
Course
Often passes in seconds to minutes; keep track of how often it comes

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • An overactive thyroid, low blood count, or a heart rhythm problem

See a doctor first if

  • Palpitations with chest pain, trouble breathing or fainting: call 999
  • Palpitations with weakness on one side, face drooping or speech trouble: call 999
  • Feeling very dizzy or nearly fainting
  • Palpitations that last a long time or keep coming back
  • You have heart disease, or a family history of sudden heart problems

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Questions and answers

What is Heart palpitations?

Feeling your heart beat fast, hard, or skip a beat. Palpitations are the feeling that your heart is pounding, fluttering or skipping. They are often harmless, but sometimes they point to a heart rhythm problem.

What are the common signs of Heart palpitations?

Racing, pounding heart · Tight, heavy chest · Feeling faint · Short of breath on stairs

Why does Heart palpitations happen?

Stress · Smoking · Too little sleep · A side effect of some medicines

How can I look after myself with Heart palpitations?

Cut down on alcohol · Stay active every day · Note down your symptoms · Ask your pharmacist

Can an AI Body Master session help with Heart palpitations?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Heart palpitations and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
