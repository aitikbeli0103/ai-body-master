Inflamed external haemorrhoids (piles)

Swollen, sore piles at the edge of the anus, outside the opening

ConditionStomach & intestinesPelvis

What it is

Piles are swollen veins around the anus. When they are inflamed, the lump outside the anus becomes sore, swollen and sometimes itchy.

  • Edge of the anus
  • Common in adults
  • Sore lump, itch
Which doctor
Family doctor, colorectal or general surgeon
Common in
Adults; people with constipation, pregnancy or long sitting
Contagious
No
Course
Often settles in days to weeks with self-care; it can come back

Common signs

More signs

  • A tender lump at the edge of the anus
  • Pain, especially when sitting or passing stools

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Sitting for a long time, or sitting long on the toilet
  • Being overweight, lifting heavy things, or a low-fibre diet

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy bleeding, dizziness or fainting: go to hospital now
  • Very bad pain, a hard purple lump, or fever
  • Blood in the stools that is dark, mixed in, or keeps coming back
  • Change in bowel habit or weight loss without trying
  • No better after about a week of self-care

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Eat more fibre (vegetables, fruit) and drink enough water
  • Do not strain or sit on the toilet for long
  • Keep the area clean and dry; pat gently, do not rub

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
