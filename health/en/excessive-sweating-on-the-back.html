Excessive sweating on the back

Sweating much more than usual on the back, enough to soak the shirt

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsBack

What it is

The skin of the back sweats a lot, so the shirt can get wet through and stick to the skin. It may happen with heat or activity, or for no clear reason.

  • The back
  • Anyone, often in hot weather
  • Wet shirt, damp skin
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor (dermatologist) if it persists
Common in
Anyone, especially in hot, humid weather or when active
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes with heat and activity

Common signs

More signs

  • Sweating in other places too, such as the underarms, chest or face
  • A sticky feeling or body smell, especially in hot weather
  • In some people, sweating with a racing heart, shakiness or weight loss

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Hot, humid weather, hot rooms or heavy clothes
  • Exercise, physical work, or a backpack that traps heat against the back
  • Stress, worry, or spicy food and hot drinks
  • Sometimes another illness, such as an overactive thyroid, diabetes or an infection, or a medicine

See a doctor first if

  • Sweating with sudden severe pain in the chest, back, arm or jaw, or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Sudden cold sweat with fainting, confusion or feeling very weak: go to hospital now
  • Soaking night sweats, fever or weight loss without trying
  • A racing heart, shaking, always feeling hot, or a lump in the neck along with the sweating
  • Heavy sweating that began after starting a new medicine: ask your doctor or pharmacist
  • Heavy sweating that is new for you and has no clear reason

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Shower after sweating, dry well and use a clean towel
  • Stay in cool, airy places and use a fan when you can
  • Carry a spare shirt or wear an absorbent undershirt when you go out

Tell your therapist

  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A heart condition
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
