Mastoiditis

Infection of the bone just behind the ear, usually after an ear infection

ConditionEars, nose & throatChest

What it is

The bone behind the ear (the mastoid) becomes infected, most often after a middle ear infection that was not fully settled. It needs a doctor.

  • Bone behind the ear
  • More often in children
  • Ear pain and swelling
Which doctor
Ear-nose-throat doctor; see a doctor promptly
Common in
Mostly young children, but adults can get it
Contagious
No, but the ear infection behind it can come from germs that spread
Course
Needs a doctor's care; with proper care many people recover

Common signs

More signs

  • The ear may stick out from the head

Why it happens

Other causes

  • An ear infection that was not fully treated or keeps returning
  • Germs from a cold or throat infection reaching the ear
  • A weaker body defence may raise the risk

See a doctor first if

  • Severe headache, stiff neck, confusion or being very drowsy: call 999 now
  • Face drooping on one side, or dizziness with vomiting: go to hospital now
  • Swelling, redness or pain behind the ear: see a doctor the same day
  • High fever with ear pain, or fluid or pus from the ear
  • A baby or child who is very unwell: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
