Broken sacrum

A break in the triangle-shaped bone at the bottom of the spine, between the hip bones

ConditionBack & spinePelvisBones & muscles

What it is

The sacrum is the triangle-shaped bone at the base of the spine; a hard fall or accident, or even a small fall when bones are weak, can break it and cause low back and buttock pain.

  • Base of spine and buttocks
  • After a fall; weak bones
  • Low back and buttock pain
Which doctor
Hospital emergency doctor; bone doctor (orthopaedic doctor)
Common in
People after a hard fall or accident; older adults with weak bones; some runners
Contagious
No
Course
Needs a doctor first; recovery often takes weeks to months, and some need an operation

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain that is worse when sitting, standing up or walking
  • Bruising or swelling over the lower back or buttocks
  • Pain that spreads to the hip, groin or back of the thigh
  • Numbness or tingling in the buttocks, legs or groin area

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Weak bones (osteoporosis): even a small fall, or no clear injury, can cause a crack
  • Repeated strain in some runners, which can cause a small stress crack

See a doctor first if

  • After a big fall or road accident: call 999, and do not move the person unless they are in danger
  • Heavy bleeding, blood in the urine, severe belly pain or feeling faint after an injury: call 999
  • Trouble passing urine or stool, or losing control of them: go to hospital now
  • Numbness around the buttocks, groin or inner thighs: go to hospital now
  • Weak or numb legs, or you cannot stand or walk: go to hospital now
  • Bad pain even after a small fall, especially if you are older or have weak bones: see a doctor the same day

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See a doctor first for a check and an X-ray or scan; ask your doctor or pharmacist about pain medicine
  • A soft cushion can make sitting easier; change position often and walk gently as allowed

Tell your therapist

  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Weak bones
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
