Gastrointestinal stromal tumour (GIST)

A tumour in the wall of the stomach or bowel that grows from cells which help move food along

ConditionCancer & tumoursAbdomen

What it is

A GIST is a tumour in the wall of the digestive tract, most often in the stomach or small bowel. Some grow slowly while others can spread, so a specialist team checks each one carefully.

  • Wall of the stomach or bowel
  • Mostly adults; rare
  • Often mild or no signs
Which doctor
Family doctor, then a cancer doctor (oncologist) and a surgeon
Common in
Rare; mostly adults in middle and older age
Contagious
No
Course
It varies: some grow slowly, some faster; regular check-ups are needed

Common signs

More signs

  • Black or bloody stools, or vomiting blood, if the tumour bleeds

Why it happens

  • Usually a gene change in cells of the gut wall that happens by chance during life
  • No cause from food, stress or lifestyle is known
  • Rarely it runs in a family, or comes with an inherited condition
  • Often the exact reason is not known

See a doctor first if

  • Vomiting blood, black or bloody stools, or feeling faint: go to hospital now
  • Sudden severe belly pain, or a hard, swollen belly: go to hospital now
  • Vomiting that will not stop, or food that will not go down
  • A lump in the belly, or belly pain that keeps coming back or lasts for weeks
  • Tiredness, paleness or weight loss with no clear reason
  • New belly pain, a lump or black stools after an earlier GIST: tell your cancer team

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Follow your cancer team's plan and keep all check-ups and scans
  • Keep taking your cancer medicine as your team says; talk to them before any change
  • Tell your team about side effects, tiredness or low mood; support is available

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
