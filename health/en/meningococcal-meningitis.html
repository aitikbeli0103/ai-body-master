Meningococcal meningitis

A serious infection of the lining around the brain and spinal cord

ConditionInfectionsHead

What it is

Meningococcus germs can infect the lining around the brain and spinal cord. It can get serious very fast and is a medical emergency.

  • Brain and spinal cord lining
  • Children and young adults
  • High fever, stiff neck
Which doctor
Go to hospital emergency; later an infection doctor
Common in
Babies, children, teenagers and people in crowded living
Contagious
Yes, close contacts can catch it from saliva and droplets
Course
Can worsen within hours; fast hospital care is vital

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Meningococcus germs living in the nose and throat that reach the blood
  • Spread by coughing, sneezing, kissing or sharing cups
  • Weak body defences or no spleen

See a doctor first if

  • High fever with a stiff neck, bad headache or dislike of light: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Purple or dark red spots on the skin that do not fade when pressed: call 999
  • Confusion, great sleepiness, fits or fainting
  • A baby that is very sleepy, will not feed, cries oddly or has a bulging soft spot
  • Cold hands and feet with fast breathing, or signs of shock

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
