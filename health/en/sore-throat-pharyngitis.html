Sore throat (pharyngitis)

Swelling and soreness of the throat that makes swallowing painful

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

The back of the throat (pharynx) becomes red, sore and scratchy, often with a dry cough or a hoarse voice.

  • Back of the throat
  • Children and adults
  • Sore, scratchy throat
Which doctor
Family doctor, ear-nose-throat (ENT) doctor
Common in
Anyone; often with colds, smoke or dry air
Contagious
Yes, when caused by a virus or bacteria; spreads by droplets and touch
Course
Often gets better in a few days to a week

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Bacteria such as strep throat

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, noisy breathing, or drooling because you cannot swallow: call 999
  • Cannot open the mouth fully, or a very muffled voice with severe one-sided pain
  • High fever, or fever that lasts more than a few days
  • A sore throat that lasts more than a week, or with a lump in the neck
  • A baby or small child who will not drink or is very unwell: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Sore throat (pharyngitis)?

Swelling and soreness of the throat that makes swallowing painful. The back of the throat (pharynx) becomes red, sore and scratchy, often with a dry cough or a hoarse voice.

What are the common signs of Sore throat (pharyngitis)?

Sore, scratchy throat · Hoarse, weak voice · Fever, feeling hot · Swollen glands in the neck

Why does Sore throat (pharyngitis) happen?

Germs (infection) · Dry air and air-conditioning · Acid coming up from the stomach

How can I look after myself with Sore throat (pharyngitis)?

Drink enough water · Stop smoking · Wash your hands often · Ask your pharmacist
