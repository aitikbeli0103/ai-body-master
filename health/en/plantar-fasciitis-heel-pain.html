Plantar fasciitis (heel pain)

Heel pain from an irritated band of tissue under the foot, often worst with the first steps

ConditionBones, joints & spineArms & legsBones & muscles

What it is

This is a common cause of heel pain. The band of tissue along the sole becomes sore from strain, so the heel hurts when you first stand up or after you have been resting for a while.

  • Heel and sole
  • Walkers, runners, standing jobs
  • Sore heel, worse after rest
Which doctor
Family doctor, podiatrist, physiotherapist, orthopaedic doctor
Common in
Adults aged middle years and older, runners, people on their feet all day
Contagious
No
Course
Often improves over weeks to months, but may come back if the cause stays

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Too much standing, walking or running
  • Thin, worn or unsupportive shoes, or flip-flops all day
  • Tight calf muscles or an unusual foot shape
  • Often more than one reason together

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden bad heel pain with a pop after exercise, or you cannot walk
  • A hot, red, swollen heel, or fever
  • Numbness, burning or tingling in the foot
  • Foot pain or sores if you have diabetes
  • Pain that keeps getting worse or lasts many months

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do slow, gentle calf and sole stretches
  • Roll the sole gently over a soft ball if it feels comfortable
  • Ask your doctor before using any pain medicine or insoles

Tell your therapist

  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Plantar fasciitis (heel pain)?

Heel pain from an irritated band of tissue under the foot, often worst with the first steps. This is a common cause of heel pain. The band of tissue along the sole becomes sore from strain, so the heel hurts when you first stand up or after you have been resting for a while.

What are the common signs of Plantar fasciitis (heel pain)?

Heel pain on first steps · Aching arches of the foot · Hard to walk far · Painful ankle

Why does Plantar fasciitis (heel pain) happen?

Extra body weight

How can I look after myself with Plantar fasciitis (heel pain)?

Wear comfortable, supportive shoes · Rest the sore joint
