Side ligament injury (knee)

A strain or tear of the bands on the inner or outer side of the knee

ConditionPelvis, hip, knee & legArms & legs

What it is

The side ligaments keep the knee steady from side to side. A knock to the side of the knee can stretch or tear them, causing pain and a wobbly feeling.

  • Side of the knee
  • Sports, falls, knocks
  • Pain on the side, wobbly
Which doctor
Family doctor, bone doctor (orthopaedic), sports doctor
Common in
Football, badminton and other sports players
Contagious
No
Course
Mild ones often settle over weeks; a doctor can advise

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A twist of the knee with the foot planted

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot put weight on the leg, or the knee looks bent or out of shape: go to hospital
  • Numb, pale or cold foot: go to hospital now
  • Heavy swelling soon after the injury
  • Knee feels very loose or keeps giving way
  • Fever with a hot, red knee

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Use a knee support or walking aid if your doctor advises
  • Return to sport slowly, after your doctor agrees

Tell your therapist
