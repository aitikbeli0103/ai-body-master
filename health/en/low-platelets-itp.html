Low platelets (ITP)

A low number of platelets (the cells that help blood clot) because the immune system attacks them

ConditionBloodBlood

What it is

In ITP the body's own immune system destroys platelets. With fewer platelets you may bruise easily, get small red spots on the skin or bleed for longer.

  • Platelets in the blood
  • Children and adults
  • Bruises and red spots
Which doctor
Blood doctor (haematologist)
Common in
Children after a virus; adults, especially women
Contagious
No
Course
In children it often settles by itself; in adults it can last longer and needs follow-up

Common signs

More signs

  • Tiny red or purple dots on the skin

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The immune system makes antibodies that destroy platelets
  • In adults it can go with other immune illnesses or some infections

See a doctor first if

  • A bad headache, confusion, vomiting or sleepiness, especially after a knock to the head: call 999
  • Bleeding that will not stop after firm pressure: go to hospital now
  • Vomiting blood, black stools or blood in the urine
  • Blood blisters in the mouth or many new red spots
  • A child with these signs: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Avoid contact sports and activities where you could fall hard
  • Brush your teeth gently with a soft toothbrush
  • Tell your dentist and every doctor about your low platelets

Tell your therapist

  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Weak bones
  • Pregnancy
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Low platelets (ITP)?

A low number of platelets (the cells that help blood clot) because the immune system attacks them. In ITP the body's own immune system destroys platelets. With fewer platelets you may bruise easily, get small red spots on the skin or bleed for longer.

What are the common signs of Low platelets (ITP)?

Bruises easily · Nosebleeds · Heavy or long periods · Shows up in a blood test

Why does Low platelets (ITP) happen?

Germs (infection) · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Low platelets (ITP)?

Keep your regular tests · Ask your pharmacist
