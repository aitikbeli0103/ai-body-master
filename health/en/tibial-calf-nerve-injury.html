Tibial (calf) nerve injury

Damage to the nerve that runs down the back of the calf to the sole

ConditionPelvis, hip, knee & legWhole body

What it is

The tibial nerve runs behind the knee, down the calf and into the foot. If it is pressed or hurt, the calf or sole may feel pain, tingling or weakness.

  • Back of calf to the sole
  • After leg or ankle injury
  • Tingling, burning, weak
Which doctor
Nerve doctor (neurologist), bone doctor (orthopaedic)
Common in
People with knee, calf or ankle injuries; some with diabetes
Contagious
No
Course
Nerves recover slowly; a doctor can guide you

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain in the calf or behind the knee
  • Weak toes or trouble standing on tiptoe
  • Less feeling in the foot, so cuts may go unnoticed

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A knee, calf or ankle injury, such as a break or deep cut
  • Pressure on the nerve at the ankle, from swelling or tight shoes
  • A lump or cyst pressing on the nerve

See a doctor first if

  • A deep wound, heavy bleeding or a cut that loses feeling: go to hospital now
  • Numb, pale or cold foot: go to hospital now
  • Sudden weakness in the foot or leg
  • A swollen, painful calf, especially after a long trip or rest in bed
  • A sore on the foot that does not heal, or pain that keeps getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See a doctor to find the cause
  • Check your feet daily for cuts, blisters or redness
  • Keep your blood sugar well controlled if you have diabetes

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
