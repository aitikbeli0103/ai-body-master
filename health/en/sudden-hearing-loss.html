Sudden hearing loss

Hearing that drops quickly in one or both ears, over hours or a few days

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

Hearing becomes much worse quite suddenly, often in one ear. It needs to be checked by a doctor quickly, ideally on the same day.

  • Usually one ear
  • Adults, any age
  • Hearing drops quickly
Which doctor
Ear-nose-throat (ENT) doctor; see one quickly
Common in
Adults; often only one ear is affected
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; seeing a doctor early gives the best chance

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Poor blood flow to the inner ear

See a doctor first if

  • Any sudden hearing loss: see a doctor or go to hospital the same day
  • Face drooping, arm weakness, speech trouble or sudden severe headache: call 999
  • Strong spinning dizziness, vomiting, or trouble walking
  • Hearing loss after a head injury or loud blast
  • Hearing loss with fluid or blood coming from the ear

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not wait to see if it gets better; go to a doctor early
  • Stay away from loud noise, and keep rested

Tell your therapist

  • High blood pressure
  • A heart condition
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pregnancy

Questions and answers

What is Sudden hearing loss?

Hearing that drops quickly in one or both ears, over hours or a few days. Hearing becomes much worse quite suddenly, often in one ear. It needs to be checked by a doctor quickly, ideally on the same day.

What are the common signs of Sudden hearing loss?

Hard to hear people · Ringing in the ears · Room feels like spinning

Why does Sudden hearing loss happen?

The cause is not fully known · Germs (infection) · An injury or fall

How can I look after myself with Sudden hearing loss?

Keep the ear dry · Ask your pharmacist
