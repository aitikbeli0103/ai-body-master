Acute epididymitis (inflamed tube behind testicle)

Swelling and soreness of the tube at the back of the testicle, starting over a short time

ConditionMen's healthReproductive

What it is

The epididymis is a coiled tube behind each testicle. When it becomes inflamed, often from an infection, the area can become painful, swollen and warm.

  • Behind the testicle
  • Men, mostly adults
  • Painful, swollen, warm
Which doctor
Family doctor, urologist
Common in
Adult men, including young men and older men
Contagious
The swelling is not, but some causes can spread through sex
Course
Often improves in days to weeks with a doctor's care; pain can linger a while

Common signs

More signs

  • Pain and swelling at the back of one testicle, sometimes both
  • Red, warm or tender skin on the scrotum

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Often a germ infection, which can spread from the urine tube or bladder
  • In younger men, infections caught through sex
  • Sometimes a urine catheter, a procedure, or strain from heavy lifting

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden, severe pain in a testicle, especially with sickness or vomiting: go to hospital now
  • High fever with chills, or feeling very unwell
  • Cannot pass urine, or blood in the urine
  • Swelling or redness that spreads, or a lump that does not go away
  • Pain that is not better after a few days: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See a doctor to find the cause; follow their advice on medicine
  • Wear snug, supportive underwear to ease the pull
  • Drink plenty of water, and ask your doctor when it is safe to have sex again

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine
