Takayasu's arteritis (large artery inflammation)

Inflammation of the body's largest blood vessels

ConditionAutoimmune & rheumaticWhole body

What it is

In Takayasu's arteritis the walls of the large arteries, such as the one leaving the heart, become inflamed. They can narrow, so less blood reaches the arms, head and body.

  • Large blood vessels
  • More often in young women
  • Weak pulse, tired arms
Which doctor
Rheumatologist or heart doctor (cardiologist), family doctor first
Common in
Mostly young and middle-aged women
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long term and needs regular check-ups with a doctor

Common signs

More signs

  • Arm or leg that tires or aches when used
  • Weak pulse or different blood pressure in each arm
  • Dizziness, headache or vision changes
  • Chest pain or shortness of breath

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body's defence system may attack the vessel walls
  • It is more common in women and often starts when young
  • It is not caused by food or lifestyle

See a doctor first if

  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Chest pain or severe shortness of breath: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Sudden loss or dimming of vision
  • Fainting or severe dizziness
  • Very high blood pressure with a bad headache

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
