Viral myocarditis (heart muscle inflammation)

Swelling and irritation of the heart muscle, often after a virus

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

The heart muscle becomes inflamed, usually after a viral infection, and may not pump as well as before.

  • Heart muscle
  • Often young, otherwise fit
  • After a flu-like illness
Which doctor
Heart doctor (cardiologist), family doctor
Common in
Young adults and children, but anyone can get it
Contagious
The heart condition is not, but the virus that starts it can spread
Course
Many people recover with rest and care, but it needs a doctor's follow-up

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Sometimes a bacterial infection or a reaction to a medicine
  • Sometimes the body's own defence system attacks the heart
  • Often the exact cause is not found

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain or pressure, or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Fainting or nearly fainting: go to hospital now
  • A racing or uneven heartbeat that does not settle
  • Chest pain, tiredness or breathlessness after a recent viral illness
  • A child or baby who is very tired, pale, breathing fast or not feeding: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

What are the common signs of Viral myocarditis (heart muscle inflammation)?

Tight, heavy chest · Racing, pounding heart · Short of breath on stairs · Tired all the time · Fever, feeling hot

Why does Viral myocarditis (heart muscle inflammation) happen?

Germs (infection)

How can I look after myself with Viral myocarditis (heart muscle inflammation)?
