Severe body wasting (cachexia)

Serious loss of weight, fat and muscle that comes with a long illness

ConditionWhole body & generalWhole body

What it is

Cachexia is a serious wasting of the body that can come with illnesses such as cancer or advanced heart, lung or kidney disease. It needs care from a doctor and is not fixed by eating more alone.

  • Whole body
  • With a long, serious illness
  • Weight and muscle loss
Which doctor
The doctor treating the main illness; a dietitian
Common in
People with advanced cancer, heart, lung or kidney disease
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the main illness; the care team can plan support

Common signs

Why it happens

  • A long illness such as cancer, or advanced heart, lung or kidney disease
  • The body uses more energy and breaks down muscle and fat
  • Poor appetite, nausea or trouble eating
  • Some treatments can affect eating

See a doctor first if

  • Weight loss you cannot explain: see a doctor promptly
  • Fainting, confusion or being too weak to stand: go to hospital now
  • Vomiting or diarrhoea that will not stop, or signs of dehydration
  • Cannot eat or drink for a day or more
  • Trouble breathing, or swelling in the legs that is getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask your doctor for a dietitian to plan meals

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • A heart condition
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Weak bones

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Severe body wasting (cachexia)?

Serious loss of weight, fat and muscle that comes with a long illness. Cachexia is a serious wasting of the body that can come with illnesses such as cancer or advanced heart, lung or kidney disease. It needs care from a doctor and is not fixed by eating more alone.

What are the common signs of Severe body wasting (cachexia)?

Losing weight without trying · No appetite · Tired all the time · Legs feel weak · Pale, tired look

Why does Severe body wasting (cachexia) happen?

A long illness such as cancer, or advanced heart, lung or kidney disease · The body uses more energy and breaks down muscle and fat · Poor appetite, nausea or trouble eating · Some treatments can affect eating

How can I look after myself with Severe body wasting (cachexia)?

Follow your care team's plan · Eat small, regular meals · Stay active every day
