Supraventricular tachycardia (SVT)

A sudden fast heartbeat that starts in the upper chambers of the heart

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

The heart suddenly beats very fast for a few minutes or longer, then often stops just as suddenly. The beat is usually regular.

  • Chest and heart
  • Can start at any age
  • Sudden racing heartbeat
Which doctor
Family doctor or heart doctor (cardiologist)
Common in
Young and middle-aged adults; also with caffeine or stress
Contagious
No
Course
Comes and goes; episodes vary, so a doctor should check it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • An extra electrical pathway or a short circuit in the heart

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, severe breathlessness or fainting during an episode: call 999
  • A racing heartbeat that does not stop after several minutes: go to hospital
  • Feeling very dizzy or confused with the fast heartbeat
  • Episodes that come more often, or a first episode: see a doctor soon

Questions and answers

What is Supraventricular tachycardia (SVT)?

A sudden fast heartbeat that starts in the upper chambers of the heart. The heart suddenly beats very fast for a few minutes or longer, then often stops just as suddenly. The beat is usually regular.

What are the common signs of Supraventricular tachycardia (SVT)?

Racing, pounding heart · Feeling faint · Short of breath on stairs · Tight, heavy chest · Sweating a lot

Why does Supraventricular tachycardia (SVT) happen?

Smoking · Stress · A side effect of some medicines

How can I look after myself with Supraventricular tachycardia (SVT)?

Cut down on alcohol · Stop smoking · Note down your symptoms
