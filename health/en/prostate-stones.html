Prostate stones

Small hard chalky stones inside the prostate gland, often found by chance on a scan

ConditionMen's healthReproductive

What it is

Tiny hard stones or chalky spots form inside the prostate, the small gland below the bladder. Many men feel nothing, but some have a pelvic ache or urine problems.

  • Prostate, below the bladder
  • Men, more often when older
  • Often silent; some pelvic ache
Which doctor
Urologist (a doctor for the urinary system and prostate), family doctor
Common in
Middle-aged and older men; often found by chance on a scan
Contagious
No
Course
Many stones cause no trouble at all; a doctor can say whether anything is needed

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Tiny mineral deposits slowly build up in the prostate, more often as men get older
  • Long-term swelling or irritation of the prostate

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine at all, or a painful, swollen lower belly: go to hospital now
  • Fever with chills, or burning when passing urine with a very unwell feeling: see a doctor today
  • Blood in the urine or in the semen
  • Severe pain in the lower belly, groin or back that does not ease
  • A weak, slow urine stream, or waking often at night to pass urine, that is getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not hold urine for long; go when you need to
  • Ask your doctor whether a check-up or follow-up is needed

Questions and answers

What are the common signs of Prostate stones?

Needing the toilet often · Waking at night to pass urine · Burning when passing urine · Dragging ache in the lower belly

Why does Prostate stones happen?

An enlarged prostate · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Prostate stones?

Drink enough water · Take breaks from sitting · Cut down on coffee and tea
