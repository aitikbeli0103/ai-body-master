Anxiety disorder

Strong worry or fear that lasts, is hard to control and gets in the way of daily life

ConditionTold by a doctor: anxiety, fears & stressHead

What it is

Everyone feels worried at times. With an anxiety disorder, the worry or fear is strong, lasts for weeks or months, is hard to switch off, and affects sleep, work or relationships.

  • Mind, with effects on the body
  • Can start at any age
  • Constant worry, feeling on edge
Which doctor
Family doctor, then a psychiatrist, psychologist or counsellor
Common in
Anyone; more likely with long stress or a family history
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes; support from a doctor or counsellor can help it ease

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A mix of causes, such as family history, personality and life experiences
  • A frightening or very hard event, now or in the past
  • Other health problems or some medicines can bring on anxious feelings
  • Too much coffee, alcohol or other substances can add to it

See a doctor first if

  • Thoughts of harming yourself or ending your life: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Chest pain, trouble breathing or fainting, even if you think it is anxiety: call 999
  • Racing heart, shaking or sweating that is new or does not settle
  • Worry or fear that stops you working, sleeping, eating or going out for weeks
  • Using alcohol or other substances to cope with the worry
  • Feeling hopeless, or too overwhelmed to look after yourself

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep regular times for sleep and meals, and walk or move gently most days
  • When worry rises, breathe in gently and out slowly for a few minutes

Tell your therapist

  • A heart condition
  • High blood pressure

  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Questions and answers

What is Anxiety disorder?

Strong worry or fear that lasts, is hard to control and gets in the way of daily life. Everyone feels worried at times. With an anxiety disorder, the worry or fear is strong, lasts for weeks or months, is hard to switch off, and affects sleep, work or relationships.

What are the common signs of Anxiety disorder?

Worried and on edge · Racing, pounding heart · Cannot fall asleep · Foggy, cannot focus · Short-tempered, on edge · Tired all the time

Why does Anxiety disorder happen?

Stress

How can I look after myself with Anxiety disorder?

Talk to family and friends · Cut down on coffee and tea · Talk to a counsellor

Can an AI Body Master session help with Anxiety disorder?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Anxiety disorder and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
