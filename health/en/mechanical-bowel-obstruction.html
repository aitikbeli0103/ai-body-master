Mechanical bowel obstruction

A blockage in the bowel that stops food, gas and stool from passing

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

Something physically blocks the bowel, so food, gas and stool cannot pass. This is serious and needs a doctor urgently.

  • Small or large bowel
  • Often after belly surgery
  • Cramps, vomiting, swelling
Which doctor
Emergency doctor; surgeon
Common in
People with past belly surgery, hernia or bowel disease
Contagious
No
Course
Needs urgent medical care; the doctor decides what is needed

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Scar bands (adhesions) after belly surgery
  • A hernia where the bowel gets trapped
  • A growth or tumour in or near the bowel
  • Narrowing from long-term bowel inflammation, or the bowel twisting

See a doctor first if

  • Strong belly pain that comes in waves with repeated vomiting: go to hospital now
  • A swollen, hard belly and unable to pass wind or stool: go to hospital now
  • Vomiting green, brown or bad-smelling fluid
  • Fever, fast heartbeat, or feeling faint
  • A hernia lump that is hard, painful and will not go back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Do not wait at home: seek medical care quickly when you suspect a blockage
  • Do not eat or drink while waiting for care if you are vomiting or very swollen
  • Do not take laxatives or pain medicine without asking a doctor
  • Tell the doctor about any past belly surgery or hernia
  • After recovery, follow your doctor's advice on food and activity

Tell your therapist

  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • A heart condition
  • A kidney or liver condition

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Mechanical bowel obstruction?

A blockage in the bowel that stops food, gas and stool from passing. Something physically blocks the bowel, so food, gas and stool cannot pass. This is serious and needs a doctor urgently.

What are the common signs of Mechanical bowel obstruction?

Cramping belly pain · Bloated, gassy belly · Feeling sick, nausea · Hard to pass stools

Why does Mechanical bowel obstruction happen?

Scar bands (adhesions) after belly surgery · A hernia where the bowel gets trapped · A growth or tumour in or near the bowel · Narrowing from long-term bowel inflammation, or the bowel twisting

How can I look after myself with Mechanical bowel obstruction?
