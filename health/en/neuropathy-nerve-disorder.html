Neuropathy (nerve disorder)

A general term for problems of the nerves, causing pain, numbness or weakness

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

Nerves carry feeling and movement signals around the body. When they are damaged or not working well, you may feel pain, pins and needles, numbness or weakness.

  • Nerves of the body
  • Can affect any age
  • Pain, numbness, weakness
Which doctor
Family doctor, nerve specialist (neurologist)
Common in
People with diabetes, older adults, after injury
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; some improve, some need long-term care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Pressure on a nerve, or an injury

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden weakness, face drooping or trouble speaking: call 999
  • Weakness that quickly spreads, or trouble breathing or swallowing: go to hospital now
  • Loss of control of urine or stools with numb legs
  • A sore on a numb foot that does not heal
  • Numbness or pain that keeps getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See your doctor to find out the cause
  • Check hands and feet for cuts, burns or sores
  • Keep blood sugar controlled if you have diabetes
  • Keep gently active and take care to avoid falls

Tell your therapist

  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Broken, red or sore skin

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Neuropathy (nerve disorder)?

A general term for problems of the nerves, causing pain, numbness or weakness. Nerves carry feeling and movement signals around the body. When they are damaged or not working well, you may feel pain, pins and needles, numbness or weakness.

What are the common signs of Neuropathy (nerve disorder)?

Tingling in the hand · Numb, tingling feet · Legs feel weak · Unsteady when walking

Why does Neuropathy (nerve disorder) happen?

High blood sugar or blood pressure · Drinking alcohol · Germs (infection) · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Neuropathy (nerve disorder)?

Eat healthy, balanced meals
