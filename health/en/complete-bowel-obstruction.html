Complete bowel obstruction

A full blockage of the bowel, so food, fluid and gas cannot pass

ConditionStomach & intestinesAbdomen

What it is

A complete bowel obstruction means the bowel is fully blocked, so food, fluid and gas cannot get past. This is a medical emergency and needs hospital care right away.

  • Small or large bowel
  • A medical emergency
  • Strong cramps, swollen belly
Which doctor
Emergency doctor at a hospital; a surgeon or bowel specialist
Common in
Any age; more often after belly surgery, or with a hernia or bowel disease
Contagious
No
Course
Needs urgent hospital care; a doctor decides the next steps

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A hernia that traps part of the bowel
  • A lump or growth in the bowel, or pressing on it from outside
  • Bowel that twists, or narrows from long-lasting inflammation

See a doctor first if

  • Strong belly pain with vomiting, a swollen belly, and no stool or wind: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Vomit that is green, dark brown or smells like stool: go to hospital now
  • Belly pain that is constant and getting worse, a hard belly, or fever: go to hospital now
  • Feeling faint, very weak, cold and sweaty, or a fast heartbeat: call 999
  • A baby or child with repeated vomiting, a swollen belly or bloody stool: see a doctor right away

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Do not wait at home: go to a hospital emergency department straight away
  • Do not eat or drink until a doctor says it is safe
  • Do not use laxatives or enemas if there is strong belly pain and vomiting
  • Tell the doctor about past belly surgery, hernias and the medicines you take
  • Afterwards, keep your follow-up visits and ask how to lower the chance of it happening again
