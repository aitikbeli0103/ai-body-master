Iridocyclitis (anterior uveitis)

Swelling inside the front of the eye, causing a painful, red eye

ConditionEyesHead

What it is

Inflammation (swelling and irritation) of the coloured part of the eye and the tissue just behind it. It needs to be seen by an eye doctor soon, because it can harm sight if it is not looked after.

  • Front part inside the eye
  • Adults of any age
  • Painful, red eye, hates light
Which doctor
Eye doctor (ophthalmologist), seen promptly
Common in
Adults; people with some inflammatory or joint conditions
Contagious
No
Course
Can come back; an eye doctor decides the follow-up

Common signs

More signs

  • Eye pain or aching

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Linked to some joint, bowel or skin conditions that cause inflammation
  • Some infections
  • An eye injury

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden loss of vision or a sudden change in vision: go to hospital now
  • A painful red eye with light sensitivity: see an eye doctor the same day
  • Severe eye pain with headache and feeling sick: go to hospital now
  • An eye that gets worse or does not settle with the treatment your doctor gave

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • See an eye doctor early; do not wait for it to settle by itself
  • Use eye drops or medicine only as your eye doctor says
  • Wear dark glasses if light hurts
  • Do not rub the eye, and go to every follow-up

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pregnancy
