Cushing's syndrome

A condition where the body has too much of the stress hormone cortisol for a long time

ConditionBlood sugar, fats, uric acid & thyroidWhole body

What it is

Too much cortisol over a long time can cause weight gain around the belly and face, easy bruising, thin skin and high blood pressure.

  • Whole body
  • Mostly adults
  • Round face, fuller belly
Which doctor
Hormone doctor (endocrinologist), family doctor
Common in
Adults; also people on long-term steroid medicine
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; a doctor can explain tests and options

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A small growth in the pituitary gland under the brain
  • A growth on an adrenal gland, usually not cancer
  • Sometimes the cause is found elsewhere in the body

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain or trouble breathing: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • One leg suddenly swollen, red or painful: go to hospital
  • Very low mood or thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999
  • A bone that hurts after a small knock, or constant back pain

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Cushing's syndrome?

A condition where the body has too much of the stress hormone cortisol for a long time. Too much cortisol over a long time can cause weight gain around the belly and face, easy bruising, thin skin and high blood pressure.

What are the common signs of Cushing's syndrome?

Gaining weight easily · Bruises easily · Cuts heal slowly · Tired all the time · Lower back pain

Why does Cushing's syndrome happen?

Long-term use of some medicines

How can I look after myself with Cushing's syndrome?

See your doctor for check-ups · Eat healthy, balanced meals · Stay active every day

Can an AI Body Master session help with Cushing's syndrome?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Cushing's syndrome and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
