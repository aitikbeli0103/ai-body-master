Neck-heart syndrome (cervical angina)

Chest pain or tightness that may be linked to a neck problem

ConditionBones, joints & spineNeck

What it is

Some people with neck spine problems feel chest pain, tightness or a racing heart. A doctor must first check that the heart is healthy before a neck cause is considered.

  • Neck and chest
  • Often with neck problems
  • Chest tightness, racing heart
Which doctor
Family doctor first, heart doctor (cardiologist), spine doctor
Common in
Adults with neck spine wear and tear; long hours at a desk
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; it depends on the neck problem behind it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Long hours of looking down at a phone or desk work
  • Irritated nerves in the neck that may affect the chest wall
  • Not fully understood; the heart must always be checked first

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, pressure or tightness that is new or does not ease with rest: call 999
  • Chest pain with sweating, feeling faint, being sick, or pain going to the arm or jaw: call 999
  • Trouble breathing: go to hospital now
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • Any chest discomfort that has not yet been checked by a doctor

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Get any chest symptom checked by a doctor first
  • Take breaks from looking down; raise your phone or screen to eye level
  • Keep a good sitting posture with your back supported
  • Do gentle neck and shoulder movements if they do not cause pain

Tell your therapist

  • A heart condition
  • High blood pressure
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Recent neck injury or surgery
  • Dizzy when turning the head
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Neck-heart syndrome (cervical angina)?

Chest pain or tightness that may be linked to a neck problem. Some people with neck spine problems feel chest pain, tightness or a racing heart. A doctor must first check that the heart is healthy before a neck cause is considered.

What are the common signs of Neck-heart syndrome (cervical angina)?

Stiff, aching neck · Tight, heavy chest · Racing, pounding heart · Tingling down the arm · Pain in the shoulder · Hard to turn the head

Why does Neck-heart syndrome (cervical angina) happen?

Wear and tear with age

How can I look after myself with Neck-heart syndrome (cervical angina)?
