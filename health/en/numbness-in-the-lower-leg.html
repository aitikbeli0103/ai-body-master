Numbness in the lower leg

Loss of normal feeling, or pins and needles, in the calf and shin area

SymptomHeart & blood vesselsArms & legs

What it is

Numbness in the lower leg means the skin or leg feels dull, tingly or 'asleep'. It may come from pressure on a nerve or from poor blood flow.

  • Lower leg and calf
  • Can affect any adult
  • Tingling or dull feeling
Which doctor
Family doctor; sometimes a nerve or bone specialist
Common in
Adults who sit long, older adults, people with diabetes or back problems
Contagious
No
Course
Short spells often pass by themselves; ongoing numbness needs a check

Common signs

Why it happens

  • Sitting with legs crossed or in one position for a long time
  • A nerve pinched in the lower back or leg
  • Diabetes or other conditions that affect the nerves
  • Poor blood flow in the leg
  • Often the cause is not found straight away

See a doctor first if

  • Numbness with face drooping, arm weakness or trouble speaking: call 999
  • Sudden numbness or weakness in one leg, or a leg that is pale, cold or very painful: go to hospital now
  • Numbness with loss of control of urine or stools: go to hospital now
  • A swollen, red, warm calf
  • Numbness that keeps coming back, spreads, or follows a fall or injury

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Change position often and avoid sitting with crossed legs
  • Stand up and walk gently every so often
  • Check your feet and legs for cuts, especially if you have diabetes

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Recent back injury or surgery

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
