Vaginitis (vaginal inflammation)

Soreness, swelling or irritation of the vagina, often with itching or an unusual discharge

ConditionWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

The lining of the vagina becomes irritated or inflamed, which can cause itching, burning, soreness or a change in discharge.

  • Vagina and vulva area
  • Women of any age
  • Itching, discharge, soreness
Which doctor
Family doctor, gynaecologist (doctor for women's problems)
Common in
Women of all ages; more likely in pregnancy, with diabetes or after menopause
Contagious
Some types can be, such as those spread through sex
Course
Often improves once the cause is found and managed; it can come back

Common signs

More signs

  • Soreness, redness or swelling of the area

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Germs passed on through sex, in some types
  • Scented soap, vaginal washes (douching), sprays, or tight, damp clothing

See a doctor first if

  • Pregnant with bleeding or strong belly pain, or heavy bleeding or fainting: go to hospital now
  • Fever, chills, or pain in the lower belly or pelvis along with the discharge: see a doctor today
  • Sores, blisters or lumps on the genitals, or a strong smell with green or grey discharge
  • Bleeding between periods or after sex, or any bleeding after menopause
  • A girl or child with soreness, discharge or itching: see a doctor promptly
  • Symptoms that do not settle, keep coming back, or start after a new sexual partner

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Wipe from front to back after using the toilet
  • Using condoms can lower the chance of infections that pass on through sex

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
