Spider naevus (spider angioma)

A small red spot on the skin with thin red lines spreading out like spider legs

ConditionLiver, gallbladder & pancreasSkin

What it is

Tiny blood vessels near the skin surface widen and show as a red dot with thin lines around it. It is often harmless, but sometimes it goes with liver trouble.

  • Skin of face, neck, chest
  • Can happen to anyone
  • Red dot with fine lines
Which doctor
Family doctor; liver doctor if advised
Common in
Children, pregnant women, and people with liver problems
Contagious
No
Course
Often stays for a long time; some fade by themselves, for example after pregnancy

Common signs

  • A small red spot with thin red lines reaching out from it
  • Often on the face, neck, chest, shoulders or arms
  • The spot turns pale for a moment when pressed, then turns red again
  • Usually painless and not itchy

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Higher oestrogen levels, such as in pregnancy
  • Long-term liver problems, which may be a sign of one
  • Often the cause is not known

See a doctor first if

  • Yellow skin or eyes, or dark urine: see a doctor soon
  • Vomiting blood or black stools: go to hospital now
  • Many new spots appearing, or a swollen belly
  • A spot that bleeds, grows or changes quickly
  • Heavy drinking or known liver disease together with these spots

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
