Atrophic rhinitis

Long-term thinning and drying of the lining of the nose, with crusts and a bad smell

ConditionEars, nose & throatHead

What it is

The soft lining inside the nose becomes thin and dry. Crusts form, the nose feels blocked even though it is wide open, and there may be a bad smell.

  • Inside the nose
  • Long-term problem
  • Dry crusts, poor smell
Which doctor
Ear, nose and throat doctor (ENT)
Common in
Adults; in dry, dusty places or after nose surgery
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long-lasting; a doctor can explain how to manage it

Common signs

More signs

  • Dry crusts inside the nose
  • A bad smell from the nose that others notice
  • A dry, uncomfortable nose and throat

Why it happens

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy nosebleed that does not stop after ten minutes of pinching: go to hospital
  • Fever with pain or swelling of the face or around the eyes
  • Bleeding from one side of the nose again and again
  • A smell or crusts that do not go away

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep indoor air from getting too dry
  • Ask your doctor about safe ways to keep the nose moist
  • Avoid smoke, dust and strong fumes

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • A severe allergy
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Atrophic rhinitis?

Long-term thinning and drying of the lining of the nose, with crusts and a bad smell. The soft lining inside the nose becomes thin and dry. Crusts form, the nose feels blocked even though it is wide open, and there may be a bad smell.

What are the common signs of Atrophic rhinitis?

Blocked nose · Loss of smell

Why does Atrophic rhinitis happen?

The cause is not fully known · Germs (infection) · After an operation · Dry air and air-conditioning

How can I look after myself with Atrophic rhinitis?

Drink enough water · Do not squeeze or pick at it
