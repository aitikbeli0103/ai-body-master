Encephalitis (brain inflammation)

Swelling and inflammation of the brain, often from an infection

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

The brain becomes inflamed, most often because of a virus. It can cause fever, headache and confusion, and it needs a doctor quickly.

  • Brain
  • Any age, even children
  • Fever and headache
Which doctor
Go to hospital; brain and nerve doctor (neurologist)
Common in
Anyone, more often children and older people
Contagious
The germ may spread, but the brain inflammation itself does not
Course
Needs hospital care; recovery time varies a lot

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Sometimes bacteria or other germs
  • Sometimes the body's own defences attack the brain
  • Sometimes the cause is not found

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with a stiff neck, confusion or a fit: go to hospital now
  • Very sleepy, hard to wake, or fainting: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • A baby or child with fever who is very unwell, floppy or not feeding: go to hospital now
  • A bad headache that keeps getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Go to hospital quickly if the warning signs above appear
  • Protect against mosquito bites with repellent and nets

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Epilepsy or fits
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy
