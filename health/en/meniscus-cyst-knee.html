Meniscus cyst (knee)

A fluid-filled lump next to the knee cushion

ConditionPelvis, hip, knee & legArms & legs

What it is

A meniscus cyst is a fluid-filled lump that forms beside the knee cushion, often after a tear. It may feel like a firm bump at the side of the knee.

  • Side of the knee
  • Often after a knee tear
  • A bump and aching
Which doctor
Bone and joint doctor (orthopaedic), family doctor
Common in
Adults who have had a knee cushion tear
Contagious
No
Course
Varies; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

More signs

  • Aching at the joint line, worse when bending
  • The lump may look bigger or smaller at times
  • Clicking or catching in the knee

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Often linked to a tear in the knee cushion
  • Joint fluid leaking into a small pocket next to the knee

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot put weight on the leg after a knee injury: go to hospital
  • Numb, pale or cold foot: go to hospital now
  • A lump that grows fast or is very hard and painful
  • A knee that locks and cannot straighten
  • Fever with a hot, red knee

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not press hard on the lump or try to burst it
  • Ask your doctor about a scan to see the cause

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
