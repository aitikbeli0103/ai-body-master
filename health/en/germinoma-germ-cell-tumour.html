Germinoma (germ cell tumour)

A tumour that grows from germ cells, often in the ovary, testis or brain

ConditionCancer & tumoursReproductive

What it is

Germ cells are the cells that make eggs and sperm. A germinoma is a tumour made of these cells and is a kind of cancer. It most often appears in the ovary, testis or brain.

  • Ovary, testis or brain
  • Mostly young people
  • A lump or pain, by place
Which doctor
Cancer doctor (oncologist); gynaecologist, urologist or brain specialist by site
Common in
Children, teenagers and young adults; it is rare
Contagious
No
Course
Differs from person to person; your cancer care team can explain what to expect

Common signs

More signs

  • In the testis: a painless lump or swelling, or a heavy feeling
  • In the brain: headaches, being sick, changes in vision or in thirst

Why it happens

Other causes

  • It comes from germ cells that did not settle in the right place early in life
  • A testis that did not move down into the sac as a baby

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden severe pain in the testis or lower belly: go to hospital now
  • Severe headache with repeated vomiting, a fit, or sudden changes in vision or speech: go to hospital now
  • A new lump in the testis or a swelling in the belly
  • Bleeding from the vagina that is not normal for you
  • Headaches that keep coming back or are getting worse
  • Weight loss without trying, or very bad tiredness

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
