Broken hip socket (acetabulum)

A break in the cup-shaped socket of the pelvis that holds the thigh bone

ConditionPelvis, hip, knee & legArms & legs

What it is

The hip socket (acetabulum) is part of the pelvis. It usually breaks after a strong force such as a road crash, and in older people with weak bones, after a fall.

  • Hip socket in the pelvis
  • After a crash or hard fall
  • Hip and groin pain
Which doctor
Emergency doctor at hospital; bone specialist (orthopaedic surgeon)
Common in
People hurt in road crashes or falls from a height; older adults with weak bones
Contagious
No
Course
Needs hospital care, sometimes an operation; recovery often takes months

Common signs

More signs

  • Bruising and swelling around the hip or buttock
  • The leg may look shorter, or feel numb or weak

Why it happens

Other causes

  • In older people with weak bones, a fall from standing height
  • Other injuries often happen at the same time

See a doctor first if

  • Hip pain and being unable to stand after a crash or fall: call 999 or go to hospital now
  • Signs of shock or bleeding inside: faint, pale, cold sweat, fast pulse
  • Belly pain, blood in the urine or trouble passing urine after the injury
  • A leg or foot that is numb, weak, pale or cold
  • Later: chest pain, sudden breathlessness or a swollen painful calf: call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Call 999 after a serious crash or fall, and do not move the person
  • Keep them warm and lying still, and do not give food or drink
  • After hospital care, follow the care team on walking aids and exercises

Tell your therapist

  • Weak bones
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • A blood clot or a new swollen leg
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
