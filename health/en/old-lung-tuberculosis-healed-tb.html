Old lung tuberculosis (healed TB)

Old scars in the lungs left by a past TB infection that is no longer active

ConditionInfectionsChest

What it is

After TB in the lungs has settled, small scars or hard spots can stay behind. They are often found by chance on a chest X-ray.

  • Lungs
  • People who had TB before
  • Often found on X-ray
Which doctor
Family doctor; lung doctor (respiratory) if needed
Common in
People who had TB in the past, including older adults
Contagious
Old scars alone do not spread; a doctor checks it is not active
Course
Scars usually stay as they are; regular checks may be advised

Common signs

More signs

  • A mild long-term cough or a little breathlessness
  • Sometimes a little phlegm

Why it happens

  • An earlier lung TB infection that settled and left scars
  • The body wrapping up old TB germs in small hard spots
  • Past TB that was not found or treated fully
  • Weak body defences later in life can wake old TB up

See a doctor first if

  • Coughing up blood, or heavy bleeding from the mouth: call 999
  • Trouble breathing or chest pain: go to hospital now
  • A new cough lasting weeks, with fever, night sweats or weight loss
  • Being told your X-ray has changed since the last one

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
