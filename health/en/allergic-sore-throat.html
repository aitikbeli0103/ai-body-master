Allergic sore throat

A scratchy, itchy or sore throat caused by an allergy

ConditionAllergiesNeck

What it is

When the body reacts to things like dust, pollen, pets or smoke, the throat can become irritated, itchy and sore, often with a dry cough and a drip at the back of the nose.

  • Throat

  • Itchy throat, dry cough

People with allergies, hay fever or a sensitive nose

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble swallowing or drooling
  • High fever or white patches in the throat
  • A sore throat or hoarse voice lasting more than two weeks

Questions and answers

What is Allergic sore throat?

A scratchy, itchy or sore throat caused by an allergy. When the body reacts to things like dust, pollen, pets or smoke, the throat can become irritated, itchy and sore, often with a dry cough and a drip at the back of the nose.

What are the common signs of Allergic sore throat?

Sore, scratchy throat · Sneezing, runny nose · Blocked nose · Dry, tickly cough · Red, itchy eyes

Why does Allergic sore throat happen?

Dust, pollen or pets · Dry air and air-conditioning · Present from birth

How can I look after myself with Allergic sore throat?

Drink enough water · Ask your pharmacist

Can an AI Body Master session help with Allergic sore throat?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Allergic sore throat and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
