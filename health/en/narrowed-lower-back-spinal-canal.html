Narrowed lower-back spinal canal

The space for the nerves in the lower spine becomes narrow

ConditionBones, joints & spineLower backBones & muscles

What it is

The channel in the lower spine that holds the nerves becomes narrower and can press on them. This may cause leg pain, numbness or tiredness, especially when standing or walking.

  • Lower back and legs
  • Mostly older adults
  • Leg pain or heaviness
Which doctor
Family doctor, orthopaedic or spine doctor, neurologist
Common in
Adults over middle age, often with a worn spine
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long-lasting and slowly changing; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Wear of the spine with age, the most common reason
  • Bony growths or thickened joints pressing into the canal
  • A bulging disc

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine or stools, or numbness around the groin or bottom: go to hospital now
  • Sudden or quickly worsening weakness in a leg or foot
  • Numbness in both legs that is spreading
  • Back pain with fever, after a fall, or with weight loss
  • Falls or being unable to walk the way you used to

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Leaning slightly forward, such as on a trolley, may feel easier for walking
  • Ask your doctor about safe exercises and any medicines for pain

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Weak leg or foot
  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Pregnancy
