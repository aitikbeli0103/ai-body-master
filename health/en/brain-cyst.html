Brain cyst

A fluid-filled sac that forms in or around the brain

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

A brain cyst is a closed sac filled with fluid. Many are small and cause no problem, and are found by chance. A doctor decides if it needs watching.

  • In or around the brain
  • Can happen at any age
  • Often no signs; some headache
Which doctor
Brain and nerve doctor (neurologist), brain surgeon
Common in
Often found by chance on a scan; children and adults
Contagious
No
Course
Many stay the same for years; some are watched with scans

Common signs

More signs

  • Headache that keeps coming back

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Some are linked to other conditions that a doctor can explain

See a doctor first if

  • A fit, fainting, or being very sleepy and hard to wake: call 999
  • Sudden severe headache with vomiting: go to hospital now
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999 now
  • Headaches that are getting worse over days to weeks
  • In a baby or child: a growing head, vomiting or sleepiness, see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Brain cyst?

A fluid-filled sac that forms in or around the brain. A brain cyst is a closed sac filled with fluid. Many are small and cause no problem, and are found by chance. A doctor decides if it needs watching.

What are the common signs of Brain cyst?

Often no signs at first · Dizzy when standing up · Fits (seizures) · Double or changed vision

Why does Brain cyst happen?

Present from birth · Germs (infection) · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Brain cyst?

See your doctor for check-ups · Find ways to relax

Can an AI Body Master session help with Brain cyst?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Brain cyst and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
