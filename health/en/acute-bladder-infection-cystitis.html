Acute bladder infection (cystitis)

A sudden infection of the bladder that causes burning and frequent urination

ConditionKidneys & urinaryPelvis

What it is

Germs get into the bladder and irritate its lining, so passing urine burns and you need to go again and again.

  • Lower belly, bladder
  • More common in women
  • Burning when passing urine
Which doctor
Family doctor; urologist if it keeps coming back
Common in
Women of all ages; also men, older adults and people with diabetes
Contagious
No, it does not pass from person to person like a cold
Course
Often settles in days once a doctor has checked it; do not leave it

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • A shorter urine tube in women makes it easier for germs to get in
  • Diabetes, a urine catheter, or not emptying the bladder fully

See a doctor first if

  • Fever with shivering, or pain in the side or back: see a doctor today, it may have reached the kidney
  • Blood in the urine
  • Feeling very unwell, confused or very drowsy: go to hospital now
  • Pregnant, or a man, a child or an older person with these signs
  • No better after a couple of days, or it keeps coming back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See a doctor so the urine can be checked; ask them about medicine
  • Do not hold your urine; empty the bladder fully
  • Wipe from front to back and keep the area clean and dry

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • A kidney or liver condition
  • Fever or an active infection
