Urine leakage (incontinence)

Passing urine by accident when you do not mean to

ConditionKidneys & urinaryAbdomen

What it is

Urine leaks out when you cough, laugh or lift, or when the urge comes so fast that you cannot reach the toilet in time.

  • Bladder and pelvic floor
  • More common with age
  • Leaking urine
Which doctor
Family doctor, urologist or gynaecologist
Common in
Women after childbirth or menopause; older adults; men with prostate problems
Contagious
No
Course
Often can improve with the right help; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

Why it happens

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden leaking with weakness or numbness in the legs, or loss of control of the bowel: go to hospital now
  • Unable to pass urine, or a very painful full bladder: go to hospital now
  • Burning, fever or blood in the urine
  • Leaking that is new, getting worse, or stopping you from going out

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask your doctor about pelvic floor exercises
  • Go to the toilet at regular times instead of waiting for a strong urge

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Recent back injury or surgery
  • Pacemaker or metal implant

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Urine leakage (incontinence)?

Passing urine by accident when you do not mean to. Urine leaks out when you cough, laugh or lift, or when the urge comes so fast that you cannot reach the toilet in time.

What are the common signs of Urine leakage (incontinence)?

Leaking when coughing · Needing the toilet often · Waking at night to pass urine

Why does Urine leakage (incontinence) happen?

Getting older · An enlarged prostate · Extra body weight · A side effect of some medicines

How can I look after myself with Urine leakage (incontinence)?

Keep a healthy weight · Drink enough water

Can an AI Body Master session help with Urine leakage (incontinence)?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Urine leakage (incontinence) and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
