Chronic non-bacterial prostatitis

Long-lasting prostate pain and urine trouble without a bacterial infection

ConditionMen's healthPelvis

What it is

The prostate area aches for weeks or months, but tests do not find bacteria. It is common and can be frustrating, and a doctor can help rule out other causes.

  • Between the legs, lower belly
  • Adult men
  • Dull ache, urine trouble
Which doctor
Family doctor; urologist
Common in
Adult men of many ages, often long hours of sitting
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes over months; a doctor can guide you

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Tight or tense muscles in the pelvis
  • Inflammation after a past infection or injury

See a doctor first if

  • Cannot pass urine at all: go to hospital now
  • Fever with shivering or feeling very unwell
  • Blood in the urine or semen
  • Weight loss without trying, or a hard lump in the groin or testicle
  • Pain getting worse or low mood that is hard to cope with

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Have a doctor check you so other causes are ruled out
  • Try gentle exercise, stretching and slow breathing
  • Warm baths may ease the ache
  • Talk to someone you trust if worry makes it worse

Tell your therapist

  • Fever or an active infection
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Blood-thinning medicine

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Chronic non-bacterial prostatitis?

Long-lasting prostate pain and urine trouble without a bacterial infection. The prostate area aches for weeks or months, but tests do not find bacteria. It is common and can be frustrating, and a doctor can help rule out other causes.

What are the common signs of Chronic non-bacterial prostatitis?

Dragging ache in the lower belly · Needing the toilet often · Waking at night to pass urine · Burning when passing urine

Why does Chronic non-bacterial prostatitis happen?

The cause is not fully known · Stress

How can I look after myself with Chronic non-bacterial prostatitis?

Take breaks from sitting
