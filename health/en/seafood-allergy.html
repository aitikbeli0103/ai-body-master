Seafood allergy

An allergy where the body reacts badly to fish, prawns, crab, shellfish or squid

ConditionAllergiesWhole body

What it is

The body treats a protein in seafood as a threat. A reaction can start within minutes to a couple of hours after eating, from mild itching to a serious emergency.

  • Skin, gut, breathing
  • Teens and adults too
  • Reacts after eating seafood
Which doctor
Family doctor, allergy doctor (immunologist)
Common in
Children and adults; often lasts for life once it starts in adults
Contagious
No
Course
Reactions come and go with each exposure; the allergy often stays for years

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The immune system reacts to proteins in fish, prawns, crab or shellfish
  • It can start at any age, even if you ate seafood safely before
  • Eating from the same pan or oil as seafood can also trigger it

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, wheezing, or a swollen tongue, lips or throat: call 999
  • Feeling faint, pale, confused or collapsing: call 999
  • Many hives all over the body with vomiting or belly pain
  • Repeated vomiting or diarrhoea after eating seafood
  • A reaction that gets stronger each time

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Avoid the seafood that caused the reaction, and ask about others
  • Read food labels and ask restaurants how food is cooked
  • Tell family and friends about your allergy
  • If your doctor gave you an emergency plan or pen, keep it with you and use it as taught
  • Ask a doctor for an allergy check before trying seafood again
