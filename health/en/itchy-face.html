Itchy face

An itch on the skin of the face, such as the cheeks, forehead, eyelids or around the mouth

ConditionSkin, hair & nailsHead

What it is

Face skin is thin and often meets sun, makeup and skin-care products, so itching is common. Most of the time it is a mild reaction or dry skin.

  • Skin of the face
  • Can follow a new product
  • Itchy, tight or dry skin
Which doctor
Family doctor; skin doctor for long-lasting itch
Common in
Anyone; more often with allergies, eczema or sensitive skin
Contagious
Some causes can spread, such as a fungal infection
Course
Often eases once the trigger is found and avoided

Common signs

More signs

  • Redness, dry flakes or small bumps
  • Scratch marks or sore, cracked skin

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Dry skin, hot weather, sweat or too much sun
  • A reaction to makeup, skin-care products, soap or hair products
  • Eczema, acne, or fungal or mite-related skin problems
  • Sometimes a nerve problem or an illness inside the body

See a doctor first if

  • Swelling of the lips, tongue or face, or trouble breathing: call 999
  • A painful rash with blisters on one side of the face, especially near the eye: see a doctor today
  • Eye pain, a change in vision or a very red eye
  • Fever, pus, or redness and swelling that is spreading
  • Itching that lasts more than a couple of weeks or keeps coming back

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

Questions and answers

What is Itchy face?

An itch on the skin of the face, such as the cheeks, forehead, eyelids or around the mouth. Face skin is thin and often meets sun, makeup and skin-care products, so itching is common. Most of the time it is a mild reaction or dry skin.

What are the common signs of Itchy face?

Itchy skin · Red, itchy eyes · Sneezing, runny nose

Why does Itchy face happen?

Dust, pollen or pets

How can I look after myself with Itchy face?

Wash gently and pat dry · Keep the skin moisturised · Try not to scratch; keep nails short · Ask your pharmacist
