Subclinical hypothyroidism

A blood test shows a slightly underactive thyroid, often with few or no signs

ConditionBlood sugar, fats, uric acid & thyroidNeck

What it is

The thyroid hormone is still in the normal range, but the body is working harder to keep it there. Many people feel well, and a doctor decides what to do.

  • Thyroid, found by blood test
  • More often in older women
  • Tired, feeling cold
Which doctor
Family doctor, hormone doctor (endocrinologist)
Common in
Women, older adults, and people with thyroid problems in the family
Contagious
No
Course
May stay the same, settle, or change later; blood tests are repeated

Why it happens

Other causes

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain or fainting: call 999
  • A growing swelling at the front of the neck

Questions and answers

What is Subclinical hypothyroidism?

A blood test shows a slightly underactive thyroid, often with few or no signs. The thyroid hormone is still in the normal range, but the body is working harder to keep it there. Many people feel well, and a doctor decides what to do.

What are the common signs of Subclinical hypothyroidism?

Often no signs at first · Tired all the time · Always feeling cold · Dry, flaky skin

Why does Subclinical hypothyroidism happen?

After an operation · A side effect of some medicines · The cause is not fully known

How can I look after myself with Subclinical hypothyroidism?

Eat healthy, balanced meals · Stay active every day

Can an AI Body Master session help with Subclinical hypothyroidism?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Subclinical hypothyroidism and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
