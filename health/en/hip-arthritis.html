Hip arthritis

Wear or swelling inside the hip joint that causes pain and stiffness

ConditionBones, joints & spinePelvis

What it is

The smooth cushion in the hip joint wears thin or becomes inflamed, so the hip can hurt, feel stiff and be harder to move.

  • Hip, groin or thigh
  • More common with age
  • Aching, stiff hip
Which doctor
Family doctor, bone and joint doctor (orthopaedic)
Common in
Older adults; people with past hip injury or extra weight
Contagious
No
Course
Usually long term and may slowly change; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Wear of the joint with age (osteoarthritis)
  • Inflammatory types of arthritis, such as rheumatoid arthritis

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden hip pain after a fall, or you cannot stand or put weight on the leg: go to hospital
  • A leg that looks shorter or turned out after a fall: go to hospital now
  • A hot, red, very painful hip with fever
  • Numbness or weakness in the leg, or trouble passing urine or stools
  • Pain at night or at rest that keeps getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Hip arthritis?

Wear or swelling inside the hip joint that causes pain and stiffness. The smooth cushion in the hip joint wears thin or becomes inflamed, so the hip can hurt, feel stiff and be harder to move.

What are the common signs of Hip arthritis?

Pain in the hip · Hard to walk far · Stiff back in the morning

Why does Hip arthritis happen?

An injury or fall · Extra body weight

How can I look after myself with Hip arthritis?

Keep a healthy weight · Make the home safe from falls · Ask your pharmacist

Can an AI Body Master session help with Hip arthritis?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Hip arthritis and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
