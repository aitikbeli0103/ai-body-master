Acute cervicitis (inflamed cervix)

Swelling and soreness of the neck of the womb (the cervix)

ConditionWomen's healthReproductive

What it is

The cervix, the lower end of the womb, becomes inflamed. Often it is caused by an infection, and sometimes it causes no signs at all.

  • Neck of the womb
  • Women in their active years
  • Discharge, sometimes no signs
Which doctor
Women's doctor (gynaecologist), family doctor
Common in
Sexually active women; also after some irritation or injury
Contagious
Some causes can be, such as infections passed on by sex
Course
Usually gets better when the cause is found and looked after

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Infections that spread through sex
  • Other germs, or a change in the normal germs of the vagina
  • Irritation from products such as douches, or from a forgotten tampon

See a doctor first if

  • Heavy bleeding, or fainting: go to hospital now
  • Pain or bleeding when you are pregnant: go to hospital now
  • Fever with strong lower belly pain
  • Bleeding after menopause
  • Discharge or bleeding that does not go away

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • See a doctor for an exam and tests so the cause can be found
  • Ask your doctor when it is safe to have sex again, and whether your partner needs a check
  • Avoid douches and strong scented products
  • Use a condom to lower the chance of infections that spread by sex

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Fever or an active infection

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Acute cervicitis (inflamed cervix)?

Swelling and soreness of the neck of the womb (the cervix). The cervix, the lower end of the womb, becomes inflamed. Often it is caused by an infection, and sometimes it causes no signs at all.

What are the common signs of Acute cervicitis (inflamed cervix)?

Unusual discharge or itching down below · Bleeding between periods · Pain during or after sex · Dragging ache in the lower belly

Why does Acute cervicitis (inflamed cervix) happen?

The cause is not fully known
