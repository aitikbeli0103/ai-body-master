Retroperitoneal tumour

A growth in the space at the back of the belly, behind the organs

ConditionCancer & tumoursAbdomen

What it is

The retroperitoneum is the space behind the belly organs. A tumour here can grow quietly for a long time before it is noticed.

  • Deep in the belly and back
  • Adults; not common
  • Fullness, belly or back ache
Which doctor
Family doctor first, then a surgeon or cancer doctor
Common in
Adults; it is not common
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the type and size; the care team can explain what to expect

Common signs

More signs

  • Feeling full after eating only a little
  • Ache in the belly or lower back
  • Swelling, tingling or numbness in a leg

Why it happens

Other causes

  • It can start from fat, muscle, nerve or other soft tissue
  • Past radiation to the area may play a part

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden severe belly pain, or a hard swollen belly with vomiting: go to hospital now
  • Vomiting blood or passing black stools
  • A belly that keeps getting bigger, or a lump you can feel
  • A leg that becomes swollen, numb or weak
  • Weight loss without trying, or belly or back pain that does not go away

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Never stop or change treatment on your own
  • Ask the team about pain, food and emotional support

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Numb or tingling leg or foot
  • Blood-thinning medicine
