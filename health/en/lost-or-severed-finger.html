Lost or severed finger

A finger, or part of a finger, that has been cut or torn off

ConditionShoulder, arm & handBones & muscles

What it is

This is an emergency. A finger or part of it comes off in an accident. Fast action and getting to hospital quickly give the best chance.

  • Finger or fingertip
  • Emergency, act at once
  • Bleeding and severe pain
Which doctor
Emergency department now; hand surgeon (plastic or orthopaedic)
Common in
Workers with machines, kitchen knives and car doors; children
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the injury and how fast care starts; the hospital team will explain

Common signs

More signs

  • Part of a finger is cut off or hanging by skin
  • Bleeding from the hand
  • Pale, cold or numb skin near the injury

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Machines, saws or other power tools
  • Kitchen knives or cutters
  • Car doors, gates or heavy objects crushing the hand

See a doctor first if

  • Call 999 or go to hospital now, do not wait
  • Bleeding that soaks the cloth or will not stop
  • Feeling faint, pale, cold and sweaty: signs of shock
  • A child with this injury needs hospital at once
  • Redness, pus or fever in the days after

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

  • Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth and raise the hand high
  • Wrap the cut-off part in a clean damp cloth and put it in a bag
  • Put that bag on top of ice, not straight on ice, and take it with you
  • Do not soak it in water or rub it
  • Sit or lie down and have someone take you to hospital

Tell your therapist

  • Broken, red or sore skin
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Numb or tingling arm

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
