Breakdown of red blood cells (haemolysis)

Red blood cells break down faster than the body can replace them

ConditionBloodBloodWhole body

What it is

Red blood cells are destroyed too early. This can cause anaemia, tiredness, yellow skin and dark urine. The cause needs to be found by a doctor.

  • In the blood
  • Many possible causes
  • Tired, pale, yellow
Which doctor
Family doctor, blood doctor (haematologist)
Common in
Any age; includes inherited blood conditions and immune causes
Contagious
No
Course
Depends on the cause; some are short, some long-term

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The immune system attacking red blood cells
  • Some infections, including malaria
  • Some medicines or substances in food
  • Problems with the spleen or blood vessels

See a doctor first if

  • Chest pain, severe trouble breathing or fainting: call 999
  • Dark, tea-coloured urine or quickly yellowing skin and eyes
  • Fever with shaking chills, especially after travel
  • Severe belly or back pain
  • A baby or child who is pale, yellow or very sleepy: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
