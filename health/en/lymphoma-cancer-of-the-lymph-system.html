Lymphoma (cancer of the lymph system)

Cancer of the lymph system, which helps the body fight germs

ConditionCancer & tumoursBlood

What it is

Lymphoma happens when white blood cells in the lymph system grow out of control. It often shows first as a painless swollen gland in the neck, armpit or groin.

  • Lymph system and blood cells
  • Adults mostly; children too
  • A painless swollen gland
Which doctor
Blood doctor (haematologist) or cancer doctor (oncologist)
Common in
Adults and children; higher risk with a weak immune system
Contagious
No
Course
Some types grow slowly, others quickly; the type decides the care plan

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Some infections, such as Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) or hepatitis C
  • Some autoimmune conditions, where the body attacks its own cells

See a doctor first if

  • Trouble breathing, a swollen face or neck, or severe chest pain: call 999
  • Severe headache with confusion, sudden weakness or a fit: call 999
  • High fever with chills or shaking, especially during cancer treatment: go to hospital now
  • A swollen gland or lump that does not go away after a few weeks, or keeps growing
  • Fever that keeps coming back, soaking night sweats, or weight loss with no reason
  • Easy bruising, bleeding that is hard to stop, or looking very pale and tired

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Questions and answers

What is Lymphoma (cancer of the lymph system)?

Cancer of the lymph system, which helps the body fight germs. Lymphoma happens when white blood cells in the lymph system grow out of control. It often shows first as a painless swollen gland in the neck, armpit or groin.

What are the common signs of Lymphoma (cancer of the lymph system)?

Swollen glands in the neck · Tired all the time · Soaking sweats at night · Fever, feeling hot · Losing weight without trying · Itchy skin

Why does Lymphoma (cancer of the lymph system) happen?

The cause is not fully known · A weak immune system · Getting older

How can I look after myself with Lymphoma (cancer of the lymph system)?

Follow your care team's plan · Wash your hands often · Rest well · Talk to family and friends
