Lewy body dementia

A brain condition that slowly affects thinking, movement, sleep and mood

ConditionBrain & nervesHead

What it is

Tiny protein clumps build up in brain cells over time. Thinking can go up and down from day to day, and movement may become slow or stiff.

  • Brain
  • Mostly in older adults
  • Confusion that comes and goes
Which doctor
Brain specialist (neurologist), old-age doctor (geriatrician)
Common in
Older adults, usually over middle age
Contagious
No
Course
Usually gets worse slowly over years; care and family support help daily life

Common signs

More signs

  • Thinking and attention that change a lot from hour to hour or day to day
  • Seeing people or things that are not there
  • Acting out dreams, shouting or moving in sleep

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Protein clumps (Lewy bodies) build up in brain cells

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden confusion or being much more drowsy than usual: see a doctor now
  • A fall with a head injury, or face drooping or weak arm: go to hospital, call 999 for stroke signs
  • Thoughts of harming yourself or others: see a doctor now or call 999
  • Frightening visions or strong agitation that the family cannot manage

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Keep a calm daily routine, with good lighting and a quiet space

  • Carers should also rest and ask for support

Questions and answers

What is Lewy body dementia?

A brain condition that slowly affects thinking, movement, sleep and mood. Tiny protein clumps build up in brain cells over time. Thinking can go up and down from day to day, and movement may become slow or stiff.

What are the common signs of Lewy body dementia?

Shaking hands · Dizzy when standing up

Why does Lewy body dementia happen?

The cause is not fully known · Getting older · Runs in the family

How can I look after myself with Lewy body dementia?

Make the home safe from falls · Ask your pharmacist

Can an AI Body Master session help with Lewy body dementia?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Lewy body dementia and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
