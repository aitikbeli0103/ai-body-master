Worn meniscus (knee)

Wear and tear of the soft cushion inside the knee

ConditionPelvis, hip, knee & legArms & legsBones & muscles

What it is

The meniscus is a rubbery cushion inside the knee. With age and use it can wear thin or fray, which may cause knee pain, stiffness and a catching feeling.

  • Inside the knee
  • More common with age
  • Ache, stiffness, catching
Which doctor
Bone and joint doctor (orthopaedic), family doctor
Common in
Middle-aged and older adults; people who squat or kneel a lot
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes over months or years; a doctor can explain the options

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Wear in the knee joint, as in knee arthritis
  • Often squatting, kneeling or climbing many stairs

See a doctor first if

  • A hot, red, very swollen knee with fever: see a doctor today
  • A knee that locks and you cannot straighten it
  • A calf that is suddenly swollen, painful or warm: see a doctor now
  • A knee that gives way or you cannot put weight on it after a fall
  • Pain at night or pain that keeps getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.
