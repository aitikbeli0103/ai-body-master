Ewing sarcoma (bone and soft tissue cancer)

A rare cancer that starts in the bones or the soft tissue around them

ConditionCancer & tumoursBones & muscles

What it is

Ewing sarcoma is a rare cancer that most often starts in the bones of the legs, pelvis, ribs or arms. It is found mostly in children, teenagers and young adults.

  • Bones and soft tissue
  • Children and young people
  • Bone pain and swelling
Which doctor
Children's cancer doctor or bone cancer specialist; see a doctor promptly
Common in
Children, teenagers and young adults; it is rare
Contagious
No
Course
Differs from person to person; the cancer care team can explain what to expect

Common signs

More signs

  • Swelling or a lump over the sore bone, which may feel warm
  • A bone that breaks after a small knock

Why it happens

Other causes

  • It is linked to changes in the genes of cells, which are not passed on from parents in most cases
  • It is not caused by a knock, a fall or sport
  • It is not caused by anything a person or parent did

See a doctor first if

  • A bone that suddenly breaks, or a limb you cannot use or put weight on: go to hospital now
  • Sudden weakness or numbness in the legs, or loss of control of the bladder or bowel: go to hospital now
  • Bone pain that lasts more than a few weeks or wakes a child at night
  • A new lump or swelling near a bone
  • Fever that does not go away, or weight loss without trying
  • Trouble breathing or a new cough that does not go away

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Ask the team about pain, food, school or work, and emotional support
  • Protect the sore bone as the doctor advises, and avoid heavy knocks

Tell your therapist

  • Cancer or cancer treatment
  • Weak bones
  • Blood-thinning medicine
  • Pregnancy

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.
