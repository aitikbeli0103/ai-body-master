Toxoplasmosis

An infection by a tiny parasite found in cat droppings, raw meat and soil; often mild or silent

ConditionInfectionsWhole body

What it is

Many people who pick up this parasite feel fine or have a mild flu-like illness, but it matters most in pregnancy and for people with a weak immune system.

  • Whole body, sometimes the eyes
  • More serious in pregnancy
  • Often none, or flu-like
Which doctor
Family doctor; your pregnancy doctor if pregnant; an eye doctor if vision changes
Common in
Anyone; cat owners and people who eat undercooked meat or handle soil
Contagious
No, except from a mother to her baby during pregnancy
Course
Often mild and settles in weeks to months; the parasite can stay quietly in the body

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Touching cat droppings (for example cat litter) and then touching your mouth
  • Eating raw or undercooked meat, especially pork, lamb or goat
  • A mother can pass it to her baby during pregnancy

See a doctor first if

  • Severe headache, confusion, a fit, weakness on one side or trouble speaking: call 999
  • Sudden blurry vision, a red painful eye or many new floating spots: see an eye doctor the same day
  • If you are pregnant and unwell with fever or swollen glands: see your doctor soon
  • A weak immune system (HIV, cancer, transplant) with fever, headache or a new cough: see a doctor promptly
  • A newborn baby who is yellow, not feeding well or very sleepy: see a doctor promptly
  • Swollen glands, fever or tiredness that last for weeks

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Cook meat all the way through and avoid raw or rare meat
  • Wash fruit and vegetables well; drink boiled or bottled water
  • If you are pregnant or have a weak immune system, ask someone else to clean the cat litter

Tell your therapist

  • Pregnancy
  • Fever or an active infection
  • Cancer or cancer treatment

Your therapist will go through this with you before starting.

Questions and answers

What is Toxoplasmosis?

An infection by a tiny parasite found in cat droppings, raw meat and soil; often mild or silent. Many people who pick up this parasite feel fine or have a mild flu-like illness, but it matters most in pregnancy and for people with a weak immune system.

What are the common signs of Toxoplasmosis?

Swollen glands in the neck · Fever, feeling hot · Aching all over · Tired all the time

Why does Toxoplasmosis happen?

Unsafe food or water · A weak immune system

How can I look after myself with Toxoplasmosis?

Wash your hands often · Rest well
