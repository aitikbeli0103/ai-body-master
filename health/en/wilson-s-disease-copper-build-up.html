Wilson's disease (copper build-up)

An inherited condition where copper slowly builds up in the liver, brain and other organs

ConditionLiver, gallbladder & pancreasAbdomen

What it is

The body cannot clear extra copper properly, so it builds up over time. It can affect the liver, nerves and mood. It runs in families and needs a specialist.

  • Liver and brain
  • Runs in families
  • Tremor, tiredness, mood change
Which doctor
Liver specialist or neurologist; genetics doctor
Common in
Young people and young adults; often others in the family
Contagious
No, it is inherited
Course
Lifelong; with regular care from a specialist, people can often stay well

Common signs

More signs

  • A brownish ring around the coloured part of the eye, seen by a doctor

Why it happens

Other causes

  • The body cannot send extra copper out through the bile
  • A brother or sister with it raises the chance
  • It is not caused by food or lifestyle

See a doctor first if

  • Confusion, fits or fainting: call 999
  • Vomiting blood or black stools: go to hospital now
  • Yellow skin or eyes with a swollen belly
  • New shaking, trouble speaking or swallowing, or clumsy walking
  • Thoughts of harming yourself: see a doctor now or call 999

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
