Kidney infection (pyelonephritis)

A germ infection that reaches one or both kidneys, often from the bladder

ConditionKidneys & urinaryAbdomen

What it is

Germs travel up from the bladder to the kidney. It can make you feel quite unwell with fever and side pain, and it needs to be seen by a doctor.

  • Kidney and side of back
  • More often in women
  • Fever, chills, side pain
Which doctor
Family doctor (see the same day); kidney or urinary specialist if it returns
Common in
Women, pregnant women, older adults, people with diabetes or stones
Contagious
No, but the germs can come from the bowel or skin area
Course
Often improves in days to weeks with a doctor's care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Germs from the bowel or skin that enter the urine opening and climb up
  • A bladder infection that was not looked after
  • Kidney stones or anything that blocks urine flow

See a doctor first if

  • Confusion, fainting, fast breathing or cold clammy skin: call 999
  • High fever with shaking chills or pain in the side: see a doctor the same day
  • Vomiting so that you cannot keep fluids or medicine down
  • Pregnancy with fever or side pain: go to hospital
  • A baby, child or older person with fever and pain: see a doctor promptly

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Do not hold in urine; empty the bladder when you need to
  • Wipe from front to back and keep the area clean and dry

What is Kidney infection (pyelonephritis)?

A germ infection that reaches one or both kidneys, often from the bladder. Germs travel up from the bladder to the kidney. It can make you feel quite unwell with fever and side pain, and it needs to be seen by a doctor.

What are the common signs of Kidney infection (pyelonephritis)?

Fever, feeling hot · Shivering with chills · Lower back pain · Burning when passing urine · Needing the toilet often · Feeling sick, nausea
