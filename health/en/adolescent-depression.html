Adolescent depression

Long-lasting sadness, loss of interest or irritability in a young person

ConditionTold by a doctor: moodWhole body

What it is

Feeling low is part of growing up, but depression is more than a bad mood. It lasts for weeks, affects school, friends and sleep, and a doctor can help.

  • Mind and feelings
  • Teens and young people
  • Low mood, no interest
Which doctor
Family doctor, paediatrician, child or adolescent psychiatrist
Common in
Teens, sometimes with school, family or friend stress
Contagious
No
Course
Can last weeks or months; many young people improve with support and care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Body changes in the teen years
  • A loss, a hard family time, or depression in the family

See a doctor first if

  • Talk of suicide or harming themselves: call 999 or go to hospital now, and stay with them
  • Cuts or injuries the young person made on purpose: see a doctor promptly
  • Not eating, not sleeping, or refusing school for a long time
  • Giving away favourite things or saying goodbye
  • Using alcohol, vape or drugs to cope

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Questions and answers

What is Adolescent depression?

Long-lasting sadness, loss of interest or irritability in a young person. Feeling low is part of growing up, but depression is more than a bad mood. It lasts for weeks, affects school, friends and sleep, and a doctor can help.

What are the common signs of Adolescent depression?

Low mood, no interest · Short-tempered, on edge · Cannot fall asleep · Tired all the time · Foggy, cannot focus

Why does Adolescent depression happen?

The cause is not fully known · Stress

How can I look after myself with Adolescent depression?

Get good sleep · Stay active every day

Can an AI Body Master session help with Adolescent depression?

A session is gentle, needle-free meridian support for your wellbeing. It does not treat or cure Adolescent depression and never replaces your medicine or your doctor. Tell your therapist about it before the session starts.
