Ulnar nerve injury (little-finger side of the hand)

Damage to the ulnar nerve that can cause numb, weak ring and little fingers

ConditionShoulder, arm & handArms & legs

What it is

The ulnar nerve runs past the inside of the elbow to the little-finger side of the hand. When it is pressed or hurt, those fingers may tingle, feel numb or grip weakly.

  • Elbow to little finger
  • After pressure or injury
  • Numb, tingling fingers
Which doctor
Family doctor, orthopaedic or nerve (neurology) doctor
Common in
People who lean on their elbows a lot, or after an elbow injury
Contagious
No
Course
Often improves when pressure is removed; severe cases need a doctor's care

Common signs

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Leaning on the elbow for long, such as at a desk or when driving
  • Keeping the elbow bent for long, such as holding a phone or sleeping with bent arms
  • Swelling or old joint changes around the elbow

See a doctor first if

  • Sudden arm weakness with face drooping or speech trouble: call 999
  • A bent or swollen elbow after a fall: go to hospital now
  • A pale, cold or bluish hand: go to hospital now
  • Weak grip, thinning hand muscles or fingers curling in
  • Numbness that keeps getting worse

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.

This page is general information. Please keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor.

Keep your normal care

Keep taking your normal medicine and keep seeing your doctor. Our session is a gentle support and does not replace medical care.

Looking after yourself

More ways to look after yourself

  • Avoid leaning on the elbow; pad it if you must rest it on a hard surface
  • Try not to keep the elbow tightly bent for long periods
  • See a doctor if it lasts, so the cause can be checked

Tell your therapist

  • Numb or tingling arm
  • Weak bones
  • Pacemaker or metal implant
  • Diabetes or less feeling in the skin
