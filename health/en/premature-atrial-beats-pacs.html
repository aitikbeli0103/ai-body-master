Premature atrial beats (PACs)

Heartbeats that come early from the upper chambers of the heart

ConditionHeart & blood vesselsChest

What it is

The upper part of the heart sometimes beats a little too early. You may feel a skipped beat, a flutter or a thump, and it is often harmless.

  • Chest and heartbeat
  • Can happen at any age
  • A skipped or fluttering beat
Which doctor
Family doctor, heart doctor (cardiologist)
Common in
Adults of all ages; more with age
Contagious
No
Course
Often comes and goes; many people have no problem

Common signs

More signs

  • A feeling that the heart skipped a beat
  • A flutter or a hard thump in the chest or neck
  • A short pause felt before the next beat
  • Sometimes feeling slightly dizzy or uneasy

Why it happens

Other causes

  • Too much coffee, tea or energy drinks
  • A heart or thyroid problem, or low body salts
  • Sometimes no cause is found

See a doctor first if

  • Palpitations with chest pain, trouble breathing or fainting: call 999
  • Face drooping, arm weakness or speech trouble: call 999
  • A fast heartbeat that does not settle
  • Beats that are getting more frequent
  • Known heart disease and new palpitations

If any apply, please see a doctor before a session.
